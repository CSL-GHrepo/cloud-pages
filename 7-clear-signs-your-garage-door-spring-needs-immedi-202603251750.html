Maintenance Tips for Parkland Garage Doors

Regular maintenance keeps your garage door performing at its best. In Parkland, where humidity and weather can impact parts, scheduled inspections are especially important. A few simple routines can extend the lifespan of your garage system.

First, lubricate moving parts like rollers, hinges, and springs every six months. Use a silicone-based lubricant to prevent rust and ensure smooth operation. Check the balance of your garage door by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting it. If it doesn’t stay in place, professional adjustment is needed.

Inspect the tracks for debris or misalignment. Clean and tighten loose bolts or screws to prevent operational issues. Test the safety sensors by closing the door and blocking the sensor’s path; the door should stop and reverse automatically.

Keep weather stripping on the bottom of the door in good condition to prevent drafts and pests. If it shows signs of wear, replace it promptly. Regularly check the cables and springs for signs of wear or damage. Never attempt to repair or replace these yourself — call professionals for safety reasons.

Scheduling annual maintenance with a trusted local technician can prevent emergencies and costly repairs. Our team offers comprehensive service plans that keep your garage door reliable, secure, and functioning smoothly year-round.
