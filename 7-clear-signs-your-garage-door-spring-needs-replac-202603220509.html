Step-by-Step Guide to Garage Door Maintenance in Parkland

Regular maintenance keeps your garage door running smoothly and extends its lifespan. Here’s a simple step-by-step guide to help you stay ahead of common issues in Parkland’s climate.

Start by inspecting the hardware and tightening any loose bolts or screws. Over time, vibrations from daily use can loosen parts, causing misalignments or noise.

Next, check the rollers and tracks for debris, dents, or corrosion. Clean the tracks with a damp cloth and lubricate moving parts with a silicone-based lubricant. This reduces wear and noise.

Test the safety features, such as reverse sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms, to ensure they work correctly. This is vital for household safety, especially with children and pets.

Examine the balance of the door by manually lifting it about halfway open. It should stay in place. If it doesn’t, spring adjustments may be needed—call our professionals for help.

Finally, test the remote and keypad for proper operation. Replace batteries if needed, and consider upgrading to a smart opener for added convenience and security.
