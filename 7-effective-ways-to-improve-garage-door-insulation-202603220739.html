Common Garage Door Problems in Parkland Homes

These issues can make your garage door unsafe or completely inoperable, disrupting your daily routine. — The Garage Door Pros serves the greater Parkland area.

What most people don't realize is how critical springs are—they bear the weight of the door and allow smooth operation. When springs break, the door may fall suddenly, posing a safety hazard. Addressing this quickly can prevent accidents and additional damage to the opener or tracks.

Another frequent concern is motor failure or worn-out rollers. Over time, motors can overheat or burn out, particularly with heavy use in busy households. Tracks can also become bent or misaligned from accidental impacts or natural wear, causing the door to jam or operate unevenly.

Sensors are essential for safety, especially in homes with children or pets. If sensors aren’t correctly aligned or are dirty, the door may refuse to close or reverse unexpectedly, leading to potential injuries or property damage.

In many cases, simple issues like loose hardware or lubrication neglect cause operational difficulties. Routine inspections and timely maintenance can solve many of these problems before they develop into costly repairs.
