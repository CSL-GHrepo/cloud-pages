Essential Real Estate Terms for Parkland Sellers

Here’s the thing—understanding common real estate terms helps you navigate the selling process confidently. One key term is ‘liens,’ which are claims against your property for unpaid debts. Clearing liens is essential before closing. Another important term is ‘power of sale,’ which allows a bank to sell a property if the mortgage isn’t paid—relevant in foreclosure situations. Lastly, ‘earnest money’ refers to the deposit a buyer makes to show serious intent, though it’s less relevant in cash transactions where we buy homes directly.

What most people don’t realize is that familiarity with legal terms like ‘title transfer’ and ‘deed’ can speed up the closing process. The ‘title’ is proof of ownership, and ensuring it’s clear is vital before sale. ‘Settlement statements’ detail transaction costs and are reviewed at closing. Knowing these terms enables you to ask informed questions and understand all documents involved in your sale.
