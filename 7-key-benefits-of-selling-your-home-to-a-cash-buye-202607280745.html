Traditional buyers often require financing, and loan approvals can fall through after days or weeks of waiting. This uncertainty means sellers risk having their contracts collapse last-minute—costing time and money.

When you sell to a cash_home_buyer, the sale usually isn’t conditioned on financing approval, drastically reducing the risk of sales falling apart unexpectedly. The buyer has the funds ready, ensuring a reliable transaction.
