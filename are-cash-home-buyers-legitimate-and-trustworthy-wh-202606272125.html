Are There Risks? How to Avoid Cash Buyer Scams

While many cash home buyers are legitimate, the market has attracted some bad actors looking to exploit vulnerable sellers. Here’s how to protect yourself:

  • Demand Transparency: Insist on clear written offers without vague terms or hidden fees.
  • Avoid Upfront Fees: Legitimate cash buyers will not require significant money before closing.
