Is Replacement Always Mandatory or Can Windows Be Repaired?

Not all hurricane-damaged windows require full replacement. Many situations allow for professional impact window repair that restores the window’s integrity and compliance:

  • Minor Glass Damage: Single-pane cracks or chips may be repaired without full replacement.
  • Frame Damage: Small repairs to window framing or seals can extend window life.
  • Seal Failures: Moisture intrusion or fogging between panes often requires resealing or glass unit replacement.

However, extensive damage to the glazing, frames, or hardware generally necessitates full replacement under mandatory window replacement Florida laws to meet hurricane impact-resistant window standards.
