Top Tips for Maintaining Your Locks in Parkland

Proper maintenance can extend the life of your locks and prevent emergencies. Regularly inspect your locks for signs of wear, rust, or misalignment. Lubricate moving parts with a graphite-based lubricant annually to keep them functioning smoothly. — our lock repair team.

Avoid using excessive force when turning keys or locking mechanisms. If you notice stiffness or difficulty, call a locksmith before the problem worsens. Immediate attention can prevent lock failures and security breaches.

Change your locks promptly after moving into a new property or experiencing a break-in. Rekeying or replacing compromised hardware ensures your safety isn’t at risk.

Install smart lock systems if you want added convenience and security. These can be remotely controlled and provide audit trails of access, especially useful for busy households or commercial spaces.

Keep keys in a secure, designated spot and avoid leaving spare keys in obvious places like under mats or plants. Consider giving copies only to trusted individuals.
