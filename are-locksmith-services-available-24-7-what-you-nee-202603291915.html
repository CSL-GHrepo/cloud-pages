Essential Lock Security Tips for Parkland Homes and Businesses

In my experience, preventing break-ins starts with proper lock security. Here are some tips for residents and business owners in Parkland to enhance your safety:

First, always use high-quality locks that meet ANSI or UL standards. Cheap locks can be easily bypassed or broken. Second, rekey your locks after any move-in or suspected security breach to prevent old keys from working.

Third, consider upgrading to electronic or smart locks for better control and audit trails. These systems allow you to monitor access remotely and change codes easily. Fourth, reinforce doors and frames to resist forced entry—adding strike plates or door jamb shields can make a difference.

Fifth, install security cameras and lighting around entry points. Visible surveillance deters potential intruders and helps identify suspicious activity.

Finally, don’t forget to maintain your locks by lubricating them annually and inspecting for wear and tear. Regular upkeep prevents unexpected failures and prolongs lock life.

  • Use high-quality, ANSI/UL-rated locks
  • Rekey after move-ins or security incidents
  • Upgrade to electronic or smart locks
  • Reinforce doors and frames
  • Install surveillance and proper lighting
  • Regular lock maintenance and inspections
