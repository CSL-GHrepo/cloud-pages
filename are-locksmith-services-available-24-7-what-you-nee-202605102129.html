Understanding Locksmith Service Costs for Emergency Situations

Price transparency is important when choosing a locksmith for urgent needs. Emergency locksmith services often carry an after-hours surcharge due to the immediacy and unconventional timing. Typical locksmith service costs for emergency calls can range from:

  • $75 to $150 for simple lockout assistance during regular hours
  • $150 to $300 or more for after-hours or weekend calls
  • Additional fees if new locks or complex lock repairs are needed

It’s advisable to inquire about all costs upfront, including mileage or service call fees, to avoid surprises. Mobile locksmith services might charge based on distance traveled, so regional variances can affect pricing.
