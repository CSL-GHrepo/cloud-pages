Maintaining Your Locks for Long-Term Security

Prevention is always better than cure. Regular maintenance of your locks can prevent unexpected failures and extend their lifespan. In Parkland, we recommend inspecting your locks at least once a year.

Lubricate locks periodically with appropriate products to keep mechanisms operating smoothly. Avoid using oil-based lubricants, which can attract dirt and grime.

Check door alignment and strike plates, ensuring the latch properly fits into the frame. Misaligned doors can cause lock issues over time.

Rekey locks after moving into a new property or if keys are lost or stolen. Rekeying is a cost-effective way to enhance security without replacing entire locks.

Upgrade to modern electronic or smart locks if you want enhanced control and convenience. These systems often require less maintenance and are more resistant to tampering.
