Essential Locksmith Glossary for Parkland Homeowners

Deadbolt: A lock bolt that extends into the door frame for extra security, often used on exterior doors.

Rekeying: Changing the internal pins of a lock so a new key can operate it, without replacing the entire lock.

Access control: Electronic systems that regulate who can enter a property, often via key cards or codes.

Master key system: A setup where a single key opens multiple locks, each with individual keys for authorized users.

Smart lock: An electronic lock controlled remotely, offering keyless entry and added convenience.
