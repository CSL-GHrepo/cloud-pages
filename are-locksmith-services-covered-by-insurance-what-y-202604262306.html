Quick Answer: Are Locksmith Services Covered by Insurance?

Locksmith services are not universally covered by insurance but may be reimbursable under certain conditions—primarily through homeowners insurance for issues related to break-ins or lost keys, or auto insurance add-ons such as roadside assistance. Always check your insurance policy locksmith section for specific inclusions, exclusions, and claim procedures.

Understanding Locksmith Insurance Coverage: The Basics

Most insurance policies do not automatically cover locksmith service expenses, but this varies greatly:

  • Homeowners Insurance: Many homeowners insurance policies include provisions for locksmith services if the lockout is due to a covered peril such as theft or vandalism. For example, if a break-in damages your locks, your policy may cover the replacement cost.
  • Auto Insurance: Standard auto insurance typically excludes locksmith services; however, roadside assistance or comprehensive coverage add-ons often reimburse emergency locksmith costs related to vehicle lockouts.
  • Renters Insurance: Similar to homeowners insurance, renters policies sometimes cover lock replacement if the keys are stolen.

What Locksmith Services Are Typically Covered?

Insurance policies vary, but commonly covered locksmith services include:

  • Lock replacement or repair following a break-in.
  • Emergency lockout assistance when keys are lost or stolen.
  • Lock upgrades if mandated after a theft to improve security.

Services generally not covered include:

  • Lockouts caused by personal negligence (e.g., forgetting keys without theft involved).
  • Lost car keys if roadside assistance is not included.
  • Routine rekeying or lock changes for convenience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does homeowners insurance typically cover locksmith service expenses?

Homeowners insurance often covers locksmith services if the lockout or lock replacement results from a covered event like theft or vandalism. However, routine lockouts or lost keys without evidence of a covered peril are usually excluded. Always check your specific policy and discuss with your insurer.

Are emergency locksmith costs covered under auto insurance policies?

Usually, standard auto insurance does not cover locksmith services. However, many drivers add roadside assistance coverage to their policies, which may include emergency locksmith services such as car lockouts. Verify your insurance policy locksmith section or contact your provider for details.

How can I get reimbursed for a locksmith service through insurance?

To get reimbursement, keep detailed invoices and, if applicable, police reports. File a claim promptly with your insurance provider, providing all documentation and following their claim procedures. Reimbursement depends on your coverage terms and the reason for the locksmith service.

Are locksmith services for lost keys always covered by insurance?

Not always. Lost key coverage varies by policy type and insurer. Lost keys resulting from theft are more likely to be covered than simple misplacement. Some policies require add-ons or endorsements to reimburse locksmith service expenses related to lost keys.
