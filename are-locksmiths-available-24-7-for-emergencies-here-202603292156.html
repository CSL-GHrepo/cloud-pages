Preventative Measures to Secure Your Property

Prevention is always better than repair. In Parkland, many residents proactively upgrade their locks to high-security models that resist picking and drilling.

Education plays a role too—keep spare keys in safe places, avoid hiding keys outside, and be cautious about who has access to your keys.
