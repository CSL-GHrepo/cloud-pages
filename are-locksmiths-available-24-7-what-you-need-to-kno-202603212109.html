Locksmith Terminology Every Parkland Resident Should Know

Understanding some common locksmith terms can make interactions much easier. Knowing what these words mean helps you ask the right questions and understand your options better. — Lockguard Locksmiths serves the greater Parkland area.

A 'deadbolt' is a lock bolt that extends into the door frame, providing extra security. It’s a standard feature in most home locks but can vary in strength and type.

A 'keyway' refers to the specific shape and design of a lock’s key slot. Different locks have different keyways, which affect key duplication and security levels.

The term 'rekeying' describes changing the internal pins of a lock so that it works with a new key, without replacing the entire lock hardware.

An 'access control system' is a security setup that restricts entry using electronic methods like keycards or biometric scans. It’s common in businesses and high-end homes.

'Pick-resistant locks' are designed to prevent lock picking, providing a higher level of security against unauthorized entry.
