Understanding Maintenance Coverage in Copier Leases

Is maintenance coverage automatically included? Often yes, but it depends on the lease agreement terms. Many copier rental maintenance plans are bundled with “full-service” leases, which means all service calls, parts, and labor are covered.

“Industry professionals recommend carefully reviewing the lease agreement to confirm what level of service and repairs are included, as variations exist depending on vendor and contract type.” – Laura Kim, Commercial Leasing Consultant

There are generally two types of agreements:

  1. Full-Service Lease: Maintenance and repairs are fully included. This often includes both preventive and breakdown repairs, reducing your risk of unexpected expenses.
  2. Net Lease or Service Contract Separate: Maintenance and repairs billed separately or a service contract must be purchased in addition to the lease.

For example, in a full-service lease, the copier leasing company might schedule quarterly maintenance visits, replace worn parts such as drums or rollers at no additional cost, and respond quickly to service calls. Conversely, if your lease excludes maintenance, you might pay hourly repair fees or purchase separate maintenance contracts.

Included Repair Costs: What Should You Expect?

Understanding included repair costs is crucial for budgeting. According to a survey by CODA Market Research (2022), businesses saved on average 25-30% on copier upkeep costs when opting for leases with maintenance coverage included.

Typical included repair costs cover:

  • Mechanical and electrical repairs resulting from normal use;
  • Parts and labor charges related to breakdowns;
  • Software updates if the copier requires them;
  • Supply replacement related to machine functionality (e.g., fusers, belts).

However, damage due to misuse, accidents, or negligence is usually excluded and billed separately. Additionally, consumables like toner or paper are often not included unless specified.

Service Level Agreements for Copier Leasing: What They Mean for You

Service level agreements (SLAs) copier policies define expected response times, resolution windows, and service quality benchmarks. High-quality copier leasing providers include SLAs to ensure prompt, reliable service—minimizing downtime.

  • Response Time: Many providers guarantee a technician visit within 24-48 hours.
  • Uptime Guarantees: Some contracts specify minimum operational uptime percentages, backed by penalties or credits if not met.
  • Remote Support: Quick troubleshooting via phone or internet may be offered to resolve issues faster.

With defined SLA terms, businesses can trust that maintenance coverage in copier leases is not just lip service but a commitment to efficiency and accountability.
