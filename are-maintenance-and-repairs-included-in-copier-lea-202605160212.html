Here are detailed items to confirm when reviewing copier leasing service packages:

Frequently Asked Questions

Are maintenance and repair services usually included in copier leasing contracts?

Maintenance and repair services are commonly included in full-service copier leasing contracts but may not be part of finance or basic leases. It is critical to review the copier service agreements carefully to confirm the scope of coverage and any additional fees.

Who is responsible for repairs in copier leasing agreements?

Responsibility for repairs depends on the lease type and contract terms. In full-service leases, the lessor typically handles repairs at no extra cost. For finance leases, the lessee generally assumes repair responsibilities unless a separate maintenance plan is purchased.

What maintenance fees can I expect for leased copiers?

Maintenance fees for leased copiers vary; some agreements bundle maintenance into the monthly lease payment, while others charge separately based on service visits, parts, and labor. Reviewing leased copier maintenance policies beforehand helps avoid unexpected fees.

How can I ensure my copier lease includes sufficient repair coverage?

To ensure adequate repair coverage, opt for full-service leases and thoroughly read the copier lease contract repairs section. Ask the leasing company for detailed service packages, response times, and exclusions to ensure comprehensive protection.
