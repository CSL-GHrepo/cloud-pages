Frequently Asked Questions

How can I verify if a pool service company in Plantation is licensed?

You can verify licensing by visiting the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) online portal and searching the company or technician’s license number. Many licensed pool companies will also provide proof of licensing upon request.

Why is insurance important when hiring a pool service company in Plantation, FL?

Insurance protects you from liability in case of accidental damage or injuries that occur during pool maintenance. General liability and workers' compensation cover most common incidents, reducing your financial risk.

Are all pool technicians in Plantation required to be licensed?

Yes, under Florida law, pool technicians performing maintenance, repairs, or inspections must be licensed by the DBPR. This ensures they meet the state-mandated skill and safety standards.

What risks do I face if I hire an unlicensed or uninsured pool company in Plantation?

Hiring unlicensed or uninsured companies can expose you to unsafe practices, poor workmanship, and significant financial liability if accidents or damages occur during service. Non-compliant companies may also violate local Plantation pool service regulations, risking legal penalties.
