Navigating Dallas’ Commercial Lease Structures with Auten Realty

Commercial leasing in Dallas involves complex agreements that vary widely depending on property type, tenant needs, and market conditions. Auten Realty’s expertise in lease structuring helps clients avoid pitfalls and negotiate terms that maximize value and flexibility.

Common Lease Types Explained

  • Gross Lease: The tenant pays a fixed rent amount, with the landlord covering most property expenses. Suitable for businesses seeking predictable costs with limited management responsibilities.
  • Net Lease: Tenants pay base rent plus some or all operating expenses such as taxes, insurance, and maintenance. Variants include Single Net (N), Double Net (NN), and Triple Net (NNN) leases offering differing expense allocations.
  • Modified Gross Lease: A hybrid structure where some costs are shared or adjusted based on negotiation outcomes.
  • Percentage Lease: Common in retail, this lease includes a base rent plus a percentage of the tenant’s gross sales as additional rent.

Critical Lease Negotiation Points

Auten Realty advises tenants and landlords on key negotiation elements that can significantly impact the long-term financial and operational viability of a lease:

  • Rent Escalations: How rent increases are calculated and timed over the lease term.
  • Tenant Improvement Allowances: Budget and scope of modifications made to customize space.
  • Renewal Options: Terms and conditions for extending the lease beyond its initial term.
  • Subleasing and Assignment Rights: Flexibility to transfer lease rights if business needs change.
  • Exit Clauses: Conditions permitting early termination without penalties.

By thoroughly understanding these components, Auten Realty ensures businesses secure leases aligned with their growth trajectories and risk tolerances.
