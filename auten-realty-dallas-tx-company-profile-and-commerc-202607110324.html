Understanding Dallas Commercial Lease Structures with Auten Realty

Commercial leases come in various configurations that significantly impact tenant costs and landlord revenues. Auten Realty’s brokers excel in explaining lease nuances, enabling clients to negotiate agreements aligned with their financial and operational objectives.

Common Lease Types in Dallas Commercial Real Estate

  • Gross Lease: Tenant pays a fixed rent, while the landlord covers most operating expenses. Suitable for office tenants seeking predictable monthly costs.
  • Net Lease: Tenant pays base rent plus some or all property expenses such as taxes, insurance, or maintenance. Variations include single net, double net, and triple net leases.
  • Modified Gross Lease: A hybrid structure where landlord and tenant share operating expenses based on negotiated terms.
  • Percentage Lease: Common in retail, where rent includes a base amount plus a percentage of tenant sales.

By dissecting the implications of each lease type for cash flow and risk allocation, Auten Realty helps businesses and investors structure agreements that optimize their bottom line and align with market standards.
