Specialized Expertise: Lease Negotiation Tactics and Custom Lease Structuring

Negotiating commercial leases in Dallas demands a nuanced approach tailored to specific asset classes and tenant requirements. Auten Realty’s brokerage team specializes in leveraging in-depth market intelligence and negotiation skills to secure favorable lease terms.

Understanding Common Lease Types

  • Triple Net (NNN) Leases: Tenants assume responsibility for property taxes, insurance, and maintenance, frequently used in industrial and retail sectors.
  • Full-Service Gross Leases: Landlord covers operating expenses; more common in office buildings seeking simplified tenant billing.
  • Modified Gross Leases: Hybrid structures where expenses are shared or allocated uniquely based on negotiations.

Key Lease Negotiation Elements

  • Rent Escalations: Gradual increases linked to Consumer Price Index (CPI) or predetermined percentages.
  • Tenant Improvement Allowances: Negotiating landlord-funded build-out budgets aligned with tenant needs and competitive market standards.
  • Renewal and Expansion Options: Structuring terms that enable tenants to scale operations flexibly without incurring prohibitive costs.
  • Co-tenancy and Exclusivity Clauses: Protecting tenants’ operational interests and business positioning within multi-tenant environments.
