  • Conventional Bank Loans: Favorable for borrowers with strong credit and steady cash flows, offering competitive interest rates and reasonable terms.

  • Build-to-Suit and Construction Loans: Supporting developments or significant renovations in emerging Dallas submarkets.

Auten Realty’s advisory team assists clients in understanding the pros and cons of each funding option and collaborates with a network of trusted lenders, including local banks and national financial institutions.
