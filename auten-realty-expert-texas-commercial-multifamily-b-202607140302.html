Financing and Capital Markets in Dallas Commercial Real Estate

Types of Financing Solutions Offered

  • Traditional Bank Loans: Tailored for stabilized assets with clear cash flow histories, often used for multifamily and office acquisitions.
  • Bridge Financing: Short-term loans designed to facilitate acquisitions or renovations prior to securing permanent financing.
  • CMBS (Commercial Mortgage-Backed Securities): Structured finance solutions suitable for larger transactions, offering competitive rates and long-term amortization.
  • Joint Ventures and Equity Partnerships: Aligning investors and operators to pool capital and expertise for complex developments or repositioning projects.
  • SBA Loans: For small- and mid-sized businesses acquiring commercial real estate, offering attractive terms backed by federal guarantees.
