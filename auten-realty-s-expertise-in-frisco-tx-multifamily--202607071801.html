Understanding Key Financial Metrics in Frisco Multifamily Investment

Investors looking to succeed in Frisco’s multifamily market must master several critical financial metrics. Auten Realty not only understands these metrics intimately but also educates clients to ensure aligned investment goals.

Net Operating Income (NOI)

NOI represents the income generated by a property after deducting operating expenses but before debt service and taxes. In Frisco's rental market, maximizing NOI means carefully balancing rent pricing with occupancy and expense control.

Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate)

The cap rate, or yield, is calculated by dividing the NOI by the property’s purchase price. Auten Realty uses cap rate analysis to compare Frisco multifamily assets relative to local and regional market benchmarks.

Cash-on-Cash Return (CoC)

CoC measures the cash income earned on the actual cash invested. Auten Realty assists clients in structuring deals that optimize CoC through financing strategies and operational efficiencies.

Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

IRR estimates the annualized return over the investment holding period, incorporating cash flow and eventual sale proceeds. Auten Realty’s financial models forecast IRR scenarios helping clients decide timing and exit strategies.
