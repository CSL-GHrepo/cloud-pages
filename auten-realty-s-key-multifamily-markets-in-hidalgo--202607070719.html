Regulatory Environment and Zoning Considerations in Hidalgo County

Understanding local regulations is essential to successful multifamily investment and development. Hidalgo County features varied zoning classifications that directly impact permitted density, building heights, and usage types.

Multifamily Zoning Districts

Main multifamily zones include “MF-1” through “MF-4,” each allowing differing density levels and setbacks. For example:

  • MF-1: Low-density multifamily, max 12 units per acre, ideal for garden-style apartments and townhomes.
  • MF-3 and MF-4: Higher density allowances enabling mid-rise structures exceeding 30 units per acre, prevalent near transit corridors.
