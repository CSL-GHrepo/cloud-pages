Understanding Financing Options for Horizon City Multifamily Investments

Financing multifamily properties requires navigation of multiple lending avenues, each with its own terms, benefits, and qualification criteria. Auten Realty collaborates with a network of Texas-based lenders and financial institutions who specialize in multifamily transactions. Key financing options include:

  • Conventional Bank Loans: Traditional fixed and adjustable-rate loans with loan-to-value (LTV) ratios typically between 70-80%. Ideal for newer properties with stable income and strong borrower credit profiles.

During the financing planning stage, Auten Realty ensures investors understand crucial metrics such as debt service coverage ratio (DSCR), loan-to-cost (LTC), and internal rate of return (IRR). This education empowers clients to select the optimal financing structure for their investment strategy.
