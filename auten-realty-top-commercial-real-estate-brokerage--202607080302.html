Commercial Lease Structuring and Negotiation Expertise

One of the critical services Auten Realty provides is expert guidance on lease structuring to protect tenant interests and maximize landlord returns. Their brokers possess deep knowledge of Dallas’ commercial leasing norms and tailor agreements to mitigate risk and optimize flexibility.

Key Lease Types and Strategic Considerations

Clients benefit from understanding varying lease structures including:

  • Triple Net (NNN) Leases: Tenants pay base rent plus property taxes, insurance, and maintenance, often preferred for industrial and retail spaces.
  • Modified Gross Leases: Costs are shared, providing tenants with clearer budgeting and landlords with stable income.
  • Full-Service Gross Leases: Common in office leasing, all operating expenses are bundled into a flat rent.

Auten Realty helps clients negotiate crucial lease provisions, such as renewal options, tenant improvement allowances, subleasing rights, and escalation clauses, ensuring lease terms align with long-term business plans.

Negotiation Success Stories

In a recent example, Auten Realty assisted a retail tenant in securing a favorable long-term NNN lease that included a tenant improvement credit and a step-down rent structure aligned with phased business expansion. The lease terms enabled the tenant to manage cash flow effectively during initial growth stages.
