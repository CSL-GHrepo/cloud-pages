Understanding Copier Lease Rates in 2024

Copier lease rates depend on several key factors:

  • Type of Copier: Simple black-and-white copiers cost less to lease—typically $50 to $100 per month—while color multifunction printers with scanning, faxing, and wireless capabilities command $150 to $300 monthly.
  • Monthly Volume: Lease agreements often include a base volume of monthly copies; exceeding this incurs additional per-page charges.
  • Lease Term: Typical leasing terms range from 24 to 60 months. Longer terms usually mean lower monthly payments but higher total cost over the contract lifetime.
  • Included Services: Maintenance, supply replenishment, and software updates can be bundled, affecting the monthly price.

For example, a mid-tier business copier with multifunction features leased over 36 months might have a monthly lease payment of approximately $180, which includes service and consumables like toner. Smaller businesses opting for basic black-and-white copiers can expect monthly copier lease payments closer to $75.

Cost of Leasing Multifunction Printers: What to Expect

Multifunction printers (MFPs) remain the most popular office copiers leased in 2024 due to their versatility. Business copier rental agreements for MFPs typically include printing, scanning, copying, and sometimes faxing, which adds complexity—and cost—to leases.

Industry professionals recommend factoring in total cost of ownership (TCO), including service contracts and consumables, when analyzing copier lease vs buy decisions. As John Matthews, an office solutions consultant, states, “Leasing an MFP offers predictable monthly costs, freeing companies from upfront capital expenses and unexpected repair bills.”

Costs to lease multifunction printers vary:

  • Entry-level MFPs: $100–$150/month
  • Mid-range MFPs with advanced scanning and finishing options: $150–$250/month
  • High-end MFPs for larger enterprises: $250–$400/month

Additionally, commercial copier rental prices often include flexible volume tiers to ensure scalability as your business grows.

Small Business Copier Leasing Expenses: Strategies for Cost Control

Small businesses face unique challenges balancing budget constraints with the need for reliable office equipment. Leasing remains a popular option for small businesses due to lower initial costs and flexible contract terms.

Here are effective strategies to manage small business copier leasing expenses:

  1. Assess Your Usage: Analyze monthly document volume to avoid paying for unnecessary copy limits.
  2. Negotiate Service Inclusions: Confirm if toner, maintenance, and repairs are included in the lease price.
  3. Compare Lease vs. Buy Analysis: Leasing reduces upfront costs, but long-term buying might save money if your usage is stable and predictable.
  4. Select the Appropriate Copier Class: Avoid overspending on high-tier machines if your printing needs are modest.
  5. Review Contract Flexibility: Look for leases with renewal options or buyout clauses to adapt as your business evolves.

Copier Lease vs Buy Analysis: Making the Right Choice in 2024

Many businesses ask whether leasing or buying copiers is more cost-effective. While purchasing offers asset ownership free of recurring payments, leasing provides predictable expenses and easy upgrades.

  • Technology Refresh: Leasing often includes upgrade options, keeping equipment current.
  • Maintenance Responsibility: Leases usually encompass maintenance and repairs, lowering unexpected costs.
  • Tax Benefits: Lease payments may be fully deductible as operating expenses.
