Key Elements Impacting Fix and Flip Returns

1. Renovation Cost vs Profit

One of the most critical factors shaping fix and flip success rates is how well renovation costs are controlled relative to the after-repair value (ARV). Over-investing in cosmetic or non-essential upgrades can erode profit margins quickly.

  • Tip: Prioritize upgrades that add measurable value, such as kitchen remodels, energy-efficient windows, and bathroom renovations.
