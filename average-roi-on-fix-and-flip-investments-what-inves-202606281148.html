How to Perform a House Flipping Investment Analysis for 2024

Investors interested in fixing and flipping must conduct detailed financial projections before committing. Here’s a streamlined process to assess potential ROI:

  • Step 1: Calculate After Repair Value (ARV) – Estimate the selling price post-renovation, using comparables (comps) in the neighborhood.
  • Step 2: Estimate Purchase Price – Factor in discounts for distressed or undervalued properties.
  • Step 3: Budget Renovation Costs – Include all materials, labor, permits, and contingency allowances.
  • Step 4: Consider Carrying Costs – Taxes, insurance, utilities, financing interest, and other holding expenses.
  • Step 5: Deduct Selling Costs – Agent commissions, closing fees, and other transaction expenses.

Using this formula:

Expected Profit = ARV – (Purchase Price + Renovation Cost + Carrying Cost + Selling Cost)

Then determine ROI by dividing expected profit by total investment costs (purchase price + renovation + holding costs).

Appropriate financial modeling like this aligns investments with your personal risk tolerance and market conditions.
