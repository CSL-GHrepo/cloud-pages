Quick Overview: How Long Does a Typical Fix and Flip Take?

On average, a fix and flip deal lasts between 3 to 6 months from purchase to resale. This timeline includes acquisition, property renovation, marketing, and sale. Industry data suggests that faster turnaround times normally lead to higher profitability, with experienced flippers completing projects closer to the 3-month mark.

According to ATTOM Data Solutions, 2022 nationwide fix and flip projects had an average duration of 6 months, with some high-performing investors reducing this to around 3-4 months.

The Real Estate Flipping Process: Key Phases & Time Allocations

The fix and flip timeline breaks down into distinct house flipping phases, each with its own time requirements and challenges:

  1. Acquisition & Initial Inspection (1-2 weeks)
    Finding the right property and performing due diligence are critical first steps. Most investors spend 1-2 weeks analyzing offers, conducting inspections, and closing the purchase.
  2. Planning & Rehab Budgeting (1 week)
    Developing a detailed rehab budget, sourcing contractors, and permitting can take about a week. Careful rehab budget planning here prevents costly overruns later.
  3. Property Renovation (6-12 weeks)
    The longest phase in the investment property turnaround is the renovation itself, which typically spans 1.5 to 3 months. The property renovation timeline depends heavily on scope—simple cosmetic upgrades run shorter, major structural or system repairs take longer.
  4. Marketing & Sale (2-4 weeks)
    Once the rehab is complete, real estate flipping strategies shift toward marketing and selling, which usually takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on market activity.

Why Renovation Duration Varies

  • Property Condition: Older or distressed properties require extensive repairs, lengthening the timeline.
  • Permits & Inspections: Local regulations can delay project timelines while approvals are secured.
  • Contractor Availability: Skilled labor shortages or scheduling issues often extend the home rehab schedule.
  • Scope of Work: Cosmetic upgrades like painting and flooring take a few weeks, while full gut rehabs may take several months.

Managing Your Fix and Flip Duration: Strategies for Success

To effectively control your fix and flip duration, consider these real estate flipping strategies:

  • Detailed Pre-Purchase Inspection: Identify costly repairs early to avoid surprises during renovation.
  • Robust Rehab Budget Planning: Allocate contingency funds (~10-15%) for unexpected expenses or delays.
  • Choose Experienced Contractors: Reliable teams expedite the renovation process and minimize hold-ups.
  • Streamline Permitting Process: Partner with local experts who understand regional building regulations.
  • Plan for Market Conditions: Timing your sale during seller-favorable markets reduces marketing time.

Regional Considerations That Impact Timeline

While fix and flip timelines are broadly similar across geographies, several regional factors influence the duration and complexity of rehab schedules:

  • Urban vs. Rural Markets: Urban projects may benefit from faster contractor access but can face stricter regulations, while rural projects might find lower costs but slower permit approvals.
  • Climate Factors: Weather can cause delays, especially in regions with harsh winters or rainy seasons.
  • Local Market Demand: Highly competitive markets facilitate quicker sales, shortening overall fix and flip duration.
