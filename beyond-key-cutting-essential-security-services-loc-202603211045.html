Locksmith Industry Terms You Should Know

Rekeying: Changing a lock so it works with a new key, without replacing the hardware. It's a cost-effective security upgrade after key loss or tenant change.

Deadbolt: A sturdy lock that extends a bolt into the door frame, providing enhanced security against forced entry. Commonly used on exterior doors.

Master Key System: A setup allowing one key to open multiple locks, ideal for property managers or multi-tenant buildings. It offers convenience and control.

Smart Lock: An electronic lock controlled via smartphone or keypad. Provides keyless entry and remote access, increasing home or business security.

latch: The part of a lock that holds the door shut when engaged. The latch mechanism is essential for door security and operation.
