Quick Overview: What Types of Damage Can Be Repaired?

Impact windows are composed of laminated glass and reinforced frames, engineered to resist storm impact and debris penetrations. Common damage types include:

  • Minor chips or surface scratches on the glass
  • Small cracks that have not penetrated the laminate layers
  • Frame dents, seal failures, or hardware malfunctions

These issues are typically suitable for impact window damage repair. Conversely, when damage compromises the laminated glass structure—such as deep cracks through both layers or full shattering—professional impact-resistant glass replacement is the only safe and effective option.

Common Damage Types and Appropriate Responses

1. Chips and Surface Scratches

These are often caused by small debris or accidental impacts. Skilled technicians use polishing and filling techniques to restore visual clarity and structural integrity, offering a cost-effective alternative to replacement.

2. Small Cracks or Sealant Failures

Cracks limited to the outer glass layer or compromised window seals—typical storm damage window fixing candidates—can be repaired with special adhesives and resealing treatments that prevent water intrusion and air leaks.

3. Frame and Hardware Issues

Frames can dent or seals can break due to impact or age. These are highly repairable, often restoring full function without replacing entire units.

4. Damaged Laminated Glass or Shattered Windows

When impact windows are shattered or cracked through both layers, they lose their storm-proof efficacy. Attempts at repair here are hazardous and do not meet Florida building code for storm protection. The only solution is full impact-resistant glass replacement.

A 2022 Florida Building Code update stresses that post-storm repairs on laminated windows must restore “original factory safety and impact ratings” to qualify for insurance or warranty coverage.
