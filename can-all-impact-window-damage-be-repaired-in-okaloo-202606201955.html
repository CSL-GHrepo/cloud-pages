Understanding the Types of Impact Window Damage

Impact windows in Okaloosa County are designed to withstand high wind speeds and flying debris from hurricanes. Despite their strength, various kinds of damage can occur:

  • Seal Failure and Fogging: Moisture trapped between panes due to broken seals.
  • Minor Glass Cracks or Chips: Small damage that does not penetrate the laminated glass fully.
  • Frame or Trim Damage: Warping, dents, or corrosion, especially in coastal environments.
  • Complete Glass Breakage: Shattered laminated glass often happens in extreme wind or impact scenarios.
  • Structural Window Frame Damage: Compromise to the window frame's integrity affecting fit and protection.

Each type has specific repair or replacement implications based on severity and safety standards.
