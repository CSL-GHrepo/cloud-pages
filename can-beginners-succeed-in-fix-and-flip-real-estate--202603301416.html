Essential Terms Every Parkland Investor Should Know

Asset-based lending means loans secured by the property itself, not the borrower’s credit score. This approach allows for faster approvals in the real estate sector. Hard money refers to short-term loans from private investors, often with higher interest rates but quicker access—ideal for urgent projects. LTV, or loan-to-value ratio, indicates how much of the property’s value is financed; a lower LTV can mean better loan terms. Rehab budget is the amount allocated for renovations; understanding this helps determine the loan size and scope.
