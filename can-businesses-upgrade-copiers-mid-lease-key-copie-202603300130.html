Key Industry Terms in Commercial Copier Leasing

Understanding industry terminology makes selecting the right copier lease easier. Here are some essential terms: — learn more.

1. Lease Term: The duration of the leasing agreement, typically ranging from 1 to 5 years. Shorter terms offer flexibility, while longer terms can reduce monthly payments.

2. Maintenance & Service: Support included in the lease, covering repairs, toner, and parts. It’s critical for minimizing downtime.

3. Buyout Option: The ability to purchase the copier at the end of the lease term, often at a reduced price.

4. Page Volume Limit: The maximum number of pages allowed per month in the lease. Exceeding this may incur extra charges.

5. Residual Value: The expected value of the copier at the end of the lease, impacting lease payments.
