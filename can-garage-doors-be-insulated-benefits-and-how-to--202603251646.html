Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Parkland Residents

Keeping your garage door in top shape requires regular maintenance, especially in a climate like Parkland’s where humidity and storms are common. One simple step is to lubricate moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and tracks every six months. This prevents squeaking and reduces wear.

Check the balance of your garage door periodically by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door. It should stay in place without sagging or feeling heavy. An unbalanced door puts stress on the opener and springs, so call us if you notice uneven movement.

Inspect the weather stripping at the bottom of your door for cracks or tears. Replacing damaged seals prevents water, pests, and debris from entering your garage, which can cause damage over time. Proper sealing also improves energy efficiency for insulated doors.

Test the safety features such as photo-eye sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms monthly. These systems are crucial for preventing accidents, especially if you have children or pets in the house.
