Understanding Locksmith Key Cutting Methods Without the Original

When the original key is unavailable, locksmiths do not resort to guessing. Instead, they apply precise and tested techniques. Here are the most common methods:

  • Key Impressioning Technique: By inserting a blank key into the lock and manipulating it, locksmiths can create marks where cuts should be made, gradually refining the blank to fit perfectly.
  • Lock Decoding: For certain pin tumbler locks, locksmiths use decoding tools to measure the lock’s pins or wafers directly, discovering the key bitting (cut depths) without a key.
  • Lock Disassembly: Sometimes locksmiths remove the lock cylinder to visually inspect the internal components and determine the required key pattern.
  • Locksmith Key Programming: For electronic car keys and high-security locks, locksmiths connect to the vehicle’s or lock’s onboard computer to program new keys.
  • Key Cloning Services: Specialized devices read the data from transponder keys or RFID chips and replicate the data onto new key fobs or smart keys.

These methods have become vital in creating spare keys and facilitating emergency key replacement when original keys are lost or damaged.

What to Expect When Requesting Non-Original Key Making

If you contact a locksmith for creating a key without the original:
