How Locksmiths Copy Keys Without the Original: The Techniques Explained

When a key is lost or unavailable, locksmiths don’t just give up; instead, they rely on specialized skills and tools to duplicate keys no original required. Some of the most common methods include:

  • Lock Decoding: Locksmiths analyze the internal mechanism of the lock to determine the correct key cut. This involves extracting detailed measurements from the lock’s pins or wafers.
  • Key Impressioning: This traditional method involves inserting a blank key into the lock and turning it gently to leave marks, which the locksmith then files down incrementally until the key works perfectly.
  • Key Cloning Technology: In automotive locksmithing, especially with transponder and smart keys, locksmiths use electronic devices to clone key data directly from the lock or the vehicle system.
  • Lock Disassembly and Manual Key Cutting: In some emergency cases, locksmiths may temporarily disassemble locks to measure the correct pattern to cut a new key.
