The Key Cutting Process: How Locksmiths Duplicate Your Keys

The process of locksmith key copying can vary depending on the type of key and its complexity. Here’s a breakdown of how locksmiths typically create duplicate keys:

  1. Key Identification: The locksmith examines the original key or lock to determine the key type and blank needed.
  2. Key Blank Selection: The locksmith selects an appropriate blank that matches the original key’s profile.
  3. Key Cutting: Using a specialized key cutting machine, the locksmith traces the original key’s grooves to create a precise duplicate.
  4. Testing & Adjustment: After cutting, the key is tested in the lock, and minor adjustments are made to ensure smooth operation.
  5. Programming (for advanced automotive keys): Many modern car keys require additional locksmith key programming to sync the duplicate with the vehicle’s security system.

This key cutting process requires precision and professional equipment, underscoring why DIY attempts often fail or cause damage.

“Precision in each step of key duplication is critical to maintain security and avoid lock damage,” explains Tom Rivers, owner of LockGuard Locksmiths. “Professionals use calibrated machines and test each duplicate thoroughly.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Can locksmiths duplicate high-security or restricted keys?

Yes, locksmiths can duplicate high-security or restricted keys but usually require proof of authorization or ownership due to legal and security regulations. They use specialized equipment and authorized key blanks to ensure security compliance.

How long does the key cutting process take?

For standard keys, key cutting typically takes only a few minutes. More complex keys, like automotive transponder keys requiring locksmith key programming, may take 30 minutes to an hour depending on the vehicle and key type.

Can a locksmith duplicate automotive keys with chips or remotes?

Absolutely. Locksmiths offer advanced automotive key duplication services, which include cutting the key blade and programming the electronic chip or remote to your vehicle, ensuring it functions seamlessly.

Is it better to duplicate keys at a locksmith or a hardware store?

Locksmiths generally provide more accurate and secure key duplication, especially for complex, high-security, or electronic keys. Hardware stores may offer basic duplication for standard house keys but lack the expertise and equipment for advanced keys.
