Industry Terms Every Resident Should Know

Deadbolt: A locking bolt that’s moved by turning a key or knob, providing higher security than standard locks. Common in residential doors.

Rekeying: The process of changing the internal pins of a lock so it works with a new key, without replacing the entire lock.

Bump key: A specially cut key used to manipulate pin-tumbler locks open without keys. A security concern that high-security locks can prevent.

Master key system: A setup where a single key opens multiple locks, while individual keys only access specific areas. Popular in businesses.

Smart lock: An electronically operated lock that connects with mobile apps or home automation systems for keyless access.
