Tips for Maintaining Your Locks in Parkland

Regular maintenance of your locks can extend their lifespan and prevent emergencies. Lubricate the keyholes and moving parts with a silicone-based spray every six months. This reduces wear and prevents sticking. — our emergency locksmith team.

If you notice difficult key insertion or lock turning, don’t force it. Call a professional locksmith to diagnose the issue before it worsens. Often, these problems are caused by dirt, corrosion, or worn-out components.

Rekey your locks promptly after moving into a new home or losing keys. This is a cost-effective way to ensure only you have access without replacing the entire lock.

Consider upgrading to electronic or smart locks for convenience and added security. These systems require minimal maintenance and can be controlled remotely.

Store spare keys in secure, discreet locations. Avoid hiding keys outside your property, which burglars often find easily. Ask a trusted neighbor or use a lockbox for added security.
