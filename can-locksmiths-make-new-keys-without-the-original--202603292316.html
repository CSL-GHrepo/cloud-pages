Terminology Every Parkland Resident Should Know

Understanding some basic locksmith terminology makes discussions with professionals clearer. One key concept is 'rekeying,' which involves changing the internal components of a lock so that a new key operates it, without replacing the entire lock.

Another important term is 'high-security locks,' designed to resist picking, drilling, and other forced entry methods. These locks are ideal for homes and businesses seeking maximum protection.

Finally, 'master key systems' allow authorized users to access multiple locks with a single key. This setup simplifies access control for property managers and business owners in Parkland.

Knowing these terms helps residents communicate their needs more effectively and ensures they get the right security solutions.
