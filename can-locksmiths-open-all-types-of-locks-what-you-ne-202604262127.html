Understanding the Different Types of Locks Locksmiths Encounter

Not all locks are created equal. Locksmiths regularly work with a variety of lock types, each designed with different security measures and entry mechanisms. Here are the most common lock types they handle:

  • Pin Tumbler Locks: The most common in homes and businesses, these rely on a series of pins that must be aligned by the correct key.
  • Wafer Locks: Often found in filing cabinets and vehicles, wafers replace pins with flat wafers; they are generally less secure but still require professional skill to pick.
  • Disc Detainer Locks: Using rotating discs instead of pins, these are moderately difficult to pick and common in bike locks and safes.
  • Tubular Locks: Frequently used in vending machines and computer locks, requiring a specialized pick tool.
  • High-Security Locks: These include Medeco, Mul-T-Lock, and other patented locks with drill-resistant components and sidebars; challenging but not impossible to open for an expert locksmith.
  • Electronic and Smart Locks: These locks may require bypassing or reprogramming rather than traditional picking.

Each lock type represents different challenges, but experienced locksmiths bring a wide array of tools and techniques to handle every situation.

Lock Picking Techniques: How Locksmiths Open Locks

Lock picking is a skilled craft combining delicate touch, specialized tools, and detailed knowledge of mechanical locks. Locksmiths employ several industry-standard methods to open locks without causing damage, such as:

  1. Traditional Lock Picking: Using tension wrenches and picks to manipulate pins inside the lock cylinder.
  2. Bumping: A technique where a specially cut key (“bump key”) is used to jar pins into place quickly.
  3. Impressioning: Creating a working key by inserting a blank and noting pin marks to cut the correct key profile.
  4. Bypassing: Exploiting weaknesses or alternative entry points, particularly in outdated locks.
  5. Decoding Electronic Locks: Using diagnostic tools to access or reset digital locking systems.

When standard picking isn’t enough, locksmiths may utilize advanced tools like electric pick guns, specialized tension tools, and manufacturer-specific bypass kits.

Can All Locks Be Opened? The Exceptions and Reality

While the vast majority of locks can be opened, some high-security and restricted keyway locks present significant hurdles even for seasoned locksmiths. These include:

  • Patented Key Systems: Locks with patented keys restrict unauthorized duplication and can thwart picking attempts.
  • Magnetic and Biometric Locks: Require electronic access methods rather than traditional picking.
  • Commercial Grade High-Security Locks: Designed with hardened steel, drill resistance, and complex internal mechanisms.
  • Sealed Locking Systems: For example, certain safe locks that incorporate multiple security layers.

Even in these cases, locksmiths often have alternative methods or protocols developed in partnership with manufacturers to service these locks. However, a locksmith will always prioritize non-destructive techniques to avoid damage, especially in emergency lockout assistance scenarios.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can locksmiths open high-security locks like Medeco or Mul-T-Lock?

Yes, experienced locksmiths are trained to open high-security locks including Medeco, Mul-T-Lock, and other patented key systems. However, these locks often require specialized tools and knowledge and may take more time. Licensed locksmiths prioritize non-destructive entry methods and can also assist with lock repair or rekeying if needed.

Are locksmiths able to open electronic or smart locks during emergencies?

Locksmiths who specialize in electronic and smart lock systems can provide emergency lockout assistance by bypassing, resetting, or reprogramming these locks. The approach varies depending on the brand and technology, so it’s important to choose a locksmith experienced with your specific electronic lock model.

What should I look for when hiring a locksmith for commercial lock services?

For commercial locks, hire a locksmith who is licensed, insured, and has experience with heavy-duty security locks, master key systems, and access control. Good locksmith services will offer lock installation, repair, rekeying, and security consultation tailored to your business needs.

How do locksmiths handle key duplication for complex lock systems?

Locksmiths use precision key cutting machines and, in some cases, authorized equipment from lock manufacturers to duplicate keys for complex or high-security lock systems. They ensure duplicated keys meet strict security guidelines and function safely without compromising the lock’s integrity.
