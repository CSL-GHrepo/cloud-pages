Learning Key Locksmith Terms & Industry Standards

Understanding industry terminology can empower you to make better security decisions. For example, 'rekeying' means changing the internal pins of a lock so old keys no longer work, without replacing the entire lock. This is a cost-effective way to secure properties after moving in or losing keys.

Another term is 'master key system,' which involves a hierarchy of keys that open different groups of locks, often used in commercial settings. This setup allows for controlled access while maintaining security.

The term 'bump key' refers to a specially crafted key used to manipulate pin tumbler locks quickly. While effective, proper lock installation and high-quality hardware minimize vulnerability against such techniques.

Industry standards like the ANSI/BHMA (American National Standards Institute / Builders Hardware Manufacturers Association) ensure that locks meet quality and security benchmarks. When selecting locks, look for these certifications to ensure durability.

Lastly, 'electronic locks' include keypad, smart locks, and biometric systems. These modern solutions provide flexible access options and are increasingly popular in residential and commercial properties.
