Maintaining Your Garage Door to Extend Its Life

Regular maintenance is key to keeping your garage door operating smoothly for years. Simple tasks like lubricating moving parts, tightening hardware, and inspecting components can prevent issues before they escalate. In Parkland, where humidity can lead to rust and corrosion, extra care is necessary to protect metal parts.

One of the most effective maintenance routines involves applying lubricant to rollers, hinges, and tracks every few months. This reduces friction and noise, ensuring quiet operation. It's also crucial to check the balance of your garage door by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door; it should stay in place without sagging.

Inspecting cables, springs, and brackets regularly helps identify wear or damage early. If you notice any fraying cables or broken springs, contact a professional immediately. Keep the tracks clean and free of debris to prevent misalignment or jamming.

Weatherstripping should be checked periodically to ensure it seals properly and keeps out water and pests. Replacing worn weatherstripping enhances insulation and energy efficiency, especially important in Parkland's humid environment.

I recommend scheduling professional inspections at least once a year. Our team can perform thorough diagnostics and preventive maintenance, saving you money and avoiding emergency repairs down the line.
