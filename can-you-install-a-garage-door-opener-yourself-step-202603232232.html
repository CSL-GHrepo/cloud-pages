Start by checking the balance of your door. Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door to see if it stays in place without assistance. If it doesn’t, there might be an issue with the springs.

Inspect the hardware regularly for rust or loose bolts. Tighten or replace parts as needed to keep everything functioning smoothly.
