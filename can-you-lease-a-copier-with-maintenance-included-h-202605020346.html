What Does “Leasing a Copier with Maintenance Included” Mean?

When you lease a copier with maintenance included, you enter into a contract where the leasing company not only provides the copier hardware but also assumes responsibility for its upkeep. This can involve:

  • Routine Maintenance – Regular inspections and preventive service to keep the copier operating efficiently.
  • Repairs and Part Replacements – Covering the cost of replacing worn or broken components.
  • Consumables Supply – Some agreements include toner, ink, drums, and other supplies as part of the plan.
  • 24/7 Support and On-site Service – Many providers offer prompt service calls to minimize your downtime.

Essentially, this is a comprehensive service package wrapped into your copier leasing agreement — often referred to as managed print services or a printer service contract. This service model contrasts sharply with buying a copier outright, where you would generally need separate maintenance arrangements.

How Copier Leasing Agreements with Maintenance Typically Work

Understanding the process can dispel uncertainties and empower you to negotiate the most favorable agreement.

  1. Assessment of Needs: The leasing provider evaluates your business volume, document workflows, and equipment requirements.
  2. Proposal of All-Inclusive Lease: You receive an offer outlining the copier lease terms plus maintenance, usually for 36 to 60 months.
  3. Installation and Setup: The equipment is delivered, installed, and configured per your needs.
  4. Regular Maintenance Visits: The service team schedules routine checkups and supplies toner or other consumables as agreed.
  5. Responsive Repairs: If the copier breaks down, you contact the service team who provides on-site or remote repairs.
  6. End of Lease Options: You can renew, upgrade, or purchase the equipment at fair market value.

Many providers also offer scalable options depending on your business size and document output to avoid overprovisioning or paying for unused capacity.
