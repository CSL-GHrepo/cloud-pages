How fast can I sell my damaged property using cash home buyers?

Selling a damaged or distressed house to cash buyers can be completed in as little as 7 to 14 days, significantly faster than traditional sales. This speed is because cash buyers do not require mortgage approvals or lengthy inspections.

Are cash offers on ugly houses lower than market value?

Cash offers often reflect the home's current state and market conditions. While these offers can be below typical market value, they account for repair needs and eliminate realtor fees, making them competitive for sellers seeking speed and certainty.

Some cash home buyers work with sellers to expedite inherited property sales, potentially before probate concludes, by purchasing rights or working with estate representatives. Consulting a cash buyer experienced with inherited properties can help accelerate the process.
