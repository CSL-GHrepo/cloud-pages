Understanding Property Liens and Their Impact on Selling Your Home

A lien is a legal claim attached to your property, usually due to unpaid debts such as mortgages, taxes, or contractor bills. When you’re selling a house with liens, these claims must usually be resolved to transfer clear ownership.

How liens impact your sale:

  • They can delay or prevent closing until debts are settled.
  • Traditional lenders and buyers often require liens to be cleared in advance.
  • Property liens reduce your net proceeds from the sale because outstanding debts must be paid.

However, cash home buyers often have the resources and flexibility to handle these liens as part of the sale process, sometimes negotiating to pay off liens directly during closing or discounting their offers to cover these costs.

According to a 2023 report from the National Association of Realtors, "More than 45% of distressed home sales involve some form of financial lien or outstanding debt on the property."
