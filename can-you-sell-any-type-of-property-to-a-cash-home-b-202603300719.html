Types of Properties Cash Buyers Typically Accept

Understanding what types of properties cash buyers accept helps you assess your own situation and what you can expect. Here’s a breakdown of the most common property types:

  • Single-Family Homes: The most common property type for cash buyers. Homes in any condition are often accepted.
  • Condos and Townhomes: Units in managed communities are accepted, though cash buyers may check association rules and fees.
  • Inherited Property: Selling inherited property fast is a typical reason sellers turn to cash buyers to avoid long probate processes.
  • Distressed or Foreclosed Properties: Homes needing repairs, condemned properties, or those with liens or back taxes.
  • Commercial Properties: Some cash buyers specialize in commercial real estate and vacant land.
  • Land and Lots: Vacant land is accepted by certain cash buyers willing to invest in redevelopment.

That said, cash buyer property requirements do vary. For example, some investors may avoid properties in severe flood zones or technical legal complications, but many cash buyers have the resources to tackle otherwise challenging sales.

Industry professionals recommend asking upfront about specific property types a cash buyer is interested in, ensuring your property fits their buying criteria to avoid wasted time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sell an inherited property fast to a cash home buyer?

Yes, many cash buyers specialize in purchasing inherited properties quickly, often helping sellers avoid lengthy probate processes. Because they buy as-is, sellers don’t have to invest in repairs or improvements, making it an efficient and cost-effective option for inherited homes.

Are there types of properties cash buyers will not buy?

While cash buyers accept a wide range of properties including distressed homes and commercial buildings, some may avoid properties with complex legal issues, severe environmental hazards, or those in extreme flood zones. It’s important to disclose all relevant information to your buyer upfront.

How fast can a cash home buying process close compared to traditional sales?

Cash sales often close within 7 to 14 days, considerably faster than the 30-60 days typical of traditional sales. This speed results from eliminating lender approval and appraisal contingencies, providing certainty and efficiency to sellers.

Do I have to pay commissions or fees when selling to a cash home buyer?

Usually, cash home sales involve fewer fees. Many cash buyers cover closing costs, and since no real estate agents are involved, sellers save on commissions, which can be around 5-6% of the sale price.
