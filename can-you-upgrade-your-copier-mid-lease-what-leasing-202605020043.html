Understanding Copier Leasing Contract Terms

When entering a copier leasing contract, the details around upgrades and replacements are usually spelled out in the lease documents. Commonly, you may encounter these terms:

  • Early Upgrade Clause: Allows tenants to trade in their copier for a newer model before the lease expires, often after a minimum lease period, such as 12 or 24 months.
  • Lease Modification Options: Some contracts permit modifying lease terms to accommodate a higher-end model, with payments adjusted accordingly.
  • Mid-Lease Copier Replacement: The contract might specify conditions under which a full mid-lease replacement is possible, such as technology obsolescence or increased volume needs.
  • Copier Rental Lease Policies: Policies on returns, buyouts, or trade-ins affecting the ability to upgrade mid-lease.

Understanding these provisions upfront saves costly surprises. For instance, leasing companies often stipulate an upgrade fee, a prorated payment recalculation, or a new contract for the upgraded copier.
