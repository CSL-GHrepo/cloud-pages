Understanding Leasing Agreement Copier Terms for Mid-Lease Upgrades

The ability to upgrade your copier during the lease depends primarily on the original leasing agreement’s terms. Copier lease contracts typically outline:

  • Lease duration and renewal options
  • Provisions for equipment upgrades or replacements
  • Costs or penalties associated with early termination
  • Lease modification policies, such as adding equipment or revising terms

If your lease includes copier lease upgrade options or clauses allowing equipment upgrade during lease, you may be able to switch to newer, faster, or more feature-rich models mid-term.

However, leases without flexible provisions may require you to:
