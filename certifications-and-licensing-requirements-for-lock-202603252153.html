Essential Locksmith Glossary for Parkland Residents

Rekeying: Adjusting a lock so a new key operates it without changing hardware. Ideal when keys are lost or stolen, or for added security after moving in. — learn more.

High-security lock: A lock designed with advanced mechanisms to resist picking, drilling, and unauthorized duplication. Popular in commercial properties and high-end homes.

Electronic access control: A system allowing entry via keycards, codes, or biometric data, offering flexible security management for businesses and homes.

Master key system: A setup where multiple locks are controlled by a single key, used mainly in commercial settings for managed access.

deadbolt: A sturdy lock component that provides extra security, typically installed on exterior doors to prevent forced entry.
