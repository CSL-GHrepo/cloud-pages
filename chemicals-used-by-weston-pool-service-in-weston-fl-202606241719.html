Key Chemicals Used by Weston Pool Service in Weston, FL

1. Chlorine and Chlorine Alternatives

Chlorine remains the gold standard for disinfection due to its cost-effectiveness and powerful oxidation properties. Weston Pool Service maintains free chlorine levels between 1-3 ppm, eliminating harmful bacteria and pathogens.

For those seeking alternatives, especially clients with sensitivities, chlorine alternatives such as bromine or saltwater chlorine generators are used. These options provide effective sanitation with reduced chlorine odor and irritation.

2. Pool pH Balancing Agents

Maintaining pool water's pH between 7.2 and 7.8 is critical to swimmer comfort and chemical efficiency. Weston Pool Service uses:

  • pH Increasers: Soda ash (sodium carbonate) to raise pH when acidic rain lowers it.
  • pH Reducers: Muriatic acid or sodium bisulfate to counteract high pH from the alkaline Weston water sources.

3. Algaecides for Swimming Pools

Algae blooms are a common problem in the humid climate of Weston, FL. Algaecides prevent and eliminate algae buildup. The service employs:

  • Polyquaternary ammonium compounds for preventative treatments.
  • Copper-based or polymeric algaecides for persistent outbreaks.

4. Calcium Hardness Increasers

Calcium hardness impacts water balance and protects pool surfaces and equipment from corrosion or scaling. According to Weston Pool Service’s assessments, they typically ensure calcium hardness levels between 200-400 ppm depending on pool construction materials.

5. Pool Shock Treatments

Shock treatments are used weekly or after heavy use/storms to rapidly oxidize contaminants and boost sanitizer levels. Common shock chemicals include:

  • Calcium hypochlorite
  • Potassium peroxymonosulfate (non-chlorine shock)

This approach is essential in Weston, where sudden heavy rainfall and high bather loads can lead to rapid water contamination.

6. Water Clarifiers for Pools

Water clarifiers are used to improve water clarity by aggregating microscopic particles for easier filtration. These polymer-based agents help pools across Weston maintain sparkling, transparent water even through variable weather conditions.
