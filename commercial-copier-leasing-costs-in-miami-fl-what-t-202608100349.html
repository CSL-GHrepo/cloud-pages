Hidden Costs to Watch for in Commercial Copier Leasing

While the initial monthly lease payment is often the headline figure, Miami businesses should be aware of potential hidden costs that can add up over time. These charges may not be clear at lease signing but could significantly affect your budgeting:

  • Excess Page Charges: Many lease agreements include a base monthly page volume allowance. Printing beyond this limit typically incurs per-page overage fees, which can be as high as 3-5 cents per page. Understanding your expected volume upfront helps avoid surprise charges.
  • Service Call Fees: Some contracts charge extra for service calls unrelated to covered maintenance or toner replacement, such as user errors or non-covered damages.
  • End-of-Lease Penalties: Failing to meet lease obligations, such as returning equipment in good working condition or exceeding allowed print volumes, may result in penalties or fees.
  • Toner and Supply Charges: Not all lease agreements include toner and consumables in the monthly cost. Some require separate purchase of supplies, which can inflate ongoing expenses.
  • Early Termination Fees: Businesses that need to exit a lease prematurely often face financial penalties that can equal several months’ worth of payments.

Carefully reviewing the lease contract line-by-line with legal or procurement advisors before signing is highly recommended to mitigate these hidden costs.
