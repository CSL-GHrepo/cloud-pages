Palm Bay, FL, is no stranger to hurricanes and severe tropical storms. These storms bring strong winds, torrential rain, and airborne debris, which can severely damage even high-quality impact windows. The most frequent forms of damage include:

  • Impact Window Cracks Palm Bay: Although designed to resist impacts, high-velocity debris can cause surface cracks or stress fractures.
  • Storm Damage Impact Windows: Shattered panes or delamination can occur during category 2 or above hurricanes.
  • Leakage and Water Intrusion: Intense storms may exploit any weak points or seal failures, allowing water to penetrate.

4. General Wear and Tear: Age and Environmental Factors

Even the strongest impact windows degrade over time in Florida’s sun-drenched, salty air environment. Prolonged exposure to UV rays can weaken window frames, while airborne salt particles can corrode metal components.
