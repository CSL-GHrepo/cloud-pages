2. Impact Window Seal Failure - Silent but Costly

Another frequent cause of degradation is impact window seal failure. The seals around impact windows are designed to keep moisture, dust, and air out. Over time, UV exposure, humidity common in Stuart, FL, and temperature fluctuations can cause the seals to shrink, crack, or detach.

The consequences of seal failure include:

  • Condensation or fogging between glass panes
  • Reduced insulation and energy efficiency
  • Potential for mold growth and wood rot around the window frame

According to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), proper window sealing can reduce energy loss by up to 25%, highlighting the importance of addressing seal failures promptly.

Impact window maintenance Stuart homeowners rely on includes regular seal inspections and timely resealing. Neglecting this can necessitate costly impact glass replacement Stuart to restore window performance.

  • Physical impacts: Accidental damage during landscaping, home improvements, or debris impact can chip or bend frames.
  • Improper installation or aging: Faulty installation or natural aging can cause frame misalignment, leading to leaks and compromised safety.

Frame damage can compromise the window's protective function, so professional impact window repair Stuart FL services are often required to repair or replace affected components. In severe cases, total window replacement may be recommended.
