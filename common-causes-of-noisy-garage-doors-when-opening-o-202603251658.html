Essential Garage Door Terminology for Parkland Homeowners

Garage door springs are components under tension that help lift and lower the door. They need proper balancing and must be replaced carefully to avoid accidents. Our technicians understand different types—torsion and extension springs—and choose the right one for your setup.

Tracks are the rails on which rollers move. They guide the garage door and need to be perfectly aligned for smooth operation. Misaligned tracks can cause noise, uneven movement, and damage to other parts.

Sensors are safety devices that detect obstructions before the door closes. Proper alignment and testing are necessary to prevent accidents. Malfunctioning sensors are a common cause of door operation issues in Parkland homes.

Cables are strong steel wires that support the door's weight, connected to the springs. Worn or frayed cables are dangerous and must be replaced by professionals to ensure safety.

Garage Door Safety Tips for Parkland Residents

Safety should always come first when dealing with garage doors. Regularly test your safety features, such as auto-reverse sensors, to ensure they’re functioning correctly. If the sensors are misaligned or dirty, the door may not reverse when it encounters an obstacle, risking injury.

Keep the area around your garage door clear of obstacles and debris. Small children and pets should be kept away during repairs or routine operation to prevent accidents.

Avoid attempting repairs on springs or cables unless you’re trained. These parts are under high tension and can cause serious injuries if mishandled.

Schedule professional maintenance annually. Routine inspections can identify potential hazards early, including rust, worn parts, or misaligned components.

Educate family members about garage door safety. Make sure everyone understands how to operate the remote and avoid standing under the door while it’s moving.

  • Test safety sensors monthly
  • Keep the area clear of obstructions
  • Never attempt spring repairs yourself
  • Schedule annual professional inspections
  • Educate family on safe operation
