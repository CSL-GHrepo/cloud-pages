Maintenance Tips for Parkland Residents

Maintaining your garage door is essential for longevity and safety. Regular inspections can catch minor issues before they escalate. In Parkland, the humid climate can accelerate wear, making routine upkeep even more important.

Lubricate moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and tracks every few months. Use a silicone-based lubricant to ensure smooth operation and reduce noise. Worn or dry parts can cause strain on springs and openers.

Check the balance of your garage door. Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway. It should stay in place. If it doesn't, realignment or spring adjustment by a professional is recommended.

Test safety features regularly. The auto-reverse function should stop and reverse the door when an object is detected. Clean the photo-eye sensors to maintain this critical safety measure.

Schedule annual professional inspections. Experts can identify issues like cable wear, spring fatigue, or track misalignment that may not be obvious to homeowners.
