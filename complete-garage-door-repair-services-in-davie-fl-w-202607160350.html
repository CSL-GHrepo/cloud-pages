Understanding the Different Types of Garage Door Springs

Garage door springs are critical components that counterbalance the weight of the door, making it easy to open and close. There are primarily two types of springs used in garage door systems, each with unique features and maintenance considerations:

  • Torsion Springs: Mounted above the door, torsion springs are wound tightly and provide torque when the door moves. They are known for their durability and smoother operation.
  • Extension Springs: Positioned alongside the door tracks, extension springs stretch and contract to assist in door movement. These are commonly found in older or lighter garage door models.

Choosing the correct type of spring during repairs or replacements is essential for safety and door performance. For example, torsion springs usually last longer but might cost more upfront. Extension springs can be more prone to wear in humid climates like Davie’s, which makes professional assessment vital.
