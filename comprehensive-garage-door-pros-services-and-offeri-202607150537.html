Understanding Garage Door Components: What Every Homeowner Should Know

Garage doors are complex mechanical systems composed of several essential components that work in unison. Knowing the basics about these parts can help Parkland residents better communicate their needs and recognize when professional service is required.

  • Door Panels: The visible part of the door, available in various materials and designs.
  • Tracks: Guide rails on either side allow smooth vertical and horizontal movement.
  • Springs: Typically torsion or extension type, they balance the door’s weight for easy lifting.
  • Rollers: Small wheels that glide along the tracks.
  • Garage Door Opener: The motorized unit responsible for automatic door operation.
  • Sensors: Safety devices detect obstructions to prevent accidents.
  • Cables and Pulleys: Connect springs to the door and assist in lifting.

Proper maintenance and timely repairs of each component, as provided by The Garage Door Pros, ensure dependable and safe operation for years to come.
