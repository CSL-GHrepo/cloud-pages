Preparing Your Garage Door for Parkland’s Weather Challenges

Parkland’s subtropical climate presents particular challenges to garage door durability and operation. Factors such as high humidity, frequent rains, and occasional tropical storms necessitate proactive care and weather-resistant solutions.

Common Weather-Related Garage Door Issues

  • Corrosion and Rust: Metal components exposed to salt-laden air or moisture can deteriorate without proper treatment.
  • Warping and Swelling: Wooden doors may absorb moisture, leading to structural deformation.
  • Motor and Opener Malfunctions: Electrical components can be adversely affected by humidity and power surges during storms.
  • Storm Damage: Strong winds and debris can cause dents, misalignment, or broken panels.
