Preventing Common Garage Door Problems: Tips from Experts

While professional maintenance is critical, homeowners can take proactive steps to minimize garage door issues between service visits.

Regular Visual Inspections

Monthly observations can reveal clues such as:

  • Unusual noises during opening or closing cycles.
  • Visible signs of wear on springs, cables, or rollers.
  • Gaps or misalignment in the door panels and tracks.

Keep Tracks and Hardware Clean

Dirt, debris, or spider webs in tracks and hinges can impede smooth movement. Use a damp cloth or vacuum attachment to clean the tracks, and check for rust or corrosion regularly.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Applying quality garage door lubricant to rollers, hinges, springs, and tracks every 6 months will minimize friction, reduce noise, and prolong component lifespan. Avoid using WD-40 or similar penetrating oils, as they can attract dirt and degrade performance over time.

Test Safety Features

Quickly test auto-reverse and sensor functions every few months by simulating obstruction with a solid object or your hand. If the door fails to reverse promptly, schedule immediate professional service to prevent injury or damage.
