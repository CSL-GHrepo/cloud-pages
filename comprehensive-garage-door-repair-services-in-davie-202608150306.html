DIY Garage Door Repairs: When to Avoid the Risk

While minor maintenance tasks like lubricating hinges or tightening screws may be manageable for skilled DIYers, many aspects of garage door repair pose significant danger without professional training.

“We advise against untrained attempts to repair springs, cables, or openers,” cautions Kevin Morales, senior technician with Garage Door Repair of Davie. “Improper handling can lead to severe injury or further property damage.”

Common DIY pitfalls include:

  • Incorrect spring winding: High tension can cause springs to snap violently.
  • Misalignment of tracks: Can lead to door jamming or premature wear of parts.
  • Electrical hazards: Faulty opener wiring or bypassing safety sensors increases risk of shocks or accidents.
  • Void warranties: Unauthorized repairs may void manufacturer warranties on doors and openers.
