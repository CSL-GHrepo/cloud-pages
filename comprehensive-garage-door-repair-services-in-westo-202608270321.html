Common Warning Signs Your Garage Door Needs Professional Attention

Being proactive with garage door care means recognizing warning signs before a minor issue becomes a major problem. Here are some indicators that it’s time to call Garage Door Repair of Weston:

  • Unusual noises: Grinding, squeaking, or banging sounds during operation often suggest worn rollers, loose hardware, or failing springs.
  • Slow or uneven movement: If the door moves sluggishly or tilts to one side, it may be due to misaligned tracks or damaged cables.
  • Door doesn’t close fully: This could indicate sensor misalignment or obstruction, presenting a security risk.
  • Inconsistent remote response: Delayed or no reaction to remote commands can be a sign of opener failure or interference.
  • Visible wear or rust: Corrosion, especially in Weston’s humid climate, weakens metal components and compromises safety.

Addressing these signs promptly with professional inspections and repairs will prevent costly replacements and maintain smooth operation.
