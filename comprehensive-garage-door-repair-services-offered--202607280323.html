Garage Door Safety Features: Protecting You and Your Family

Safety remains a paramount concern with all garage doors. Modern systems include multiple built-in safety features and mechanical upgrades to prevent accidents:

  • Auto-Reverse Sensors: Infrared sensors detect objects or persons in the door’s path, automatically reversing the door to prevent injury or property damage.
  • Manual Release Mechanisms: In case of power failure, emergency release levers allow the door to be operated manually, maintaining access and safety.
  • Pinch-Resistant Panels: Door panels designed to reduce finger pinching risk, particularly important in households with children.
  • Security Locks and Rolling Codes: Roller door locks and opener remote systems using rolling codes prevent unauthorized access and hacking attempts.
