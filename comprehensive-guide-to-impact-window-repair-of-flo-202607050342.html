Maintaining Your Impact-Resistant Windows in Florida

Regular maintenance can extend the lifespan and performance of your impact windows. Florida experts recommend:

  • Inspect windows semi-annually and after major storms.
  • Clean frames and glass with manufacturers’ recommended materials.
  • Check seals and hardware for wear or damage.
  • Schedule professional storm window maintenance at least once a year.
  • Address any cracks or chips immediately to prevent worsening damage.

Proper maintenance reduces the frequency and cost of major repairs or impact glass replacement Florida services.
