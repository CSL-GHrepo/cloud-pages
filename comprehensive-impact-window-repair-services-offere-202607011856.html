Maintaining Impact Windows: Best Practices for Florida Homeowners

Regular maintenance is key to extending the life of Florida impact-resistant windows. Consider these expert tips:

  1. Schedule bi-annual professional inspections focusing on frame integrity and glass condition.
  2. Promptly address any chips, cracks, or seal failures to prevent moisture damage.
  3. Keep window tracks and seals clean and lubricated to ensure smooth operation.
  4. Use certified impact glass cleaning products that don’t degrade film or laminate layers.
  5. Document all repair and maintenance activities for insurance and warranty purposes.
