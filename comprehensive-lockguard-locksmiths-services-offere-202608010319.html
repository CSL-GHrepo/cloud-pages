Rekeying Services: Cost-Effective Security Solutions

Rekeying is often a smart alternative to full lock replacement when security needs updating after lost keys, tenant turnovers, or ownership changes.

  • How Rekeying Works: The locksmith adjusts the internal pins of a lock cylinder to work with a new key, rendering old keys useless.
  • Benefits: Lower cost compared to replacing entire locks, rapid turnaround, and minimized downtime.
  • Applications: Ideal for landlords, property managers, and homeowners who want to secure access without extensive hardware changes.
  • Professional Execution: Lockguard Locksmiths ensures precision rekeying that does not compromise lock quality or operation.

In Parkland’s real estate market, rekeying services are frequently requested during tenant transitions, helping to maintain safety at a fraction of the cost of new installations.
