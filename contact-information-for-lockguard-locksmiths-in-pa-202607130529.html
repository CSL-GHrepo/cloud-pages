Lock Maintenance Tips from Lockguard Locksmiths

Proper lock maintenance extends security system lifespan and prevents inconvenient malfunctions.

  • Regular Lubrication: Use graphite or silicone spray to keep lock mechanisms smooth without attracting dirt.
  • Check for Wear and Tear: Inspect keys and locks for damage; replace worn keys to prevent lock jams.
  • Clean Exterior Components: Remove dirt, dust, and debris that could clog the keyway or moving parts.
  • Avoid Excessive Force: Gently turn keys to prevent breaking inside locks.
  • Schedule Annual Inspections: Lockguard can perform preventive checks to catch issues before they cause lockouts or compromise security.
