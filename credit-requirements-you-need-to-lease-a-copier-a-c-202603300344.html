Copier Leasing Credit Approval: What Leasing Companies Look For

Besides credit scores, leasing companies consider these critical factors under their leasing company credit guidelines:

  • Credit history for equipment lease: Prior successful leasing or loan payments indicate reliability.

Copier Lease Application Requirements – Step-by-Step

Meeting credit requirements is only one part of the process. A typical copier lease application demands the following documentation and criteria:

  1. Completed application form: Basic business and contact information, including EIN or tax ID.
  2. Credit consent: Authorizing the leasing company to perform a credit check for copier rental.
  3. Financial statements: Recent bank statements, profit & loss reports, or tax returns.
  4. References: Trade or vendor references may be requested.
  5. Proof of insurance: Some lessors require liability or property insurance coverage.

Being prepared with these documents accelerates approval and prevents last-minute hurdles.

How to Improve Your Chances of Copier Leasing Credit Approval
