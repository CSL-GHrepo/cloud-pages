Impact of Credit Score on Interest Rates and Loan Terms in Tampa Hard Money Loans

While credit scores might not be the primary qualification factor in private lending, they significantly influence the cost of borrowing. Tampa hard money lenders often adjust interest rates, points, and fees based on the borrower's credit profile. Here’s how credit scores affect loan costs:

  • 700+ Credit Score: Lowest interest rates typically range from 8% to 10%, with minimal origination fees and flexible repayment terms.
  • 600-699 Credit Score: Interest rates increase to 10%-14%, often combined with a 2%-4% origination fee to offset higher risk.
  • Below 600: Rates can rise above 14%-18%, plus higher upfront fees; lenders may also require additional reserves or a larger down payment.

Additionally, lenders may adjust the loan-to-value (LTV) ratio in relation to credit. Borrowers with higher credit scores may secure loans up to 75% LTV, while those below 600 may face a 65% or lower LTV cap.

It's important to remember that the overall risk profile, including project quality and borrower experience, also influences rates more than credit scores alone in Tampa’s hard money market.
