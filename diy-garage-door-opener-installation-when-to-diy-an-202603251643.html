Maintaining Your Garage Door in Parkland

Regular maintenance keeps your garage door functioning smoothly and extends its lifespan. A few simple checks, like lubricating moving parts and inspecting for damage, can prevent costly breakdowns.

In Parkland, humid weather can accelerate rust and corrosion. Applying a protective lubricant on rollers, hinges, and tracks every few months is recommended. Keep an eye out for signs of wear or misalignment.

Test safety features like auto-reverse sensors monthly. Properly functioning sensors prevent accidents and are often required by safety standards. Adjust or replace them if they’re not working correctly.

Check the balance of your garage door by disconnecting the opener and manually opening the door halfway. If it doesn’t stay in place, call a professional. Proper balance reduces strain on the opener and extends component life.
