While cash buyers buy homes as-is, they conduct thorough due diligence to assess the property's condition. This evaluation informs their cash offers for houses. Key factors include:

Generally, no. Real estate investors buying homes specialize in no repairs needed cash buyers scenarios. However, exceptions may occur in rare cases:

Still, these cases are uncommon. Most cash property buyers prefer negotiating price adjustments rather than repair demands to keep the sale fast and simple.
