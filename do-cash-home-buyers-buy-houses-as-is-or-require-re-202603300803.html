What Does "As-Is" Really Mean in the Cash Home Buyers Process?

When a cash home buyer agrees to purchase your property as-is, it means exactly that: they accept the home in its current state, regardless of condition. This includes:

  • Structural issues such as foundation cracks or roof damage
  • Cosmetic problems like peeling paint or outdated fixtures
  • Functional problems including plumbing or electrical issues
  • Soft spots such as mold, water damage, or pest infestations

Because cash buyers are investors or companies prepared to rehabilitate or resell the property, they factor the cost of repairs into their cash offer homes value. This is why you typically won’t be asked to fix anything yourself, nor will you incur repair expenses out-of-pocket.

Do Cash Home Buyers Ever Require Repairs Before Buying?

While most cash home buyers buy properties as-is, there may be exceptions depending on:

  • The severity of damage impacting safety or legality (e.g., hazardous materials, code violations)
  • The buyer’s business model—some prefer minor repairs to increase resale value
  • Regional regulations or lender requirements if the property is financed afterward

However, it’s rare for cash buyers to require sellers to complete significant repairs. Instead, these costs are deducted from the cash offer homes price, allowing sellers to sell house without repairs.

  • Some states require thorough disclosures even for as-is sales, affecting timelines.
