Key Industry Terms You Should Know When Selling for Cash

Cash offer: A proposal to buy a property with immediate payment, avoiding the delays of traditional financing. It ensures a quick, guaranteed sale.

As-is sale: Selling a property in its current condition without repairs or renovations. Many cash buyers purchase homes as-is, saving sellers time and money.

Closing costs: Fees associated with transferring property ownership, including title insurance, escrow fees, and taxes. Reputable cash buyers often cover or reduce these costs.

Contingency: Conditions that must be met for a sale to proceed, such as financing or inspection. Cash deals typically eliminate most contingencies for faster closure.

Market value: The estimated price a property can fetch in an open market, based on comparable sales and local demand. Accurate valuation is key to fair offers.
