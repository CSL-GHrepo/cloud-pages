Quick Answer: Do Cash Home Buyers Buy Homes As-Is Without Repairs?

Yes. Most cash home buyers and real estate investors specialize in purchasing properties in any condition, which means they buy homes as-is without requiring sellers to invest in repairs. Why Do Cash Property Buyers Prefer to Buy Homes As-Is?

Cash home buyers, especially real estate investors, are experienced in evaluating properties for their potential rather than their current condition. Here are several reasons why they choose to buy without requiring repairs:

  • Speed of Transaction: Eliminating the need for repairs accelerates the sale, benefiting buyers who want rapid closing.
  • Control Over Renovations: Investors prefer to handle repairs themselves, selecting contractors and managing renovations to fit their business model.
  • Risk Management: Buyers price properties accordingly to factor in repair costs, making it a calculated investment rather than uncertainty reliant on seller repairs.
  • Accessibility: Buying as-is opens up a larger market of sellers who may not have the time or resources to fix their homes.

According to a 2022 survey by the National Association of Realtors, around 85% of cash property buyers purchase homes in “as-is” condition, without requiring sellers to make repairs prior to closing.
