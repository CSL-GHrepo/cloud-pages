Quick Answer: Do Cash Home Buyers Buy Houses As-Is?

Yes, most cash home buyers purchase properties "as-is," which means they accept homes in their existing condition without requiring sellers to perform repairs or renovations. Understanding As-Is Property Sales: What Does It Mean?

When you hear “buying as-is,” it’s important to clarify what this entails. In real estate, an as-is property sale means the buyer agrees to accept the house in its current condition, without negotiating repairs or improvements before closing. This can include homes with:

  • Structural issues
  • Outdated or damaged systems (plumbing, electrical, HVAC)
  • Cosmetic problems such as peeling paint or worn flooring
  • Unpermitted additions or code violations

Industry studies show that approximately 60% of real estate investors actively seek properties in need of repair, as these homes offer profitable opportunities after renovations.1 This demand underpins the cash home buyer model.

How Property Condition Impacts the Cash Offer

While cash buyers purchase properties as-is, the home's condition naturally influences the cash offer advantages you receive. Buyers perform a home buyer inspection or evaluation to determine repair costs and potential resale value. Then, they subtract estimated renovation expenses from your offer price.

  • Better condition = higher cash offer
  • Severe damage = lower offer to account for repair costs
  • Obvious safety hazards may delay or complicate the sale

For example, a home with minor cosmetic issues might have an offer just 5-10% below market value, while one with a faulty foundation could see offers 25-40% lower.
