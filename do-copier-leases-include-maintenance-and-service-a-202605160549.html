Understanding Copier Lease Maintenance Coverage

Copier lease contracts often emphasize maintenance coverage as a core benefit. This coverage usually entails:

  • Preventive Maintenance: Scheduled inspections and routine servicing to optimize performance and prevent breakdowns.
  • Repairs: Replacement or repair of worn parts and components at no additional charge.
  • Supply Refills: Some leases include toner and other consumables as part of maintenance, although this varies by contract.

According to a 2023 industry study by IDC, over 75% of commercial copier leases include some form of maintenance or service agreement, underscoring how standard this practice has become.

What Is Typically Not Included?

While maintenance coverage is common, it’s essential to clarify what's excluded. For example:

  • Misuse or accidental damage: Damage caused by improper use may not be covered under the maintenance agreement and can incur extra charges.
  • Upgrades and New Equipment: Maintenance generally does not cover moving to newer models unless explicitly stated.
  • Out-of-Warranty Services: Some leases limit coverage to warranty periods or predefined terms.

Comparing Copier Lease Contract Terms: What To Look For

Not all copier lease contracts are created equal. The key differences typically lie in maintenance fees, scope of service, and support responsiveness. When evaluating copier leasing options, pay attention to these terms:

  2. Response Times: What is the guaranteed turnaround for service calls? Minimal downtime is crucial for business continuity.
  3. Service Limits or Caps: Some agreements set maximum service calls or expenses before extra charges apply.
