Why Are Service Agreements Included in Copier Leases?

Including maintenance and service agreements in copier leases benefits both the leasing company and the lessee:

  • Equipment Longevity: Regular maintenance ensures the copier stays efficient and reduces the risk of major breakdowns.
  • Leasing Companies Maintain Asset Value: Well-maintained equipment holds higher residual value for the lessor.
  • Convenience and Reliability: Lessees get expert technical support without needing an in-house technician.

Key Copier Leasing Terms and Conditions to Review

While maintenance and service coverage is common in copier leases, the level of coverage and specific terms can vary. When evaluating copier lease service inclusions, watch for the following:

  1. Scope of Maintenance: Does the coverage include all parts or only certain components?
  2. Response Time for Repairs: Is there a guaranteed response or fix time, and are there penalties for delays?
  3. Consumables Coverage: Are toner and staples included, or are they billed separately?
  4. Exclusions and Additional Charges: Are accidental damage or user errors covered?
  5. Lease Term Impact: Does the lease require commitment to maintenance for the entire term?

Understanding these conditions helps you avoid surprises later and ensures your lease meets your business needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do all copier leases come with included maintenance and service agreements?

While most copier leases typically include maintenance and service agreements, the exact inclusions depend on the leasing company and contract terms. Some leases may offer maintenance plans as optional add-ons, so always review the copier lease service contracts thoroughly before signing.

What is usually covered in copier lease maintenance plans?

Copier lease maintenance plans generally cover regular equipment inspections, replacement of worn parts, toner replenishment, technical support, and repair services. Coverage specifics vary, so clarify what is excluded, such as accidental damage or user-related faults.

Can I get leased copier repair services quickly?

Many leasing companies promise rapid response times—often within 24-48 hours—for leased copier repair services. Service agreements for leased copiers usually include guaranteed repair windows to minimize downtime and maintain business productivity.

How do copier lease service agreements affect my total leasing cost?

Maintenance and service agreements may be included in your monthly lease payments or billed separately. Including these services in your lease typically leads to more predictable costs and can save money on unexpected repairs and supplies over the lease term.
