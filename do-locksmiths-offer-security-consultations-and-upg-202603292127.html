Locksmith Terminology Every Resident Should Know

Understanding some common locksmith terms can help you make informed decisions. For example, 'rekeying' involves changing the internal components of a lock so that a new key works, without replacing the entire lock. This can save money if you’ve lost your keys or moved into a new property.

Another important term is 'deadbolt,' which is a sturdy lock that provides added security for residential doors. Upgrading to high-security deadbolts can significantly reduce the risk of break-ins. Lastly, 'access control systems' refer to electronic locks or keycard systems that restrict entry to authorized personnel, ideal for business settings in Parkland.
