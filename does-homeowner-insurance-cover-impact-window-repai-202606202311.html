Quick Answer: Does Homeowner Insurance Cover Impact Window Repair in Florida?

Most Florida homeowner insurance policies provide coverage for impact window repair or replacement when the damage is caused by covered events such as hurricanes, tropical storms, or wind damage. However, coverage depends on the specifics of your policy, including your deductible, coverage limits, and exclusions related to your windows.

Understanding Florida Impact Window Insurance: What’s Typically Covered?

Florida has some of the most stringent building codes in the country, especially for windows, due to the high risk of hurricane damage. Consequently, many homeowners have upgraded to impact-resistant windows. When damage occurs, knowing how your Florida home insurance claims process handles these is crucial.

  • Covered Perils: Most policies cover damage from natural disasters such as hurricanes, tropical storms, hail, and flying debris.
  • Exclusions: Damage from neglect, normal wear and tear, or faulty installation typically isn’t covered.
  • Deductibles: Florida often imposes hurricane-specific deductibles ranging from 1% to 5% of the home's insured value, which can be significant for window repairs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Florida homeowner insurance cover all types of impact window damage?

No, coverage typically applies only to damage caused by covered perils such as hurricanes, storms, or wind. Damage resulting from neglect, wear and tear, or accidental breakage may not be covered under standard homeowner policies.

How do hurricane deductibles affect impact window repair claims in Florida?

Florida policies often have a hurricane-specific deductible, which is a percentage of your home's insured value, rather than a flat fee. This deductibles ranges from 1% to 5%, meaning you could be responsible for thousands before insurance pays for repairs.

Can I get insurance discounts for having impact-resistant windows in Florida?

Yes. Many Florida insurers provide wind mitigation credits or discounts if your home has certified impact windows, shutters, or other storm-resistant features documented by a professional inspection.

What should I do if my insurance denies my impact window damage claim?

If denied, review the claim carefully and gather additional evidence of storm-related damage. You can appeal to the insurance company, consult a public adjuster, or seek legal advice to explore further options for claim resolution.
