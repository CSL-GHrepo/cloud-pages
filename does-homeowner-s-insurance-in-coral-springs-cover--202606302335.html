Understanding What Homeowner’s Insurance Covers for Water Damage in Coral Springs

Homeowner’s insurance policies in Coral Springs cover water damage resulting from:

  • Sudden Pipe Bursts: A common cause of water intrusion, typically covered under dwelling protection.
  • Accidental Overflows: For example, washing machine or dishwasher leaks.
  • Emergency Water Extraction Insurance: Some policies offer coverage for emergency mitigation services to minimize damage.

What is generally not covered:

  • Flood Damage: Coverage for storm-related flooding isn’t standard and requires separate flood insurance.
  • Gradual Leaks: Slowly occurring leaks or ongoing maintenance issues often fall outside coverage.
  • Negligence or Lack of Maintenance: Damage resulting from homeowner neglect typically is excluded.

In Coral Springs, where flooding and tropical storms are common, it’s vital to verify your policy’s flood endorsements and limitations. The National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) offers additional protection but requires a separate policy.
