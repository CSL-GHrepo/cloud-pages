What Does Not Qualify for Coverage? Common Exclusions

It is equally important to know when impact window repairs won't be covered by homeowners insurance:

  • Flood Damage: Flood-related window damage usually requires separate flood insurance through the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP).
  • Wear and Tear or Poor Maintenance: Damage due to aging seals, corrosion, or failure to perform routine upkeep is excluded.
  • Improper Installation: Insurance policies typically don't cover damage resulting from faulty installation or manufacturing defects.
  • Disasters Not Listed as Covered Perils: Damage from events like earthquakes or intentional acts may not be included.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does homeowners insurance cover hurricane impact window insurance claims in Florida?

Yes, most Florida homeowners insurance policies cover impact window repairs when damage results from hurricanes, as hurricanes are considered covered perils under these policies. However, coverage varies by policy, so it's important to ensure your policy includes hurricane impact window insurance and understand your deductible, which can be a percentage of the home's value.

How does storm damage window repair Florida coverage differ from regular window coverage?

Storm damage coverage typically addresses sudden, accidental damage caused by wind, flying debris, or hail, whereas regular window coverage might exclude damage from gradual wear or neglect. Florida’s hurricane-prone environment often requires separate or enhanced storm damage coverage to address impact-resistant window repair needs adequately.

What should I do if my impact window insurance claim is denied?

First, review your Florida insurance policies for windows carefully to understand the reason for denial. You can then appeal the decision by providing additional documentation such as photos, repair estimates, and quotes from reputable contractors. Consulting an insurance claims expert or attorney specializing in Florida home insurance claims can also improve your chances of overturning a denial.

Are impact-resistant windows mandatory for Florida homeowners insurance discounts?

While not always mandatory, many Florida insurance providers offer discounts or preferred rates to homeowners who install impact-resistant windows that meet state building codes. These windows reduce the likelihood of damage during storms, lowering the insurer’s risk and often translating to significant savings on premiums.
