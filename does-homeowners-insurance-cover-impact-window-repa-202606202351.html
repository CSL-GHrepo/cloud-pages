Quick Answer: Coverage of Impact Window Repair in Florida Homeowners Insurance

Most Florida homeowners insurance policies cover impact window repair or replacement if damage is caused by a covered peril such as hurricane winds or flying storm debris. Damage due to wear and tear, poor maintenance, or non-covered events generally won’t be reimbursed. Your policy’s deductible and specific coverage terms—like limits on windstorm damage—will affect out-of-pocket costs. Always confirm with your insurer and maintain documentation for impact window repair claims.
