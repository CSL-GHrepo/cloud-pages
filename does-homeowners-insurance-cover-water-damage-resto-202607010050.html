Understanding Your Homeowners Policy Water Damage Coverage in Miami

Homeowners policies typically cover residential water damage Miami caused by unexpected incidents such as:

  • Burst or frozen pipes
  • Accidental overflow from plumbing, heating, or cooling systems
  • Water heater leaks
  • Sudden appliance malfunctions spilling water

However, it’s critical to know what is excluded:

  • Flood damage: Miami is a flood-prone area; floods from heavy rains, storm surges, or hurricanes are typically excluded and require separate flood insurance.
  • Gradual leaks: Damage due to ongoing leaks or neglect is not covered because it is considered maintenance-related.
  • Seepage or ground water: Slow water intrusion over time is generally excluded.

Insurance coverage for water repairs usually covers not only the water extraction and drying but also repairs to damaged structures up to policy limits.
