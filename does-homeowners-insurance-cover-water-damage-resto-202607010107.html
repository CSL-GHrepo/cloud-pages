Flood vs Water Damage Insurance: What Melbourne Residents Must Know

Distinguishing between flood and water damage insurance is crucial when navigating claims.

  • Water damage insurance: Part of your standard homeowners policy; covers sudden leaks, plumbing failures, accidental discharge.
  • Flood insurance: Separate policy needed for damage caused by external flooding, heavy rain accumulation, storm surge, and backups from public water systems.

For Melbourne homeowners, especially those near coastal areas or low-lying regions, evaluating flood risk and investing in appropriate flood insurance can prevent catastrophic financial loss.
