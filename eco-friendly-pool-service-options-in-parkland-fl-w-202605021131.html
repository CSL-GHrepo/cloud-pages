Water Conservation Pool Tips for Parkland Homeowners

Water conservation plays a critical role in sustainable swimming pool care, especially in Parkland where seasonal dry spells can strain water supplies.

  1. Use Pool Covers: Covers reduce evaporation by up to 90%, helping preserve water and maintain temperature.
  2. Optimize Backwashing Frequency: Overly frequent backwashing wastes water; scheduling it based on pressure gauges saves water.
  3. Regular Leak Inspections: Fixing leaks promptly prevents significant water loss.
  4. Rainwater Harvesting: Some homeowners use collected rainwater to top off pools, reducing municipal water dependency.

These water-saving practices combined with environmentally safe pool management strategies can reduce your pool's water usage by at least 25% annually.
