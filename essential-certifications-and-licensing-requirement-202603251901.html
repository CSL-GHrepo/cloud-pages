Key Locksmith Certifications to Look for in 2024

  • Certified Registered Locksmith (CRL): Offered by ALOA, this foundational certification confirms completion of locksmith training programs and mastery of basic locksmith skills.
  • Certified Professional Locksmith (CPL): A mid-level credential, CPL certifies more advanced capabilities including commercial and automotive locksmithing.
  • Certified Master Locksmith (CML): This prestigious certification is awarded to locksmiths with comprehensive experience and expertise across all locksmith disciplines.
  • Security Certifications: Specialized certificates for those trained in electronic locks, access control systems, and security technology integration.
  • State or Local Licensing: Many states and municipalities require locksmith licensing, often involving fingerprinting, background checks, and proof of insurance.

For example, in California, locksmiths must register with the Bureau of Security and Investigative Services (BSIS) and complete a 6-hour locksmith course approved by the state. Similar requirements exist in Texas, Florida, and New York, each with its own nuances.
