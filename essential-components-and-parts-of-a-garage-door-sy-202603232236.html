Step-by-Step Guide to Garage Door Maintenance in Parkland

First, assess your garage door for visible damage or signs of wear. Look for rust, loose hardware, or frayed cables. Operate the door slowly to observe its movement and listen for unusual noises.

Next, clean the tracks with a damp cloth and remove debris that could hinder smooth operation. Use a soft brush or vacuum to keep rollers free of dust and dirt.

Lubricate all moving parts, including hinges, rollers, and springs, with a high-quality garage door lubricant. Avoid over-lubricating, which can attract dirt and cause buildup.

Check the balance of your garage door by disconnecting the opener and manually opening the door halfway. It should stay in place. If it doesn’t, spring tension adjustments may be needed—call a professional.

Finally, test the safety features of your garage door, such as the auto-reverse mechanism and sensors. Regular testing ensures your system responds correctly to obstacles, preventing accidents and ensuring compliance with safety standards.
