Practical Tips for Maintaining Your Locks in Parkland

In Parkland’s humid subtropical climate, locks are exposed to moisture and temperature fluctuations, which can affect their performance. Regular maintenance is essential to keep them functioning smoothly. A simple tip from my experience is to lubricate your locks annually with graphitized or silicone-based lubricant. This prevents sticking and rust, especially vital during the rainy season.

Checking and tightening screws and plates is another straightforward maintenance task that can prevent lock failure. Over time, exposure to weather can cause components to loosen, leading to security vulnerabilities. Our technicians recommend inspecting your locks periodically and addressing any issues promptly to avoid costly repairs later.

Rekeying your locks after moving to a new home or if you suspect key loss is a must-do step in maintaining security.
