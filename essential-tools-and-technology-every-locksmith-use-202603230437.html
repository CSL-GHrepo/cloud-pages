How to Maintain Your Locks and Keys

Proper maintenance extends the lifespan of your locks and keys, reducing emergency calls and costly repairs. Dirt, debris, and corrosion are common culprits that impair lock operation, especially in humid areas like Parkland.

Lubricating your locks with a graphite or silicone-based lubricant every 6-12 months keeps the mechanism functioning smoothly. Avoid oil-based lubricants, which can attract dirt.
