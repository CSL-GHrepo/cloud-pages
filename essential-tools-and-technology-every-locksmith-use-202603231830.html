Helpful Tips for Maintaining Your Locks

Maintaining your locks regularly can save you money and prevent emergencies. Simple steps like lubricating locks annually can prevent sticking and corrosion. We recommend using graphite or silicone-based lubricants—avoid oil-based products that can attract dust and dirt.

Another tip is to avoid forcing locks or turning keys with excessive force. If you notice difficulty operating your lock, call a professional before it breaks or gets damaged. Our team can perform inspections and minor repairs to keep everything functioning smoothly.

Change locks promptly if you lose keys or experience a security breach. Rekeying is quick and affordable, providing peace of mind without replacing the entire lock. It’s especially important after moving into a new home or losing keys.

Keep your keys in a safe place, and avoid hiding spare keys outside your home where they can be easily found. Instead, consider giving a spare to a trusted neighbor or family member.

Finally, schedule regular security assessments to identify vulnerabilities and upgrade outdated locks. Modern technology offers many options for enhanced safety, so staying current is crucial for long-term security.
