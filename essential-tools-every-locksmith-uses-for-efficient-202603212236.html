Key Maintenance and Secure Storage Tips

In my experience, many homeowners overlook the importance of key and lock maintenance until an emergency occurs. Regularly lubricating locks with graphite or silicone spray keeps them functioning smoothly. Also, inspecting keys for wear and tear can prevent breakage inside locks, saving you costly repairs.

Secure storage of spare keys is crucial. Never hide spare keys in obvious spots outside your house. Instead, consider a lockbox or leave a spare with a trusted neighbor or friend. This reduces the risk of lockouts or unauthorized access, which are common concerns in neighborhoods like Quail Ridge or Pine Tree.

I recommend having duplicate keys made at a reputable locksmith rather than a hardware store, as the quality and duplication accuracy are often better. Keep a record of all keys assigned to your property, and periodically review who has access. This simple step enhances your overall security posture.

For those with electronic or smart locks, change access codes periodically. Ensure firmware updates are performed if the system allows. These small ongoing maintenance tasks significantly extend the lifespan of your security investments and prevent potential vulnerabilities.
