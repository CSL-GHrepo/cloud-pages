Essential Types of Locks & Security Devices Locksmiths Commonly Handle

Being locked out feels like time stops. Your keys are right there—on the other side of a door that won't budge. Quick Overview: Locksmith Services and the Security Devices They Handle

When you call a locksmith, you’re not just getting a person who can open a locked door; you’re getting an expert in security technology and physical protection measures. Below are the essential types of locks and devices locksmiths commonly install, service, or repair:

  • Deadbolt Locks
  • Smart Lock Systems
  • Keyless Entry
  • High-Security Locks
  • Padlocks
  • Electronic Access Control Systems
  • Lock Rekeying
  • Master Key Systems

Deadbolt Locks: The Classic Security Staple

Deadbolt locks remain one of the most widely used and trusted security devices in residential and commercial properties. A deadbolt provides higher resistance against forced entry compared to spring bolt locks because it physically extends a solid metal bolt into the door frame.

  • Single-cylinder deadbolts use a key from the outside and a thumb turn on the inside.
  • Double-cylinder deadbolts require a key from both sides, offering added security but can pose an emergency risk.
“According to the FBI’s Uniform Crime Reporting, the use of deadbolt locks reduces the chance of residential burglary by approximately 30%.”

Locksmiths often install, repair, or upgrade deadbolt locks to high-quality models that meet ANSI Grade 1 or 2 standards, ensuring enhanced durability and security for property owners.

Smart Lock Systems & Keyless Entry: The Future of Convenience Meets Security

Technological advancements have revolutionized how we secure our properties. Locksmiths are increasingly skilled in handling smart lock systems that enable homeowners and business managers to control access remotely via smartphones, Bluetooth, or Wi-Fi.

  • Smart Locks integrate with home automation setups and can be programmed for temporary codes or biometric entry.
  • Keyless Entry Systems eliminate traditional keys altogether, often utilizing numeric keypads, RFID cards, or fingerprint scanners.
“Industry professionals recommend smart locks for users seeking both convenience and robust security, noting that properly installed systems can reduce unauthorized access risk significantly.”

Alongside installation, locksmiths provide troubleshooting, firmware updates, and integration of these systems with existing lock hardware, ensuring seamless operation.

High-Security Locks and Master Key Systems: Advanced Protection for Complex Needs

For businesses or multi-tenant properties, locksmiths frequently handle high-security locks designed to resist picking, drilling, bumping, or other forced entry tactics.

  • These locks feature patented key control, meaning unauthorized key duplication is almost impossible.
  • Master key systems enable hierarchical access control, letting administrators issue varying access levels—ideal for property managers or facility supervisors.

By installing high-security locks, locksmiths provide robust solutions tailored to environments where security is paramount. These systems help reduce the risk of internal theft or unauthorized entry by managing who can open which doors.

Padlocks & Electronic Access Control: Portable and Scalable Security Solutions

Padlocks continue to offer portable security solutions largely used for gates, storage units, lockers, and toolboxes. Locksmiths stock and handle various padlock types, including weather-resistant, combination, and high-security variants.

Electronic access control systems are often installed in commercial settings that require scalable and auditable access management. These systems may combine door readers, electric strikes, and keypads to deliver customized security settings.

“Industry studies show electronic access control systems can reduce unauthorized access incidents by up to 50%, thanks to features like audit trails and customizable scheduling.”

Lock Rekeying: Cost-Effective Security Without Replacement

One of the most common locksmith services is lock rekeying, which involves altering the internal mechanism of an existing lock so that old keys no longer work and new ones do.

  • Rekeying is ideal for new homeowners, businesses managing employee turnover, or when keys are lost or stolen.
  • Locksmiths can rekey almost any lock, providing an affordable security upgrade without the expense of full replacement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of locks do locksmiths most commonly work with?

Locksmiths commonly work with deadbolt locks, smart lock systems, high-security locks, padlocks, keyless entry devices, and electronic access control systems. They also provide services like lock rekeying and master key system setup.

How do smart lock systems differ from traditional locks?

Smart lock systems offer remote control, temporary entry codes, and biometric access options, whereas traditional locks rely on physical keys. Locksmiths ensure smart locks are properly installed and integrated for maximum security and convenience.

What is lock rekeying and when should I consider it?

Lock rekeying involves changing a lock’s internal tumblers so old keys no longer work. It is ideal after moving into a new home, losing keys, or employee turnover to maintain security without replacing whole locks.

Why would a business benefit from a master key system?

Master key systems allow different access levels by creating a hierarchical key system. Businesses can restrict or grant access easily while reducing the number of keys carried, which enhances security and convenience.
