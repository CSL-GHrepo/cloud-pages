Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Parkland Residents

Proactive maintenance helps prevent unexpected breakdowns and extends garage door longevity. Due to Parkland’s weather patterns—including hot summers, rain, and occasional storms—seasonal maintenance is particularly critical.

Spring Maintenance Checklist

  • Inspect for rust or corrosion on springs, cables, and tracks.
  • Lubricate moving parts with a high-quality garage door lubricant to ensure smooth operation.
  • Test the door's balance and adjust the tension if the door does not stay open on its own.
  • Check weatherstripping for signs of wear and replace to keep out moisture and pests.
  • Verify remote batteries and functionality before the busy summer months.

Summer Maintenance Checklist

  • Regularly clean the door surface to prevent salt buildup from ocean breezes.
  • Inspect door hardware for expansion caused by heat and adjust as necessary.
  • Ensure vents and drainage points are clear to avoid water accumulation during heavy rains.
  • Schedule a professional tune-up mid-summer for safety inspections and thorough servicing.

Fall and Winter Maintenance Checklist

  • Check and tighten loose bolts or screws from seasonal expansion and contraction.
  • Test safety sensors and auto-reverse features to keep your family and pets safe.
  • Inspect battery backups for openers to prepare for winter storms and power outages.
  • Clear leaves and debris from around the door tracks to prevent jamming.

These seasonal care routines, combined with expert servicing by The Garage Door Pros, help avoid costly repairs and ensure reliable operation year-round.
