Tips to Extend the Life of Your Garage Door in Parkland’s Coastal Climate

Parkland’s proximity to the coast introduces challenges such as salt air, high humidity, and strong sun exposure. These factors can accelerate wear and tear on garage door components unless carefully managed.

  • Regular cleaning: Wash doors with mild soap and water to remove salt deposits and prevent corrosion.
  • Lubricate moving parts: Use specialty garage door lubricants on springs, rollers, and tracks to maintain smooth operation and reduce rust.
  • Inspect weather seals: Replace worn seals to block moisture and pests from entering your garage.
  • Apply protective finishes: For wood or composite doors, reseal or stain surfaces annually to safeguard against sun and water damage.
  • Schedule professional check-ups: Annual assessments by experts such as The Garage Door Pros identify hidden issues before they escalate.
