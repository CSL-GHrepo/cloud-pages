Safety First: Essential Garage Door Safety Tips for Parkland Residents

Garage doors are heavy, powerful mechanisms that require respect and awareness to avoid accidents. The Garage Door Pros prioritize safety education as part of their service, empowering Parkland homeowners with practical advice:

  • Test Auto-Reverse Features Monthly: Ensure sensors immediately stop the door if an object obstructs its path.
  • Keep Remote Controls Out of Reach of Children: Prevent unintentional operation that could lead to injury.
  • Inspect Cables and Springs for Wear: Avoid DIY repairs on these components due to risk of sudden failure and injury.
  • Regularly Clean and Clear Tracks: Remove debris to prevent misalignment and jams.
  • Schedule Professional Safety Inspections: Annual checks by licensed technicians ensure compliance with safety standards and reduce liability risks.
