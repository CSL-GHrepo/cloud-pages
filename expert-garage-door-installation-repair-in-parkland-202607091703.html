How to Maintain Your Garage Door for Longevity and Performance

Proactive maintenance can extend the life of your garage door and prevent unexpected failures. The Garage Door Pros recommend the following routine tasks for Parkland residents:

  1. Monthly Visual Inspections: Look for worn springs, frayed cables, and damaged panels.
  2. Lubrication: Use a garage door lubricant on moving parts like rollers, hinges, and springs every 3-6 months.
  3. Test Safety Features: Regularly test auto-reverse mechanisms and photo eye sensors to ensure secure operation.
  4. Check and Adjust Balance: A door that doesn’t stay in place could indicate spring issues needing professional attention.
  5. Professional Tune-Ups: Schedule annual service with trusted garage door technicians to catch hidden problems early.

Taking these steps not only protects your investment but also ensures your family, employees, and property remain safe.
