Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for South Florida Homeowners

Florida’s subtropical climate demands extra attention to garage door maintenance to combat humidity, salt air, and frequent storms. Here are actionable tips to keep your garage door operating smoothly year-round:

  • Spring and Summer: Inspect and lubricate hinges, rollers, and springs monthly to prevent rust and ensure silent operation during humid months.
  • Post-Storm Checks: After hurricanes or severe thunderstorms, examine the door’s alignment, tracks, and opener functionality to identify damage early.
  • Air Circulation: Keep garage vents clear to reduce condensation buildup that can corrode metal parts.
  • UV Protection: Apply UV-resistant coatings or paint touch-ups to prevent sun damage, especially on wooden and painted steel doors.

Following these practices, advised by The Garage Door Pros technicians, extends equipment life and prevents unexpected failures during critical times.
