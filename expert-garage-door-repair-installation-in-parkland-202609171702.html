Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for Parkland Residents

Florida’s year-round warm climate and distinct wet seasons pose unique challenges for garage door maintenance. To help extend the life and reliability of your garage door, The Garage Door Pros recommend seasonal care practices tailored to Parkland’s environment.

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Thorough cleaning: Remove accumulated dirt, bugs, and pollen from tracks and door surfaces to reduce wear and improve appearance.
  • Lubricate moving components: Apply premium garage door lubricant more frequently due to humidity accelerating corrosion on metal parts.
  • Check weather seals: Inspect and replace damaged seals to prevent moisture intrusion during summer rains.
  • Test opener responsiveness: Ensure electric components function well under higher temperatures which can affect electronics.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Inspect for rust and corrosion: Despite mild winters, moisture can cause rust; prompt treatment prevents damage escalation.
  • Adjust door tension: Temperature fluctuations may affect spring tension; ensure smooth operation and reduce opener strain.
  • Test safety sensors: With earlier sunsets and increased darkness, sensor reliability is critical for night-time safety.
  • Clear drainage: Ensure proper drainage around garage doors to prevent water pooling or entry during seasonal storms.

By following these seasonally tailored tips, Parkland homeowners can minimize unexpected failures and maintain optimal garage door performance year-round.
