Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Plantation, FL

To avoid unexpected breakdowns, Plantation homeowners and business owners should consider these maintenance tips:

  1. Schedule Annual Inspections: Have Plantation garage door technicians assess all mechanical parts and safety features.
  2. Lubricate Moving Parts: Use silicone-based lubricants to reduce friction and wear on rollers, hinges, and springs.
  3. Test Auto-Reverse Features: Ensure sensors and automatic reversing functions activate properly.
  4. Keep Tracks Clean: Remove dirt and debris from door tracks to prevent jams.
  5. Replace Weather Seals: Prevent moisture intrusion, which can accelerate rust and wood warping.
