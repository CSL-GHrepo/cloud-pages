Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door Between Professional Visits

While expert technicians at Garage Door Repair of Plantation conduct thorough inspections, homeowners can extend the life of their doors by following these maintenance tips:

  1. Regular Lubrication: Use a silicone-based lubricant on springs, rollers, and hinges every six months.
  2. Visual Inspection: Check for rust, loose hardware, and frayed cables monthly.
  3. Balance Test: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door about halfway. It should stay in place. If it doesn’t, the springs might need adjustment.
  4. Clean Tracks: Remove debris from tracks and avoid using grease; light cleaning with mild detergent is preferable.
  5. Test Safety Features: Test the garage door opener’s auto-reverse and photo-eye sensors monthly to ensure safe operation.

These simple steps can prevent premature failures and keep repair costs low.

Advanced Safety Features and Sensors

Safety is a major concern with garage doors, as malfunctioning components can cause serious injuries or property damage. Modern doors include a range of safety features that must be tested and maintained regularly:

  • Auto-Reverse Mechanism: Detects obstructions under the door and reverses its movement to prevent harm.
  • Photo-Eye Sensors: Infrared beams monitor the path of the door to stop closing if objects or people are detected.
  • Manual Release Handles: Allow quick disconnection of the door from the opener during emergencies or power outages.
  • Force Settings: Control the strength needed to open or close the door, preventing overexertion that can damage the system.

Garage Door Repair of Plantation trains its technicians to rigorously inspect and calibrate these features during service visits to ensure compliant and secure operation.
