Understanding Common Garage Door Problems and Their Causes

Although garage doors appear simple, many interconnected components can develop issues over time. Knowing the common problems and their causes can help you identify when professional service is necessary.

Problems with Garage Door Springs

  • Symptoms: Door won’t open or closes abruptly; loud snapping noises.
  • Causes: Springs wear out due to tension fatigue, rust, or lack of lubrication.
  • Impact: Can cause door imbalance and pose serious safety hazards if left unaddressed.

Garage Door Opener Troubles

  • Symptoms: Door fails to respond to remote; intermittent operation; sluggish movement.
  • Causes: Electrical issues, sensor misalignment, worn gears, or outdated openers.
  • Solutions: Repair or replacement with modern units enhances reliability.

Track and Roller Problems

  • Symptoms: Door jerks, sticks, or makes grinding noises during movement.
  • Causes: Bent or misaligned tracks, worn rollers, debris accumulation.
  • Treatment: Track realignment, roller replacement, and thorough cleaning improve operation.

Sensor and Safety Mechanism Issues

  • Symptoms: Door reverses unexpectedly or fails to close fully.
  • Causes: Misaligned or dirty photoelectric sensors, wiring faults.
  • Importance: Proper sensor function is critical for preventing accidents and injuries.

Recognizing these common problems early and contacting a professional like Garage Door Repair of Weston can prevent costly damages and safety risks.
