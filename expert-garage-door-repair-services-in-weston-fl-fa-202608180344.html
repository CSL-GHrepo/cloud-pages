Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for Weston’s Climate

Weston’s subtropical climate presents unique challenges for garage door upkeep. Seasonal maintenance is essential to prevent weather-related damages and keep systems running smoothly year-round.

Spring and Summer Garage Door Maintenance

  • Inspect for Water Damage: Frequent thunderstorms in spring can cause water intrusion and rust on metal components. Inspect seals and weather stripping to prevent leaks.
  • Lubrication: High humidity can cause parts to stick or corrode. Apply garage-door-specific lubricants to rollers, hinges, and springs during spring cleaning.
  • Test Safety Sensors: Dirt and debris buildup during the rainy season can impair sensor function, so regular monthly testing is critical.

Fall and Winter Garage Door Maintenance

  • Check Door Alignment: Temperature fluctuations can cause contraction and expansion of materials. Misaligned doors can cause strain on openers and affect sealing.
  • Seal Inspection and Replacement: Replace weather stripping if cracked or brittle to maintain energy efficiency as temperatures drop.
  • Prepare for Storms: Secure any loose components and clear gutters around garage areas to avoid hurricane-related damage during late summer and fall.

Following these seasonal tips helps Weston residents reduce emergency repairs and extend the lifespan of their garage doors, especially when combined with annual professional inspections.
