Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Weston Homeowners

To keep your garage door running smoothly between professional visits, follow these simple maintenance tips tailored for Weston’s climate:

  • Lubricate moving parts: Use silicone-based lubricants on rollers, springs, and hinges every six months to reduce wear and noise.
  • Inspect weather seals: Replace cracked or brittle seals to improve insulation and prevent water intrusion during rainy seasons.
  • Test the balance: Manually lift the door halfway and see if it stays put. If it moves, springs may need adjustment or replacement.
  • Keep tracks clean: Remove debris and ensure tracks are straight to prevent misalignment.
  • Check safety features: Verify sensors and auto-reverse functions are operational to avoid injury.

Incorporating these practices into your home routine reduces the likelihood of emergency repairs and extends the lifespan of your garage door system.
