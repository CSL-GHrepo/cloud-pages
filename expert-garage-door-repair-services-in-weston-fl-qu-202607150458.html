Preventative Tips for Homeowners and Business Owners in Weston

While expert services are essential for garage door upkeep, proactive care by owners can further reduce damage and extend service life. Here are actionable tips from the professionals:

  • Conduct monthly visual inspections: Look for signs of wear, rust, or loose components

  • Avoid forcing the door: Never attempt to manually lift a door with broken springs or cables
  • Test safety features regularly: Press the auto reverse button and observe sensor functionality
  • Schedule annual professional maintenance: Even if your door seems fine, expert tune-ups catch subtle issues early
