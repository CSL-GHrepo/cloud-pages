4. Garage Door Troubleshooting Tips

While some minor issues can be diagnosed at home, Garage Door Repair of Weston advises caution. Here are key troubleshooting tips before calling a technician:

  • Check batteries and remote controls for opener problems
  • Inspect tracks and rollers for debris or damage
  • Listen for unusual noises indicating spring or cable issues
  • Manually operate the door to test balance (with the opener disengaged)

These simple steps can help identify problems but always call a professional for repairs involving springs, cables, or complex electrical components.
