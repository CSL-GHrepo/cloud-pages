Swimming Pool Maintenance Tips from Deerfield Beach Pool Technicians

Industry professionals recommend the following best practices for pool owners seeking to maintain their pools between professional visits:

  1. Regular Skimming and Brushing: Remove debris from the water surface daily and brush pool walls weekly to prevent algae buildup.
  2. Maintain Correct Water Levels: Keep the pool water at the midpoint of the skimmer intake to ensure proper pump function.
  3. Test Water Chemistry Weekly: Monitor pH, chlorine, and alkalinity levels using reliable test kits to avoid imbalances.
  4. Clean Pool Filters Monthly: Both cartridge and sand filters require routine cleaning to sustain water clarity.
  5. Inspect Pool Equipment: Look for leaks, strange noises, and wear on pumps and heaters.
