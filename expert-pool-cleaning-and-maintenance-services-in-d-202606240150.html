The Importance of Pool Water Testing and Balancing in Deerfield Beach

Accurate water testing and chemical balancing form the cornerstone of pool health. Industry professionals recommend weekly water tests that assess:

  • pH levels (ideal range: 7.2–7.8)
  • Sanitizer concentration (chlorine or bromine)
  • Alkalinity and calcium hardness
  • Phosphate and cyanuric acid levels
