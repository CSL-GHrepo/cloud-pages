Expert Advice When Facing Water Damage in Florida

In the face of water damage, The Florida Restoration Pros recommend the following immediate steps while waiting for professional help:

  1. Turn off the main water supply if safe to do so to prevent further flooding.
  2. Avoid walking through standing water to prevent electrical hazards.
  3. Remove small appliances and electronics from affected areas.
  4. Document damage with photos or videos for insurance purposes.
  5. Contact certified restoration professionals immediately for emergency water extraction and remediation.
