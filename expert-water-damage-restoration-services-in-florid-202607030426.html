Preventative Tips and Immediate Steps for Water Damage Victims in Florida

Acting quickly can drastically reduce the extent of damage. Here are critical steps recommended by The Florida Restoration Pros:

  • Ensure Safety: Shut off electricity and avoid contaminated water to prevent injury.
  • Stop the Source: Repair leaking pipes or roof damage causing water intrusion if safe and possible.
  • Document Damage: Take photos and videos before cleanup for insurance claims.
  • Call Professionals: Contact The Florida Restoration Pros immediately for an expert assessment and rapid deployment.
  • Avoid DIY Drying: Home fans and heaters may drive mold growth by pushing moisture deeper inside walls.
