Quick Comparison: Fair Market Value Lease vs Fixed Purchase Option

  • Fair Market Value Lease: Typically lower monthly payments; flexibility to return, renew, or purchase at fair market value at lease end.
  • Fixed Purchase Option Lease: Higher monthly payments but guaranteed purchase price at lease conclusion, leading to ownership.

Understanding key differences in equipment leasing terms will help you optimize your copier investment.

Understanding Copier Lease Options: What is a Fair Market Value Lease?

A Fair Market Value (FMV) lease is a popular leasing agreement where the monthly payments are based on the depreciated value of the copier over the lease term, rather than the full purchase price. At the end of the lease, businesses have multiple end-of-lease options including:

  • Return the equipment with no additional obligation (common with FMV leases)
  • Renew or extend the lease
  • Purchase the copier at its current fair market value

This flexibility enables companies to upgrade to the latest models more easily, especially important in fast-evolving technology environments.

According to industry research by Equipment Leasing & Finance Association (ELFA), more than 60% of businesses prefer leases with flexible end-of-lease options to remain agile in technology acquisition.

Benefits of FMV Leases

  • Lower Monthly Payments: Lease payments are usually lower than fixed purchase leases, which helps preserve working capital.
  • Technology Upgrades: Ability to trade-in and upgrade to newer equipment at lease end without the hassle of ownership.
  • Reduced Risk: You’re not locked into ownership, which mitigates the risk of obsolescence or equipment failure.
  • Tax Advantages: Lease payments can often be deducted as a business expense.

What is a Fixed Purchase Option Lease?

A Fixed Purchase Option lease sets a predetermined price that you can pay to purchase the equipment at the end of the lease term. This type of copier leasing agreement blends aspects of leasing and buying, giving companies predictability and eventual ownership.

Unlike FMV leases, the financial terms are fixed upfront for the purchase price, giving peace of mind to those planning long-term equipment use.

Benefits of Fixed Purchase Option Leases

  • Predictable Costs: Knowing your eventual purchase price upfront helps with budgeting and financial planning.
  • Ownership: After paying the buyout price, you own the copier outright and can use it beyond the lease term without additional payments.
  • Asset Equity: Owning the equipment allows you to capitalize it as an asset on your balance sheet.

For businesses confident in their copier usage over many years, this route can be more cost-effective in the long run.

Lease vs Buy Copier: Factors to Consider

Before finalizing a copier leasing agreement, ask yourself:

  1. How long will I use the equipment? Shorter-term needs often favor FMV leases; longer-term use may justify fixed purchase.
  2. Do I want ownership? If owning the copier is important, fixed purchase provides certainty.
  3. What is my upgrade cycle? Frequent upgrades benefit from FMV lease flexibility.
  4. What is my budget for monthly payments? FMV leases typically offer lower payments, freeing up cash flow.

For example, a mid-sized marketing firm leasing copiers outsourced their lease to a local South Florida provider and chose an FMV lease to keep up with changing print demands and technology. Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main differences between Fair Market Value and Fixed Purchase Option copier leases?

Fair Market Value leases offer lower monthly payments and flexibility to return, renew, or buy the copier at the end of the lease, while Fixed Purchase Option leases have higher payments but guarantee ownership at a set price. The choice depends on your business’s desire for flexibility versus ownership.

Which copier lease option offers better tax advantages?

Both lease types provide tax benefits, but FMV leases generally allow for full monthly payment deductions as a business expense. Fixed Purchase Option leases allow depreciation and interest expense deductions after purchase. Consult a tax professional for your specific situation.

Can I upgrade my copier easily with a Fixed Purchase Option lease?

Upgrading is more straightforward with Fair Market Value leases since you can return the current equipment at lease end. Fixed Purchase Option leases require you to own the copier first, which may delay upgrades and add resale challenges.

How do end-of-lease options differ between FMV and Fixed Purchase Option leases?

FMV leases typically offer returning the equipment with no further obligation, renewing the lease, or purchasing at market value. Fixed Purchase Option leases usually revolve around buying the copier at a predetermined price to obtain ownership.
