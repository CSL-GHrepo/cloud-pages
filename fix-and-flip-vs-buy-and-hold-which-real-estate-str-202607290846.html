Important Terms for Parkland Investors

Understanding key industry terms can help you navigate hard money lending more effectively. Let’s go over some of the most common terms tailored for our local Parkland investors. — our fix and flip loans team.

First, 'Loan-to-Value' (LTV) ratio indicates the percentage of the property’s value financed through the loan. Most hard money lenders in Parkland offer LTVs between 65% and 75%, allowing some equity for your investment. This ratio impacts your loan terms and risk exposure.

'Interest-only payments' are common in hard money loans, meaning you pay only the interest during the loan term, which helps keep monthly payments manageable during renovation or flipping phases. Once the project is complete, you typically refinance or pay off the principal balance.

A 'bridge loan' provides short-term financing to bridge the gap between buying a property and securing long-term financing. This is especially useful in Parkland’s competitive real estate environment where quick closings are necessary.

'Rehab budget' refers to the estimated costs for renovating or improving a property. Accurate budgeting is essential for securing a hard money loan because lenders want assurance that the project will enhance the property’s value and support loan repayment.
