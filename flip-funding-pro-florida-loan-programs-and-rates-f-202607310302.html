Understanding the Renovation Draw Process with Flip Funding Pro

The process generally includes:

  • Initial Loan Disbursement: A portion of the loan is released upon closing to cover the acquisition costs.

  • Inspections: Flip Funding Pro’s field representatives perform inspections to verify the scope and quality of the work before approving draw payments.

This controlled draw method protects both the lender and borrower by ensuring loan funds are used appropriately and that the property’s value increases as anticipated, reducing the risk of overruns or project delays.
