Industry Terms Every Parkland Investor Should Know

Interest-Only Loan: A loan where you pay only interest during the term, reducing monthly costs during rehab or holding periods. Principal is paid at the end.

Loan-to-Value (LTV): The ratio of the loan amount to the property’s appraised value. Higher LTV means more financing relative to property worth, but may require higher interest.

Bridge Loan: A short-term loan that helps you 'bridge' the gap until long-term financing or sale occurs. Ideal for quick acquisitions or rehab projects.

ARV (After Repair Value): The estimated value of a property after renovation. Critical for determining loan size and project feasibility.

Hard Money Lender: A private lender offering short-term, asset-based loans typically used for real estate investment projects.
