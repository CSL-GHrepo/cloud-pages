Common Risks in Hard Money Lending

  • Higher Interest Rates: Compared to traditional loans, hard money loans typically carry higher interest rates due to increased risk taken by the lender.
  • Short Loan Terms: Repayment periods are often short (6 to 18 months), putting pressure on investors to carefully plan exits, such as property sales or refinancing.
  • Project Delays: Unforeseen issues like contractor delays, cost overruns, or permit problems can jeopardize project timelines and loan repayment.
  • Property Market Fluctuations: Shifts in Florida’s housing market or economic conditions can impact the property’s resale value during the loan term.
  • Loan-to-Value Risks: Overestimating property values or rehab costs can lead to cash flow problems if the final sale price falls short.
