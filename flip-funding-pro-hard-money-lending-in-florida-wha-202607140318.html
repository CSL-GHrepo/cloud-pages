These loans primarily rely on the property’s current or after-repair value (ARV) to determine loan eligibility and amount, rather than traditional lending metrics such as credit scores or income verification.

In Florida, where the real estate market is dynamic and fast-paced, hard money loans serve investors who need quick funding for purchasing undervalued properties, financing renovations, and reselling at a profit. These loans typically range from 6 months to 2 years — ideal for short-term projects such as fix and flip investing.
