Key Financial Metrics to Consider Before Engaging Flip Funding Pro

Investors interested in applying for a hard money loan through Flip Funding Pro should carefully analyze crucial financial metrics to ensure project viability and sustainable returns. Important considerations include:

Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV)

LTV is the loan amount divided by the property's appraised value or purchase price, whichever is lower. Flip Funding Pro typically approves up to 75% LTV, but prudent investors should calculate conservative LTVs to provide a buffer against market fluctuations.

After Repair Value (ARV)

ARV estimates the potential resale value upon project completion. This figure is critical in Flip Funding Pro’s underwriting and guides loan structuring. Investors must base ARV on comparable sales and realistic renovations to avoid overvaluation.

Return on Investment (ROI)

Calculating expected ROI includes all costs—purchase, renovation, financing fees, holding costs—and projected sale price. Because hard money loans carry higher interest rates, factoring in these costs upfront helps determine if the project meets required profit thresholds.

Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)

While more common in commercial real estate, DSCR measures net operating income relative to debt obligations. In fix and flip projects, ensuring that projected cash flows or sale proceeds comfortably cover loan repayment minimizes risk of default.

Holding Period

The duration between loan disbursal and property sale affects total interest and holding costs. Flip Funding Pro’s short loan terms necessitate tight project timelines; underestimating this can increase carrying costs and erode profits.
