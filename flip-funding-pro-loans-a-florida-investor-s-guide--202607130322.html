  • Short Repayment Periods: Loan terms generally range from 6 to 18 months, requiring investors to have a clear exit strategy such as quick resale or refinance. Failure to repay within the term can lead to refinancing challenges or default.
  • Loan-to-Value (LTV) Limits: Although Flip Funding Pro offers up to 75% of ARV, there is still a need for investor equity or additional financing to cover the gap, representing upfront capital commitment.

  • Property Eligibility Restrictions: Some types of properties may not qualify, such as those with title issues, environmental concerns, or in extreme disrepair beyond feasible rehab scope.

Careful due diligence, realistic budgeting, and maintaining liquidity reserves are critical to mitigating these risks. Investors should also work closely with Flip Funding Pro’s underwriting team to understand the specific loan terms and conditions before committing.
