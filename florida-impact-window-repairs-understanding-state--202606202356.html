Florida Impact Window Warranty: What Homeowners Should Know

Florida impact windows are often installed to protect property against hurricane-force winds and flying debris, so warranties are vital to ensure long-term reliability. Here’s what Florida impact window warranties typically cover:

  • Manufacturer’s Warranty: Covers glass breakage, frame defects, and hardware failures generally for 10-20 years.
  • Installation Warranty: Provided by the contractor, this covers workmanship issues such as leaks, improper sealing, or frame alignment and usually lasts 1-5 years depending on the company.
  • Florida Building Code Compliance: Warranties implicitly guarantee compliance with Florida Building Codes, which require impact windows to withstand certain wind loads and pressures.
