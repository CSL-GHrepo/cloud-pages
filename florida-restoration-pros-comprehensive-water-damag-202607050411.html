Preparing for Water Damage in Florida: Prevention and Response Tips

While restoration companies can salvage much of your property, prevention is always better:

  1. Inspect and maintain plumbing and roofing annually
  2. Ensure proper drainage around your property
  3. Install sump pumps and backflow valves in flood-prone areas
  4. Keep an emergency plan and restoration contact readily accessible

If water damage occurs:

  1. Shut off electricity and water sources if safe
  2. Contact Florida Restoration Pros immediately for expert emergency water removal
  3. Document the damage for insurance claims
  4. Avoid using HVAC systems that could spread contaminants until inspected

Swift action with a trusted restoration partner limits the impact on your home or business and your peace of mind.
