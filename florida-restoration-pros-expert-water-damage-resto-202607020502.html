Understanding Water Damage in Florida: Why Timely Restoration Matters

Florida’s subtropical climate and frequent storms expose residents to unique water damage risks year-round.

  • Frequent flooding and hurricanes: According to the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Florida averages 7 tropical storms or hurricanes per year, many bringing heavy rains that cause flooding and structural damage.
  • High humidity: The state’s average relative humidity hovers around 74%, accelerating mold growth if water intrusion isn’t promptly addressed.
  • Infrastructure vulnerability: Older homes and coastal properties often face heightened risk of water penetration during storms and high tides.
“According to the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), even 24-48 hours of moisture exposure can lead to harmful mold growth, compromising indoor air quality and health.”

Delaying restoration allows water to soak deeper into building materials, raising costs and hazards exponentially. That is why immediate professional water damage restoration is critical, especially in Florida.
