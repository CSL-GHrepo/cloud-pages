Essential Tips for Water Damage Prevention & Response in Florida

While Florida Restoration Pros excels in remediation, prevention is always better. Here are expert recommendations to reduce water damage risk:

  • Install sump pumps with battery backup for flood-prone areas.
  • Maintain gutters and downspouts to ensure proper drainage away from foundations.
  • Seal cracks in walls and foundations regularly.
  • Use water alarms in basements and crawl spaces.
  • Prepare an emergency plan and contact list, including a trusted restoration company like Florida Restoration Pros.
