Step-by-Step Process: How The Florida Restoration Pros Handle Water Damage

1. Emergency Water Extraction and Safety

The first priority is removing water to prevent structural collapse or mold growth. The Florida Restoration Pros employ industrial-grade pumps and vacuums for efficient water extraction, typically within hours of arrival. Quick action limits water absorption and secondary damage.

2. Thorough Moisture Damage Restoration

After removing water, drying begins immediately. Using moisture meters and thermal imaging, the team identifies hidden moisture pockets. Structural drying experts deploy high-velocity air movers and dehumidifiers to target even the most inaccessible areas.

Because Florida’s humidity fosters mold, remediation is essential. The Florida Restoration Pros conduct mold inspections and remove contaminated materials safely. They use EPA-registered antimicrobial agents and HEPA air filtration to eliminate spores and prevent recurrence.
