Why Florida is Particularly Vulnerable to Water Damage

Florida’s geographic and climatic conditions make it highly susceptible to water damage incidents:

  • Frequent storms and hurricanes: According to the National Hurricane Center, Florida records an average of one major hurricane every three years, causing widespread storm damage restoration needs.
  • High humidity levels: The state maintains an average relative humidity of 74%, which accelerates mold growth following water intrusions.
  • Flood-prone areas: Many communities in Florida are located near coasts and rivers, increasing the risk of flooding and requiring flood damage repair.

"According to FEMA, nearly 25% of all flood claims come from properties outside designated high-risk flood zones, highlighting the need for expert water damage cleanup experts regardless of location."

Actionable Steps When Facing Water Damage in Florida

If you’re experiencing water damage, follow these steps before The Florida Restoration Pros arrive:

  1. Ensure Safety: Turn off electricity if it is safe to do so and avoid contact with contaminated water.
  2. Stop the Source: Shut off the main water line or fix the leak if accessible.
  3. Document Damage: Take photos and videos for insurance purposes.
  4. Contact the Professionals: Call The Florida Restoration Pros immediately for emergency water extraction and mitigation services.
  5. Communicate with Your Insurance: Notify your provider and use assistance services from The Florida Restoration Pros to streamline claims.
