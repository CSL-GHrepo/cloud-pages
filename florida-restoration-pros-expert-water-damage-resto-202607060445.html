Essential Tips for Homeowners and Business Owners Facing Water Damage in Florida

  1. Contact a certified water damage restoration professional immediately.
  2. Do not attempt to dry carpet or drywall yourself—improper drying leads to mold.
  3. Document the damage with photographs before beginning cleanup.
  4. Keep detailed records of all communication with your insurance company.
  5. Prioritize mold remediation services after water extraction to prevent health risks.

Following these tips can reduce costs and improve the effectiveness of restoration.
