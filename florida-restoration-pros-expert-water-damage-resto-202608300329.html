Infrared Thermal Imaging for Moisture Detection

One of the biggest challenges in water damage restoration is detecting hidden moisture that is invisible to the naked eye. Florida Restoration Pros utilizes advanced infrared thermal imaging cameras to pinpoint areas where moisture has saturated building materials behind walls, under floorboards, or within ceilings. The technology facilitates early identification of potential mold growth sites and structural compromise by revealing cold spots caused by water presence.

For example, during a condominium restoration project in Fort Lauderdale, infrared scanning revealed trapped moisture behind drywall that was missed during the initial visual inspection. By addressing these hidden wet spots early, the company prevented mold infestation and extensive secondary damage, saving the client from costly repairs down the line.
