Essential Garage Door Components and Their Maintenance

Understanding the parts of your garage door can help you identify issues early. The springs, for example, bear the weight of the door and are subject to wear. Regular inspection, especially for signs of rust or fraying, can prevent sudden breakage. In Florida, humidity can accelerate corrosion, so applying protective coatings is advisable.

The tracks guide the door along its path and must stay properly aligned. Debris, rust, or warping can cause the door to stick or come off track. Cleaning and lubricating tracks regularly keeps them running smoothly. Our team often recommends using silicone-based lubricants for optimal results.
