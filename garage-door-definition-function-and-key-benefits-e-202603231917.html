Key Terms Every Parkland Homeowner Should Know

Understanding industry terminology helps you make informed decisions about your garage door. One essential term is the 'torsion spring,' which counterbalances the door's weight and makes lifting easier. When a torsion spring breaks, it needs immediate professional replacement to ensure safety.

Another important term is 'track alignment,' referring to the placement of the metal tracks that guide the door. Proper alignment prevents uneven wear and ensures smooth operation. Misaligned tracks are common causes of garage door jams or noisy operation.

Lastly, 'safety sensors' are devices that detect obstacles in the path of the door, preventing accidental injury or damage. These sensors are crucial for modern garage doors and must be regularly tested and maintained. Knowing these terms helps you understand service needs and communicate effectively with technicians.
