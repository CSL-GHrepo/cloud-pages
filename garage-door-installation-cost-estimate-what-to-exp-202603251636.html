Maintaining Your Garage Door in Parkland

Proper maintenance extends the life of your garage door and keeps it functioning safely. In Parkland, where weather conditions can cause wear and tear, regular upkeep is especially important.

Lubricating moving parts like hinges, rollers, and springs every six months prevents friction and reduces noise. Use a silicone-based lubricant for best results.

Checking the balance of your garage door ensures that it opens and closes smoothly. An unbalanced door can strain the opener and lead to early failure.

Cleaning tracks and inspecting for rust or debris helps maintain proper alignment. Use a damp cloth and avoid harsh chemicals that could damage the components.

Testing safety features, such as sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms, once a month can prevent accidents. It’s a quick process and highly recommended for safety compliance.
