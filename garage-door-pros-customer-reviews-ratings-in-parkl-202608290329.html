Tips from Parkland Garage Door Pros for Extending the Life of Your Garage Door

Experts agree that proper care extends the functional lifespan of your garage door and minimizes costly repairs—an essential aspect for Parkland homeowners striving to protect their investments.

  • Regular Cleaning: Washing your garage door quarterly to remove salt residue, dirt, and debris prevents material degradation.
  • Seasonal Inspections: Schedule professional check-ups, especially before hurricane season, to catch early signs of wear or damage.
  • Lubrication Routine: Apply lubricant specifically formulated for garage door components every 6 months to maintain smooth operation.
  • Balance Testing: Test door balance periodically to avoid stress on the opener and mechanical parts.
  • Prompt Repairs: Address small issues like frayed cables or worn rollers immediately to prevent more extensive damage.

The Garage Door Pros often provide maintenance packages that incorporate these tips and have educated many Parkland customers on simple upkeep habits that prevent inconvenient breakdowns.
