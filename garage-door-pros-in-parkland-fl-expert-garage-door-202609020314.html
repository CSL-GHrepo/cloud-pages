Garage Door Safety Tips Residents Should Follow

Proper user behavior complements professional services to minimize accidents and prolong garage door lifespan. Garage Door Pros educates its customers on essential safety practices:

  • Regularly test safety sensors: These should automatically reverse the door if an obstacle is detected; manual testing ensures functionality.
  • Keep clear of moving doors: Children and pets should never be near the door during operation.
  • Do not attempt DIY repairs: Springs and cables store high tension and require specialized tools and knowledge to handle safely.
  • Lubricate moving parts annually: This prevents undue friction and premature failure but should be done with appropriate products.
  • Inspect weather seals seasonally: Replace any cracked or torn seals to maintain insulation and prevent water ingress.

Following these guidelines helps avoid injury, reduces repair costs, and keeps doors functioning reliably.
