Maintenance Tips Homeowners Should Know Between Professional Visits

While The Garage Door Pros recommend professional maintenance both spring and fall, simple homeowner actions can help prevent common issues and extend door lifespan between visits.

  • Regular Visual Inspections: Look for unusual wear, rust, dents, or damage to panels and tracks.
  • Keep Tracks Clear: Remove any debris, dirt, or leaves to ensure smooth roller movement.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Safety: Place a small object under the door and close it; ensure the door reverses before hitting the object.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Vinegar or silicone-based lubricants applied to rollers and hinges every 6 months keep operations smooth.
  • Secure Weather Seals: Check bottom seals for cracking or gaps, which can allow water intrusion or pests.

These preventive steps help maintain safety and functionality until the next professional checkup.

Understanding Common Garage Door Materials: Pros and Cons

Choosing the right material for your garage door depends on many factors including durability, maintenance, insulation, and cost. The Garage Door Pros provide in-depth guidance on the following options:

Steel

  • Pros: Strong, affordable, low maintenance, and available with insulation.
  • Cons: Susceptible to dents and rust if not properly coated.

Aluminum

  • Pros: Lightweight, corrosion-resistant, and great for modern designs, often used with glass inserts.
  • Cons: Less durable against impacts, can bend more easily than steel.

Wood

  • Pros: Beautiful natural look, can be customized extensively.
  • Cons: Requires regular maintenance, prone to warping and rot in humid climates.

Fiberglass

  • Pros: Lightweight, resistant to denting and warping, can mimic wood grain finishes.
  • Cons: Can become brittle in extreme temperatures, less insulating.

The Garage Door Pros help Parkland customers weigh these factors based on intended use, aesthetic preferences, and climate durability needs.
