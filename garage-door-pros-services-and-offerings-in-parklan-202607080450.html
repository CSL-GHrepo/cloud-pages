Safety Tips and Best Practices for Garage Door Owners in Parkland, FL

The Garage Door Pros are not only installers and repair specialists but also educators committed to helping the community use their garage doors safely and effectively. Here are several expert recommendations they provide to Parkland residents:

  • Regularly Test Safety Sensors: Make sure auto-reverse sensors are clean and properly aligned. These prevent the door from closing on people, pets, or objects.
  • Never Attempt DIY Spring Repairs: Garage door springs are under high tension and can cause serious injuries if mishandled. Always rely on professionals.
  • Keep the Tracks Clear: Debris, dirt, or leaves can clog the tracks causing operational issues or damage.
  • Maintain Proper Balance: An unbalanced door strains the opener and hardware. Have professionals check the balance during routine maintenance.
  • Child Safety: Teach children that garage doors are not play areas to prevent accidents.
