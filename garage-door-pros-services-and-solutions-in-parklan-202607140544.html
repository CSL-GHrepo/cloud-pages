Essential Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Parkland Homeowners

Maintaining a garage door in a humid, coastal environment like Parkland requires special care to prevent corrosion and mechanical wear. The Garage Door Pros recommend these essential tips:

  1. Regular Lubrication: Use lithium-based lubricants on rollers, hinges, and springs every six months to reduce friction and noise.
  2. Check Weather Stripping: Replace worn seals to protect against moisture and pests.
  3. Test Auto-Reverse Sensors: Ensure safety sensors are clean and functional.
  4. Tighten Hardware: Regularly inspect and tighten bolts and screws that can loosen over time.
  5. Balance Testing: A well-balanced door prevents motor strain—perform a simple manual lift test or call a professional.

The Garage Door Pros offer scheduled maintenance programs that include comprehensive inspections and tune-ups, minimizing the risk of unexpected breakdowns and costly fixes.
