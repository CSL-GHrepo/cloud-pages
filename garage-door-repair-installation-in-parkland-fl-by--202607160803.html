Maintenance Tips and Best Practices from The Garage Door Pros

While professional servicing is essential, homeowners in Parkland can take proactive steps between visits to maintain their garage doors and prevent costly repairs. The Garage Door Pros recommend the following routine maintenance tips:

Monthly Visual Inspection

  • Check for visible signs of wear or damage to springs, cables, rollers, and tracks.
  • Verify that weather seals are intact and flexible without cracks.
  • Ensure that the door opens and closes smoothly without unusual noises or jerking motions.

Lubrication of Moving Parts

Proper lubrication reduces friction and wear on components, extending the life of your garage door system.

  • Use manufacturer-recommended garage door lubricants on springs, hinges, rollers, and tracks.
  • Avoid over-lubrication, which can attract dirt and grime causing buildup.

Test Safety Features Regularly

  • Test the auto-reverse mechanism by placing objects under the door during closing to ensure it stops and reverses appropriately.
  • Inspect photo-eye sensors for alignment and cleanliness, as misaligned or obstructed sensors can prevent door operation.

Seasonal Adjustments and Weather Preparation

Given Parkland's climate variability, preparing your garage door system ahead of summer storms and heavy rains is crucial.

  • Trim vegetation near the door and track area to avoid interference during high winds.
  • Inspect drainage around the garage entrance to prevent flooding and water damage.
  • Schedule a professional tune-up before hurricane season to reinforce door components and ensure secure installation.
