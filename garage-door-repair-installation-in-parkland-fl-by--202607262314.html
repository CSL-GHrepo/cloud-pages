Understanding Garage Door Safety Features and Compliance in Parkland

Safety is paramount in garage door design and installation. The Garage Door Pros work closely with Parkland building officials and adhere strictly to safety regulations to protect families and businesses alike.

Key Garage Door Safety Components

  • Auto-Reverse Mechanism: Sensors detect obstructions and automatically reverse the closing door to avoid injury or property damage.
  • Photoelectric Safety Sensors: Installed along the bottom or sides of doors, these infrared beams prevent the door from closing when an object or person interrupts the signal.
  • Emergency Release Cord: Allows manual disengagement of the opener for quick door operation during power outages or emergencies.
  • Spring Containment Systems: Modern torsion spring setups and containment brackets minimize the risks associated with spring failure or breakage.

By ensuring that all garage door systems comply with ASTM F2200 standards and Florida building codes, The Garage Door Pros reduce hazards and provide homeowners with confidence in their garage’s safety.
