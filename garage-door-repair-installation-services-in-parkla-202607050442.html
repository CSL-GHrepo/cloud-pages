Maintenance Tips to Avoid Costly Garage Door Repairs in Parkland, FL

According to the International Door Association, regular maintenance can extend your garage door’s lifespan by up to 50%. Here are key maintenance tips recommended by The Garage Door Pros:

  • Lubricate moving parts like rollers, springs, and hinges every six months.
  • Perform monthly visual inspections to spot wear or damage early.
  • Check the balance of your door to prevent motor strain—if it doesn’t stay mid-open, call a professional.
  • Test safety sensors regularly to ensure they stop the door’s closing when objects are detected.
  • Schedule annual professional maintenance with The Pros to address hidden issues and keep your door in prime condition.
