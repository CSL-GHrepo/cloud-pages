Maintaining Your Garage Door in Miramar's Climate

Proper maintenance is key to extending the life of your garage door. Given Miramar’s humid and salty air, regular inspections are vital. We recommend scheduling a professional checkup annually, especially before hurricane season.

Lubricating moving parts like rollers, hinges, and springs prevents rust and ensures smooth operation. Use high-quality lubricants designed for garage doors for optimal results.

Tighten loose hardware—bolts and brackets—since vibrations can loosen fittings over time. This simple step can prevent costly repairs later.

Inspect cables and rollers for signs of wear or fraying. Addressing issues early saves money and maintains safety.

Clear debris and obstructions from tracks and the area around the door. This promotes efficient movement and reduces strain on the system.
