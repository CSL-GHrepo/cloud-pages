Maintaining Your Garage Door: Affordable Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Coral Springs Residents

Routine maintenance can prevent unexpected failures and extend the life of your garage door system. Consider these actionable tips:

  1. Regular Inspection: Check springs, cables, rollers, and tracks for wear or damage at least twice yearly.
  2. Lubrication: Use silicon-based lubricants on moving parts every 3-6 months to reduce friction and prevent squeaking.
  3. Opener Testing: Test automatic sensors monthly to ensure safe operation;
  4. Professional Tune-Ups: Schedule affordable garage door maintenance with licensed technicians annually.
