How to Keep Your Garage Door in Top Shape in Parkland

Proper maintenance begins with regular inspections. Check for loose hardware, frayed cables, and unusual noises. Addressing small issues early prevents costly repairs later.

Lubricate moving parts like rollers, hinges, and tracks every six months. Use a silicone-based lubricant to reduce friction and noise, especially important in humid climates like Parkland.

Test safety features, including auto-reverse sensors, once a month. Properly functioning sensors prevent accidents and are required by safety standards.

Keep the area around your garage door clear of debris, snow, and ice. Obstructions can cause misalignment or damage to the tracks and hardware.
