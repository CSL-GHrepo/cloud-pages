Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Parkland

Keeping your garage door in top condition requires regular maintenance. In Parkland's humid climate, corrosion and wear happen faster, so routine checks are essential. Start by inspecting the door's hardware—springs, cables, rollers, and tracks—every few months. — The Garage Door Pros serves the greater Parkland area.

Lubrication is also key. Use a silicone-based lubricant on moving parts to reduce friction and noise. Be cautious not to over-lubricate, as excess can attract dirt and grime.

Test the safety features regularly. Place an object under the door and see if it reverses when it contacts the obstacle. This simple test ensures your sensors are functioning correctly.

Check the balance of your garage door. Disconnect the opener and lift the door manually. It should stay in place smoothly. If it feels heavy or is difficult to move, professional adjustment is necessary.

Remember, some maintenance tasks are best handled by local experts. Scheduling annual tune-ups helps prevent unexpected breakdowns and prolongs the life of your garage door.
