Legal and Regulatory Considerations for Hard Money Lending in Dresslerville, NV

Hard money lending operates under state and federal laws that protect both borrowers and lenders. Dresslerville investors should be mindful of the following regulatory nuances to ensure compliance and avoid pitfalls.

State Licensing Requirements

In Nevada, hard money lenders must often register as mortgage lenders or brokers, depending on the loan type and volume. Reputable lenders adhere strictly to these rules, which include:

  • Acquiring proper state licensing through the Nevada Division of Mortgage Lending.
  • Following disclosure laws that outline fees, terms, and borrower rights.
  • Complying with usury laws setting maximum permissible interest rates to prevent predatory lending.

Lenders must provide clear, written disclosure of all loan costs prior to funding. This transparency helps borrowers fully understand the financial obligations, including:
