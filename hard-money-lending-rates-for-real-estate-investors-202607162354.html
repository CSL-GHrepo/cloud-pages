Key Industry Terms in Hard Money Lending

Loan-to-Value (LTV): The ratio of the loan amount to the property's appraised value. It impacts how much you can borrow and your risk profile. — Flip Funding Pro serves the greater Parkland area.

Points: Fees charged by lenders, usually a percentage of the loan amount, paid upfront at closing. They compensate for higher risk or quick processing.

Bridge Loan: A short-term loan used to 'bridge' the gap until longer-term financing or sale occurs. Common in fix and flip projects.

Draw Schedule: A plan that outlines when and how funds are disbursed during a project, based on completed milestones.

Hard Money Lenders: Private lenders who provide short-term, asset-backed loans with quicker approval times, often used for real estate investments.
