How Property Valuation Influences Hard Money Loan Approvals and Rates in Artesia

Property valuation is a cornerstone of the underwriting process for hard money lenders. Since these loans are asset-based rather than credit-based, the property's current and after-repair value (ARV) dictate loan terms and funding limits.

Current Market Value vs. After Repair Value (ARV)

Current Market Value: This represents what the property would likely sell for in its present condition. Lenders use this as a baseline for risk assessment, especially if the loan is for purchase.

After Repair Value (ARV): For rehab projects, lenders evaluate the projected value after completion of renovations. This figure is essential in calculating the loan amount, ensuring that the combined loan and rehab costs do not exceed a safe percentage of ARV (typically 70%-75% LTV).

Impact on Loan Terms

  • Higher ARV, Better Loan Terms: If the projected ARV supports a strong loan-to-cost ratio, lenders may offer lower interest rates or higher loan amounts.
  • Low or Uncertain Valuations: Properties in less stable neighborhoods or those with substantial repair unknowns may face stricter terms or higher rates due to increased risk.

Valuation Examples in Artesia

For example, a vacant lot in Artesia valued at $150,000 might qualify for 65% LTV funding, thus offering a loan amount up to $97,500. Conversely, a rehab candidate townhouse with a current value of $180,000 and projected ARV of $250,000 could secure up to 70% of ARV, or $175,000, enabling both acquisition and rehab costs to be covered.
