Understanding the Costs Beyond Interest: Fees and Obligations With Caliente Hard Money Loans

Real estate investors must account for all costs attached to hard money loans to correctly calculate project feasibility and expected returns. In Caliente, the following fees and obligations are commonly encountered:

Origination fees typically range between 2% to 4% of the loan amount and cover loan processing expenses. Some lenders may also charge document preparation or underwriting fees.

Prepayment Penalties

Although hard money loans are short-term, some agreements impose prepayment penalties if loans are paid off earlier than the agreed timeline. Understanding these terms avoids unexpected costs during refinancing or early sale scenarios.

Additional Miscellaneous Costs

  • Inspection fees: Lenders may require environmental or structural inspections.
  • Title insurance: Protects lender’s interest and often mandated.
  • Escrow fees: Charged when a third-party manages loan funds disbursement.

Investors should request full loan disclosures upfront and factor all these costs into their financial modeling.
