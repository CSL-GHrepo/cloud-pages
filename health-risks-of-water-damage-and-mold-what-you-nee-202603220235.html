Key Industry Terms Explained

Water Intrusion: Water entering structures through leaks, cracks, or openings, risking damage to walls, floors, and personal belongings.

Mold Remediation: The process of removing mold growth caused by excess moisture, vital for health and structural integrity.

Flood Cut: A method where affected drywall is removed above the water line to dry out walls and prevent mold spread.

Dehumidification: Removing excess moisture from the air, essential for thorough drying and mold prevention after water damage.

Water Extraction: The process of removing standing water using specialized equipment, the first critical step in restoration.
