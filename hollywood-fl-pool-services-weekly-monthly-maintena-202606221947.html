How Do Monthly Pool Care Packages Differ?

Monthly pool care packages are a cost-effective option generally preferred by pool owners who use their pools less frequently or have automated systems that reduce manual upkeep.

  • Includes a comprehensive cleaning and chemical balance check once per month.
  • May include basic pool equipment inspection but typically excludes more detailed service like weekly filter clearing.
  • Suited for commercial pool services where routine daily cleaning is managed onsite, and monthly specialist visits handle overall maintenance.
  • Often bundled with seasonal services such as winterizing or opening pools in Hollywood’s climate.

Residential customers choosing monthly plans must be mindful that pools may require occasional manual intervention between visits, particularly in the summer months when algae growth and debris accumulation can accelerate.
