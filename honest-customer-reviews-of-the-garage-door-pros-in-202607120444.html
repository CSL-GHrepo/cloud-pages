Common Garage Door Problems and How The Garage Door Pros Address Them

Understanding typical garage door issues can help customers identify when professional intervention is necessary. The Garage Door Pros handle a broad range of common malfunctions with expert diagnosis and repair.

Spring Breakage

Garage door springs endure significant tension and can snap unexpectedly, rendering the door inoperable. Technicians replace springs safely using specialized tools and comply with safety protocols to minimize risk.

Opener Failures

Problems with garage door openers, including remote control glitches, motor failures, or circuit board issues, are swiftly troubleshooted. The Garage Door Pros provide repairs or recommend modern replacements tailored to customer preferences.

Sensor Malfunctions

Misaligned or damaged sensors can cause the door to stop closing or reverse improperly. The company’s team calibrates and replaces sensors to restore proper safety function.

Track and Roller Issues

Worn or misaligned tracks prevent smooth door motion and can cause loud noises or jamming. Technicians straighten tracks, replace rollers, and lubricate components during service calls.
