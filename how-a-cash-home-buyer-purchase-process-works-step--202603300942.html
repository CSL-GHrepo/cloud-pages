Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the cash home sale process usually take?

The cash home sale process typically takes between 7 to 14 days from initial contact to closing, significantly faster than traditional sales which can take 30-60 days or longer due to financing and inspections. Because cash buyers bypass mortgage approvals, the timeline is streamlined.

Is the cash offer lower than market value?

Yes, cash offers are generally below market value. This is because cash property buyers bear the risk of repairs and market fluctuations, and they require a margin for profit. However, the tradeoff is faster closing and less hassle for the seller.

Can I sell my house “as-is” to a cash buyer?

Absolutely. One of the main advantages of selling to cash home buyers is the ability to sell your property “as-is,” without the need for costly repairs, renovations, or staging, which is ideal for homeowners looking to avoid additional expenses.

Are there fees involved when selling to a cash home buyer?

Most reputable cash home buyers do not charge upfront fees or commissions, unlike traditional realtor sales which typically involve at least 5-6% commission. Always read contracts carefully to confirm that no hidden fees apply.
