Technical Glossary for Weston Homeowners

Spring Tension: The force exerted by garage door springs to counterbalance the door’s weight, enabling smooth opening and closing.

Track Alignment: The proper positioning of the rails guiding the garage door, crucial for safe and smooth operation.

Photo-Electric Sensors: Safety devices that detect obstructions, preventing the door from closing on objects or people. Proper alignment is essential for operation.

Torque: The rotational force applied by garage door motors, affecting the opener’s efficiency and power.

Cable Drum: A component attached to the motor that winds the cables, playing a key role in balancing and lifting the door.
