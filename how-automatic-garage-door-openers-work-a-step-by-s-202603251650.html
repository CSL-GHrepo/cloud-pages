Key Terms to Know for Garage Door Maintenance in Parkland

Spring Torsion: The mechanism that supports the weight of the door, usually located above the opening. Springs can be extension or torsion types.

Track Alignment: Proper positioning of the metal tracks that guide the door’s movement. Misalignment can cause uneven operation or jams.

Rollers: Small wheels that run inside the tracks, facilitating smooth door movement. Worn rollers need replacement to reduce noise and strain.

Safety Sensors: Devices that detect obstructions before the door closes, preventing accidents. They must be properly aligned and functional.

Wind-Resistant Doors: Specially reinforced doors designed to withstand Florida’s storm winds, reducing damage and ensuring security.
