Lease Accounting for Copiers: What Businesses Should Know

The accounting treatment of leases changed drastically under the new ASC 842 and IFRS 16 lease standards. Understanding how these rules impact copier leases is crucial:

  • Operating vs Finance Lease Classification: Most copier leases qualify as operating leases, meaning companies recognize a right-of-use asset and a lease liability but can still expense payments similarly to rent.
  • Balance Sheet Reporting: Unlike older standards, operating leases now show on the balance sheet but typically with less impact than capitalized purchases.
  • Tax vs Accounting Treatment: Tax deductions generally follow the actual payment schedule, not the accounting expense recognition. This provides predictable tax deductibility for leased equipment.

Businesses should work closely with their accountants to optimize both tax reporting and lease accounting compliance.
