Understanding Commercial Copier Leasing: The Basics

At its core, commercial copier leasing in Miami means renting office copiers under a formal lease agreement rather than outright purchasing them. This distinction is crucial, especially for small to mid-sized businesses that prefer preserving working capital or lack immediate funds for large equipment investments. The lease terms typically range from two to five years and include options for service, maintenance, and supplies management.
