Types of Commercial Copier Lease Agreements Available in Miami

Miami commercial copier providers typically offer varied leasing structures to fit business needs. The common types include:

  1. Fair Market Value (FMV) Lease: Most popular in Miami; allows businesses to lease the equipment at lower monthly rates and decide at lease-end whether to return, upgrade, or purchase the copier at its fair market value.
  2. Capital Lease (or Finance Lease): Similar to a purchase agreement; businesses treat the copier as an owned asset and often own it at lease-end.
  3. Single Payment Lease: Businesses pay the full lease term amount upfront, which can result in cost savings and tax advantages.

Choosing the right agreement depends on your company’s cash flow, balance sheet considerations, and long-term business goals.
