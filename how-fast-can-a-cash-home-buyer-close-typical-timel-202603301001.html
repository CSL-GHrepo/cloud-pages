Typical Closing Periods: What to Expect

The timeline of a cash home sale varies but broadly falls within these brackets:

  1. Initial Contact and Property Evaluation (1-3 days): The cash buyer evaluates your property, often with a quick walkthrough or virtual tour.
  2. Offer Presentation and Acceptance (1-2 days): You receive a quick cash offer, usually within 24-48 hours of the evaluation.
  3. Contract Signing and Title Search (2-5 days): Once you accept, the paperwork is signed, and a title search is done to ensure clear ownership.
  4. Closing and Funds Transfer (1-4 days): Upon clearing title, closing is scheduled, and funds are transferred to your account quickly.

Some cash home buyers streamline these steps even further, closing in as little as 7 days. Others may take up to 2 weeks depending on the seller’s and buyer’s availability, and any outstanding issues discovered during the title search. Still, this is remarkably faster than traditional sales, which frequently extend beyond 30 days.

A study by ATTOM Data Solutions revealed that cash sales in the U.S. typically close 20 to 30 days faster than financed properties.

Although cash_home_buyer transactions are fast by design, certain factors can affect the speed of closing:
