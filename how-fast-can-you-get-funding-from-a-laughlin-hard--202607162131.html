Hard money loans in Laughlin generally feature interest rates ranging from 8% to 15%, depending on several factors:

  • Borrower Experience and Creditworthiness: Experienced investors with proven track records often receive more favorable rates.

Common Fees and Points

Aside from interest, borrowers should anticipate additional fees often referred to as "points":

  • Origination Fee: Typically 1-3% of the loan amount, covering lender administrative costs.
  • Underwriting and Processing Fees: Flat fees or small percentages for paperwork review and risk analysis.
  • Prepayment Penalties: Some lenders charge fees if the loan is paid off earlier than agreed.
  • Title and Closing Costs: Standard costs related to transferring ownership and recording the loan.

It’s vital for investors to review all terms carefully and factor these costs into their project budgets.

Loan Terms and Repayment Structures in Laughlin Hard Money Lending

Hard money loans are usually designed as short-term loans, often lasting from 6 to 24 months. This timeframe aligns with typical renovate-and-sell or refinance strategies common among real estate investors in Laughlin.

Interest-Only Payments vs. Balloon Payments

Many Laughlin hard money loans offer interest-only payment options during the term of the loan, which helps keep monthly outflows manageable. At the end of the loan term, borrowers often have a balloon payment representing the full principal, due when they sell, refinance, or otherwise pay off the loan.

  • Interest-Only Payments: Borrowers pay only the interest accrued monthly.
  • Balloon Payment: The entire principal is due at the loan’s maturity.

Some lenders offer flexibility or negotiated terms for borrowers expected to refinance or sell before the balloon comes due.

Loan Rollovers and Extensions

For projects requiring more time, some Laughlin hard money lenders allow loan extensions or rollovers, although these often come with additional fees or slightly higher interest rates. Extensions can provide peace of mind but may increase project costs.
