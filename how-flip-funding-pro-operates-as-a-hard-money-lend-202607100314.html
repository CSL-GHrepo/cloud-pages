  • Qualification Criteria: Traditional banks rely heavily on credit scores, income verification, and debt-to-income ratios, which can be restrictive for investors with fluctuating incomes or limited documentation. Flip Funding Pro’s hard money loans focus on the property’s value, allowing more flexible underwriting.
