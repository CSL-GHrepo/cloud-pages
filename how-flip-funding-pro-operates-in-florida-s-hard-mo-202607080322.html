Legal and Regulatory Considerations for Hard Money Loans in Florida

Flip Funding Pro operates under Florida’s lending laws, including adherence to the Florida Finance Lenders Law, which governs interest rates, licensing requirements, and disclosure obligations for non-bank lenders.

Although many hard money loans are exempt from some conventional federal consumer loan protections, Flip Funding Pro ensures clear disclosure of terms, fees, and costs to borrowers pursuant to TILA requirements when applicable.

Florida’s usury laws limit the maximum interest rates that lenders can legally charge. Flip Funding Pro structures loan terms and rates to remain compliant, typically ranging from 8% to 12% interest, depending on project risk and loan size.
