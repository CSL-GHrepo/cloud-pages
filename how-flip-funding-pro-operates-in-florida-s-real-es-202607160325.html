What Are Hard Money Loans and Why Are They Vital in Florida?

Hard money loans are asset-based financing options primarily secured by real estate property. Unlike traditional bank loans that hinge heavily on borrower creditworthiness and income verification, hard money loans focus on the value of the collateral property. In Florida’s competitive and often fast-moving property markets—such as Miami, Tampa, and Orlando—this financing method allows investors to act quickly to secure and renovate properties.
