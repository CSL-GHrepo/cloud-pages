Understanding Loan-to-Value (LTV) and Its Impact on Your Flip Funding Pro Loan

Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio is a critical metric used by Flip Funding Pro and other hard money lenders to determine loan eligibility and terms. It compares the loan amount requested against the appraised value of the property, either as-is or after renovation (ARV).

Here’s why LTV matters to Florida real estate investors:

  • Risk management: Lower LTV ratios reduce lender risk, often translating to better interest rates and fees.
  • Funding capacity: Understanding your LTV limits helps plan the scope of renovation and purchase price to maximize leverage.
  • Exit strategy clarity: Accurate ARV assessments ensure sufficient equity upon sale or refinance for timely loan repayment.

Flip Funding Pro typically offers LTVs ranging from 65% to 75% depending on property type and borrower profile. For example, a residential fix-and-flip property appraised at $200,000 post-renovation could qualify for a loan amount between $130,000 and $150,000. This range helps investors maintain sufficient equity cushion while accessing adequate capital.

In Florida’s variable market, where coastal properties can appreciate less predictably than inland homes, leveraging Flip Funding Pro’s local appraisal expertise ensures LTV calculations reflect realistic market conditions.
