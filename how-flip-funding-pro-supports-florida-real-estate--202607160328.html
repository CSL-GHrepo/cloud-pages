What Are Hard Money Loans and Why Florida Investors Need Them

Hard money loans are short-term real estate loans secured by the value of the property itself rather than borrower creditworthiness. Unlike traditional loans that may take months to approve, hard money loans provide expedited funding often within days—a critical advantage in the Florida real estate market, where properties frequently move fast.
