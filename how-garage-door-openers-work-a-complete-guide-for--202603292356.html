Maintenance Tips to Extend Garage Door Lifespan

Preventative maintenance is key to avoiding costly repairs and extending your garage door’s lifespan. In Parkland, regular inspections and simple tasks can make a big difference, especially given the local humidity and storm exposure.

Lubricating moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and springs every six months keeps everything running smoothly. Use a silicone-based lubricant for best results. Avoid grease or oil, which can attract dust and debris.

Check the balance of your garage door by disconnecting the opener and manually opening it halfway. If it doesn’t stay in place or feels uneven, it’s time for professional adjustment or spring replacement. Our team can perform these adjustments safely.

Keep the tracks clean and free of debris. Use a damp cloth to wipe them and tighten any loose hardware. Routine inspections help catch problems early before they develop into costly repairs.
