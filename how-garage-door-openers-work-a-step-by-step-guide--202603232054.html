Key Industry Terms Every Homeowner Should Know

Spring system: These are heavy-duty tension springs that support the weight of the garage door. Worn or broken springs require immediate professional replacement.

Track alignment: The vertical and horizontal rails guiding the door. Properly aligned tracks are essential for smooth operation and safety.

Safety sensors: Devices that detect obstructions in the door's path and reverse its movement. Critical for preventing injuries and accidents.

Cables: Steel cables help lift and lower the door, transmitting force from the springs to the door. Frayed or broken cables are dangerous and need quick repair.

Insulation: Material inside the door that improves energy efficiency by reducing heat transfer. Better insulation can lower cooling costs in Florida homes.
