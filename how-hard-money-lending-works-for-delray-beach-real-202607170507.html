Florida-Specific Lending Laws

Florida regulates private lending under the Department of Financial Services and other regulatory bodies. Key compliance points include:

  • Lending Licenses: Hard money lenders must obtain appropriate state licenses to operate legally.
  • Disclosure Requirements: Clear disclosure of all loan terms, including APR, fees, and prepayment penalties, is mandatory to prevent consumer deception.

Investors borrowing hard money should ensure lenders comply fully and seek legal advice if uncertain.
