In Florida, private lenders may be required to obtain appropriate licenses if engaging in loan brokering or multiple transactions. Verifying a lender’s licensing status helps avoid conflicts and ensures adherence to state financial laws.

Hard money lenders must provide clear disclosures of terms, fees, and penalties in loan agreements. Thoroughly reviewing contracts—ideally with legal counsel—prevents misunderstandings and enforces borrower protections.
