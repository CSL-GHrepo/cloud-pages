Frequently Used Terms and Concepts Related to Boynton Beach Hard Money Loans

  • After Repair Value (ARV): The projected market value of a property after renovations are complete.
  • Points: Upfront fees expressed as a percentage of the loan amount. Usually 2-5% in hard money loans.
  • Exit Strategy: The method an investor uses to repay the loan—typically selling the property or refinancing.

Regulatory and Legal Considerations for Hard Money Loans in Boynton Beach

Investors should be aware of specific legal frameworks and regulatory factors affecting hard money lending in Boynton Beach and Florida at large:

  • Florida Usury Laws: Florida limits interest rates on private loans to protect borrowers, but hard money loans often qualify for exemptions due to their risk profile and private nature. It’s essential to verify that your lender complies with these statutes.
  • Disclosure Requirements: Hard money lenders must disclose all fees, rates, and loan terms transparently under Florida lending regulations. Look for clear loan documents to avoid hidden costs.
  • Licensing: Some hard money lenders operate under mortgage broker or lender licenses. Verify the lender’s status through the Florida Office of Financial Regulation to ensure credibility.
  • Title and lien priority: Ensure your lender records the mortgage and maintains first lien position to avoid legal disputes.

Consulting a qualified real estate attorney familiar with Florida law can provide invaluable safeguards during negotiation and closing.

Typical Hard Money Loan Costs and Fees Explained in Detail

Aside from interest rates, hard money loans carry several additional costs investors should anticipate:

  • Points: Upfront fees usually ranging from 2-5% of the loan principal. For example, a $200,000 loan with 4 points would require $8,000 at closing.
  • Origination Fees: Some lenders charge a flat fee or percentage for processing the loan application.
  • Underwriting and Processing Fees: Covers the cost of property appraisal, title search, and administrative tasks.
  • Prepayment Penalties: Certain loans impose fees if the borrower pays off the loan ahead of schedule, though many hard money loans are flexible in this regard.
  • Inspection Fees: Some lenders send third-party inspectors to verify project progress, charging the borrower.

Understanding the full cost picture helps investors calculate true project profitability and negotiate terms effectively.
