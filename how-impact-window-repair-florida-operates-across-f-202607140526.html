State-of-the-Art Glass Technology

Modern impact windows use laminated glass comprised of two panes bonded together with an interlayer, typically polyvinyl butyral (PVB), which holds the glass intact even when shattered. Impact Window Repair of Florida exclusively installs glass that meets or exceeds Miami-Dade County’s Large Missile Impact Standard (ASTM E1886 and ASTM E1996), providing top-level protection against flying debris.

  • Self-Healing Sealants: New elastomeric sealants can recover from minor cracks autonomously, reducing maintenance frequency and improving water tightness.
  • Low-E Coatings: Low emissivity coatings enable better thermal regulation, a crucial factor in Florida’s hot climate that helps reduce cooling costs.
  • UV-Resistant Laminates: These protect the interlayer and glass from discoloration or weakening due to the state’s intense sun exposure, extending product longevity.

Advanced Frame Materials and Reinforcement

The frames play a critical role in sustaining overall window integrity during high-impact events. Impact Window Repair of Florida evaluates frame materials and upgrades damaged components as part of their comprehensive repair service:

  • Aluminum Frames With Thermal Break: Combining strength and energy efficiency, the thermal break prevents heat transfer, improving overall window performance.
  • Composite and PVC Frames: These materials resist corrosion from salt air exposure better than traditional metal frames, particularly important for coastal homes.
  • Reinforced Anchors and Fasteners: Proper anchoring into building structures is essential for impact resistance. The company uses upgraded anchoring systems that distribute loads more effectively during hurricanes.
