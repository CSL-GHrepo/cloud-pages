Understanding the Importance of Impact Windows in Florida’s Hurricane Zones

Florida experiences over 1,200 hours of hurricane-related winds annually, according to the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA). This constant risk necessitates windows that provide more than just clear visibility—they must be storm-proof and resilient to flying debris and extreme pressure changes.

Impact windows use specially manufactured impact-resistant glass, often consisting of two panes with a durable polymer interlayer that holds the glass together under impact. These windows significantly reduce the risk of breakage during hurricanes compared to standard glass.

  • Increased Safety: Protect homeowners and their families from flying debris.
  • Structural Integrity: Help maintain the building envelope, preventing wind-driven rain and pressure damage.
  • Insurance Benefits: Homes with impact windows often qualify for lower premiums in Florida.
