Non-Destructive Evaluation (NDE) Methods

To ensure accurate diagnosis of window integrity without causing further damage, the company employs several Non-Destructive Evaluation (NDE) methods:

  • Ultrasonic Testing: This technology detects internal flaws, micro-cracks, or delamination in laminated glass by sending high-frequency sound waves through the windowpane, providing detailed imaging of hidden damage.
  • Infrared Thermography: By using infrared cameras, technicians identify moisture intrusion and heat loss patterns around window frames, revealing potential seal failures or areas of deteriorated insulation.
  • Laser Scanning: Precise 3D laser scanning maps the window frame’s shape and structure down to millimeter accuracy, facilitating custom fabrication of replacement components and ensuring perfect fits in complex or unusual window designs.

Advanced Repair Materials

Materials science advancements have introduced new repair products that improve durability under Florida’s climate:

  • Polyurethane-Based Sealants: Offering superior flexibility and UV resistance compared to traditional silicone, these sealants maintain waterproof barriers longer in high-sun environments.
  • Corrosion-Resistant Frame Coatings: Innovative nano-ceramic coatings applied to aluminum and metal frames act as invisible shields against salt-induced oxidation and extend frame lifespan.
  • Laminated Glass Retrofitting: When full glass replacement isn’t feasible, technicians employ retrofit laminate applications—thin interlayers bonded to existing panes—restoring impact resistance with minimal disruption.
