Why Storm-Resistant Windows Are Vital to Florida Home Protection

Florida’s unique location makes it especially vulnerable to hurricanes and tropical storms, which bring intense winds exceeding 100 mph and hazardous flying debris. According to the National Hurricane Center, the Atlantic hurricane season brings approximately 12 named storms annually, many threatening the Florida coastline.

“Having properly maintained hurricane-impact windows can reduce windborne debris damage by up to 70%, protecting homes and families during storm events.” – Florida Building Commission

Storm-resistant windows, often called impact windows, are laminated glass units designed to withstand these forces without shattering. Unlike standard glass, impact glass installation uses multiple layers including a polyvinyl butyral (PVB) interlayer that holds the glass together on impact. However, any damage caused by impacts or age-related wear can compromise these windows. This is where reliable impact window repair services come in, restoring the integrity of your windows and your home’s storm defense.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of hiring a professional impact window repair service in Florida?

Professional impact window repair services provide specialized knowledge about Florida building codes and storm conditions. They ensure repairs restore the window’s storm resistance, prevent water intrusion, extend window lifespan, and maintain energy efficiency, all crucial for effective Florida home protection.

How often should impact windows be inspected and maintained in Florida?

It's recommended to inspect and maintain impact windows at least once a year, preferably before hurricane season begins. Routine maintenance includes checking seals, cleaning glass and frames, and repairing minor damage to prevent storm damage and prolong window life.

Can damaged impact windows be repaired or do they need full replacement?

Many damages, such as small cracks, broken seals, or hardware issues, can be repaired by expert technicians. However, severe structural damage or multiple broken panes might require full replacement to ensure proper storm resistance.

What makes impact glass installation essential for Florida’s coastal homes?

Impact glass installations feature a laminated interlayer that prevents shattering and shields homes from windborne debris during hurricanes. This is critical in coastal regions of Florida, which regularly face powerful storms and require superior wind-resistant window solutions.
