Frequently Asked Questions

How soon should I repair my impact windows after a storm in Florida?

It’s important to have your impact windows inspected within a few days after any hurricane or tropical storm. Even if damage isn’t immediately visible, seals or frames may be compromised, leading to water leaks or wind penetration during subsequent storms. Prompt hurricane window repair prevents worsening damage and costly structural issues down the line.

Can I repair impact windows myself or should I hire a professional?

Impact window repair requires specialized knowledge and equipment due to the laminated glass and reinforced frames designed for wind resistance. Professionals like those at Impact Window Repair of Florida ensure repairs meet Florida building codes and maintain your window warranty, making them the safest and most reliable choice.

How often should Florida homeowners perform maintenance on their impact windows?

Industry experts recommend scheduling maintenance at least once a year, ideally before hurricane season begins. Regular Florida impact window maintenance includes inspecting seals, locks, hardware, and glass integrity to ensure peak performance during storms.

What are signs that my impact windows need repair or replacement?

Common indicators include cracks or chips in the glass, difficulty opening or closing windows, visible damage to seals or weather stripping, drafts, and water leakage during heavy rain. Addressing these issues quickly helps maintain tropical storm protection and avoids expensive storm damage restoration.
