Frequently Asked Questions

What types of damage do impact window repair services in Florida typically address?

Impact window repair services commonly fix cracks or chips in the impact-resistant glass, replace damaged seals that compromise waterproofing, repair or replace bent or corroded frames (especially common in coastal areas), and restore faulty hardware such as locks or hinges. These repairs maintain the window’s integrity for storm resistance and energy efficiency.

How often should I schedule impact window maintenance in Florida?

Experts recommend scheduling impact window maintenance at least once per year, ideally before hurricane season begins. Regular inspections help identify and address issues like seal failure or minor cracks early, preventing larger problems during storms. Additionally, after severe weather events, windows should be inspected promptly for possible damage.

Can impact windows be repaired after a hurricane hit Florida?

Yes. Professional hurricane window repair services can often restore impact windows affected by storm debris, leaks, or frame damage. Prompt repairs ensure windows regain their protective capabilities. In cases of severe damage where repair isn’t feasible, replacement may be necessary.
