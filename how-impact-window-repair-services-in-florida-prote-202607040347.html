Frequently Asked Questions

What does impact window repair typically involve in Florida?

Impact window repair in Florida usually includes inspecting the window for cracks, seal failures, or broken glass, followed by carefully removing and replacing damaged panes or seals with impact-resistant materials that comply with Florida building codes. Repairs also address frame or hardware issues to restore full functionality and protection.

How often should I schedule impact window maintenance in Florida?

Experts recommend scheduling impact window maintenance at least once annually and after any significant storm event. Regular maintenance involves inspecting seals, frames, and glass integrity to ensure compliance with wind zone requirements and to detect minor damage before it worsens.

Can impact window repair services help with insurance claims after storm damage?

Yes. Prompt impact window repair can significantly strengthen your insurance claim by documenting damage mitigation and restoration efforts. Many Florida insurance companies require evidence of maintenance and timely repairs to process claims related to storm damage effectively.

How do I know if my windows meet Florida's wind zone compliance after repairs?

Reputable impact window repair services in Florida will perform inspections and certify that repairs and replacements meet or exceed Florida Building Code requirements for the specific wind zone your home is located in. They can provide official documentation to confirm compliance for insurance and legal purposes.
