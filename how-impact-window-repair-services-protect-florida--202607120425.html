Understanding the Importance of Impact Windows in Florida

Florida’s geographical location exposes it to frequent hurricanes and severe storms, making storm-resistant windows a vital feature in many homes. Impact windows consist of laminated glass that combines a durable outer glass layer with an inner polymer interlayer. This design prevents shattering upon impact, significantly reducing the risk of injury and interior damage.

Industry studies highlight the importance of these windows: according to the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA), homes equipped with impact-resistant windows reduce window breakage by up to 90% during hurricanes. Additionally, the Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety (IBHS) notes that storm-resistant windows can reduce insurance premiums by up to 15% in high-risk areas.

Maintenance Tips to Prolong Impact Window Lifespan

Proactive impact window maintenance minimizes the need for expensive hurricane window repair and strengthens storm damage prevention throughout Florida’s storm seasons:

  1. Regular Inspections: Schedule professional inspections at least once a year or after significant storms.
  2. Clean and Lubricate Frames: Salt buildup and humidity can corrode metal frames—cleaning and lubricating regularly prevents premature wear.
  3. Check for Seal Failures: Look for fogging or condensation between glass layers—a sign the seal is compromised.
  4. Trim Nearby Trees: Prevent branch impacts during wind events by maintaining clearances around windows.
