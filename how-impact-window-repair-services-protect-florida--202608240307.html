Common Myths About Impact Window Repair Debunked

Despite widespread adoption in Florida, misconceptions about impact window repair persist, leading some homeowners to delay necessary upkeep or avoid professional services altogether. Clearing these myths helps ensure informed decisions.

Myth 1: Impact windows don’t require maintenance once installed

Reality: Although impact windows are manufactured tough, environmental factors such as salt spray, humidity, and UV exposure degrade seals, hardware, and frames over time. Proactive, periodic maintenance is essential to extending their effectiveness and lifespan.

Myth 2: Repairing impact glass is unreliable and expensive—replacement is better

Reality: Many impact glass damages, such as chips or minor cracks, can be expertly repaired without full replacement, providing cost-effective restoration that meets code. Replacement is only necessary for severe or irreparable damage.

Myth 3: Any glass repair company can properly fix impact windows

Reality: Impact window repair requires specialized knowledge of laminated glass properties, Florida Building Code standards, and certified materials. Only contractors with expertise in impact window systems can guarantee storm-compliant repairs.

Preparing Your Impact Windows for Florida’s Hurricane Season: A Detailed Timeline

Strategic preparation enhances window resilience and homeowner safety. A comprehensive maintenance timeline can optimize impact window performance before storm season arrives.

  • January - March: Schedule initial professional window inspection post-winter when humidity is lower and access is easier.

Following these steps annually empowers Florida homeowners to confidently face the unpredictability of hurricane season.
