Frequently Asked Questions

What types of damage does storm window repair in Florida typically address?

Storm window repair in Florida commonly addresses cracks or chips in impact glass, broken seals causing fogging or leaks, damaged or bent window frames, and faulty hardware. These repairs are essential to maintain the protective function of impact windows against hurricanes and tropical storms.

How often should hurricane window maintenance be performed?

Experts recommend scheduling hurricane window maintenance at least once a year and especially after any major storm event. Regular inspections help identify issues early, ensuring windows remain in full compliance with Florida’s building codes and effective against wind damage.

Can impact glass be restored, or does it always require full replacement?

Minor damage such as surface scratches or small chips can often be repaired through impact glass restoration services. However, significant cracks or compromised panes typically require full hurricane impact window replacement to ensure safety and code compliance.

Does repairing impact windows increase home insurance eligibility in Florida?

Yes, many Florida insurance providers offer better coverage terms or lower premiums for homes with well-maintained impact windows. Documented repairs and maintenance provide proof of proactive protection measures, which can improve eligibility and claims processing.
