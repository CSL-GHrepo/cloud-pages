Quick Answer: Typical Fix and Flip Timeline From Purchase to Sale

The fix and flip process typically spans 3 to 6 months—from acquiring the property through completing renovations to closing the sale. On average, the rehab phase alone takes approximately 30 to 90 days, with the pre-purchase and post-rehab sale phases varying by market conditions and project complexity. Industry research shows that successful flips average around 5.3 months total.

According to ATTOM Data Solutions’ 2023 Home Flipping Report, “The median house flipping duration was 161 days in 2022, representing the average time from purchase to resale.”
Realtor.com reports, “Experienced flippers recommend budgeting at least 90 days for rehab to avoid costly delays that lengthen the fix and flip timeline.”

The Complete Fix and Flip Process: Stage-by-Stage Timeline Breakdown

A fix and flip generally follows these key property flipping stages:

  1. Property Acquisition (1-4 weeks):
    Finding the right property, negotiating, conducting inspections, and closing can take between one to four weeks. Strong due diligence here is crucial to prevent surprises later.
  2. Planning and Permitting (1-3 weeks):
    After purchase, planning your rehab includes getting contractor bids, finalizing designs, and applying for permits (if needed). This stage can sometimes overlap with property acquisition in experienced projects.
  3. Rehab and Renovations (30-90 days):
    The actual renovation phase is typically the longest segment. Simple cosmetic upgrades may take as little as 30 days, but extensive structural repairs or adding square footage can extend timeline to three months or more. Keeping the rehab timeline on schedule is vital to controlling costs.
  4. Staging, Marketing, and Sale (2-6 weeks):
    Once renovations are complete, the property is marketed, staged, and shown. Depending on market demand, it may take a few weeks to secure an offer and close escrow.

Example Fix and Flip Project Schedule

  • Week 1-3: Property search, offer, and closing
  • Week 3-5: Rehab planning and permitting
  • Week 6-14: Renovations and inspections
  • Week 15-17: Staging, listing, and sale process

This simulated schedule adds up to about four months, a typical duration for many property flips.

Factors Influencing the House Flipping Duration

Your fix and flip timeline varies depending on:

  • Property condition and rehab scope: Extensively damaged homes require longer rehabs.
  • Experience of team and contractors: Seasoned contractors help keep rehab on track.
  • Permitting and inspections: Regions with lengthy permitting processes add time.
  • Market conditions and sales speed: In hot markets, properties sell quickly post-renovation.
  • Financing method: Cash-funded flips often proceed faster than those relying on traditional loans.
