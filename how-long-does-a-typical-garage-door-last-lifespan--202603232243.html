Top Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Parkland Residents

Maintaining your garage door is key to ensuring safety and longevity. In Parkland, I recommend scheduling a professional tune-up annually. Regular lubrication of moving parts reduces wear and noise, keeping your system smooth.

Check the balance of your garage door monthly. Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door to see if it stays open or closes on its own. An unbalanced door can strain springs and other components, leading to costly repairs.

Inspect the tracks for dirt, debris, or misalignment. Clean and tighten any loose bolts or screws. Properly aligned tracks support smooth operation and prevent door derailment.

Test safety features like sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms monthly. These are crucial for preventing accidents, especially if you have children or pets in the home.

If your garage door squeaks or makes grinding noises, it’s time for lubrication. Use a silicone-based lubricant on rollers, hinges, and tracks. Regular upkeep saves money and extends your door’s lifespan.
