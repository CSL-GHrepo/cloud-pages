Storm Window Repair Process and What to Expect

The storm window repair process in Florida generally follows a systematic procedure designed for speed and quality:

  • Emergency Board-Up (If Needed): For broken or shattered impact glass, contractors may secure the window area temporarily to protect from elements.
  • Detailed Damage Evaluation: Structural impacts to frames or hardware are checked along with glass condition.
  • Repair vs. Replacement Decision: Contractors decide whether to repair seals, replace glass panes, or remove entire windows.
  • Removal & Disposal: Old glass and framing are carefully removed in an environmentally responsible way.
  • Installation of New Impact-Resistant Glass or Window Unit: New panes are installed, sealed, and tested for water and air tightness.

Experienced contractors incorporate an impact window maintenance schedule recommendation during the process to ensure long-term protection against storms. Industry experts emphasize the importance of proactive maintenance to reduce emergency repair needs.
