Top Tips for Maintaining Your Locks in Florida

Living in Florida means dealing with humidity, salt air, and storms—all of which can affect your locks’ longevity. Regular maintenance is key to keeping them functioning smoothly. I recommend cleaning your locks regularly with a gentle lubricant and checking for signs of rust or corrosion. If you notice stiffness or difficulty turning the key, it’s best to call a professional before the problem worsens. Preventive care saves you money and hassle in the long run.

Another tip is to rekey locks if you’ve recently moved into a new property or experienced a security breach. Rekeying is a cost-effective way to change your lock’s internal pins so old keys no longer work, without replacing the entire hardware.
