Key Industry Terms Every Parkland Resident Should Know

Understanding locksmith terminology helps you make better security decisions. One common term is 'rekeying,' which involves changing the internal pins of a lock so old keys no longer work, offering a cost-effective security upgrade.

Another important term is 'high-security locks,' which are designed to resist picking, drilling, and bumping. They provide enhanced protection compared to standard locks, ideal for securing residential and commercial properties.

Finally, 'electronic access control' refers to digital locks or keypad systems that allow keyless entry. These systems often include remote management features and can integrate with larger security setups for comprehensive protection.
