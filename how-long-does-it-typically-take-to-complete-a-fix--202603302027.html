Quick Answer: How Long Do Fix and Flip Projects Take?

Industry data shows that most fix and flip projects take roughly three to six months from purchase to resale. According to ATTOM Data Solutions’ 2022 report, the average “hold period” for house flips in the U.S. was 168 days, or about 5.5 months. This timeline includes purchasing, renovating, and preparing the property for sale. The actual renovation project completion time within this period usually ranges from 30 to 90 days, depending on the project's scope.

Breaking Down the Fix and Flip Timeline

The real estate flipping process generally involves three primary phases:

  1. Acquisition and Due Diligence (1-4 weeks)
    This is the stage where you find the right property, evaluate market conditions, and conduct inspections to assess repair needs. Quick, thorough due diligence helps set accurate renovation timelines.
  2. Renovation/Property Rehab (30-90 days)
    This is the most variable part of the fix and flip project schedule. The home renovation duration depends on factors like the property's initial condition, contractors’ availability, and required permits. Minor cosmetic upgrades can be completed in a few weeks, while full-scale rehabs may take several months.
  3. Marketing and Sale (2-6 weeks)
    Once the renovation is complete, staging, listing, and closing on the sale take place. Market demand and pricing strategy will impact this phase length.

Key Factors Affecting Renovation Project Completion Time

  • Project Scope: Cosmetic versus structural work radically changes timelines.
  • Permitting and Inspections: Local regulations can slow down or speed up the rehab process.
  • Contractor Efficiency: Experienced crews can shorten the average remodeling period.
  • Supply Chain and Materials: Delays in materials can add weeks to the schedule.
  • Regional Market Conditions: Seasonal weather or labor availability can influence project duration.

Example of a Typical Fix and Flip Project Schedule

To illustrate, here’s a breakdown from an actual 4-month flip:

  • Week 1-3: Property acquisition and detailed inspection
  • Week 4-8: Demolition and structural repairs
  • Week 9-12: Electrical, plumbing, HVAC upgrades
  • Week 13-14: Interior finishes — flooring, paint, cabinets
  • Week 15-16: Final inspections, staging, and listing
  • Week 17: Sale and closing

This timeline aligns well with the industry average of 3 to 6 months total turnaround time, balancing quality workmanship with market timing.

Strategies to Optimize Your Fix and Flip Project Timeline

To keep your project on track, consider these actionable tips from seasoned investors:

  • Plan meticulously: A clear scope with contingencies saves weeks in unexpected delays.
  • Hire experienced contractors: Trusted teams enable accurate scheduling and minimize rework.
  • Secure permits early: Start the permit application process simultaneously with closing.
  • Maintain constant communication: Weekly progress updates help catch delays early.
  • Prepare for contingencies: Budget time for unforeseen issues, like hidden damage or supply disruptions.

Regional Considerations and Market Impact on Project Duration

While the core renovation project completion time can generally stay consistent across markets, regional factors play an important role:

  • Urban vs. Rural: Urban areas may have stricter permitting laws but access to skilled labor is often better.
  • Seasonal Weather: Harsh winters or rainy seasons can extend exterior work schedules.
  • Local Supply Chains: Remote areas may face longer wait times for materials.

Plan your fix and flip project schedule with these in mind to avoid unexpected delays.
