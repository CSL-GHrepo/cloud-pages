Quick Answer: How Long Does It Usually Take to Complete a Fix and Flip?

The average fix and flip time varies but typically ranges from 3 to 6 months from purchase to sale. According to ATTOM Data Solutions, the median holding period for house flips in 2023 was approximately 180 days, including time spent on renovation and selling. Some investors successfully complete projects in as little as 60 to 90 days by limiting renovations to cosmetic upgrades and working with specialized contractors.

The Typical Fix and Flip Project Timeline: Breaking Down the Phases

The house flipping process duration is influenced by multiple variables like the property’s condition, renovation complexity, contractor availability, and market dynamics. Here’s a step-by-step outline of the typical flipping a house phases with estimated time frames:

  1. 1. Property Acquisition and Inspection (1-2 weeks)

    The first step is locating a suitable property below market value, conducting a thorough inspection, and securing financing. An experienced investor can close this phase quickly by having pre-approved loans and a reliable inspection team.

  2. 2. Planning and Permitting (1-3 weeks)

    Once acquired, you’ll finalize your renovation plan, budget, and timeline. Some renovations require city permits, which can add time depending on local regulations. Efficient project management helps streamline this stage.

  3. 3. Renovation Phase (4-12 weeks)

    This is the most variable phase and largely dictates the total property rehab timeline. Cosmetic fixes such as painting, flooring, and lighting updates are faster, while structural repairs, roofing, or major systems upgrades take longer.

    • Minor cosmetic updates: 2-4 weeks
    • Medium renovations (kitchen/bath remodel): 6-8 weeks
    • Major rehab (foundation, electrical, plumbing): 10-12 weeks or more

  4. 4. Staging and Marketing (1-3 weeks)

    After renovation, homes are often staged to appeal to buyers. This phase also includes listing the property and holding open houses, which might require some flexibility depending on market demand.

  5. 5. Sale and Closing (2-6 weeks)

    Once an offer is accepted, closing can take anywhere from two to six weeks. During this period, inspections and appraisals may occur, potentially affecting the timeline.

Real Estate Renovation Schedule Tips: How to Shorten Your Fix and Flip Timeline

Understanding the phases is essential, but how can you accelerate your fix and flip timeline without compromising quality?

  • Work With Experienced Contractors: Partner with contractors familiar with quick turnarounds and who have good local reputations to avoid delays.
  • Plan Thoroughly Upfront: Detailed project plans reduce costly change orders and keep the rehab on schedule.
  • Obtain Permits Early: Start the permitting process as soon as possible to prevent bottlenecks.
  • Focus on High-ROI Upgrades: Especially in hot markets, prioritize cosmetic updates and kitchen/bath improvements that sell quickly.
  • Pre-market the Property: Create buzz before completion to line up buyers and reduce time on market.

According to the National Association of Realtors, properties that require only cosmetic renovation typically spend 20-30% less time on the market, which can significantly improve investment returns.

“Efficiency in each phase of the property rehab timeline is key. Experienced flippers can cut the average fix and flip time in half through planning and execution strategies,” says renovation expert Alan McBride.

Common Challenges That Impact the Fix and Flip Project Timeline

Delays are common and often unavoidable, but understanding potential pitfalls can help you prepare and adapt:

  • Unexpected Structural Issues: Discovering hidden damage can extend renovation by weeks.
  • Supply Chain Delays: Material shortages can stall progress, especially in high-demand markets.
  • Permit Delays: Local government backlogs often slow down approval.
  • Labor Shortages: Skilled labor availability is critical to meeting timelines.
  • Market Fluctuations: Changing real estate conditions may affect sales timing.

Being proactive and realistic about these challenges helps investors mitigate risk and make informed decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What factors influence the house flipping process duration?

The primary factors that influence the house flipping process duration include the property's initial condition, scope of renovation (cosmetic vs. major rehab), contractor availability, permitting timelines, and local market demand. Unexpected repairs and material delays can also extend the fix and flip timeline.

Is it possible to complete a fix and flip in under 90 days?

Yes, it is possible to complete a fix and flip in under 90 days, especially if the renovation is minor (such as painting, flooring, or small repairs), the investor has strong project management skills, and local permitting processes are fast. Quick turnarounds are ideal in competitive markets where speed can maximize returns.

How can I estimate a realistic property rehab timeline?

To estimate a realistic property rehab timeline, thoroughly inspect the property upfront, consult with contractors for detailed labor and materials estimates, factor in permitting durations, and add buffer time for unforeseen delays. A typical rehab ranges from 4 to 12 weeks depending on scope.

Do regional differences affect the average fix and flip time?

Yes, regional differences such as local permitting processes, labor availability, and market conditions can impact the fix and flip project timeline. For example, urban areas with fast real estate turnovers might have shorter selling phases than rural locations, but could face stricter permitting delays.
