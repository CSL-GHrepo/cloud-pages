In most cases, the property resale duration—the time it takes from completing renovations to closing a sale—ranges from 30 to 90 days. What Influences the Selling Time of a Fix and Flip Property?

The time it takes to sell a flipped property can be influenced by multiple factors, including:

  • Market conditions: In a seller’s market, properties typically sell faster, sometimes within just a few weeks. Conversely, a buyer’s market can extend resale duration significantly.
  • Location and regional considerations: Urban areas with high demand often see quicker sales, whereas rural or less sought-after neighborhoods may prolong the selling cycle.
  • Quality and scope of renovations: Homes that have been renovated thoughtfully, with modern finishes and attention to detail, tend to attract buyers faster.
  • Pricing strategy: Overpricing can deter offers and lengthen the time a property sits on the market. Competitive pricing aligned with current fix and flip market trends encourages quicker transactions.
  • Marketing and exposure: Utilizing effective listing platforms, professional photography, open houses, and social media promotion accelerates interest and showings.

Statistics to Consider

According to ATTOM Data Solutions’ 2023 Home Flipping Report, the average holding period for flipped properties in the U.S. was 164 days, incorporating both renovation and resale phases.

The National Association of Realtors’ 2022 data reveals that properties that spent less than a month on the market before sale sold at prices closer to or above their listing price.

Breaking Down the Fix and Flip Timeline

Understanding each phase of the fix and flip timeline helps set realistic expectations for resale duration:

  1. Acquisition and inspection: 1-2 weeks to identify and purchase a suitable property.
  2. Home renovation period: Typically 1-3 months, depending on the extent of repairs or upgrades.
  3. Listing and marketing: Immediately after the renovation, with an expected 30-90 day selling window.
  4. Offer negotiation and closing: Approximately 30-45 days, including inspections and financing contingencies.

This means that while renovations may take up to three months, the home renovation selling time after completion is often 1 to 3 months with proper market positioning.
