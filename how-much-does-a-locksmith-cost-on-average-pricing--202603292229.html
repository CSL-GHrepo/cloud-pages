Locksmith Jargon Made Simple

Understanding industry terms can help you communicate better with your locksmith. Here are a few common terms used in our line of work:

- **Rekeying:** Changing the internal pins of your lock so a new key is needed, without replacing the entire lock.

- **Deadbolt:** A sturdy lock mechanism that provides extra security, often installed on exterior doors.

- **Bump Key:** A specially filed key that can open traditional pin tumbler locks if not properly protected.

- **High-Security Lock:** A lock designed with advanced features to resist picking, drilling, and unauthorized duplication.

- **Electronic Lock:** A keypad, biometric, or smart lock that uses electronic systems for access control.
