Understanding Lock & Key Industry Terms

Rekeying: Changing the internal pins of a lock so it works with a new key, without replacing the entire lock. It’s a cost-effective security upgrade after key loss.

Deadbolt: A lock that extends a solid metal bolt into the door frame, providing strong security. Often used on front doors and commercial entrances.

Electronic Lock: A keypad or biometric lock that uses electronic components for access. Offers keyless security and remote control features.

Smart Lock: An advanced electronic lock that connects to Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, allowing remote access and control via smartphone app.

Lock Bumping: A method of opening pin tumbler locks using specially crafted bump keys. An issue that highlights the importance of high-security locks.
