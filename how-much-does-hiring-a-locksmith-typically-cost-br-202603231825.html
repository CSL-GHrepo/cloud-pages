Quick Overview of Locksmith Service Prices

On average, a locksmith charges between $50 and $150 for standard services such as unlocking a door or making a duplicate key. Emergency locksmith fees—especially during nights or weekends—can add an additional $50-$100. More complex jobs, like lock replacement or security system installation, can range from $150 to $350 or more.

According to HomeAdvisor, the average locksmith cost in the U.S. ranges from $50 to $150 per service call, with hourly locksmith rates averaging $75 to $150.

Breaking Down the Typical Locksmith Costs

Understanding the elements that influence locksmith pricing helps you budget better and choose the right service.

1. Locksmith Hourly Rates vs. Flat Fees

  • Hourly Rates: Some locksmiths charge by the hour, usually between $50 and $150, depending on expertise and complexity.
  • Flat Fees: Many services, such as lockout assistance or key duplication, come with flat fees ranging from $50 to $100.

2. Emergency Locksmith Fees

Emergency locksmith services—such as late-night lockouts or urgent lock repairs—generally come with surcharges to compensate for the inconvenience:

  • Additional $50-$100 for nighttime or weekend service
  • Faster response times may also increase cost

Industry professionals recommend always confirming emergency locksmith fees upfront to avoid unexpected charges.

3. Residential Locksmith Pricing

  • Lockout services typically cost $50-$120
  • Rekeying locks averages between $60 and $140 per lock
  • Cost of lock replacement varies from $100 to $300 depending on lock type (standard, smart lock, deadbolt)

4. Commercial Locksmith Cost

Commercial locksmith services tend to be more expensive due to the complexity and security requirements:

  • High-security lock install: $150-$400 per lock
  • Access control systems and master key systems can cost upwards of $500
  • Maintenance contracts are often available for ongoing service

Factors That Affect Locksmith Service Charges

The final cost you pay is not just about the service itself, but several other considerations:

  • Time of Call: After-hours, holidays, or emergency calls usually mean higher rates.
  • Location: Locksmith costs vary regionally; urban areas typically see slightly higher prices than rural locations.
  • Service Complexity: Simple lockouts cost less than sophisticated security system installations.
  • Type of Lock: Smart locks, electronic keypads, and other advanced hardware will cost more to service or replace.

Expert Tips to Minimize Locksmith Costs

Saving money on locksmith services doesn’t mean sacrificing quality or security. Here’s how to get the best value:

  1. Request a Written Estimate: Always get a breakdown of locksmith service charges before work begins.
  2. Check for Licensing and Insurance: Licensed locksmiths generally provide a fairer price and reliable service.
  3. Avoid Third-Party Dispatch: Calling a locksmith directly often reduces markups from dispatch services.
  4. Consider Non-Emergency Scheduling: Schedule routine work during regular business hours to avoid emergency fees.
  5. Research Local Locksmith Rates: Prices vary; comparing local rates gives you better negotiating power.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are typical locksmith service prices for residential lockouts?

Residential lockout services usually range between $50 and $120. The price can increase if you require assistance during late-night hours or holidays due to emergency locksmith fees. Always ask for a quote up front to understand locksmith service charges.

How much does it cost to replace a lock?

The cost of lock replacement varies depending on the type of lock. Basic deadbolt replacements typically cost $100 to $150, while high-security or smart locks can cost $200 to $350 or more. Labor charges are included in these prices but can vary.

Are emergency locksmith fees standard across the industry?

Emergency locksmith fees are common due to the urgent nature of the service. These fees usually add $50 to $100 to your bill and apply during nights, weekends, and holidays. It’s important to clarify these fees beforehand to avoid surprises.

What influences commercial locksmith costs?

Commercial locksmith costs are influenced by the complexity of security systems, the number of locks, and specialized services such as access control or master key systems. Expect commercial locksmith service charges to be higher than residential services, often ranging from $150 to $500 or more.
