Maintenance Tips for Garage Doors in Parkland

Routine maintenance is essential to keep your garage door functioning smoothly. In Parkland, where humidity and storms are common, regular checks can prevent rust and damage. Simple tasks like lubrication and visual inspections make a significant difference.

Lubricate moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and springs every few months with a silicone-based lubricant. This reduces friction and noise, extending component lifespan. Avoid using grease or WD-40, which can attract dirt.

Check the balance of your garage door by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door. It should stay in place without drifting. If it’s unbalanced, call a technician for adjustment.

Inspect the tracks for debris, dents, or misalignment. Clean and realign as necessary. Properly aligned tracks ensure smooth operation and prevent undue stress on springs and cables.

Test safety features like auto-reverse mechanisms and sensor alignment monthly. If sensors are misaligned or obstructed, your door may not operate correctly, posing safety risks.
