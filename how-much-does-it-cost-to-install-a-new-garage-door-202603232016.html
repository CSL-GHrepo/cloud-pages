Key Safety Tips for Parkland Homeowners

Safety is the top priority with garage doors. First, always test your auto-reverse feature monthly. Place an object in the door’s path; if it doesn’t reverse upon contact, call us for an inspection. Second, keep sensors clean and aligned. Misaligned sensors are common and can cause the door to malfunction or pose safety risks.

Third, avoid DIY repairs on springs or cables. These components are under high tension and can cause serious injury if mishandled. Instead, schedule professional service for any spring issues or system adjustments. Fourth, ensure your garage door is properly balanced. An unbalanced door puts extra strain on the opener and shortens its lifespan.

Finally, upgrade to modern safety features, including rolling code openers and security sensors. These improve safety and prevent unauthorized access. Regular inspections and maintenance ensure your garage door operates safely year-round, protecting your family and property.

If you notice strange noises, slow operation, or difficulty opening your garage door, consult a professional immediately. Early intervention reduces the risk of accidents and costly repairs.
