Quick Answer: How Much Does Copier Leasing Cost Per Month?

Generally, monthly copier lease rates range from $50 to $300. Small or basic models may start as low as $50 monthly, whereas high-volume, multifunction commercial copiers can command rates upward of $300 or more. Most leases include maintenance fees and sometimes supplies, so your monthly payment covers much more than just rental.

According to a survey by Industry Analysts Group, 70% of businesses report monthly copier rentals between $100 and $250, with maintenance included as a standard part of leasing agreements.

What Influences Your Monthly Copier Rental Cost?

Several factors contribute to the total monthly expense of leasing a copier. Understanding these can help you select the best configuration for your office needs.

  • Type and Model of Copier: Basic black-and-white machines cost less, while advanced multifunction copiers with color printing, scanning, fax, and finishing options increase lease rates.
  • Lease Agreement Terms: Length of lease (usually 24-60 months), upfront fees, and included services dramatically affect monthly costs.
  • Monthly Page Volume: Lease agreements often specify a page volume threshold. Exceeding limits may trigger per-page overage fees, increasing overall costs.
  • Maintenance and Service Plans: Some leases bundle maintenance, while others charge separately, especially for repairs or supplies like toner and drums.
  • Regional Market Rates: While copier leasing is fairly standardized, location-based pricing differences can arise due to competition and service availability.
  • Office Equipment Leasing Prices & Bundles: Leasing multiple devices or pairing copiers with printers and scanners might offer discounted packages.
