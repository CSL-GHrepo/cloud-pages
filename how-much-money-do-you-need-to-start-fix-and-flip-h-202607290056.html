Glossary of Common Hard Money Terms in Parkland

Asset-Based Lending: Funding secured primarily by the value of the property, not the borrower's credit, ideal for quick rehab projects.

Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV): The percentage of the property's appraised value that a lender is willing to lend, often up to 70-80% for fix-and-flip loans in Parkland.

Interest-Only Payments: A repayment method where only interest is paid during the loan term, reducing monthly payments but increasing the total interest paid.

Rehab Loan: A short-term loan used to finance property renovations, paid off once the project is completed or refinanced.

Bridge Loan: A temporary loan that helps investors bridge the gap between property purchase and long-term financing or sale.
