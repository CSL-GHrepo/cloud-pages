How to Maintain Your Garage Door in Parkland

Maintaining your garage door is easier than you might think. Regular upkeep not only prolongs the lifespan of your system but also prevents costly repairs. In Parkland’s climate, keeping components lubricated and parts aligned is particularly important due to humidity and storm exposure. A few simple steps can make a big difference.

First, check the balance of your garage door monthly. Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway. It should stay in place; if it slips, spring tension adjustments may be needed. Next, lubricate moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and tracks with a recommended lubricant. This reduces wear and keeps the door opening smoothly.

Third, inspect the tracks for debris or dents. Clean and align them if necessary. Tighten loose bolts or brackets as they can loosen over time. Fourth, test the safety features—like auto-reverse—monthly to ensure they’re functioning correctly. Finally, schedule professional inspections annually to catch issues early and keep everything in top shape.
