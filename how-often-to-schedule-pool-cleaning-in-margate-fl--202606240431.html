Pool Cleaning Tips for Florida Homeowners

To help maintain your pool between professional visits, consider these Florida-specific pool cleaning tips:

  • Regular Skimming and Brushing: Remove leaves and algae before they settle.
  • Monitor Water Chemistry: Use test kits weekly—especially after rain events.
  • Maintain Filter Systems: Clean or replace filters as recommended to ensure efficient water circulation.
  • Control Phosphates and Nitrates: Use phosphate removers to prevent algae growth.
  • Schedule Professional Inspections: Seasonally check pumps, heaters, and salt chlorinators for optimal operation.
