Why Avoiding Realtor Fees Matters

Realtor fees traditionally consume a large portion of a home sale's proceeds. According to the National Association of Realtors, the average agent commission ranges from 5% to 6% of the sale price, which can equate to thousands of dollars lost by the seller. For a home selling at $300,000, the agent’s fee could easily reach $15,000 to $18,000.

These fees are often negotiable but rarely waived completely because agents invest significant resources into marketing, staging, and negotiating. However, for many homeowners seeking a simpler, quicker, and more cost-efficient route to sell, paying these commissions isn’t always worth it.

> “Avoiding real estate commissions can save sellers an average of $16,000 per transaction, based on median home price data from the U.S. Census Bureau.”

How Selling to a Cash Home Buyer Helps You Bypass Realtor Fees

Cash home buyers are investors or companies that purchase homes outright using their own funds. Because there is no middleman in the form of a realtor facilitating the sale, sellers can keep 100% of their home’s sale price minus any minimal closing costs.

  • Direct Negotiation: Sellers negotiate directly with the buyer, which eliminates agent commissions from the equation.
  • Faster Closings: Cash buyer home sales often close within days or weeks, as they don’t rely on lengthy mortgage approvals.
  • No Repairs Required: Many cash buyers purchase homes “as-is,” saving sellers money on costly repairs or upgrades.

Because cash buyers pay with their own funds, there’s no waiting on lenders or banks, helping sellers sell property fast cash without uncertainties.
