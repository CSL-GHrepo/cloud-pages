Top Copier Lease Qualification Tips for Small Businesses

Boosting your chances to qualify for a copier lease in 2024 means applying strategic steps:

  1. Know your credit situation: Obtain your business and personal credit reports. While good credit helps, many programs accept newer or weaker credit profiles.
  2. Demonstrate stable cash flow: Leasing companies want proof that your business can make consistent payments. Provide bank statements or evidence of steady revenue.
  3. Shop around for flexible lease terms: Some lessors offer business copier payment plans with low or no down payment options and shorter lease durations.
  4. Consider using a co-signer: For startups or new businesses, a co-signer with a stronger credit profile can increase approval odds.
  5. Choose equipment wisely: Leasing newer, reliable copiers with maintenance packages can be more appealing to leasing companies.
