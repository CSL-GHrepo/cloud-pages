Step 1: Comprehensive Damage Assessment

Immediately following a hurricane or severe storm, the first step is a thorough inspection by licensed window contractors in Florida. They examine the following:

  • Cracks, chips, or breaks in tempered and laminated impact-resistant glass
  • Damage to window seals, frames, and locks
  • Potential water intrusion and structural impacts

According to safety experts, about 76% of window failures in hurricanes are due to frame or seal damage, not glass breakage alone (Florida Building Commission, 2023).

Licensed contractors also verify compliance with Florida building codes impact windows, which dictate the required strength and installation specifications. This critical step ensures repairs restore the window’s hurricane resilience.

Step 2: Choosing Between Repair and Replacement

Based on the assessment, the window contractor determines whether to proceed with impact window repair or recommend an impact window replacement Florida. Key considerations include:

  • If the tempered glass shows minor cracking, specialized tempered glass repair process techniques may restore the window without full replacement.
  • Broken seals or frame deformation often require full window replacement to maintain integrity.
  • Cost of impact window repair versus replacement typically hinges on damage severity and compliance with code requirements.

According to industry data, the average cost of impact window repair in Florida ranges from $300 to $900 per window, while replacements can cost between $800 and $1,500 per unit depending on size and materials (Florida Remodeling Insights, 2023).

Step 3: Professional Impact-Resistant Glass Installation

Once a repair or replacement plan is finalized, licensed window contractors proceed with the installation of impact-resistant glass designed for hurricane protection. This involves:

  1. Careful removal of damaged glass and window components
  2. Preparing frames to meet manufacturer and Florida building code specifications
  3. Installing laminated glass panes with interlayers that prevent shattering
  4. Sealing and weatherproofing to prevent leaks and maintain energy efficiency
  5. Testing the window for fit, seal, and durability

The impact-resistant glass installation process is critical. As one Florida-based contractor noted,

“Proper installation is just as important as the glass used; even the best materials will fail without expert fitting and sealing.”

Step 4: Post-Repair Inspection & Documentation

After installation, a final inspection ensures all work meets code standards and manufacturer guidelines. Many licensed contractors provide documentation certifying compliance with Florida building codes impact windows, which is vital for insurance claims and resale value.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the typical hurricane window repair steps for impact windows in Florida?

The typical hurricane window repair steps include initial inspection to assess damage, cleaning and preparing the area, repairing or replacing tempered impact-resistant glass, resealing frames, and ensuring compliance with Florida building codes. Licensed contractors perform a final inspection to verify safety and durability.

How much does the impact window repair cost in Florida?

The cost of impact window repair varies but typically ranges from $300 to $900 per window depending on damage severity. Full impact window replacement costs between $800 and $1,500 per window. Factors influencing cost include glass type, frame damage, labor, and compliance requirements.

Can tempered glass be repaired, or does it always need replacement after damage?

Minor chips or cracks in tempered glass may be addressed through a specialized tempered glass repair process, but significant damage generally requires full replacement due to safety concerns. Only licensed window contractors should evaluate and perform repairs to meet Florida’s strict building codes.

Why is it important to hire licensed window contractors for impact window repair in Florida?

Licensed window contractors are trained to comply with Florida building codes, ensuring repairs meet hurricane impact standards. They guarantee quality workmanship, proper materials, and provide warranties and certification necessary for insurance and safety compliance—critical factors in Florida’s storm-prone environment.
