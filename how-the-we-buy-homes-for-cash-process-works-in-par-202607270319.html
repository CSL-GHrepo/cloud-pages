Selling your home — whether for cash or financed buyers — may trigger capital gains tax liabilities depending on ownership duration and profit:

Large cash transactions require compliance with federal laws, such as:

Professional legal or tax advice is highly recommended to navigate these areas confidently and avoid unintended liabilities.
