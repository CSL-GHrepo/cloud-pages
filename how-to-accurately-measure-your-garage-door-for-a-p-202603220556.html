Garage Door Terminology Every Parkland Homeowner Should Know

Spring: a high-tension component that counterbalances the weight of the door, allowing easy opening and closing. Common types are torsion and extension springs.

Track: the metal guides mounted on both sides of the door frame that direct the movement of rollers. Proper alignment is crucial for smooth operation.

Opener: the motorized device that automates opening and closing. Modern models include smart technology for remote control and integration.

Cables: steel wires that connect springs to the bottom of the door, helping lift the door evenly. Frayed or broken cables require immediate attention.

Safety sensors: devices installed near the ground that detect obstructions, preventing the door from closing on objects or people.
