Check the tracks for debris or misalignment, and tighten loose hardware to avoid further damage. We recommend using silicone-based lubricants on rollers, hinges, and springs for smooth operation. Avoid using WD-40 or petroleum-based products, as they can attract dirt or degrade parts over time.
