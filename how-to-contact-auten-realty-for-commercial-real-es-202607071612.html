Understanding Commercial Real Estate Terminology with Auten Realty

When entering the multifamily investment space, terms like cap rate, net operating income (NOI), and due diligence will frequently arise. Auten Realty goes beyond basic brokerage by educating clients on industry terminology, empowering investors to make informed decisions.

  • Cap Rate: The capitalization rate is a key metric that expresses the expected annual rate of return on a property, calculated by dividing net operating income by the property value.
  • Net Operating Income (NOI): This is the total income from a property minus all reasonably necessary operating expenses, excluding debt service and taxes.
  • Letter of Intent (LOI): A preliminary agreement outlining the basic terms and price before a more formal contract is drafted.
  • Due Diligence: The investigative process involving property inspections, financial audits, and legal review prior to finalizing a transaction.
  • Build-to-Rent (BTR): A development model focused on residential communities constructed explicitly for the rental market, increasingly popular in Katy’s expanding suburbs.

Auten Realty’s brokers assist in demystifying these terms during your interactions and provide clear explanations tailored to your current knowledge level.
