Step 1: Verify Credentials and Certifications

Before hiring, ensure the service provider employs certified impact window technicians. Certified technicians have passed rigorous training and adhere to Florida’s stringent building codes for hurricane resilience.

  • Check for licenses: Florida requires window contractors to hold a license specific to impact window installation and repair. Request to see these documents.
  • Certifications: Confirm technicians are certified by recognized bodies such as the American Architectural Manufacturers Association (AAMA) or have passed manufacturer-specific training for impact-resistant glass replacement.
  • Insurance: Verify general liability and workers’ compensation insurance to protect yourself from liability in case of on-site accidents during repair.

Florida window contractors who meet these criteria demonstrate accountability and a higher level of professionalism, reducing the risks of subpar work or delays.
