Step 1: Verify Licensing and Certifications

“Licensed locksmith services are your first line of defense against fraud and substandard workmanship.” Industry professionals recommend always confirming a locksmith’s license to ensure compliance with your state’s regulations. Licensing requirements vary by region, but valid credentials typically include:

  • State-issued locksmith license or registration
  • Certification from recognized organizations such as Associated Locksmiths of America (ALOA)
  • Insurance coverage to protect against damages during service

According to the Bureau of Labor Statistics, “Certified locksmith technicians tend to earn higher trust and ratings, as certification demonstrates proven knowledge and experience.”
