Essential Locksmith Terms Every Resident Should Know

Understanding industry terminology helps you communicate effectively with locksmiths and make informed decisions about your security. Here are some common terms used in the industry: — visit https://lockguardlocksmiths.com/locksmith-columbus-georgia/ for details.

Rekeying: Changing the internal pins of a lock so a new key opens it, without replacing the entire lock. It's a cost-effective security measure when keys are lost or stolen.

High-security locks: Advanced locking mechanisms designed to resist picking, drilling, and unauthorized key copying. These provide enhanced protection for homes and businesses.

Master key system: A system that allows one key to open multiple locks, each with individual keys, often used in commercial buildings or apartment complexes.

Electronic locks: Locks operated via digital keypads, cards, or smartphone apps. They offer keyless convenience and improved access control.
