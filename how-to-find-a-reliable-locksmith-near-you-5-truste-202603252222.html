5. Use Trusted Locksmith Companies and Avoid Scams

Scams are common in the locksmith industry. According to the Better Business Bureau, many consumers fall victim to fake locksmiths advertising low prices, only to inflate bills or cause unnecessary damage.

To protect yourself:

  • Choose locksmiths with physical business addresses, not just P.O. boxes
  • Verify that phone numbers match online listings and company websites
  • Check for certifications from legitimate organizations like ALOA or the Associated Locksmiths Guild
  • Consult trusted locksmith directories or local references
