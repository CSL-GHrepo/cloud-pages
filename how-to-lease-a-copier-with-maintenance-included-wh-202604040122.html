Frequently Asked Questions

Can I lease a copier with maintenance included in the lease agreement?

Yes. Many suppliers offer full-service copier leases that include both rental and comprehensive maintenance coverage, including parts, labor, and sometimes consumables. This approach ensures your copier remains functional without unexpected repair costs.

What is the difference between leasing a copier with maintenance and just leasing the device?

Leasing a copier with maintenance usually refers to a full-service lease where the vendor handles servicing and repairs, while a basic lease may only include the equipment rental, making you responsible for maintenance and repair costs.

Are copier maintenance plans included in managed print services offerings?

Often, yes. Managed print services typically bundle leasing, maintenance plans, consumables, and print management software services to optimize your print environment completely.

How do I decide between leasing vs. buying a copier when maintenance is considered?

Leasing with maintenance offers predictable costs, less upfront investment, and expert service, making it ideal for businesses wanting to avoid surprises. Buying might be more cost-effective for very long-term use if you can manage maintenance independently. Consider your budget, volume needs, and technical support preferences when deciding.
