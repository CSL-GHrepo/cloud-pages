Financial Considerations and Tax Implications in Burkburnett Multifamily Sales

Selling a commercial multifamily property involves various financial and tax aspects unique to investment real estate. Auten Realty’s experts often collaborate with financial advisors and tax professionals to ensure sellers understand these factors.

Capital Gains Taxes and 1031 Exchanges

One of the key considerations is capital gains tax on profits from the sale. Many investors explore a 1031 exchange to defer taxes by reinvesting proceeds into another like-kind property:

  • Deferral advantage: Postpones capital gains taxes, freeing up more capital for reinvestment.
  • Strict timelines: The replacement property must be identified within 45 days and purchased within 180 days from sale closing.
  • Professional guidance: Auten Realty works with qualified intermediaries to facilitate a smooth 1031 exchange process aligned with your sale.

Depreciation Recapture and Other Cost Basis Adjustments

Besides capital gains, sellers should consider depreciation recapture tax, which applies to the depreciation expense claimed during ownership and is taxed differently. Thorough preparation of your property’s cost basis and understanding taxable events upfront can prevent surprises and optimize net proceeds.
