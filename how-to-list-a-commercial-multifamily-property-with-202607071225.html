Investment Structures and Their Impact on Transaction Complexity

Buyers may structure purchases in various ways that influence due diligence and contract negotiation phases, including:

  • Asset Purchase: Straightforward acquisition of the property and its physical assets.
  • Entity Purchase: Buying the LLC or corporation that owns the property, often preferred by institutional buyers for tax benefits but involving complex legal considerations.
  • Joint Ventures: Partnerships formed between sellers and buyers to share ownership and management responsibilities post-closing, requiring nuanced agreements.

Auten Realty’s expertise ensures transactions proceed smoothly regardless of the investment structure, providing tailored advice and facilitating communication among attorneys, accountants, and investors.
