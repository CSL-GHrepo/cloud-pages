Evaluating Multifamily Property Financials: Key Metrics Explained

Understanding financial metrics is essential for informed decision-making. Auten Realty educates buyers and sellers on interpreting multifamily property data specific to Center, TX market conditions.

Net Operating Income (NOI)

NOI represents total property income minus operating expenses but before debt service and taxes. It's a primary indicator of the property’s profitability.

Example: A 10-unit apartment generating $120,000 annually in rent with $40,000 in expenses results in an NOI of $80,000.

Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate)

The cap rate is the ratio of NOI to the property’s purchase price, reflecting the expected rate of return irrespective of financing.

Example: If the same property is listed for $1,000,000, the cap rate would be 8% ($80,000 / $1,000,000).

Cash-on-Cash Return (CoC)

This metric compares the annual pre-tax cash flow to the actual cash invested, helping investors evaluate cash income relative to capital outlay.

Gross Rent Multiplier (GRM)

GRM is the property price divided by gross rental income, offering a quick measure of value based on income-generating potential alone. It’s less detailed but useful for initial screening.

Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)

DSCR measures the ability of NOI to cover debt payments. Lenders often require minimum DSCR figures, commonly around 1.20, to mitigate risk.
