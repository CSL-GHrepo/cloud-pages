Maintenance Tips to Keep Your Garage Door in Top Shape

Regular maintenance is the key to extending your garage door’s lifespan and preventing inconvenient breakdowns. In Parkland, where weather can vary, proactive care ensures your door remains reliable year-round. Basic routines like lubricating moving parts, inspecting cables, and tightening hardware are simple yet effective steps every homeowner can perform.

Lubricate hinges, rollers, and tracks with a silicone-based spray every few months. This minimizes friction and prevents squeaks during operation. Check the emergency release handle and ensure it functions smoothly. Also, examine the springs for signs of wear or corrosion, as these are critical components under high tension.

Inspect your door’s balance by unplugging the opener and manually opening the door halfway. It should stay in position. If it doesn’t, misalignment could be the issue, and you should call a professional. Regular inspections of cables and pulleys help catch fraying or damage early.

Cleaning your garage door with mild soap and water keeps it looking fresh and prevents dirt buildup that can cause corrosion. For wooden doors, additional sealing or staining may be beneficial to withstand the Florida humidity and storms.
