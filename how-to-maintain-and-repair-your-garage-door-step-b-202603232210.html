Common Garage Door Problems Specific to Parkland Homes

Parkland homes often face unique garage door issues related to local climate and construction styles. Recognizing these problems early can prevent further damage and costly repairs. Some common problems include broken springs, misaligned tracks, and faulty safety sensors.

Broken springs are one of the most frequent problems. Since springs hold a significant amount of tension, they can snap unexpectedly, leaving your garage door stuck or unable to open. We recommend replacing springs before they fully fail to avoid emergency situations.

Track misalignment often results from frequent storms or accidental bumps. When tracks are bent or misaligned, the door may jerk during operation or become stuck. Realignment and tightening of bolts usually resolve this issue quickly.

Faulty safety sensors are another common problem, especially in areas with high humidity. If the sensors become dirty or misaligned, the door may refuse to close. We routinely repair or replace sensors to ensure safety and compliance.

Wear and tear on garage door openers can also be aggravated by frequent usage or power surges. Recognizing the signs early helps in scheduling timely repairs or replacements, maintaining security and convenience.
