Safety Tips for Parkland Homeowners

Garage doors are heavy and equipped with powerful springs and cables—proper safety precautions are essential. When working around these systems, always disconnect power to avoid accidental activation.

Never attempt to replace springs or cables yourself unless you’re trained; these are under high tension and can cause injury. Call a professional for any repairs involving springs or tension components.

Regularly test your safety sensors. If the sensors are misaligned or dirty, they can prevent the door from closing properly, risking accidents. Clean and adjust sensors as needed.

Keep children and pets away from garage doors, especially during operation. Educate family members about the dangers of playing near or under a moving door.

Finally, always use a professional for repairs or installations. Proper tools, experience, and knowledge ensure safe, reliable service for your home.
