Essential Glossary for Parkland Hard Money Investors

Asset-Based Lending: Loans secured by the property’s value rather than borrower credit. Focuses on the property’s potential for quick funding approval. — visit https://flipfundingpro.com/winnemucca/ for details.

ARV (After Repair Value): The estimated value of a property after renovations, vital for calculating loan amounts and potential profits.

Points: Fees paid upfront to the lender, usually a percentage of the loan amount, affecting the overall cost of borrowing.

Bridge Loan: Short-term financing used to bridge gaps between purchasing and refinancing or selling a property.

Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV): The ratio of the loan amount to the appraised value of the property, influencing loan terms and approval chances.
