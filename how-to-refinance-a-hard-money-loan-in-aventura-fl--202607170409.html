Understanding Costs Associated with Refinancing Hard Money Loans in Aventura

When refinancing a hard money loan in Aventura, it’s essential to grasp the full spectrum of costs involved beyond just the interest rate. These expenses can impact your overall investment returns and should be factored into your decision-making process.

  • Origination Fees: Typically 1% to 3% of the new loan amount, these fees compensate the lender for processing the loan application.
  • Appraisal Fees: Expect to pay between $300 and $600 for a professional property appraisal, which validates the current market value and supports your loan application.
  • Title Search and Insurance: Title search fees ensure that the property has a clear title, free from liens or disputes, while title insurance protects lenders and borrowers from title defects. These can range from $400 to $1,000 depending on the property's value.
  • Recording and Legal Fees: Local government recording fees, attorney fees for reviewing documents, and notary fees may total several hundred dollars.
  • Prepayment Penalties: As noted earlier, some hard money loans impose penalties for early payoff, typically from 1% to 5% of the outstanding loan balance. Be sure to verify this with your lender before refinancing.

When calculating your refinancing budget, add these closing costs to your loan payoff amount and compare it against potential monthly savings and long-term benefits.
