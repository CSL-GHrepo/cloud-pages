Key Terms You Should Know About Selling in Parkland

Understanding real estate terminology makes the selling process smoother. Here are some key terms you’ll encounter: — our cash home buyer team.

‘As-is’ means selling your property in its current condition without repairs or improvements. This is ideal for homeowners looking to sell quickly without additional costs.

‘Contingency’ refers to clauses that must be satisfied before the sale can close, such as inspections or financing approval. Cash buyers usually remove these contingencies for a faster process.

‘Equity’ is the difference between your home’s market value and what you owe on your mortgage. Knowing your equity helps determine your options when selling quickly.

‘Closing costs’ are expenses incurred at the final stage of a sale, including legal fees, title services, and transfer taxes. Selling to a cash buyer often reduces these costs.
