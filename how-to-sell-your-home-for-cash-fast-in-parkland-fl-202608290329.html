Tax Implications When Selling Your Home for Cash in Parkland, FL

Converting your home into cash through a sale has potential tax consequences that every seller should understand:

  • Capital Gains Tax: If your home has appreciated in value since purchase, part of your profit may be subject to capital gains tax. However, the IRS offers exemptions for primary residences under certain conditions.
  • 1099-S Reporting: Sellers receive a Form 1099-S reporting the transaction to the IRS, so accurate recordkeeping is critical.
  • State and Local Taxes: Florida does not have a state income tax, so no state-level capital gains tax applies, but local property tax prorations and documentary stamp taxes on the deed transfer may factor into your net proceeds.

Consulting a certified public accountant (CPA) or tax advisor ensures you understand liabilities and can plan for any tax payments related to your cash sale.
