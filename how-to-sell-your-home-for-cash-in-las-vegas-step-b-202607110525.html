Step 10: Tax Implications When Selling Your Home for Cash in Las Vegas

Many sellers overlook tax considerations when completing a cash sale, but it’s important to understand how the transaction might affect your tax situation:

  • Capital Gains Tax: If your home appreciated significantly, you may owe capital gains tax on the profit — unless you qualify for the home sale exclusion (up to $250,000 for single filers or $500,000 for married filing jointly).
  • Reporting Requirements: Cash sales must still be reported to the IRS using forms like 1099-S, which title companies usually handle during closing.
  • Potential Deductions: Selling as-is means you may not have deductible repair expenses, but closing costs and certain selling expenses may be deductible.
  • State Taxes: While Nevada has no state income tax, property taxes and other local real estate taxes still apply and should be considered.

Consulting a tax professional before finalizing your sale ensures you understand potential liabilities and opportunities to minimize taxes.
