Tax Implications of Selling Your Home for Cash in Parkland

Selling your property for cash has the same fundamental tax considerations as a traditional sale, but it’s helpful to understand them to avoid surprises.

  • Capital Gains Tax: If your home’s value has appreciated since purchase, you may owe capital gains tax on the profit. The IRS provides exemptions up to $250,000 for single filers and $500,000 for married couples if the home was your primary residence for at least two of the last five years.
  • Reporting the Sale: Cash sales must be reported on your tax return using IRS Form 8949 and Schedule D.
  • Property Taxes & Prorations: At closing, property taxes are typically prorated so you pay only for the period you owned the home.
  • Consult a Tax Advisor: Given the complexity of tax rules and individual circumstances, consulting a qualified tax professional before closing is recommended to optimize your tax outcome.
