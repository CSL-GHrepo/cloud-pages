What It Means to Sell Your House As-Is

Selling a house “as-is” means you offer your property in its current state, with no repairs or improvements made prior to sale. This approach is ideal if your home has:

  • Outdated interiors or appliances
  • Deferred maintenance or worn landscaping
  • Damage due to fire, water, or natural wear
