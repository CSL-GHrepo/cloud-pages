Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sell my house as-is to a cash home buyer even if it needs major repairs?

Yes. Cash home buyers specialize in purchasing properties in any condition, regardless of repairs needed. They factor repair costs into their cash offers, enabling a hassle-free sale. This means you don’t have to spend time or money on renovations.

How quickly can I sell my house as-is for cash?

The timeline varies but most cash home buyers can close within 7 to 14 days. This is significantly faster than traditional sales, which can take several months. Factors influencing speed include local laws, property title status, and buyer readiness.

Do I need a realtor to sell my house to a cash home buyer?

No, you don’t necessarily need a realtor. Many homeowners successfully sell directly to cash buyers, saving on realtor commissions and fees. However, having professional advice can be beneficial if you’re unfamiliar with the process.

Are cash offers for homes usually lower when selling as-is?

Typically, yes, cash offers reflect the cost buyers will incur to repair and resell the home. However, the tradeoff is a quick, guaranteed sale with no additional expenses or repairs required on your part.
