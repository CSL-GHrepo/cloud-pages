If your property has appreciated significantly since purchase, capital gains tax may apply once you sell—regardless of payment method. The good news is that Nevada has no state income tax, so federal regulations primarily govern capital gains.

  • Primary Residence Exclusion: If you’ve owned and lived in the home for at least two of the last five years, you may exclude up to $250,000 of gains ($500,000 for married couples filing jointly) from taxable income.
  • Investment Properties: Gains from non-primary residences are subject to capital gains tax without exclusions.

Cash sales in Las Vegas must resolve all existing mortgages or liens before or at closing. The sale proceeds typically satisfy these debts, but confirm detailed payoff amounts before acceptance.
