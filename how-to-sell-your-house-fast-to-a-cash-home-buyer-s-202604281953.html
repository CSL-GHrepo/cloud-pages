  2. Schedule an evaluation: Most cash buyers conduct a quick in-person or virtual walkthrough to assess the property’s condition.
  3. Receive your initial cash offer: Based on their evaluation, market data, and projected costs, cash buyers will present an offer—often within one or two business days.

Step 3: Review and Accept Your Cash Offer

Cash offers often differ from traditional sales in that they are firm and based on the home's current state. You should:

  • Compare the offer to your expected sale price: While cash offers usually are slightly below market value, the speed and convenience often outweigh this difference.
  • Ask questions: Clarify terms regarding closing costs, fees, and contingencies (typically minimal or none).
  • Make a decision: Once you accept, the buyer will typically begin preparing for a quick closing.
