Step 4: Close Quickly and Get Paid

Once you accept the offer, the buyer’s team handles title searches, paperwork, and closing arrangements, often coordinating directly with title companies or escrow agents. Here’s how the fast home sale process unfolds:

  1. Sign a purchase agreement: This legally binds the sale terms.
  2. Schedule closing date: Typically within 7-14 days, sometimes sooner when urgent.
  3. Final walkthrough (optional): Buyer confirms condition as agreed.
  4. Close the sale: Money transfers via cashier’s check or wire transfer.

Many cash real estate transactions skip the bank appraisal and mortgage contingencies, so closings happen much faster than traditional sales, which average 45-60 days (U.S. Housing Data Reports, 2023).

Remember: Once closed, you own no further obligations on the property, and the buyer assumes all responsibility.
