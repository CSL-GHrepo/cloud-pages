Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical timeline for selling a house to a cash home buyer?

Selling a house to a cash home buyer can take as little as one to two weeks. After contacting the buyer and providing your home details, expect an offer within 24-48 hours. Once accepted, closing can happen quickly because there’s no mortgage approval waiting period or extensive inspections. This fast house closing is one reason homeowners choose this method.

How does selling house for cash help avoid realtor fees?

When selling your home to a cash property buyer, you often transact directly with the buyer or their investment company, eliminating the need for listing agents. This means you avoid realtor commissions—which typically range between 5-6%—resulting in higher net proceeds and a simpler home selling process.

Are cash offers homes usually lower than market value?

Cash offers tend to be slightly below traditional market listing prices because cash_buyers factor in repair costs, holding costs, and quick turnaround benefits. However, sellers save money and time by avoiding agent fees, repair costs, and lengthy negotiations, balancing out lower offer prices with convenience.

Can I sell a home in any condition to instant home buyers?

Yes, one key advantage of instant home buyers or cash_home_buyers is they purchase properties “as-is.” You do not need to make repairs, update the home, or stage the property. This saves time and upfront costs and appeals to sellers looking for quick home sale tips that streamline the process.
