Quick Overview of the Copier Lease Termination Process

When you need to cancel a copier lease early, the key steps involve:

  • Reviewing your lease contract for early termination clauses
  • Understanding any early lease cancellation fees or penalties
  • Exploring buyout options or alternate solutions like lease transfers
  • Returning the equipment according to the lease’s early return policy

Each lease is unique, so a tailored approach is critical. Below, we break down how to end your copier lease agreement while managing costs and legal exposure.

Step 1: Review Your Copier Lease Agreement Thoroughly

The first and most crucial step in terminating commercial copier lease contracts early is carefully reviewing your agreement. Look specifically for:

  • Early termination clause: Defines conditions and penalties for ending the lease early.
  • Early return policy: The procedures and responsibilities if you return the copier before term.
  • Buyout options: Details regarding purchasing the equipment outright to end the lease.
  • Contract breach implications: What constitutes a breach and related liabilities.

According to industry data, nearly 65% of copier lease agreements contain early termination fees ranging from 25% to 50% of the remaining lease payments (Source: Copier Industry Financial Report, 2023).

Step 2: Contact Your Leasing Provider Early and Transparently

Once you've understood your lease terms, reach out to your leasing company as soon as you anticipate ending the lease. Proactive communication allows you to:

  • Discuss your reasons for cancellation (financial hardship, business downsizing, upgrades)
  • Request detailed fee schedules and cancellation policies
  • Explore buyout options or alternative resolutions
  • Negotiate potential fee reductions or payment plans

Industry professionals recommend approaching the leasing firm diplomatically, as cooperative negotiations often result in more favorable outcomes than unilateral contract breaches.

Step 3: Evaluate Copier Lease Buyout and Alternative Options

Terminating a lease isn’t always limited to paying hefty cancellation fees. Consider these alternatives:

  • Copier lease buyout: Purchasing the copier at a predetermined price can sometimes save money over continued payments or penalty fees.
  • Lease transfer: Subleasing or transferring the lease to another qualified party may be permitted, depending on your contract.
  • Equipment upgrade programs: Some leasing companies offer early upgrade options that reset your lease term or modify costs.
  • Early return policies: Returning equipment early under negotiated terms might reduce liabilities.

A survey of business customers found that 42% successfully negotiated buyout options to minimize early lease termination fees (Source: Business Equipment Leasing Association, 2022).

Step 4: Follow Proper Procedures to End the Copier Lease Agreement

Once clearance is obtained from the leasing company, take these key steps:

  1. Document your formal notification of intent to terminate or cancel the lease in writing, including dates and signatures.
  2. Arrange for inspection and return of the copier equipment per the lease’s early return policy.
  3. Settle any outstanding fees, damages, or penalties promptly to avoid further legal action.
  4. Obtain written confirmation from the leasing company that the lease has been successfully closed.

Doing so ensures you protect your credit and business reputation, reducing risk of contract breach disputes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are common early lease cancellation fees for copier leases?

Early lease cancellation fees typically range between 25% to 50% of the remaining lease payments, depending on the contract. These fees compensate the leasing company for lost revenue and administrative costs. Some leases may also include penalty interest or costs for damaged equipment.

Can I return my leased copier early without penalty?

Most commercial copier leases have an early return policy that specifies conditions for early return. While some leasing agreements allow early returns, usually they involve predetermined penalties or require you to pay the remaining lease balance. Negotiating with your leasing company beforehand may reduce these costs.

What does a copier lease buyout involve?

A copier lease buyout allows you to purchase the leased equipment, either at a predetermined price or fair market value, before the lease term ends. This option can help end the lease early while potentially saving money compared to paying cancellation fees. Always compare buyout costs to remaining lease payments before deciding.

Is it possible to transfer my copier lease to someone else?

Many copier leases permit lease transfers or subleases to another business or qualified party. This option helps avoid early termination fees but requires leasing company approval and screening of the new lessee. It’s an effective alternative if you no longer need the equipment but still want to honor your contract.
