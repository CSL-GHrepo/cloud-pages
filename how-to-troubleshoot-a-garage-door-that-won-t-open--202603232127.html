Key Industry Terms Every Parkland Homeowner Should Know

Spring tension: The force exerted by garage door springs that balances the weight of the door. Proper tension is vital for smooth operation and safety.

Photo-eye sensors: Devices that detect obstructions in the garage door’s path. They help prevent accidents by reversing the door when something is underneath.

Track alignment: The correct positioning of the tracks guiding the door's movement. Misaligned tracks can cause noisy operation or derailment.

Insulation R-value: Indicates the thermal resistance of an insulated garage door. Higher values mean better insulation and energy savings.

Torsion springs: Springs mounted above the door that wind and unwind to assist in lifting the door. They require professional handling due to high tension.
