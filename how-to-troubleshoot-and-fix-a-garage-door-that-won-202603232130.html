Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Parkland Residents

Routine maintenance is the best way to prolong your garage door’s lifespan and avoid costly repairs. Simple tasks like lubricating moving parts, tightening hardware, and cleaning tracks can make a big difference. Our team also recommends scheduling professional inspections annually to identify potential issues early.

Here’s the thing—dirt and debris can build up in tracks, causing misalignment or jamming. Regular cleaning and lubrication help prevent these problems. Use a silicone-based lubricant on rollers, hinges, and springs for optimal performance.

Checking balance is a key maintenance step. Disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door can reveal if springs are functioning properly. If the door feels heavy or doesn’t stay open, call us for a spring assessment.

Inspecting safety features like sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms is crucial. Faulty safety features can pose hazards or prevent your garage door from operating correctly.
