Step 1: Start With Your Financial Basics

Before diving into any calculator, gather the following information:

  • Gross monthly income: Include salary, bonuses, and other income sources.
  • Monthly debt payments: Think car loans, credit cards, student loans.
  • Down payment savings: The amount you can contribute upfront.
  • Credit score insight: To estimate mortgage rates.

Industry professionals recommend having a debt-to-income ratio (DTI) below 43% for mortgage approval, though aiming for under 36% offers more financial flexibility.

According to the National Association of Realtors, “Buyers with a DTI under 36% have a significantly higher chance of loan approval and smoother home buying experience.” (NAR, 2023)
