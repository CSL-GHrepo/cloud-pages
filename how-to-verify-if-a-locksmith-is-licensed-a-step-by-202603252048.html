Key Industry Terms You Should Know

Rekeying: Changing the internal pins of a lock so old keys no longer work, providing a quick and affordable security upgrade without replacing the entire lock.

Master key system: A setup that allows one key to open multiple locks, commonly used in commercial properties for easier access management.

High-security locks: Locks designed with advanced features like drill resistance and pick resistance, offering increased protection for homes and businesses.

Electronic locks: Locks operated with digital keypads, card readers, or biometric scanners, providing keyless entry and remote control options.

Lock bumping: A technique that uses a specially crafted bump key to open pin tumbler locks, highlighting the importance of choosing bump-resistant locks.
