  • Check with the Florida DBPR: Licensed pool contractors must be registered with the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation. Visit their online license verification tool and enter the company or technician’s name to confirm license status.
  • Request Proof of License: A reputable company should readily provide copies of their pool service company licensing and individual technician licenses.
  • Confirm Licensing Type: Ensure the license covers pool cleaning, maintenance, and repairs specific to your needs.

How to Verify Pool Service Insurance in Lauderdale Lakes

Insurance protects you and the pool service company from financial liability if accidents or damages occur.

  • Ask for Certificates of Insurance (COI): Request a current COI that clearly lists the insurance company, coverage limits, and effective dates.
  • Confirm General Liability Insurance: This insurance covers property damage or bodily injury related to pool work.
  • Check for Worker’s Compensation Insurance: This protects you from liability if a worker is injured on your property.
  • Contact the Insurance Provider: Call the insurer directly to verify the policy is active and covers the services promised.
