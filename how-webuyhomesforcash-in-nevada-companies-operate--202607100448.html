  • Consulting Tax Professionals: Before finalizing a cash sale, sellers should consult accountants or tax advisors to understand potential liabilities and plan accordingly.

One of the financial benefits of cash sales is avoiding agent commissions, which typically range from 5% to 6% of the sale price in Las Vegas.
