Step 1: Ensure Safety and Prevent Further Damage

Your first priority after water damage is personal safety:

  • Turn Off Electricity: Water and electricity are a dangerous combination; de-energize your property at the main circuit breaker before entering flooded areas — unless unsafe to do so.
  • Stop the Water Source: If a burst pipe or appliance failure causes flooding, shut off the water supply immediately to minimize the volume of water entering your property.
  • Protect Yourself: Avoid contact with contaminated floodwaters which may contain bacteria or chemicals common in storm runoff around Bonita Springs.

According to the EPA, prompt action within the first 24 hours can significantly reduce the extent of water damage and prevent health hazards.

Step 3: Begin Mold Prevention After Water Damage

Mold can start to develop within 24 to 48 hours after water damage if moisture isn’t eliminated promptly. Alongside professional drying and dehumidification, you can take these initial steps:

  • Remove wet materials like carpets, upholstery, and drywall if safe to do so.
  • Improve ventilation by opening windows and running fans (only if there is no ongoing water intrusion).
  • Avoid using fans or blowers directed at wet drywall or wood to prevent spore spread until professionals assess the situation.
  • Document damaged property with photos to support insurance claims and restoration plans.

Industry studies show that homes with proper drying and mold prevention experience 25-30% fewer secondary damages during flood cleanup.
