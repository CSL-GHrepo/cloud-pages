Common Issues Encountered in Impact Window Repairs

Impact windows, while robust, face particular challenges that frequently result in repair needs. Understanding these common problems can help homeowners recognize early signs and address issues before they worsen.

Seal Failure and Moisture Intrusion

One of the most recurring issues with impact windows is the failure of the seal around the glass panes. The dual-pane construction of impact windows uses a sealed air space to provide insulation and energy efficiency. Over time, exposure to UV rays, temperature fluctuations, and salt air (especially in coastal areas) can degrade sealants.

  • Signs of seal failure include visible condensation between panes and fogging.
  • If left untreated, moisture intrusion can lead to mold growth, frame rot, and decreased insulation performance.
  • Seal repair or sealant replacement costs vary but often range from $150 to $300 per window depending on size.

Glass Damage from Impact and Weather

Although designed to withstand flying debris, impact windows aren’t invincible. Storms, hail, or accidental impacts can crack or shatter glass.

  • Partial pane cracks can sometimes be repaired with resin injections, but most glass damage requires full pane replacement.
  • Specialty laminated glass used in Florida impact windows necessitates ordered replacements, which increases lead time and cost.
  • Replacing the glass alone will generally be less expensive than entire window replacements, though prices vary based on glass thickness and coatings.

Frame Corrosion and Structural Deformities

Frames constructed of aluminum or metal alloys are vulnerable to corrosion caused by salt-laden air and water intrusion. Wood frames can also suffer moisture damage leading to warping or deterioration.

  • Corroded frames can compromise window seal integrity and security.
  • Repairs can include sanding, repainting, rust treatment, or in severe cases, partial frame replacement.
  • Cost ranges for frame repair typically fall between $200 to $600 based on damage extent and materials.

Hardware Malfunctions

Locks, hinges, and other operating mechanisms may wear out over time, especially in humid Florida climates. Hardware failure can compromise window security and functionality.

  • Replacing locks or hinges is generally straightforward but must comply with impact window standards.
  • High-quality, hurricane-rated hardware replacements could add $50-$200 per window depending on complexity.
