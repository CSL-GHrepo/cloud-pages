Impact Window Replacement vs. Repair in Tradition, FL: What’s More Cost-Effective?

In the face of hurricane damage or wear and tear, many homeowners wonder if they should repair or replace their impact windows. Here is how to evaluate:

  • Repair: Ideal for cracks, chips, or minor damage where the window frame and seals remain intact. Repairing is less disruptive and usually more affordable upfront.
  • Replacement: Necessary when the structure is compromised or the window has multiple cracks affecting its impact resistance. This option comes with higher initial costs but guarantees restored protection.
