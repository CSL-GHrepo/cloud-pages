Quick Overview: What Is Impact Window Repair?

Impact window repair is a specialized service focused on fixing damage to impact-resistant glass, which is engineered to withstand strong winds, flying debris, and other hurricane-related threats common in Florida’s coastal regions. Unlike standard window repair, this process involves handling laminated glass and reinforced frames to ensure full structural recovery and compliance with Florida codes.

According to the Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety (IBHS), homes with impact-resistant windows reduce the likelihood of hurricane-related damage by up to 42%, making repairs essential to maintain protective benefits.
