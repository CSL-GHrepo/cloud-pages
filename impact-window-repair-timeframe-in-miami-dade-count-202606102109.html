  • Permit and Inspection Requirements: Miami-Dade enforces strict building codes for hurricane-resistant windows, which often necessitate permitting and inspection stages affecting repair duration.
  • Contractor Availability: Scheduling with qualified impact window contractors can be challenging during hurricane season and following major storm events.
  • Supply Chain Factors: Replacement glass or hardware may have lead times, especially for custom or wind-resistant window products.
