When evaluating your impact window warranty in Brevard County, here are the major components typically covered:

What Isn’t Covered by Typical Impact Window Repair Warranties?

While warranties provide strong protection, understanding typical exclusions is equally vital. Common non-covered issues include:

  • Damage from improper use or accidents: For example, scratches or cracks due to homeowner negligence.
  • Wear and tear: Normal aging, fading, or minor blemishes over time generally don’t qualify.
  • Acts of God beyond design specs: Extremely rare or catastrophic events exceeding building code assumptions may not be covered.

Review your specific policy and discuss exceptions with your repair provider to avoid surprises.
