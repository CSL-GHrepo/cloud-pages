Start by cleaning your locks periodically with a gentle spray of lubricant to keep internal mechanisms moving smoothly. Next, inspect your locks and keys for signs of wear or damage. If your key becomes difficult to turn or shows signs of bending, it’s time to get a duplicate or replacement.
