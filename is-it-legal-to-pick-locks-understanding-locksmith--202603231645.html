Essential Locksmith Terminology for Parkland Homeowners

Understanding key terms can help you communicate effectively with your locksmith. For example, ‘deadbolt’ refers to a lockbolt that extends into the doorframe for added security, often used on exterior doors.

‘Rekeying’ involves changing the internal pins of a lock to operate with a new key without replacing the entire lock. This is a cost-effective way to restrict access if keys are lost or stolen.

‘High-security locks’ are designed with features like drill resistance, pick resistance, and restricted keyways, making tampering more difficult. They provide enhanced protection against break-ins.

‘Access control systems’ regulate who can enter your property through electronic locks, keycards, or biometric authentication. These are commonly used in commercial settings but also suitable for homes.

‘Lockout’ refers to being unable to access your property due to lost keys or malfunctioning locks. Quick response locksmiths can resolve this with minimal damage or inconvenience.
