Understanding Copier Lease Maintenance Coverage

Copier lease maintenance coverage generally consists of:

  • Preventive maintenance: Routine service visits to clean, calibrate, and check machinery to avoid breakdowns.
  • Repair services: Fixing or replacing parts due to normal wear or technical failure without extra charges.
  • Consumables management: Supplying toner, drums, and occasionally paper, depending on the agreement.

However, the scope of this coverage depends on your copier leasing contract. Some agreements offer full-service maintenance with no hidden fees, while others may only cover basic repairs with additional printer maintenance fees billed separately.

What is Usually Not Covered by Copier Lease Maintenance?

While many issues are covered, some copier lease maintenance coverage exclusions are common:

  • Damage from misuse or accidents: Repairs needed due to intentional or accidental damage may not be included.
  • Consumables not listed in agreement: Some leases charge separately for staples, specialty paper, or extended warranty parts.
  • Upgrades or feature additions: New software packages or hardware enhancements are typically outside maintenance scope.

Reviewing copier lease terms and conditions will clarify these limitations upfront, avoiding surprises.
