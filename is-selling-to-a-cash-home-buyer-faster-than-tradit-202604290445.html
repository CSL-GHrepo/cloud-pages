How the Home Selling Process Differs with Cash Home Buyers

Traditional home sales involve multiple parties, including lenders, inspectors, appraisers, and real estate agents. Each stage can introduce delays—for example:

  • Mortgage approval: Buyers often face weeks-long approval processes.
  • Appraisals and inspections: Required to satisfy lenders and buyers, causing renegotiations or repair demands.
  • Contingencies: Buyers can back out if inspections reveal issues or financing falls through.
  • Agent commissions and marketing: Selling through an agent can add 6% in fees and require significant listing time.

By contrast, cash property buyers are often real estate investors who pay upfront without contingencies or the need for financing. They tend to purchase properties in their current condition, which means:

  • They buy as-is, so you don’t have to invest in repairs or upgrades.
  • There are no agent commissions, resulting in a no commission home sales process.
  • Closing can occur quickly because there’s no lender or buyer approval needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much faster is selling to cash property buyers compared to traditional sales?

Selling to cash property buyers can close in as little as 7 to 14 days, while traditional sales often take 30 to 60 days or more due to financing, inspections, and contingencies. This significant difference is largely because cash buyers pay outright and buy the home as-is with fewer hurdles.

Yes. One of the primary benefits of selling to a cash home buyer is the ability to sell house as-is. Cash buyers, often real estate investors, purchase properties regardless of condition, allowing sellers to avoid costly repairs and expedite the sale process.

Are there any commission fees when I sell to a cash home buyer?

Typically, no. Most cash home buyers complete no commission home sales because they buy directly from homeowners without involving real estate agents, which saves sellers from paying the usual 5-6% agent commissions common in traditional sales.
