Frequently Asked Questions

What are the primary copier leasing benefits compared to buying?

Copier leasing benefits include lower upfront costs, predictable monthly payments, bundled maintenance, easier equipment upgrades, and often tax-deductible lease payments. These advantages help businesses reduce both unexpected expenses and long-term copier ownership costs.

How does lease copier tax advantages work for businesses?

Lease payments are usually fully deductible as ordinary business expenses under IRS guidelines, reducing taxable income. This differs from buying, where depreciation schedules apply. Are there any hidden costs involved in buying a copier?

Yes, buying a copier can involve hidden costs such as maintenance fees, toner and parts replacement, software updates, potential financing interest, and eventual resale or disposal expenses. These long-term copier expenses can add up and exceed initial purchase prices.
