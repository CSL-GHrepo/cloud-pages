Quick Financial Overview: Leasing Copiers vs Buying Refurbished Copiers

When comparing leasing copiers to buying refurbished copiers outright, leasing often requires less upfront capital and includes maintenance, while purchasing refurbished copiers offers cost savings on acquisition but may incur higher ongoing expenses. According to a 2023 industry study by IDC, companies leasing office equipment experience up to 30% lower total cost of ownership over five years due to bundled services and predictable payments.

The Benefits of Leasing Copiers: More Than Just Monthly Payments

Leasing copiers can often appear costly compared to buying refurbished units, but the benefits extend well beyond the initial financials. Here are key advantages:

  • Lower Upfront Costs: Leasing eliminates the need for heavy capital outlays, freeing up funds for other business priorities.
  • Included Maintenance and Support: Many leasing contracts cover copier maintenance costs, toner replacements, and repairs, reducing unexpected expenses.
  • Technology Upgrades: Lease terms for copiers, typically lasting 2-4 years, allow businesses to upgrade to newer models more easily without reselling used equipment.
  • Tax Advantages: Lease payments are often fully deductible as a business expense, providing potential tax benefits.
  • Predictable Budgeting: Fixed monthly payments help organizations manage budgets without worrying about sudden equipment failures.

For example, a mid-sized marketing firm in Chicago shifted to refurbished copier leasing and saved 25% in comparative costs over 3 years—primarily due to avoided maintenance and downtime.

Buying Refurbished Copiers: The Cost Savings and Pitfalls

Investing in a refurbished copier can offer notable cost savings on your initial purchase price—often 40-60% less than new machines. However, the decision entails its own considerations:

  • Lower Capital Requirement: Paying upfront for a used copier avoids ongoing lease payments and interest.
  • Immediate Ownership: Owning the equipment offers freedom from contract terms and usage limits.
  • Refurbished Copier Warranty: Reputable sellers provide warranties that guarantee machine condition for at least 6 to 12 months.
  • Potential Higher Maintenance Costs: Unlike leasing, maintenance costs—especially for older models—fall solely on the buyer, which can be substantial.
  • Technology Obsolescence: Older machines may lack current features affecting efficiency and compatibility with new software.

According to the EPA, businesses using well-maintained refurbished equipment can reduce equipment waste by 70%, though the potential increase in copier maintenance costs must be weighed carefully.

Comparing Copier Maintenance Costs: Leasing vs Buying Refurbished

Maintenance represents a critical factor in the leasing vs buying refurbished copiers debate. Data from the Equipment Leasing Association indicates that maintenance can account for up to 25% of total copier-related expenses over the machine’s lifecycle.

  • Leasing: Most leasing agreements bundle maintenance within payments, eliminating surprise repair bills and toner costs.
  • Buying Refurbished: Buyers typically pay for repairs and consumables out of pocket, which can escalate especially as the device ages.

In real-world terms, a law office leasing refurbished copiers noticed an annual $500 savings when factoring bundled maintenance vs its previous ownership model, where one major repair averaged $1,200.

Used Copier Financing Options: Bridging the Gap

For businesses preferring to buy refurbished machines but constrained by capital, used copier financing options can be a middle ground. Financing plans allow you to spread out payments while owning the asset, yet often lack the maintenance and upgrade perks of leasing.

It's important to review financing terms carefully—interest rates, payment schedules, and any hidden fees can affect the overall saving potential. In some cases, leasing proves more financially prudent than financing for ownership.

Making the Right Choice: Factors to Consider

  • Cash Flow: Does your business prefer predictable monthly expenses or can it accommodate upfront capital expenditure?
  • Usage Volume: High volume copy needs may benefit from leasing plans with included maintenance.
  • Upgrade Preference: If your business desires the latest technology regularly, leasing terms for copiers provide a smoother path.
  • Maintenance Risk Tolerance: Buying refurbished means assuming responsibility for maintenance—evaluate your capacity accordingly.
  • Contract Flexibility: Leasing agreements have defined terms; buying provides full control but requires managing resale or disposal.

Ultimately, the answer depends on your operational priorities. Companies with limited upfront budgets and a desire for hassle-free servicing often find that refurbished copier leasing gives the best blend of cost savings and reliability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main benefits of refurbished copier leasing compared to buying outright?

Refurbished copier leasing offers lower upfront costs, bundled maintenance that reduces unexpected repair expenses, and flexible lease terms that allow for easier upgrades to newer models. In contrast, buying outright demands a significant initial investment and places full responsibility for repairs and ongoing maintenance on the buyer.

How do lease terms for copiers affect overall cost savings?

Lease terms typically range from 24 to 48 months and include fixed monthly payments covering maintenance and supplies. This predictability can lead to total cost savings of up to 30% compared to owning and maintaining a machine independently, as it avoids large unexpected repair costs and obsolescence risks.

Can buying a refurbished copier save money long-term?

While buying refurbished copiers saves money upfront, maintenance costs often increase as the device ages. Without a warranty or maintenance plan, these costs can reduce overall savings. Businesses with in-house technical support may benefit more from buying, while others might find leasing more economical.

Are there used copier financing options that combine benefits of both leasing and buying?

Yes, used copier financing allows businesses to purchase refurbished equipment through installment payments, blending lower upfront costs with eventual ownership. However, unlike leasing, financing often excludes maintenance services, so buyers must budget separately for upkeep and repairs.
