Impact of Federal Regulations on Local Hard Money Lending Practices

Though the focus is often on state and local laws, federal regulations play an important role in shaping the hard money lending landscape in Lamoille, NV. Understanding how these overlays interact with Nevada statutes is critical for comprehensive compliance.

Truth in Lending Act (TILA) and Hard Money Loans

Federal TILA requirements demand clear disclosure of annual percentage rates (APRs), loan fees, and terms, regardless of the loan type. Hard money lenders operating in Lamoille must ensure disclosures meet these federal standards, adding a layer of transparency beyond state law.

Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA)

Although many hard money loans are exempt from RESPA, lenders must still verify potential applicability, especially on loans involving owner-occupied properties or certain loan servicing arrangements. Missteps could result in penalties or rescission rights for borrowers.

Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance

Licensed hard money lenders in Nevada are subject to BSA/AML reporting obligations designed to prevent financial crimes. This includes suspicious activity monitoring and record-keeping, ensuring that hard money financing is not exploited for illicit purposes.
