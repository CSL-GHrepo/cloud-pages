Expert Tips for Maintaining Your Locks

Regular maintenance is key to ensuring your locks provide reliable security. In my experience, many issues stem from neglect, such as dirt buildup, rust, or misalignment. Simple tips can prolong the life of your hardware and prevent costly repairs.

One useful tip is to lubricate locks periodically with graphite or silicone spray, especially in Florida’s humid climate. This prevents sticking and corrosion. Also, check for signs of wear or damage, and replace worn components promptly.

It's beneficial to have a professional inspect your locks annually, especially for high-security setups or commercial properties. They can identify vulnerabilities and recommend upgrades if needed.

Be cautious about DIY repairs unless you are experienced, as improper handling can cause further damage. Always consult a licensed locksmith for complex issues or lock replacements.

Finally, keep spare keys in a safe place and avoid hiding keys in obvious spots. A well-maintained lock is your first line of defense in home and business security.
