Understanding Locksmith Terminology in Parkland

Before calling a locksmith, it’s helpful to know some common terms. ‘Rekeying’ means changing the internal pins of a lock so a new key operates it, without replacing the entire hardware. I often explain this to residents in Parkland as a cost-effective way to secure a property after losing keys.

‘High-security locks’ are designed with complex keyways and reinforced components, making them more resistant to picking or forced entry. Many local businesses and homeowners are upgrading to these types of locks for added protection. Understanding lock ‘bumping’ is also important—it's a method used by intruders to quickly open standard locks, which is why high-security options are recommended.

When discussing electronic security, terms like ‘smart locks’ and ‘access control systems’ come up frequently. These allow keyless entry via smartphones or coded keypads, providing convenience and enhanced security. Knowing these terms helps you make informed decisions about your property’s safety.

Familiarity with locksmith jargon can save you time and money, ensuring you get exactly what you need. Our team is happy to explain these terms and help you understand your options when you contact us.
