Industry Terminology Explained for Parkland Customers

Understanding key industry terms helps you make informed decisions about your security. Here are some common locksmith concepts explained simply:

Rekeying: The process of altering a lock so a new key works, rendering old keys useless. It’s a quick way to enhance security after losing keys or moving in.

Master key system: A setup where a single key grants access to multiple locks, often used in commercial settings to simplify security management.

High-security locks: Locks designed with advanced features like drill resistance and key control, providing enhanced protection against intruders.

Access control: Electronic systems that regulate who can enter or exit a property, often using keycards, biometrics, or codes. They improve security and convenience.
