Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do first if I am locked out of my house?

First, remain calm and check for any possible unlocked entrances. If none are available, avoid attempting forced entry that can damage locks or doors. Contact a professional locksmith offering emergency locksmith services and 24/7 lockout assistance. They use specialized tools to unlock your doors safely and often can rekey your locks quickly if you worry about lost keys.

Can a locksmith help if my car keys are inside the vehicle?

Yes. Automotive locksmith experts have specialized tools to open locked cars without damaging them. They also provide car key replacement services, including programming transponder keys or key fobs on-site, making them your best resource for vehicle lockouts.

How long does it take for a locksmith to respond to a lockout?

Response times vary by location and availability but most mobile locksmith services arrive within 20-30 minutes in urban or suburban areas. Emergency locksmith services aim to provide fast lock opening, usually completing the job in under an hour from arrival.

What is fast lock rekeying and when should I consider it?

Fast lock rekeying involves changing the internal pins of a lock so old keys no longer work, without replacing the entire lock. It is a quick, affordable way to secure your home after lost keys or when moving into a new residence. Many locksmiths offer this as part of house lockout solutions to enhance security immediately following a lockout.
