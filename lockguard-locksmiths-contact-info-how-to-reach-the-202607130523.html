What is a Master Key System?

A master key system uses a hierarchy of keys allowing different levels of access within a building. For example, a tenant key will open their apartment but not common areas or other apartments. A manager’s master key can open all doors, while a super master key gives access to multiple buildings.

Benefits of Master Key Systems

  • Enhanced Security: Limits access to authorized areas only, preventing unauthorized entry.
  • Cost Savings: Reduces the number of keys that must be issued, managed, and replaced.
  • Emergency Control: Allows quick access for emergency personnel or maintenance without compromising tenant privacy.

Lockguard’s experts analyze your facility’s layout and access needs, then customize and install a master key system that balances convenience with stringent security standards.
