DIY Tips for Basic Lock Care

While some lock maintenance requires professional expertise, homeowners and business managers can perform simple yet effective care measures, such as:

  • Applying graphite-based lubricant (never oil) to keyholes to keep pins moving smoothly.
  • Keeping keys clean and free from grime to avoid transferring dirt into locks.
  • Checking door alignment regularly to prevent strain on locks and mechanisms.
  • Ensuring that outdoor locks have weather-resistant covers to guard against rain, dust, and insects.

However, if any issues are noticed during routine home or business inspections, contacting Lockguard Locksmiths promptly prevents escalation.
