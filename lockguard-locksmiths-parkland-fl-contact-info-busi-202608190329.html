Understanding the Importance of Regular Lock Maintenance

  • Increased difficulty turning keys or deadbolts
  • Keys sticking or jamming during operation
  • Visible rust, wear, or damage on locks or cylinders
  • Loose door hardware or misaligned locks
  • Frequent lockouts caused by mechanical failure
