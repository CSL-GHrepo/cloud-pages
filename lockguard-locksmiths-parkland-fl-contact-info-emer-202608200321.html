How Lockguard Locksmiths Handles Rekeying and Lock Replacement

Rekeying and lock replacement are two fundamental locksmith services that are often misconceived as interchangeable. Lockguard Locksmiths educates and assists customers in choosing the appropriate option based on their specific needs.

Rekeying Explained

Rekeying entails changing the internal pins of an existing lock so that old keys no longer operate it. This service is cost-effective and fast, ideal for situations such as moving into a new home or when keys are lost but locks are still in good condition.

  • Advantages of Rekeying: Saves money by avoiding new locks; maintains your current hardware and aesthetic.
  • Typical Situations for Rekeying: After tenant turnover in rental properties, lost or stolen keys, or changes in trusted personnel at commercial properties.

Lock Replacement and Upgrade

In contrast, lock replacement involves removing the entire lock and installing a new one. Lockguard Locksmiths recommends replacement when locks are old, damaged, or no longer meet security standards.

  • Advantages of Replacement: Improved security with newer, tougher locks; opportunity to upgrade to smart or high-security models.
  • Typical Situations for Replacement: Broken or malfunctioning hardware, outdated lock models vulnerable to picking or bumping, or when integrating with advanced home or business security systems.

Lockguard Locksmiths’ professionals conduct thorough assessments to advise clients and ensure cost-effective, security-focused solutions.

Understanding Key Types and Their Uses: From Traditional to High-Tech

Lockguard Locksmiths works with a broad spectrum of key types, each suited for different security applications. Knowledge of these key varieties assists customers in choosing the right solution for their property.

  • Traditional Mechanical Keys: These include standard pin tumbler keys, primarily used in residential and some commercial settings. They provide basic security but are vulnerable to lock picking and unauthorized duplication without control.
  • High-Security Keys: Featuring patented designs and restricted keyways, these keys require special authorization for duplication. Lockguard installs systems with these keys to increase protection against unauthorized copies.
  • Transponder and Smart Car Keys: Automotive locksmith services often involve these electronic keys that communicate with a vehicle’s computer system to start the engine. Loss or malfunction requires specialist programming and key cutting, skillfully handled by Lockguard’s automotive team.
  • Smart Keys and Keypads: Keyless entry devices provide convenience and advanced control. Lockguard recommends and installs keypad locks or key fobs, especially for commercial properties needing multiple user access without physical keys.

Educating clients about these key types enables Lockguard Locksmiths to tailor security upgrades optimally, balancing convenience and protection.
