Tips for Maintaining Your Locks and Enhancing Security

While professional services are critical, everyday care and proactive measures can greatly extend the life of locks and reduce risks. Lockguard Locksmiths recommends:

  • Lubricate Locks Regularly: Use graphite or silicone-based lubricants (avoid oil-based products) to maintain smooth operation.
  • Inspect for Wear and Damage: Check keys and locks for signs of damage or unusual stiffness and contact a locksmith if issues arise.
  • Secure Spare Keys: Avoid hiding spare keys outside; instead, entrust them to a trusted neighbor or use a secure lockbox with limited access.
  • Update Security After Moving: Rekey or replace locks when moving into a new home or leasing a new office space.
  • Stay Informed About Security Trends: Consider consulting with locksmith professionals regularly to evaluate potential vulnerabilities and new technology upgrades.
