Tips for Maintaining Your Locks and Security Systems Year-Round

Regular maintenance of locks and security components is essential for ensuring long-term safety and preventing unexpected failures. Lockguard Locksmiths recommends the following practical steps for homeowners and business managers in Parkland:

  • Lubricate locks biannually with graphite or silicone-based lubricants to prevent corrosion, particularly important due to South Florida’s humid climate.
  • Inspect electronic locks and access control panels monthly for battery life, firmware updates, and physical damage.
  • Test emergency exit devices and panic bars quarterly to ensure smooth operation during potential fire or security emergencies.
  • Replace weather seals and door sweeps annually to protect locks from moisture intrusion and rust.
  • Schedule a professional security system audit once per year to identify vulnerabilities and implement upgrades as needed.

Following these guidelines not only prolongs equipment lifespan but also helps avoid costly emergency repairs and potential security breaches.
