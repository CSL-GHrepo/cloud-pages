Tips for Preventing Lockouts and Enhancing Everyday Security

While Lockguard Locksmiths offer rapid emergency response services, prevention remains a key component of effective security. Here are actionable tips recommended by their technicians to help Parkland customers avoid lockouts and bolster security:

  • Establish a Spare Key Strategy: Store spare keys with trusted neighbors, family members, or use secure key safes installed discreetly outside the home.
  • Regular Lock Maintenance: Clean and lubricate locks at least bi-annually to prevent jams and wear that can cause accidental lockouts.
  • Adopt Keyless Entry Systems: Consider smart locks or keypad entries that reduce dependence on physical keys and allow remote access control.
  • Routine Checks of Vehicle Keys and Remotes: Replace worn key fobs or batteries regularly to avoid unexpected vehicular lockouts.
  • Utilize Master Key Systems for Multiple Occupants: Reduces the number of keys needed, lowering the chance of losing specific keys and simplifying access management.
