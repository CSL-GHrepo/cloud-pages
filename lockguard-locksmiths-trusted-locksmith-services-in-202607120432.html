Advanced Rekeying Services: Enhancing Security Without Full Replacement

One of Lockguard Locksmiths’ most valued services is advanced rekeying. This cost-effective solution is often overlooked, yet it delivers substantial security improvements, especially in transitional situations such as moving into a new home or after employee turnover in businesses.

What is Rekeying?

Rekeying involves altering the internal mechanism of an existing lock so that old keys no longer operate it, while new keys are provided. This avoids the expense and hassle of replacing the entire lock system while maintaining protection against unauthorized key holders.

Benefits of Professional Rekeying Services

  • Cost Efficiency: Rekeying is significantly less expensive than full lock replacement and upgrades.
  • Speed: Lockguard Locksmiths technicians can perform rekeying quickly, minimizing disruption to your day.
  • Key Control: Systems such as master key configurations can be established or modified, improving access management.
  • Security Assurance: Rekeying ensures previous key holders, such as former tenants or ex-employees, can no longer gain entry.

Lockguard Locksmiths also offers key control audits and systems reviews to help customers manage and track key distribution effectively.

When to Consider Rekeying

  • After purchasing or renting a new property.
  • Following lost or stolen keys.
  • Post-employee termination or staff reorganization.
  • When upgrading to higher-security lock hardware that supports rekeying.
  • During scheduled maintenance to update or simplify key systems.
