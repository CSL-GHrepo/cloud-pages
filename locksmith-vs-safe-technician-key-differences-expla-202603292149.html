Learning About Lock Systems and Industry Standards

Understanding lock systems helps you make better security choices. A lock’s quality often depends on its manufacturer and the standards it meets. Locks certified by organizations like the American National Standards Institute (ANSI) provide reliable security and durability.

In Parkland, we recommend installing locks that meet or exceed ANSI Grade 1 standards for residential and commercial properties. These locks are tested for strength against forced entry and wear over time. Knowing the difference between mechanical locks and electronic systems can also impact your security posture.

We also educate our clients on lock rekeying — changing the internal pins so the old keys no longer work. This service is cost-effective when replacing keys after moving into a new home or losing keys. It’s faster and cheaper than replacing the entire lock, especially for high-traffic properties.
