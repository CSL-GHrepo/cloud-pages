Defining Locksmith Terminology

A **deadbolt** is a lockbolt that extends into the door frame, providing extra security compared to standard spring latch locks. It’s common in residential and commercial doors.

**Rekeying** involves changing the internal pins of a lock so it works with a new key, without replacing the entire lock. It’s a cost-effective security measure after moving or loss.

A **smart lock** uses electronic technology to enable keyless entry, often controlled through smartphones or keypads. It offers convenience and enhanced security.

**Bypass locks** are designed to be opened without a key, often used by locksmiths or emergency responders. They can involve tools to manipulate the lock mechanism.

**Access control systems** regulate who can enter a property with electronic authentication, such as keycards, codes, or biometric scans, ideal for businesses.
