Maintaining Your Garage Door to Last Longer

Proper maintenance is key to extending the life of your garage door in Parkland. Regular inspections and small adjustments can prevent costly repairs and ensure safety. In my experience, a little effort goes a long way in keeping everything running smoothly.

Start with visual checks—look for signs of rust, wear, or misalignment. Lubricate moving parts like rollers, hinges, and tracks at least twice a year to reduce friction and noise. Use grease or silicone-based lubricants recommended for garage doors.

Test the safety features, such as auto-reverse sensors, monthly. These systems are crucial for family safety and must function correctly at all times. Adjust or replace sensors if they are dirty or misaligned.

Keep the tracks clean and free of debris, which can cause sticking or uneven movement. Check the balance of the door by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting it—if it doesn’t stay open or feels heavy, call us for adjustments.

Regular professional inspections can detect potential issues early. Our recommended schedule is twice a year, especially after storm seasons or severe weather events. This proactive approach saves money and ensures peace of mind.
