Traditional Financing: Conventional Loans and Bank Partnerships

Many multifamily investors in Temple pursue conventional loans through local or regional banks. These loans commonly require:

  • 20% to 30% down payment depending on property size and financial profile.
  • Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) of 1.2 or higher—a metric reflecting property's ability to cover debt through net operating income.
  • Clean property titles and compliance with local ordinances.

Auten Realty maintains strong relationships with several Texas-based lenders experienced in multifamily portfolios, easing the approval process for qualified buyers.

For specific property types and investor profiles, government-backed loans may provide affordable financing. Key programs include:

Understanding Temple’s Zoning Ordinances

Multifamily properties in Temple are generally governed under specific zoning districts such as Multi-Family Residential (MF) zones. Key zoning considerations include:

  • Density limits—maximum number of units per acre.
  • Height restrictions—limiting building stories depending on neighborhood context.
  • Setback requirements—distance buildings must maintain from property lines and streets.
  • Parking mandates—number of spaces per unit or per bedroom.
