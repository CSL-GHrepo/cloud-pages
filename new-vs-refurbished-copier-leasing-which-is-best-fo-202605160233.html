Quick Answer: Leasing a New Copier vs Leasing a Refurbished Copier

Leasing new copiers is ideal for companies aiming to leverage the latest technology and minimize downtime with strong manufacturer warranties. Refurbished copiers, on the other hand, present an affordable copier leasing alternative that reduces upfront costs while supporting sustainability goals. Ultimately, your choice should weigh budget constraints against operational priorities.

Understanding the Cost Differences: New vs Used Copier Costs

One of the most influential factors driving the decision between new and refurbished copier leasing is cost. The upfront lease rates, maintenance fees, and expected longevity impact your total cost of ownership.

  • New Copier Leasing Costs: Typically, new copiers command higher monthly lease payments due to advanced features and warranties. According to an industry survey by InfoTrends, new copier leases can cost 20-40% more monthly than refurbished units.
  • Refurbished Copier Benefits: Refurbished copiers can reduce lease payments by up to 50%, offering a budget-friendly option especially for small to mid-sized businesses. These units are professionally reconditioned to meet manufacturer standards, ensuring dependable performance.
"Businesses looking to balance affordability with reliability often find refurbished copier leasing an effective strategy—cutting costs without compromising functionality." – Jane Miller, Office Tech Analyst, TechInsights Report 2023

Remember, while the new vs used copier costs are important, you should also factor in operating expenses such as toner, repairs, and service contracts when comparing options.

Performance and Reliability: What to Expect from Refurbished vs New Office Equipment

Performance concerns frequently dissuade businesses from choosing refurbished copiers. However, modern refurbishment processes include rigorous testing, replacement of worn components, and quality assurance checks.

  • New Copiers: Offer the latest technology, including advanced scanning and cloud integration, plus manufacturer-backed warranties covering repairs and service—ensuring minimal downtime.
  • Refurbished Copiers: Benefit from extensive testing and part replacements, and reputable leasing providers often include service plans and warranties comparable to new equipment, though sometimes with shorter terms.
"Industry professionals recommend considering certified refurbished copiers for businesses that require dependable, high-volume printing but want to avoid the premium price of new models." – Mark Thompson, VP of Sales, Copier Solutions Inc.

Businesses with highly specialized demands or those requiring cutting-edge features may lean toward new models. Others, especially those prioritizing cost-effectiveness, will find refurbished units meet their needs effectively.

Environmental Impact and Sustainability Considerations

Choosing refurbished copiers supports your company's environmental responsibility by extending the lifecycle of office equipment and reducing electronic waste. The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) reports that reusing office technology significantly lowers the carbon footprint compared to manufacturing new machines, aligning with corporate sustainability goals.

"Refurbished copier leasing not only benefits your budget but also promotes sustainable business practices by diverting electronic waste from landfills and reducing resource consumption." – EPA Electronic Waste Report, 2022

For companies with green initiatives, leasing refurbished commercial copier solutions can be a win-win, delivering in-office efficiency while contributing positively to environmental stewardship.

Comparing Business Copier Rental Plans: Flexibility and Support

Leasing plans vary widely between new and refurbished copier offerings:

  1. New Copier Leasing Plans: Often come with flexible upgrade options, remote monitoring, and comprehensive service contracts that include parts, labor, and toner replacement.
  2. Refurbished Copier Rental Plans: May offer shorter lease terms or customized plans designed to meet tight budgets, with options for maintenance packages to ensure operational continuity.

When evaluating commercial copier solutions, ask providers about service response times, replacement policies, and equipment upgrade paths. Reliable support can be as important as the equipment quality itself.

Leasing vs Buying Copiers: Why Leasing Often Makes Sense

Aside from the new vs refurbished debate, deciding between leasing or outright buying copiers is crucial.

  • Leasing Advantages: Allows businesses to preserve capital, access the latest technology regularly, benefit from tax deductions on lease payments, and avoid the risks of obsolescence.
  • Buying Drawbacks: Higher upfront investment, responsibility for maintenance, and potential resale challenges when equipment becomes outdated.

Given the rapid pace of technological advancement and evolving office needs, affordable copier leasing—whether new or refurbished—is frequently the prudent choice for most businesses.

Key Takeaways: Making the Right Copier Leasing Decision

  • Assess your budget: If upfront cost is a priority, refurbished copier leasing offers significant savings while still delivering reliable operation.
  • Define your technology needs: For the latest features and long-term warranties, new copier leases provide peace of mind and cutting-edge performance.
  • Consider sustainability goals: Refurbished units reduce environmental impact and align with corporate social responsibility initiatives.
  • Evaluate service and support: Choose leasing providers with strong maintenance plans tailored to your business volume and workflow demands.

By carefully weighing these factors and working with a trusted copier leasing provider, you can avoid the gamble and confidently select the best commercial copier solution for your business.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main advantages of leasing a refurbished copier compared to a new one?

Leasing a refurbished copier typically costs significantly less—often up to 50% less than a new machine. Refurbished copiers are professionally repaired and tested, guaranteeing reliable performance for most business needs. They also reduce environmental impact by extending equipment life. However, refurbished units might have shorter warranty periods and fewer cutting-edge features than new ones.

How do office copier leasing options differ between new and refurbished machines?

New copier leasing plans generally include longer-term contracts, upgrade options, and comprehensive servicing with full manufacturer warranties. Refurbished copier leasing tends to offer more affordable rates and flexible shorter-term or customized contracts, though sometimes with more limited support. Both options frequently include service and maintenance packages tailored to your business’s volume.

Is leasing a new copier always better than buying one outright?

Not necessarily. Leasing provides companies with flexibility, lower upfront costs, and the ability to upgrade technology regularly. It also often includes maintenance services bundled into the lease. Buying requires a larger initial investment, carries resale risk, and leaves the business responsible for maintenance. Leasing is generally preferred in fast-changing office environments.

Can refurbished copiers handle high-volume commercial printing?

Yes, certified refurbished copiers from reputable providers are often refurbished to meet or exceed manufacturer standards and can support high-volume printing without reliability issues. It's important to verify the provider’s refurbishment process and service agreements to ensure the copier fits your business’s workload demands.
