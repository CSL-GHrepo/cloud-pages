Pool Barrier Laws in Plantation: Your First Line of Defense

One of the most critical aspects of pool safety requirements Plantation FL imposes is the barrier law, designed to prevent accidental drownings—a leading cause of child fatalities nationwide.

  • Height Requirements: Barriers must be at least 48 inches tall with no gaps larger than four inches.
  • Self-Closing and Self-Latching Gates: Gates must automatically close and latch at a minimum height of 54 inches.
  • Non-Climbable Zones: The area within 18 inches of the barrier can have no climbable objects.
