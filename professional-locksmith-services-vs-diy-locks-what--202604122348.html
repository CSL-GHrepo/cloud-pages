Common DIY Lock Installation Risks and How They Impact Your Security

Although DIY approaches can be tempting due to cost savings or convenience, they come with significant drawbacks and risks that can jeopardize your property's safety:

  1. Incorrect Fit and Alignment: DIYers often struggle with precise measurements, resulting in locks that don’t sit properly, making them easier to force open.
  2. Reduced Durability: Inexperienced handling can damage lock components, leading to premature failures or vulnerabilities.
  3. Lack of Advanced Security Features: DIY kits usually come with standard lock designs lacking modern lock picking prevention technology.
  4. Voiding Manufacturer Warranties: Installing locks improperly may void warranties or guarantees offered by manufacturers.
  5. Increased Risk During Emergencies: Improper installation may cause locks to jam, complicating emergency lockout situations or fire escapes.

According to a 2022 Care.com survey, 65% of homeowners who attempted DIY lock installation encountered operational issues within six months, emphasizing the drawbacks of self-installed solutions.
