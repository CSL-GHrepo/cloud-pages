Key Terms for Selling Homes in Parkland

Understanding industry terminology can make the selling process less intimidating. One common term is 'AS-IS,' which means you sell your home in its current condition without repairs. This is often appealing to homeowners who want to sell quickly or can't afford renovations.

Another important term is 'Closing Costs,' which include fees paid at the end of a real estate transaction, such as title insurance, escrow fees, and taxes. Our team handles most of these costs, providing you with a clear, straightforward deal.

The term 'Equity' refers to the difference between your home's market value and any remaining mortgage. Knowing your equity helps determine your net profit after a sale. When working with our local experts, we consider your equity to offer fair and competitive prices.

A 'Contingency' is a condition that must be met for a deal to close, such as financing approval or inspections. Cash buyers eliminate these contingencies, offering faster, more secure sales for homeowners in Parkland.

Finally, 'Pre-closure Inspection' refers to a review of property conditions before closing. We often skip this step in cash deals, expediting the process and reducing delays for homeowners eager to sell.
