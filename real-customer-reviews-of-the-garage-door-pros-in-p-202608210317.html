Handling Common Garage Door Problems Efficiently and Effectively

Every garage door experiences issues over time, but how quickly and expertly they are resolved can save significant frustration and expense. The Garage Door Pros have documented solutions for typical problems frequently reported by Parkland residents.

Problem: Garage Door Won’t Open or Close Completely

Causes include sensor misalignment, debris blocking the track, or malfunctioning openers. The Garage Door Pros technicians meticulously diagnose the root cause. They realign sensors, clean and lubricate tracks, or replace faulty opener parts ensuring system responsiveness.

Problem: Loud Noises During Operation

Grinding, squeaking, or clanking noises often stem from worn rollers, loose hardware, or lack of lubrication. The team replaces or repairs damaged components swiftly, restoring quiet operation appreciated by many Parkland neighbors living near busy streets.

Problem: Uneven Door Movement or Sudden Jerking

This can indicate track misalignment or tension problems with springs and cables. Skilled technicians use specialized tools to adjust track angles and balance door tension, preventing permanent damage to the mechanism.
