SBA 504 and 7(a) Loan Programs

Small Business Administration (SBA) loans offer attractive options for multifamily buyers targeting smaller properties, typically under 250 units. The SBA 504 program offers long-term, fixed-rate financing for property acquisition and renovation, while the 7(a) program provides more flexibility on loan uses. Auten Realty’s network includes lenders well-versed in SBA financing, ensuring clients can evaluate these alternatives during deal structuring.

Bridge and Mezzanine Financing

In cases where properties require improvements or repositioning, short-term bridge loans or mezzanine debt can provide the necessary capital infusion.
