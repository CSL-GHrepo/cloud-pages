Best Practices to Maintain Your Garage Door in Davie, FL

Industry studies show that regular maintenance can reduce garage door repairs by up to 70% (Source: Garage Door Safety Institute). To keep your garage door in optimal condition, follow these expert recommendations:

  1. Schedule professional inspections at least twice a year.
  2. Lubricate moving parts including rollers, hinges, springs, and tracks yearly.
  3. Test the automatic reverse mechanism monthly for safety compliance.
  4. Keep the garage door tracks clean and clear of debris or obstacles.
  5. Check weather seals for wear and replace to improve energy efficiency.

For residents in Davie dealing with salt air and humidity, additional corrosion-resistant treatments may be recommended by your technician.
