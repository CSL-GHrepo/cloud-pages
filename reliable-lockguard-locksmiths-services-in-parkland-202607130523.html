Understanding Lock and Key Technology: A Guide for Parkland Residents

Knowing how locks work and the types available helps homeowners and business owners make informed decisions regarding their security.

Common Lock Types Explained

  • Pin Tumbler Locks: The most widespread lock design using pins of variable lengths to prevent unauthorized key access.
  • Deadbolt Locks: Provide an added layer of security by extending a solid metal bolt deep into the door frame.
  • Wafer Locks: Common in vehicles and cabinets, using flat wafers instead of pins.
  • Electronic and Smart Locks: Use digital codes, biometrics, or smartphone connectivity to manage access without traditional keys.

Key Types and Their Functions

  • Standard Keys: Simple mechanical keys cut to fit a specific lock.
  • Transponder Keys: Embedded with microchips for increased vehicle security, requiring programming to function.
  • Restricted Keys: Designed to prevent unauthorized duplication and used in high-security environments.

Lockguard Locksmiths’ technicians are knowledgeable about all lock and key types and can advise the best options for your property’s security demands in Parkland.
