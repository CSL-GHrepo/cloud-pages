Tips for Maintaining Your Locks and Security Systems in Parkland’s Climate

Parkland, FL’s subtropical climate brings unique challenges to lock maintenance, from high humidity to occasional storms. Proper upkeep is essential to prolonging the life of your security systems and preventing malfunctions.

  • Regular Lubrication: Apply a graphite or silicone-based lubricant every 6 to 12 months to door locks and padlocks to prevent rust and ensure smooth operation. Avoid oil-based lubricants that attract dust and grime.
  • Weatherproofing: Use weather-resistant locks and covers on exterior doors and gates exposed to rain and sea air, which can corrode metal components over time.
  • Routine Inspection: Schedule periodic checks for wear and tear, loose screws, or alignment issues that may impair lock function. Early detection allows for timely repairs and avoids lockouts.
  • Battery Maintenance for Electronic Locks: For smart or electronic locks, regularly replace batteries and test backup power options to prevent lock failures during power outages or storms.
  • Update Security Settings: Frequently update access codes and biometric registrations on smart locks and security systems, especially after changes in residence or staff turnover.

Lockguard Locksmiths offer maintenance contracts tailored for Parkland residents and businesses, providing hassle-free servicing and emergency readiness year-round.
