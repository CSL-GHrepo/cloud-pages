Quick Answer: What Are Standard Residential Garage Door Sizes?

The most common standard single garage doors measure 8 feet or 9 feet wide and are usually 7 feet tall. Double garage doors typically range from 16 to 18 feet in width and also have a height of 7 feet. Although 7 feet tall doors dominate the market, 8-foot and even 10-foot heights are increasing in popularity due to oversized vehicles and design trends. These sizes cover 85% of residential applications according to industry surveys.

According to the International Door Association, “Most residential garage doors sold in the U.S. are 9 feet wide and 7 feet tall for singles and 16 feet wide by 7 feet tall for doubles.” (IDA, 2023)

Understanding Standard Garage Door Measurements

1. Width and Height

Residential garage doors come in a variety of widths and heights tailored to different garage types and vehicle sizes. Knowing these dimensions upfront saves you from installation headaches and operation issues later.

  • Single Garage Doors: Typically 8’, 9’, or 10’ wide, with 7’ heights being common
  • Double Garage Doors: Most popular widths are 16’ and 18’, also with 7’ height
  • Heights: While 7’ is the norm, 8’ and 10’ heights are available, especially for bigger vehicles or RV garages

Garages with oversized doors often feature custom widths and heights. However, adhering to common dimensions is cost-effective and simplifies maintenance and hardware availability.

How to Measure for Residential Garage Door Installation

Accurate residential garage door measurements ensure the door fits perfectly and operates smoothly without gaps or binding. Here’s how to measure your garage door opening properly.

  1. Measure the Width: Take three measurements of the opening width, from the inside of one side jamb to the other. Use the smallest measurement for accuracy.
  2. Measure the Height: Measure from the floor to the underside of the header (the frame above the opening). Again, use the smallest measurement.
  3. Check the Side Room: You need at least 3.5” to 5” of clearance on each side of the opening to accommodate the door tracks.
  4. Check the Headroom: Minimum headroom is generally 10” to 12” from the ceiling or header to the top of the opening, but this can vary based on door style and spring system.
  5. Backroom Space: The distance from the garage door opening toward the back wall must permit the door to retract fully without obstruction, usually a length equal to the door height plus 18”.

Professional installers often include a thorough site inspection to confirm these dimensions and check structural conditions for a smooth installation process.

Regional and Vehicle Considerations for Garage Door Sizes

Vehicle preferences and regional construction practices influence garage door dimensions. For example:

  • In areas with larger trucks and SUVs, garage door height and width requirements tend to be higher than the standard 7-foot height and 9-foot width.
  • Regions with severe weather may favor insulated, secure doors that sometimes come in non-standard sizes for added durability.
  • Some local building codes may have requirements for emergency egress or fire safety that impact garage design.

Industry professionals recommend discussing your specific needs and regional requirements with a certified garage door installer to select the best garage door size and model for your home.

Common Garage Door Types and Their Typical Sizes

Understanding common garage door openings and size options helps homeowners visualize what fits their property best:

  • Standard Single Doors: Ideal for single vehicles and storage, single doors usually come in widths of 8’ to 9’ and 7’ height.
  • Double Doors: Used for wider garage spaces, these span 16’ to 18’ in width, perfect for two cars side by side.
  • RVs and Oversized Vehicles: Custom or tall garage doors can reach heights of 10’ to 12’ and widths over 18’.
  • Carriage Style and Custom Designs: Even with decorative or bespoke doors, standard framing dimensions often apply, simplifying retrofit or replacement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the most common garage door dimensions for residential homes?

The most common residential garage door dimensions for single doors are 8 to 9 feet wide by 7 feet tall, while double garage doors typically measure 16 to 18 feet wide by 7 feet tall. These sizes accommodate standard vehicles comfortably, with options for taller doors if needed.

How do I accurately measure my garage door opening?

Measure the width inside the frame from jamb to jamb at three points, using the smallest number. Measure the height from the floor to the underside of the header, again using the smallest measurement. Also, measure side room clearance, headroom, and backroom to ensure sufficient space for installation and operation.

Are there variations in garage door sizes based on region or vehicle type?

Yes, larger or taller doors are often needed in regions with bigger vehicles like trucks or SUVs, or for RV garages. Climate and building regulations can also influence door selection and size, so it's important to consider local conditions.

Can I get a custom-sized garage door if my opening doesn't match typical dimensions?

Absolutely. Custom garage doors are available for non-standard openings. However, they tend to cost more and may require special installation expertise. It's recommended to consult an expert to weigh the pros and cons before proceeding.
