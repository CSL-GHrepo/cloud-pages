Key Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Florida

Living in Parkland means dealing with humidity, storms, and temperature fluctuations that can speed up wear and tear on garage doors. Regular maintenance is essential to keep everything functioning safely and smoothly. A few simple checks and routine service visits go a long way in preventing unexpected breakdowns. — learn more.

Start with visual inspections. Look for signs of rust, corrosion, or damage on panels, tracks, and hardware. Keep the tracks clean and free of debris, which can cause misalignment or noisy operation. Lubricate moving parts like rollers and hinges with a silicone-based lubricant to reduce friction and prolong lifespan.

Test safety features such as sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms monthly. Proper function of safety sensors prevents accidents and is required by code. If sensors are misaligned or dirty, the door may not close properly, creating hazards.

Schedule professional tune-ups at least once a year. Our technicians check the springs, cables, rollers, and opener to ensure everything is in top condition. Regular maintenance can identify potential issues before they become costly repairs, saving you money and stress.
