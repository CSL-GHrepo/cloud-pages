Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical timeline for selling a house to a cash home buyer?

The timeline to sell your home to a cash buyer is typically much shorter than a traditional sale, often closing within 7 to 14 days. This is because there are fewer contingencies, no lender financing delays, and simplified paperwork.

How do cash home buyers determine their cash offer for a house?

Cash buyers evaluate your property based on market comparables, condition of the home, and local demand. They usually buy homes “as-is,” which means they factor repair costs into the offer to present a fair price that aligns with their investment strategy.

Are there any fees involved when selling to a cash home buyer?

Reputable cash home buyers usually charge no realtor fees or commissions. However, sellers should always verify if there are any administrative or closing fees upfront to avoid surprises and ensure a transparent transaction.

What are the main benefits of selling my home to a cash buyer versus listing with a realtor?

Benefits include faster closing times, selling as-is without repairs, avoiding realtor commissions (which average 5-6%), and greater certainty that the sale won’t fall through due to financing contingencies.
