Exploring Industry Terms and Key Statistics in Hard Money Lending

Understanding industry terminology can empower you to make smarter financing decisions. Terms like 'loan-to-value ratio' (LTV), 'interest reserves,' 'draw schedule,' and 'rehab budget' are fundamental in evaluating hard money deals. Familiarity with these concepts ensures you negotiate better terms and remain within your financial limits.

For example, the loan-to-value ratio indicates the maximum loan amount compared to the property value. In Parkland, lenders often offer LTVs up to 70-75%, depending on the property's condition and location. Interest reserves are funds set aside to cover interest payments during rehab, minimizing financial strain. A clear draw schedule outlines how funds are disbursed as work progresses, reducing the risk of over-borrowing and ensuring project milestones are met.

Statistics show that hard money loans account for about 8-10% of all real estate investment financing in local markets, reflecting their significance in quick-turnaround projects. The average interest rate in the industry hovers around 10-12%, but local lenders may offer more competitive rates depending on the project and borrower profile.
