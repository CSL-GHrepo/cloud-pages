  • Approval Timeline: Hard money loans offer rapid approval, often within days, compared to the weeks or months traditional loans require.
  • Underwriting Criteria: Unlike banks that emphasize credit scores and income documentation, hard money lenders focus primarily on the property’s value and potential.
  • Loan Terms: Hard money loans are typically short-term (6–24 months), whereas traditional loans are structured for longer periods (15–30 years).
