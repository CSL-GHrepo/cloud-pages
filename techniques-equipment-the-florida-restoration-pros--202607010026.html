The Florida Restoration Pros employ proven structural drying methods Florida’s climate demands. After water is removed and moisture levels are assessed, their team initiates controlled drying using:

  • High-Velocity Air Movers: Accelerate evaporation from wet surfaces, targeting drywall, wood, and carpets.
