Expert Tips for Florida Residents Facing Water Damage

The Florida Restoration Pros recommend the following for Florida homeowners:

  • Act immediately to call a restoration professional after water intrusion.
  • Document damage for insurance claims with photos and videos before cleanup.
  • Keep the property ventilated but avoid turning on HVAC units to prevent mold spread.
  • Use dehumidifiers to reduce interior moisture levels until help arrives.
  • Never ignore even minor water damage, as Florida’s climate accelerates mold growth.
