Tips for Homeowners Facing Water Damage in Florida

In Florida’s climate, acting swiftly is vital. Here are some expert tips from The Florida Restoration Pros for immediate steps when experiencing water damage:

  • Stop the water source: If possible, turn off water main or stop leaks immediately.
  • Contact professionals early: Don’t delay calling a trusted water damage restoration company like The Florida Restoration Pros.
  • Avoid mold risk: Remove wet materials (carpets, furniture) if safe, and increase ventilation until experts arrive.
  • Document damage: Take photos for insurance claims.
