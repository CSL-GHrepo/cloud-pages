Tips to Minimize Water Damage While Waiting for Restoration

While you wait for professional help, The Florida Restoration Pros recommend these critical steps:

  • Stop the source of the water if safe (e.g., turn off the main water valve).
  • Remove valuable items from affected areas to prevent further loss.
  • Do not attempt to dry large areas yourself; improper drying can worsen damage or cause mold.
  • Avoid using electrical appliances in flood-affected areas.
  • Document all damage with photos for insurance claims.

Following these tips can reduce your property loss and health risks before restoration professionals arrive.
