Tips for Florida Residents: How to Prepare and Minimize Water Damage

While The Florida Restoration Pros are ready to assist anytime, proactive steps can reduce damage severity:

  • Regularly inspect gutters and downspouts to ensure proper drainage away from your property.
  • Seal cracks and foundation openings to prevent seepage during heavy rains.
  • Elevate appliances and electronics in flood-prone areas of your home.
  • Keep storm drains and nearby ditches clear to facilitate water flow during storms.
  • Have emergency water shut-off tools handy, and know how to use them.
