What to Do When Facing Water Damage in Florida

If you suspect water damage, taking immediate action is critical to minimizing loss:

  • Notify a professional service promptly—The Florida Restoration Pros offer 24/7 emergency response.
  • Do not attempt to dry large water areas yourself—improper handling can cause mold growth.
  • Inspect your property for signs of hidden damage such as warped floors, discolored walls, or musty odors.
  • Contact your insurance company and document all damage with photos and videos.
