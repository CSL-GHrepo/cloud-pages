Water Damage Mitigation Florida: What Homeowners and Businesses Need To Do

When water damage strikes, immediate action is critical to mitigate losses. The Florida Restoration Pros recommend the following steps:

  1. Contact a certified restoration company immediately to assess damage and start water extraction.
  2. Document the damage with photos and detailed notes for insurance purposes.
  3. Avoid using household fans or DIY methods — improper drying can lead to mold and structural issues.
  4. Secure your property by shutting off electricity, moving valuables to dry areas, and preventing further water entry where possible.
  5. Schedule a professional mold inspection if water has been present for more than 24 hours.
