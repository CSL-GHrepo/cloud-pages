Understanding Florida’s Unique Water Damage Challenges

Florida is one of the most hurricane- and flood-prone states in the U.S.—making water damage an unfortunately common and costly dilemma. With an average of 13 named storms per hurricane season (NOAA, 2023), Florida residents and business owners face high risks of flooding and water intrusion.

Key Florida-specific challenges include:

  • High Humidity & Mold Risk: Florida’s subtropical climate encourages rapid mold growth after water damage incidents.
  • Storm and Flood Events: Hurricanes, heavy rains, and tropical storms frequently trigger emergency water damage situations.
  • Saltwater Intrusion: Coastal properties often suffer saltwater flooding, which complicates restoration due to corrosion and healthier remediation needs.

Industry professionals recommend immediate action to minimize the lasting effects of water damage on Florida properties.

  1. Shut off water sources and electricity to prevent hazards.
  2. Remove family and pets from the affected area to avoid exposure.
  3. Move valuable or sensitive items to higher ground.
