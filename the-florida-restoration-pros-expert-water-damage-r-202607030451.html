Actionable Steps for Homeowners and Business Owners in Florida

If you are confronting water damage, here’s what The Florida Restoration Pros recommend doing immediately:

  • Safety first: Avoid electrical hazards; if safe, turn off main water supply.
  • Contact professionals promptly: Delaying can worsen damage and increase restoration costs.
  • Document the damage: Take photos/videos for insurance claims.
  • Start water extraction: Only if you have proper equipment and knowledge—otherwise, wait for expert service.
  • Prevent mold growth: Increase ventilation if possible but avoid using regular fans that can spread spores.
