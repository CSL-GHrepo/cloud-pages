Tips for Florida Residents Facing Water Damage

Water damage in Florida requires immediate attention, and homeowners or business operators should keep these tips in mind:

  • Contact professionals immediately: The longer water sits, the worse the damage.
  • Document damage for insurance: Take photos and keep records to support claims.
  • Avoid DIY mold removal: Improper handling can spread spores and worsen health risks.
  • Ensure thorough drying: Use professional structural drying services to prevent long-term deterioration.
  • Schedule regular inspections: Particularly in high-risk flood zones during hurricane season.
