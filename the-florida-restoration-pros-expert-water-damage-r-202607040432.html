Taking Action: What to Do When Water Damage Occurs in Florida

Here are immediate steps recommended by industry experts and implemented by The Florida Restoration Pros:

  1. Ensure safety by shutting off electricity if water is present near outlets or electrical appliances.
  2. Contact a professional restoration company like The Florida Restoration Pros immediately.
  3. Document damages for insurance by photographing the affected areas.
  4. Do not attempt extensive DIY repairs, which can worsen the damage or promote mold growth.
  5. Remove valuables and furniture from waterlogged areas if safe to do so.

Following these steps can drastically improve the chances of successful water damage repair and reduce long-term health risks.
