Tips to Protect Your Florida Property from Future Water Damage

Preventing future incidents is as important as restoration work. The Florida Restoration Pros recommend these practical steps for Florida property owners:

  • Regularly inspect and maintain gutters, roof seals, and windows.
  • Install sump pumps and backflow valves in flood-prone areas.
  • Keep landscaping graded to direct water away from foundations.
  • Address minor leaks promptly to avoid escalation.
  • Use moisture-resistant building materials when renovating.
  • Schedule annual water damage inspections with a restoration professional.
