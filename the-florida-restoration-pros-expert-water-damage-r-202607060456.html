Steps to Take After Water Damage in Florida

If you are dealing with water damage, here are professional recommended steps to minimize harm before experts arrive:

  1. Ensure your safety and avoid electrical hazards.
  2. Turn off the water source if possible.
  3. Contact an emergency water removal service immediately (like The Florida Restoration Pros).
  4. Document damage by taking photos for insurance purposes.
  5. Remove valuables and dry salvageable items if safe.
  6. Contact your insurance provider to report the damage.
