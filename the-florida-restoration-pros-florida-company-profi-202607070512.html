Deep Dive: Mold Remediation Process at The Florida Restoration Pros

Mold poses serious health and structural risks if left untreated. The Florida Restoration Pros follow a strict, multi-phase protocol tailored to Florida’s humid conditions.

Inspection and Assessment

Upon arrival, the team performs a visual inspection supported by moisture mapping and air quality testing to identify mold presence and extent. This initial phase informs the remediation plan and helps determine containment areas.

Containment and Protection

To prevent mold spores from spreading to unaffected areas, containment barriers such as polyethylene sheeting and negative air pressure machines are deployed. Technicians wear PPE including respirators and disposable suits to ensure their safety.

Removal and Cleaning

The team removes contaminated materials that cannot be salvaged, such as drywall or insulation. For salvageable surfaces, EPA-registered fungicides and antimicrobial cleaners are applied, followed by HEPA vacuuming to capture residual spores.

Drying and Dehumidification

Since moisture fuels mold growth, thorough drying using industrial-grade dehumidifiers is critical. The Florida Restoration Pros monitor humidity levels continuously until optimal dryness is achieved.

Final Clearance Testing

Before completing remediation, post-removal air and surface tests are conducted to confirm that mold levels have returned to safe, baseline levels as defined by industry guidelines.

"The best mold remediation not only removes visible mold but also creates an environment where it cannot easily return," says lead technician Maria Lopez, emphasizing The Florida Restoration Pros' commitment to lasting solutions.
