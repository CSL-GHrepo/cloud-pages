Traditional drying methods often fail to address moisture deep within structural materials. The Florida Restoration Pros employ industrial-grade dehumidifiers combined with high-velocity air movers, a technique known as structural drying. This method:

  • Prevents warping, cracking, and long-term structural damage

Moreover, their teams continuously monitor moisture levels daily to ensure drying benchmarks are met before restoration proceeds.
