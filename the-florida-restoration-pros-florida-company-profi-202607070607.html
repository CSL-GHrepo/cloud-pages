Detailed Restoration Process: Step-by-Step Breakdown

Understanding the restoration process can help property owners feel more in control during a difficult time. The Florida Restoration Pros follow a meticulously planned procedure that ensures thorough recovery of your home or business after water damage.

1. Initial Assessment and Inspection

Upon arrival, the restoration team conducts a comprehensive assessment to evaluate the extent of water intrusion and damage. Using advanced moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras, they detect hidden pockets of water in walls, ceilings, and flooring. This critical phase informs the entire restoration plan.

2. Water Removal and Extraction

Next, specialized high-powered pumps and vacuums extract standing water rapidly. Depending on the severity, this can include sump pumps for deep flooding or portable extractors for smaller to moderate-sized water presence. Immediate removal helps prevent secondary issues like swelling, warping, or mold growth.

3. Drying and Dehumidification

Following extraction, drying equipment such as air movers and commercial dehumidifiers are strategically placed to remove remaining moisture from structural materials and air. This phase can last several days and requires constant monitoring to ensure surfaces reach safe dryness levels.

4. Cleaning and Sanitization

After drying, the team cleans and sanitizes all impacted surfaces to eliminate bacteria and contaminants introduced by floodwaters or sewage backups. EPA-registered antimicrobial agents are deployed to prevent mold spores and odors from developing.

5. Mold Prevention and Remediation

In certain cases, mold may have already begun to grow or risk growing if not addressed. The Florida Restoration Pros’ certified mold remediation specialists contain and remove mold colonies using negative air pressure chambers and HEPA filtration systems, reinstating a healthy indoor environment.

6. Structural Repairs and Reconstruction

The final phase involves repairing damaged materials and, if necessary, reconstructing parts of the building to pre-loss conditions. The company coordinates with licensed contractors to restore drywall, flooring, cabinetry, and roofing, ensuring structural integrity and aesthetic restoration.
