Understanding Different Categories and Classes of Water Damage

The Florida Restoration Pros apply industry-standard classifications to analyze and treat water damage appropriately, improving outcomes and reducing health risks. Clients benefit from this precise approach through tailored treatment plans.

Categories of Water

  • Category 1 (Clean Water): Water from clean sources like broken pipes or rainwater; treatment focuses primarily on removal and drying.
  • Category 2 (Gray Water): Water with some contamination, such as from dishwasher or washing machine overflow; requires more thorough cleaning and disinfection.
  • Category 3 (Black Water): Highly contaminated water from sewage backups, floods with river water, or standing water containing pathogens; necessitates full protective gear, removal of affected materials, and extensive sanitization.

Classes of Water Damage

  • Class 1: Minor water exposure affecting only a small area or low-porosity materials.
  • Class 2: Significant intrusion that affects entire rooms or carpets but little structural damage.
  • Class 3: Greatest amount of water absorption, affecting ceilings, walls, insulation, and subflooring.
  • Class 4: Specialty drying situations, such as hardwood, plaster, and concrete, where water is trapped deeply.
