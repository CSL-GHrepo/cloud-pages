Expert Advice on Preventing Water Damage in Florida Homes and Businesses

While The Florida Restoration Pros are experts in restoration, prevention is critical in Florida’s climate. They advise:

  • Regularly inspect roofs and gutters to prevent leaks during storms.
  • Install sump pumps and proper drainage systems to combat flooding.
  • Maintain HVAC systems to reduce indoor humidity and mold risk.
  • Seal foundation cracks prone to water seepage during heavy rains.
  • Keep landscaping graded away from property foundations.
