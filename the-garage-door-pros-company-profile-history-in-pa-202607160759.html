Materials Matter: Choosing the Right Garage Door for Parkland’s Climate

Selecting the appropriate garage door material significantly impacts durability, appearance, and maintenance needs in Parkland’s challenging environment. The Garage Door Pros guide their customers through these choices with expert advice about pros and cons tailored for local weather.

Common Garage Door Materials and Their Regional Suitability

  • Steel Doors: Durable and low maintenance, steel doors offer excellent resistance to impact, which is vital during hurricane season. The Garage Door Pros supply doors with galvanized steel and rust-resistant coatings to further extend lifespan.
  • Aluminum Doors: Lightweight and resistant to corrosion, aluminum is often preferred for its modern aesthetic and suitability in humid conditions, though it can be more susceptible to dents.
  • Wood Doors: While wood provides a classic, warm appearance, it requires rigorous maintenance in Florida due to moisture risks. The Garage Door Pros offer expert sealing and refinishing services to protect wooden garage doors.
  • Fiberglass Doors: Resistant to dents, rot, and corrosion, fiberglass can replicate the look of wood without the extensive upkeep. However, it may discolor over time due to UV exposure.

Energy Efficiency Considerations

High-performance insulation and energy-efficient garage doors are critical in Parkland to reduce heat gain and energy costs. The Garage Door Pros recommend doors with high R-values and polyurethane insulation cores, particularly for homes where the garage shares walls with living spaces.

These energy-efficient models contribute to maintaining a cooler garage interior during Florida’s hot summers and warmer spaces during the milder winter, ensuring comfort and cost savings throughout the year.
