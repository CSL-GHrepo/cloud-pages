Maintaining Your Garage Door: Tips from The Garage Door Pros

Proper garage door maintenance is essential to prevent headaches and costly repairs. The Garage Door Pros recommend following these simple tips to keep your garage door in top shape:

  1. Regular Lubrication: Apply lubricant to rollers, hinges, and tracks every six months to reduce friction and wear.
  2. Visual Inspections: Inspect cables and springs monthly for signs of wear or damage.
  3. Test Auto-Reverse Safety Features: Ensure sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms are functioning to prevent accidents.
  4. Clean Tracks: Remove debris from tracks to avoid operational blockages.
  5. Schedule Professional Maintenance: A yearly professional inspection by The Garage Door Pros can identify and fix problems early.

Consistent maintenance not only enhances safety but aligns with industry advisories that well-maintained garage doors can last up to 20 years, compared to 10-15 years with neglect (Garage Door Manufacturers Association).
