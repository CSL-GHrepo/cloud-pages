  • ANSI/DASMA 102 – The Door & Access Systems Manufacturers Association standard covering performance requirements and testing for garage door components
  • Florida Building Code – Enforces strict regulations for wind load resistance and impact protection in high-risk coastal zones
  • OSHA Guidelines – Occupational Safety and Health Administration recommendations for safe servicing and installation practices

Common Garage Door Hazards and Prevention

Despite their size and mechanical complexity, everyday hazards relating to garage doors are often preventable through proper use and timely professional service. Common risks include:

  • Pinch points: Fingers or hands can get caught in moving parts; regular inspections and safety feature testing reduce this risk
  • Broken springs: Torsion or extension springs can fail suddenly and cause severe injury; only certified technicians like The Garage Door Pros should handle spring repairs
  • Automatic reverse failure: If the auto-reverse feature is not functioning, the door may not stop upon obstruction, risking harm to children, pets, or property
  • Remote control security: Outdated systems without rolling code technology are vulnerable to remote hijacking; updates safeguard access

The Garage Door Pros routinely educate customers on these hazards and provide safety inspections to catch potential problems early, fostering responsible ownership and accident prevention.
