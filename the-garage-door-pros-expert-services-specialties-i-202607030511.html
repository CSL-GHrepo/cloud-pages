Maintaining Your Garage Door for Longevity and Safety

Regular upkeep is crucial in extending your garage door’s service life and preventing costly emergency repairs. The Garage Door Pros recommend the following maintenance tips:

  1. Inspect and lubricate moving parts semi-annually.
  2. Test balance and auto-reverse safety features every month.
  3. Schedule professional garage door opener maintenance at least once a year.
  4. Replace worn weatherstripping to protect against Parkland’s humid climate and occasional storms.

Proper maintenance helps prevent one of the most common failure points – spring breakage – which accounts for nearly 50% of service calls in South Florida, according to service data from The Garage Door Pros.
