Comprehensive Safety Measures: Protecting Parkland Homes and Families

Garage doors are heavy, complex mechanical systems that require strict safety standards to prevent accidents and injuries. Recognizing these risks, The Garage Door Pros prioritize safety throughout their services, from installation to maintenance and repairs.

Safety Features and Compliance

All garage door installations adhere to the latest codes and regulations established by the Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) and local Parkland ordinances. Key safety features incorporated include:

  • Auto-Reverse Mechanisms: Doors automatically reverse if an obstruction is detected during closing to prevent injury or damage.
  • Photo-Eye Sensors: Infrared beams across the doorway detect objects and people, triggering immediate door reversal if the beam is broken.
  • Manual Release Handles: Enable easy disengagement of the opener mechanism to manually operate the door during power outages or emergencies.
  • Spring Containment Systems: Securely containing high-tension springs to prevent potential hazards from spring failures.

Technicians thoroughly test and adjust these features during every service call to ensure optimal performance and peace of mind.
