Broken Springs and Cable Failures

Garage door springs endure significant tension cycles — often up to 10,000 or more — until they eventually weaken and snap. Broken springs can render a door inoperable or unsafe.

  • Replacement and Upgrades: The Garage Door Pros replace broken torsion or extension springs with heavy-duty, weather-resistant models designed for South Florida conditions.
  • Safety Inspections: Thorough checks ensure cables and pulleys are aligned and undamaged to prevent further damage or accidents.

Garage Door Opener Malfunctions

Opener systems may suffer from wiring issues, faulty sensors, or remote control failures.

  • Diagnostics: Technicians use specialized tools to pinpoint electrical faults or connectivity problems.
  • Sensor Realignment: Misaligned photo-eye sensors are recalibrated for proper automatic stopping and reversing functions.
  • Remote Reprogramming: Remotes are reset or replaced to ensure reliable entry control.

Tracks and Rollers Problems

Worn rollers or bent tracks can cause noisy operation and uneven door movement, leading to premature wear.

  • Roller Replacement: Nylon rollers are recommended for quieter operation and durability.
  • Track Repair: Straightening or replacing damaged tracks to guarantee smooth, safe door travel.
  • Lubrication: Periodic lubrication reduces friction and extends component life.
