Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Doors in Parkland

To avoid emergency repairs, follow these essential maintenance tips recommended by The Garage Door Pros:

  1. Inspect and lubricate moving parts every 6 months to prevent rust and wear.
  2. Test the auto-reverse safety feature monthly to ensure it stops and reverses if obstructed.
  3. Check the garage door balance annually by manually lifting the door halfway; it should hold in place.
  4. Replace worn springs and cables promptly—delaying increases risk of sudden failure and injury.
  5. Keep tracks clean and aligned to avoid binding and noisy operation.
