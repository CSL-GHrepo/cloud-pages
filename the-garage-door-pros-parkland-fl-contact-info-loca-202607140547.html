Maintenance Tips to Prolong the Life of Your Garage Door

Preventive maintenance is key to keeping your garage door operating smoothly and extending its lifespan. The Garage Door Pros in Parkland, FL, not only provide repair and installation services but also offer expert advice and maintenance plans tailored for the local climate and usage patterns.

Regular Inspection Checklist

Homeowners and business owners can proactively monitor their garage doors by conducting monthly visual and functional inspections. Key areas to focus on include:

  • Springs and Cables: Look for signs of wear, rust, or fraying cables. Springs should be intact without gaps or deformities.
  • Rollers and Tracks: Ensure rollers move freely without wobbling and tracks are clear of debris or dents that could impede travel.
  • Door Balance: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway. It should stay in place without falling or rising.
  • Weather Stripping: Check for cracks or gaps in the bottom seal and side weather stripping to prevent water and pests from entering.
  • Safety Sensors: Test the auto-reverse function by placing an object in the door’s path to ensure sensors stop the door before contact.

Lubrication and Cleaning

Proper lubrication reduces friction and noise while promoting longer component life. The Garage Door Pros recommend the following for upkeep:

  • Use a silicone-based lubricant or lithium grease on springs, rollers, hinges, and bearings every six months.
  • Clean metal parts with a cloth to remove dust, dirt, and surface rust before applying lubricant.
  • Avoid spraying lubricants on the door panels or tracks as excess buildup may attract debris or cause operational issues.

Routine cleaning of the door panels with mild detergent and water helps preserve door appearance, especially important for upscale Parkland homes where curb appeal is a priority.
