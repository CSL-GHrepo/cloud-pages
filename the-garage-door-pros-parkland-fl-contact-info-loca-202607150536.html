Common Garage Door Materials and Their Benefits

  • Steel: The most popular material due to its strength, relatively low cost, and low maintenance requirements. Steel doors can be insulated to improve energy efficiency and soundproofing. Perks include resistance to dents and corrosion treatments tailored for humid environments.
  • Aluminum: Lightweight and corrosion-resistant, aluminum garage doors are ideal for coastal areas like Parkland where salt air can accelerate rust. These doors often feature modern designs with glass panels for increased natural lighting.
  • Wood: Known for natural beauty and versatility, wood garage doors offer unmatched customization to match classic or upscale home styles. However, wood requires more upkeep, including regular sealing or painting due to Parkland’s humid and rainy climate.
  • Fiberglass and Composite: These materials deliver the appearance of wood without the drawbacks of warping or rotting. They perform well in high humidity climates and are a good choice for homeowners seeking low-maintenance options with decorative detail.

Insulation Types and R-Value Ratings

Garage door insulation is measured by R-value, which indicates resistance to heat flow. Higher R-values mean better insulation, which is essential to combat Parkland’s hot, humid summers and occasional cooler winter mornings.

  • Polystyrene (EPS) Panels: A common and cost-effective insulation material offering moderate R-values around 6.5 to 9.5.
  • Polyurethane: Provides higher R-values (up to 18), superior thermal performance, and increased noise reduction, making it ideal for attached garages used as workshops or home offices.
  • Reflective Insulation: Some doors incorporate reflective foil faces to minimize radiant heat transfer, helping keep the garage cooler under direct sunlight.

Weatherstripping and Door Seals

Proper sealing around garage doors helps maintain cooler indoor temperatures and prevent moisture and pests from entering. The Garage Door Pros install and replace high-quality bottom seals, side jamb weatherstripping, and threshold sweeps engineered to withstand Parkland’s climate conditions.
