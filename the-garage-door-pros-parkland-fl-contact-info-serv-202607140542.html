Understanding Garage Door Components: A Guide for Parkland Homeowners

Knowing the key parts of your garage door system can empower you to identify issues early and communicate more effectively with technicians. Here is a breakdown of the main components serviced by The Garage Door Pros:

Door Panels

The visual and functional heart of your garage door, panels can be made of steel, wood, aluminum, or fiberglass. Damage here affects insulation, security, and appearance.

Springs

There are two main types: torsion springs (mounted above the door) and extension springs (located alongside the tracks). These are crucial for counterbalancing the door’s weight, making opening and closing manageable.

Cables

Steel cables attach the door to the springs and help lift it safely. Frayed or broken cables are a common cause of door failure and can be hazardous.

Tracks and Rollers

Guiding the door’s movement, tracks and rollers must be aligned and lubricated for smooth operation. Misaligned tracks often cause noisy or jamming doors.

Garage Door Opener

The mechanized unit responsible for automated door movement. Includes the motor, drive system, sensors, and control unit.

Safety Sensors

Infrared beams installed near the floor that detect objects or people in the door’s path, ensuring the door reverses if obstructed.

Understanding these components helps when scheduling maintenance or repairs with The Garage Door Pros, allowing technicians to quickly diagnose and remedy the issue.
