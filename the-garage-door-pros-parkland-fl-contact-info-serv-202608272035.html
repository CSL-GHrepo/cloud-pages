Energy-Efficient Garage Doors

With hot and humid weather typical in South Florida, energy efficiency is a crucial consideration. Modern insulated garage doors reduce heat transfer between the garage interior and the outside environment, helping regulate temperatures and reducing strain on home cooling systems.

  • Materials and Insulation: Doors with polyurethane or polystyrene cores offer superior insulation values (measured by R-value ratings).
  • Weather Sealing: Advanced weather stripping along the door edges prevents moisture and drafts, protecting your garage contents and garage door mechanism from damage.
