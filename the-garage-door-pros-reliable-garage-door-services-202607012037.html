Maintenance Tips to Keep Your Garage Door in Top Shape in Parkland, FL

Besides hiring professionals, customers can take proactive steps to maintain their garage doors:

  • Inspect and lubricate hinges, rollers, and tracks every six months.
  • Clear debris from tracks to avoid misalignment.
  • Test the auto-reverse safety feature regularly.
  • Check the garage door balance by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door halfway. Proper balance means it stays in place without sliding.
  • Keep sensors clean and aligned to prevent malfunctions.

Regular upkeep can extend the life of your door by up to 10 years according to industry research from the Door & Access Systems Manufacturers Association International (DASMA).source
