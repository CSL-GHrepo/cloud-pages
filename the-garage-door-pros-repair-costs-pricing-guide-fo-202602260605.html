How to Maintain Your Garage Door in Parkland

Proper maintenance of your garage door can drastically reduce repair costs and extend its lifespan. The first step is regular visual inspections—checking for rust, worn hardware, or frayed cables. Lubricating moving parts like rollers, hinges, and springs with silicone-based lubricants helps prevent corrosion and reduce noise. It's advisable to do this every six months, especially after the rainy season in Parkland.

Track cleaning is often overlooked but crucial. Accumulated dirt or debris can hinder smooth operation. Use a damp cloth to wipe down the tracks and ensure they are free of obstructions. Also, tighten loose bolts and screws periodically to maintain structural integrity. Our team recommends scheduling professional tune-ups annually to catch issues early.

Sensor and safety feature checks are vital for secure operation. Test the auto-reverse function by placing an object in the door’s path—it should reverse upon contact. Replace any worn sensors or cables immediately to prevent accidents. Remember, a well-maintained garage door not only operates smoothly but also keeps your family safe.

In storm-prone areas like Parkland, securing the garage door during high winds protects against damage. Consider installing wind-resistant doors or reinforcing existing ones. When storms pass, check the hardware and seals for damage. Overall, consistent maintenance saves money and keeps your garage door functioning reliably in any weather.
