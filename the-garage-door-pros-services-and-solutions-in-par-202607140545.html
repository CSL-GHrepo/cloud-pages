Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips Specific to Parkland’s Climate

Florida’s unique weather patterns—characterized by high humidity, intense sun, hurricanes, and sudden thunderstorms—demand specialized care for garage doors. The Garage Door Pros provide tailored maintenance advice to help residents prepare for each season.

Summer and Hurricane Season

  • Preventative Lubrication: High humidity can cause hinges and rollers to seize up without proper lubrication.
  • Storm-Proofing: Reinforcing garage doors with bracing kits and impact-resistant panels to withstand hurricane-force winds.
  • Weatherstripping Checks: Ensuring seals are intact to keep out moisture, wind-driven debris, and pests.
  • Battery and Backup Testing: Verifying battery backups on openers function during potential power outages.

Dry and Cooler Months

  • Track and Roller Inspection: Dust and sand accumulation can cause damage over time, so cleaning is critical.
  • Adjusting Springs and Cables: Temperature fluctuations may affect tension—professional adjustment preserves smooth operation.

Implementing these seasonally adapted practices reduces the risk of unexpected failures and costly repairs.
