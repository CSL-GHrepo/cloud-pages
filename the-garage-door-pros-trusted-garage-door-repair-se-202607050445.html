Maintaining Your Garage Door in Parkland, FL: Best Practices

To keep your garage door working flawlessly, The Garage Door Pros recommend the following local maintenance tips:

  • Schedule an annual professional inspection to check springs, cables, rollers, and door balance.
  • Keep tracks clean and lubricate moving parts using recommended garage door lubricants to reduce wear.
  • Test safety sensors monthly to ensure the door reverses when encountering an obstacle.
  • Check opener batteries and remotes regularly, replacing as needed to avoid unexpected failures.
  • Inspect weather seals around the door perimeter, especially given South Florida’s humidity and seasonal rains, to prevent water intrusion and energy loss.

Following these maintenance steps can reduce the risk of emergency garage door services, saving time and money and improving safety.
