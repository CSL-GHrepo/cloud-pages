How often should I schedule garage door maintenance?

It’s recommended to have your garage door professionally inspected and maintained at least once a year. For heavy-use or commercial doors, bi-annual maintenance is advised to ensure optimal performance and safety.

Warning signs include unusual noises, slow or jerky movement, doors not opening or closing fully, worn cables or springs, and malfunctioning sensors. Ignoring these symptoms can lead to costly repairs or unsafe conditions.
