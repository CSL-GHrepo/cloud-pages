Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance Tips Specific to Parkland's Climate

Parkland’s subtropical climate exposes garage doors to humidity, heavy rains, intense sunlight, and occasional storms. The Garage Door Pros offer expert seasonal maintenance guidance to help residents protect their investment and avoid operational issues.

Spring Maintenance Checklist

  • Inspect and lubricate springs and rollers: Prevent corrosion and reduce friction for smooth operation.
  • Check weather seals and gaskets: Replace damaged or dried-out seals to stop water ingress during rainy months.
  • Test safety sensors: Ensure alignment and responsiveness to avoid accidents as humidity affects electrical components.

Summer Maintenance Checklist

  • Monitor door balance and motor function: High temperatures can affect opener motors; verify proper function.
  • Inspect paint or finish for sun damage: Touch up or reseal to protect against UV degradation.
  • Clean tracks and remove debris: Prevent build-up that can cause jams or noisy operation.

Fall and Winter Maintenance Checklist

  • Check for moisture damage: Florida winters can be damp; inspect doors and frames for mold or swelling.
  • Test insulation efficiency: Ensure doors maintain temperature control to protect stored items.
  • Schedule professional inspection: Take advantage of milder weather to perform comprehensive maintenance and repairs.
