Garage Door Safety Features: Protecting Your Family and Property

Garage doors are heavy moving structures that can pose significant risks if safety features are outdated or neglected. The Garage Door Pros prioritize safety by incorporating and inspecting the latest safety mechanisms in every repair and installation job.

Essential Safety Components

  • Auto-Reverse Sensors: These infrared sensors detect obstructions beneath the door and automatically reverse its motion to prevent injury or damage.
  • Manual Release Mechanisms: Allow homeowners to disengage the opener from the door during power outages or emergencies, enabling manual operation.
  • Spring Containment Systems: Torsion and extension springs store a significant amount of energy and can cause injuries if broken. Containment cables and quality spring systems reduce these hazards.
  • Battery Backup Openers: Maintain functionality during electrical outages for uninterrupted access and security.

Regular testing and timely replacement of worn components drastically reduce the risk of garage door accidents. The Garage Door Pros provide detailed safety inspections and educate Parkland residents on best practices for safe garage door operation.

Safety Tips for Garage Door Owners

  • Never allow children to play near or operate the garage door unsupervised.
  • Keep remote controls out of reach of children to prevent unintended operation.
  • Avoid DIY repairs on springs and cables, which can cause serious injury.
  • Regularly test auto-reverse features every month to ensure they are functioning properly.
  • Schedule professional maintenance checks at least once a year.
