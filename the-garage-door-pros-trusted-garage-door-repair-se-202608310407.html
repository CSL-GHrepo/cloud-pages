Tips for DIY Garage Door Maintenance: What You Can Do Safely

While professional maintenance is essential, The Garage Door Pros encourage homeowners to perform simple, safe routine checks to extend the life of their garage doors and identify issues early.

  • Visual Inspections: Regularly look for wear on springs, cables, rollers, and tracks. Notice any unusual noises or sluggish movements.
  • Keep Tracks Clean: Remove dirt, leaves, and debris to prevent obstructions.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Use manufacturer-approved garage door lubricants on hinges, rollers, and springs every six months.
  • Test Safety Features Monthly: Ensure auto-reverse and photo sensors function by carefully testing with an object in the door’s path.
  • Keep the Area Clear: Remove items and clutter near the garage door’s movement path to prevent accidents.

Important: Never attempt major repairs or spring replacements without professional training due to the high risk of injury.
