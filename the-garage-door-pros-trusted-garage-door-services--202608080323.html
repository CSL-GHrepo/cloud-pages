Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips: Preparing for Parkland’s Climate

Parkland’s subtropical climate presents unique challenges for garage doors throughout the year. The Garage Door Pros recommend proactive seasonal care to maintain door functionality and appearance across the changing weather conditions:

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Inspect weather seals for cracks or wear to prevent moisture intrusion during Florida’s rainy season.
  • Lubricate moving parts every 3-6 months to counteract humidity-induced corrosion.
  • Check garage door openers and sensors regularly to ensure reliable performance in hot, damp conditions.
  • Clean garage door surfaces with mild detergents and rinse thoroughly to remove salt and dirt buildup.

Fall and Winter Preparation

  • Adjust spring tensions to respond correctly to cooling temperatures that can cause metal contraction.
  • Test backup battery systems on openers before the storm season to guarantee operation during outages.
  • Trim nearby landscaping to prevent debris buildup around door tracks.
  • Schedule a professional inspection with The Garage Door Pros to identify early wear signs before holiday visits.

Routine seasonal maintenance prevents unexpected breakdowns and prolongs the lifespan of garage doors in Parkland’s climate.
