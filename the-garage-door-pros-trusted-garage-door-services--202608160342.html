Understanding Garage Door Safety Features and Compliance

Safety is paramount when it comes to garage doors, which are heavy mechanical devices operating under significant tension. The Garage Door Pros adhere strictly to safety standards to protect homeowners, families, and their property in Parkland.

Key Garage Door Safety Mechanisms

  • Auto-reverse function: Sensors detect obstacles in the door’s path and stop or reverse door movement to prevent injury or damage.
  • Photoelectric eyes: Positioned near the floor on either side of the door, these invisible beams detect motion and halt closing doors upon interruption.
  • Manual release handles: Enable emergency disengagement of the door opener for manual operation during power outages or malfunctions.
  • Spring containment systems: Specialized cables or tubes hold broken springs in place, minimizing hazardous flying debris.
"Ensuring that your garage door safety sensors are calibrated and tested regularly is vital to prevent accidents and injuries," emphasizes Mark Jenkins, safety engineer at the Garage Safety Institute.
