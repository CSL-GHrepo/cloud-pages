Safety Tips Every Parkland Homeowner Should Follow

Garage doors are among the heaviest moving objects in a home and have the potential to cause serious injury or property damage if not properly maintained or used. The Garage Door Pros emphasize education and awareness as part of their service philosophy.

  • Keep Remote Controls Secure: Avoid leaving garage door remotes in vehicles or open areas where unauthorized access may occur.
  • Test Safety Sensors Monthly: Ensure automatic reversal systems function correctly to prevent accidents involving pets, children, or objects.
  • Never Attempt DIY Repairs on Springs: Garage door springs are under extreme tension and require trained professionals to handle safely.
  • Clear Tracks and Obstructions: Debris or damage in tracks can cause door misalignment or sudden stops.
“A garage door that won’t reverse properly or has frayed cables is a hazard waiting to happen,” warns safety specialist Maria Lopez. “Routine inspections by trusted technicians like The Garage Door Pros are essential to keep families safe.”
