Maintaining Your Garage Door for Longevity in Parkland

Regular maintenance is the key to extending your garage door’s life and preventing costly repairs. In Parkland, where humidity and storms are common, seasonal checks can make a big difference. Simple tasks like lubricating moving parts, tightening hardware, and inspecting the tracks can keep your system running smoothly. — our garage door repair team.

One of the most overlooked maintenance points is checking the balance of your garage door. A balanced door requires less stress on the springs and opener, reducing wear and tear. Our technicians can perform a professional balance test during routine visits or inspections.

Lubricating hinges, rollers, and tracks helps prevent squeaking and ensures smooth operation. Using the correct lubricant recommended by manufacturers is essential. Avoid WD-40 or similar products that can attract dirt and cause more issues over time.

Inspecting the weather seals around the bottom of your door can prevent drafts, water intrusion, and pest entry. Replacing or repairing these seals improves energy efficiency and protects your garage’s interior.
