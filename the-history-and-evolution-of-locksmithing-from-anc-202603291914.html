Expertise in Locksmith Terminology and Industry Standards

Understanding industry terms can help you communicate better with locksmith professionals. Terms like 'bump-proof locks,' 'deadbolt,' 'keyway,' and 'smart lock' are common in our field and may influence your decision-making.

A 'bump-proof lock' offers resistance to lock bumping—a method burglars sometimes use to open traditional locks quickly. We often recommend these for extra security in residential and commercial settings.

The 'deadbolt' is a standard lock type that provides high security when properly installed. We install and repair all kinds of deadbolts, ensuring they meet current standards and security needs.

The 'keyway' refers to the shape of the lock's internal profile. Choosing the right keyway can prevent unauthorized duplication and enhance overall security.

A 'smart lock' allows keyless entry via smartphones or access codes. It’s a growing trend in Parkland homes and businesses aiming for convenience without compromising safety.
