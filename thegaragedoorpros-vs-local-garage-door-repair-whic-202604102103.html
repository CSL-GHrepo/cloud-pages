Garage Door Maintenance Tips to Prolong Your Door’s Life

Regardless of your repair company choice, proactive maintenance helps avoid costly repairs. Experts recommend:

  1. Regularly lubricate moving parts with appropriate garage door lubricants.
  2. Inspect springs, cables, and rollers for signs of wear or damage monthly.
  3. Test the door balance and auto-reverse safety feature every six months.
  4. Keep tracks clean and clear of debris.
  5. Schedule annual professional inspections.

Following these garage door maintenance tips reduces unexpected breakdowns and enhances overall safety.
