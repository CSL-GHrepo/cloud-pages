According to the National Association of Realtors, the typical home sale commission averaged 5.45% in 2023. Avoiding these fees by selling to a cash buyer can significantly increase your take-home payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does it mean to sell my house as-is to a cash home buyer?

Selling as-is means you can sell your property in its current condition without making repairs or improvements. Cash home buyers accept the home knowing it may require work, which saves you money and time compared to traditional sales where inspections typically trigger repair negotiations.

How quickly can I expect a fast property closing with a cash home buyer?

Most cash transactions close within 7 to 14 days, sometimes even sooner if all documentation is ready. This is significantly faster than the average 30-60 days for traditional sales requiring mortgage approval and inspections.

Are there any fees or commissions when selling to a cash home buyer?

Typically, no. Cash home buyers usually purchase directly, eliminating real estate agent commissions and many closing costs. This means you keep more of your sale price without hidden fees.

Can I sell an inherited property to a cash home buyer?

Yes. Cash buyers specialize in quick, as-is sales and can simplify the process for inherited properties, which often require fast liquidation without repairs or lengthy listing periods.
