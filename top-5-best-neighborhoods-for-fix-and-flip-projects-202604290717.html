Top 5 Best Neighborhoods for Fix and Flip Projects in 2024

Below is a curated list of neighborhoods grounded in data, expert insight, and real-world experience that offer the most promising opportunities for fix_and_flip investors in 2024:

  1. Austin, TX – East Austin

    East Austin exemplifies a booming neighborhood with a rich mix of affordable single-family homes and rising demand in tech-driven employment markets. The region’s median home price increased approximately 7.3% in 2023, surpassing the national average.

    • Strong renovation project hotspots with new construction invigorating adjacent areas
    • High buyer demand from young professionals and families
    • Average flip ROI in East Austin exceeds 25%, according to local investment groups

  2. Charlotte, NC – Plaza Midwood

    Plaza Midwood is an eclectic neighborhood in Charlotte with rising popularity among millennials and urban professionals. Neighborhood revitalization efforts and historic preservation combine to increase home values.

    • Median sale prices rose by over 6% in 2023, with flips selling quickly
    • Active community and infrastructure improvements support growing real estate investment neighborhoods
    • Profitable fix and flip areas with available properties at below-market prices

  3. Phoenix, AZ – Maryvale

    Maryvale is gaining traction as an ultra-affordable entry point in Phoenix with substantial redevelopment incentives. Investors find significant opportunities in single-family homes needing cosmetic and structural renovation.

    • High ROI flipping neighborhoods averaging 28-30% on completed renovations
    • Emerging real estate markets benefiting from Phoenix’s overall population growth
    • Significant demand driven by first-time buyers and rental market interest

  4. Tampa, FL – West Tampa

    West Tampa is a classic renovation project hotspot with increasing buyer interest thanks to proximity to downtown and waterfront amenities. The area is part of Tampa’s broader urban renewal plans.

    • Median home prices appreciating at a rate of nearly 5.5% per year
    • Flips have a rapid turnaround, often under 40 days on market
    • Investor-friendly environment with strong contractor and lender networks

  5. Columbus, OH – Near East Side

    Columbus’s Near East Side blends historic charm with affordability, making it attractive to fix_and_flip investors seeking high ROI and moderate risks.

    • Neighborhood median prices increased 5-6% recently with healthy supply of undervalued homes
    • Strong community engagement supporting neighborhood revitalization
    • High potential for value-add through modernization upgrades
