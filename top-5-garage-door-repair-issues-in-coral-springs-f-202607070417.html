Understanding Garage Door Spring Types: Torsion vs. Extension Springs

Garage door springs are essential for the door’s smooth and safe operation, but not all springs are created equal. In Coral Springs, understanding the difference between torsion springs and extension springs can help homeowners communicate better with repair technicians and make informed decisions about replacements or upgrades.

Torsion Springs

Torsion springs are mounted horizontally above the garage door opening on a metal shaft. When the door closes, these springs twist and store mechanical energy, which helps lift the door during opening. They are generally considered safer, quieter, and have a longer lifespan compared to extension springs.

  • Pros: Balanced lifting, less noise, better durability, easier to maintain tension.
  • Cons: More expensive to install and replace, requiring specialized tools and professional technicians.

Extension Springs

Extension springs run along the horizontal tracks on either side of the door, stretching and contracting to counterbalance the door’s weight. They are more common in older or budget garage door models.

  • Pros: Typically lower initial cost, simpler system design.
  • Cons: More prone to wear and breakage, louder operation, greater risk of snapping if not equipped with safety cables.
"In South Florida environments, torsion springs are often recommended over extension springs to combat the accelerated corrosion effects of humidity and salt air." – Coral Springs Garage Door Specialists

Tip: When replacing springs, discuss with your technician whether upgrading to torsion springs is feasible for improved longevity and safety.

DIY Garage Door Maintenance: What You Can and Cannot Safely Do

Many homeowners want to handle minor garage door maintenance themselves. While this can save money, it’s vital to understand safe limits and when to call in professionals, especially in Coral Springs.

Safe DIY Maintenance Tasks

  • Lubrication: Applying lubricant to rollers, hinges, and tracks is generally safe and extends component life.
  • Visual Inspection: Checking for visible signs of damage, rust, or loose hardware.
  • Cleaning Sensors: Gently wiping photo-eyes with a soft cloth to ensure clear operation.
  • Tightening Hardware: Using hand tools to tighten bolts and screws that have loosened due to vibration.

Tasks to Avoid Doing Yourself

  • Spring Adjustments or Replacement: Springs hold extreme tension and improper handling can cause serious injury or death.
  • Track Realignment: Heavy tools and precise leveling are often required.
  • Electrical Repairs: Garage door openers are complex electronics; incorrect troubleshooting risks damage or shock.
  • Replacing Safety Sensors: Requires proper alignment and wiring expertise.

Always consult a licensed and insured Coral Springs garage door repair company for high-risk repairs.
