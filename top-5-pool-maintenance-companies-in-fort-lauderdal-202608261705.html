Seasonal and Storm Preparedness for Fort Lauderdale Pool Owners

Given Fort Lauderdale’s vulnerability to hurricanes and tropical storms, pool maintenance extends beyond routine care to include preparedness and recovery strategies that minimize damage and health risks.

Pre-Storm Pool Preparation

  • Lowering water levels to prevent overflow and damage to pool infrastructure.
  • Securing or removing delicate equipment and accessories vulnerable to strong winds.
  • Shocking the pool pre-storm to reduce bacterial growth from debris and contamination.

Post-Storm Recovery and Sanitation

  • Immediate water testing and chemical rebalancing to address contamination from runoff and debris.
  • Inspection of pumps, filters, and electrical components for storm damage or water infiltration.
  • Professional cleaning services to remove leaves, dirt, and sediment quickly to prevent algae bloom.

Pool maintenance companies like Premier Pool Management and AquaCare have dedicated storm response teams, often available 24/7 during hurricane seasons to assist homeowners and business owners in timely recovery.
