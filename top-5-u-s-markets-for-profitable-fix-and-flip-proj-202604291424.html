Top 5 U.S. Fix and Flip Markets in 2024

1. Phoenix, Arizona

Phoenix remains a hotspot for property flippers due to its growing population, job market strength, and affordable home prices relative to demand. According to ATTOM Data Solutions, the average gross flip ROI in Phoenix for Q1 2024 is over 46%, making it one of the nation’s highest.

“Phoenix offers a balance of affordability and rapid price appreciation that fix and flip investors crave,” notes real estate consultant Mark Dalton.

  • Median purchase price: $280,000
  • Average days on market for renovated homes: 30-45 days
  • Popular renovation projects: Kitchen and bathroom upgrades, energy efficiency improvements

2. Austin, Texas

Austin’s expanding tech industry and influx of new residents fuel strong demand for updated homes. The city’s housing market supports quick sales post-renovation, with average flip profits reaching 38% in early 2024.

  • Median purchase price: $340,000
  • Strong fix and flip financing options available through local lenders
  • Focus areas: Older neighborhoods with potential for structural upgrades and modern finishes

3. Tampa, Florida

Tampa’s affordable housing prices combined with robust demand due to favorable climate and tax benefits make it ideal for flipping projects. Renovation ROI analysis indicates kitchens and outdoor living spaces add significant value here.

“Tampa's real estate hot spots continue to surprise investors by combining affordable acquisition with high buyer demand,” states Lindsey Parker, Chairwoman of the Florida Real Estate Investors Association.

  • Median purchase price: $260,000
  • Average gross flipping profit: 35%
  • Preferred renovations: Flooring upgrades, curb appeal enhancements

4. Charlotte, North Carolina

Charlotte’s growing financial sector and population make it a promising fix_and_flip market in the Southeastern U.S. The city features a diverse housing stock, including many undervalued homes suited for renovation.

  • Median purchase price: $310,000
  • Quick sales: renovated properties sell within 40 days on average
  • Most profitable renovations: Interior remodeling and adding extra bathrooms

5. Atlanta, Georgia

Atlanta’s thriving economy and migration patterns have increased demand for move-in ready homes in a market still favorable to investors. Renovations here can yield a solid 33-40% ROI.

  • Median purchase price: $280,000
  • Market trend: Increasing interest in energy efficient and smart home upgrades
  • Well suited for: Mid-range renovations targeting first-time buyers and young families
