Frequently Asked Questions

What does selling a house "as-is" to a cash home buyer mean?

Selling "as-is" means you do not need to make any repairs or upgrades to your home before selling. The cash home buyer agrees to purchase your property in its current condition, saving you money and time often spent on preparing the house for traditional buyers.

How fast can I sell my house to a cash property buyer?

Cash property buyers usually can close within 7 to 14 days after the offer is accepted. This is much faster than the standard real estate market sales cycle, which often takes several months.

Are there any fees or commissions when selling to a cash home buyer?

Typically, no. Unlike traditional sales involving realtors who charge commissions (usually 5-6% of the sale price), cash transactions usually avoid these fees, allowing you to keep more of the sale proceeds.

Can selling to a cash buyer help me avoid foreclosure?

Yes. Selling your house quickly to a cash home buyer can provide the funds needed to pay off debts and stop foreclosure proceedings, helping protect your credit rating and financial future.
