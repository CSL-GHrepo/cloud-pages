Frequently Asked Questions

What does it mean to sell my house to a cash home buyer as-is?

Selling your house as-is means you don’t need to fix repairs or make upgrades before selling. Cash home buyers purchase the property in its current condition, saving you time, money, and the hassle of preparing the house for traditional buyers or inspections.

How fast can I expect to close when selling to cash real estate buyers?

Cash transactions typically close within 7 to 14 days, depending on the buyer and circumstances. Since the buyer is not reliant on financing approval, the process is much quicker than traditional sales which can take 30 to 60 days or more.

Are there any fees or commissions when selling my property to a cash home buyer?

No. One of the key advantages of selling to a cash home buyer is avoiding agent fees and commissions, which typically range from 5% to 6% in a traditional sale. This means more money stays in your pocket after the sale.

Can selling to a cash home buyer help me avoid foreclosure?

Yes. Many homeowners use cash home buyers to quickly sell their properties before foreclosure occurs. The immediate cash offer can satisfy your outstanding mortgage balance and help you avoid the negative impact of foreclosure on your credit rating.
