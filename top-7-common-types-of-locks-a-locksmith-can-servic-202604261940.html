Top 7 Common Types of Locks a Locksmith Can Service Explained

Being locked out feels like time stops. Your keys are right there—on the other side of a door that won't budge. Whether you're standing outside your home, car, or business, that moment of realization brings a unique kind of frustration. Fortunately, professional locksmiths are equipped to handle all types of locks efficiently, restoring access and security quickly.

Understanding the common types of locks a locksmith can service helps you make informed decisions when upgrading your security or handling emergencies. Below, we dive into the most frequently serviced door lock types and explain what makes each unique.

Quick Overview: Common Lock Types Serviced by Locksmiths

  • Pin Tumbler Locks: Standard residential and commercial locks.
  • Deadbolt Locks: Essential security locks for residential doors.
  • Knob Locks: Often used for interior doors but also external in some cases.
  • Mortise Locks: Heavy-duty commercial lock systems.
  • Electronic and Smart Locks: Advanced technology-driven security solutions.
  • Padlocks: Portable security for gates and lockers.
  • Cam Locks: Used in cabinets, mailboxes, and small safes.

1. Pin Tumbler Locks – The Most Common Residential Locks

Pin tumbler locks are the backbone of residential security, found on a majority of homes. They operate via pins inside the lock cylinder, which align in order to allow the key to turn. Locksmiths often perform key duplication services and lock repair services on these popular locks.

“Approximately 80% of residential door lock systems are pin tumbler types, making them the most common lock a locksmith will service,” says John Richards, a veteran locksmith in the security industry.

Locksmiths use specialized lock picking techniques to open these locks when keys are lost and can rekey them to maintain security after moving or break-ins.

2. Deadbolt Locks – Enhancing Security for Homes & Businesses

Deadbolts provide an additional layer of protection and are often mandatory for exterior doors. They come in single-cylinder and double-cylinder varieties. Locksmiths repair, install, and rekey deadbolts to address issues like worn pins or broken bolts.

  • Single-cylinder deadbolts are operated by a key on one side and a thumb turn on the other—ideal for residential locks.
  • Double-cylinder deadbolts require a key on both sides, common in commercial lock systems where additional security is necessary.

The strength of deadbolt locks is underpinned by industry standards. According to the Canadian Standards Association, deadbolt locks tested under security lock solutions must withstand forced entry attempts to some degree, making them a trusted choice.

3. Knob Locks – Common but Less Secure Door Lock Type

Knob locks are typically found on interior doors, where security needs are minimal, but they can also be used on entry doors combined with deadbolts. Locksmiths often service knob locks for residential clients who require lock repair services after wear or accidental lockouts.

While convenient, knob locks have inherent security vulnerabilities; expert locksmiths recommend pairing them with deadbolts or upgrading to more secure lock types for entry doors.

4. Mortise Locks – Heavy-Duty Commercial Lock Systems

Mortise locks are standard fixtures in commercial settings due to their durability and complexity. These locks fit into a pocket within the door itself, offering superior strength and security.

Locksmiths skilled in commercial lock systems often troubleshoot mortise lock malfunctions that include jammed deadbolts or worn internal components. They also provide key duplication services for businesses that require master key systems.

“In commercial environments, mortise locks combine heavy-duty construction with versatile keying options—making them a cornerstone of security lock solutions,” explains Maria Gonzales, a commercial locksmith with over 15 years of experience.

5. Electronic Locks and Smart Locks – Modern Security Innovations

Electronic locks use keypad codes, proximity cards, or biometric data to grant access. Smart locks, a subset of electronic locks, integrate with smartphones or home automation systems, offering remote control and monitoring.

Locksmiths servicing electronic locks require specialized knowledge of wiring, battery systems, and software troubleshooting. Common services include lock repair, firmware updates, and programming new user credentials.

Industry studies indicate that over 30% of households in urban areas consider electronic locks a key component of home security, signaling growing demand for locksmith expertise in this field (Source: Consumer Technology Association, 2023).

6. Padlocks – Portable Security Solutions

Padlocks secure gates, sheds, lockers, and storage units. Locksmiths frequently service padlocks, performing lock picking when keys are lost and replacing or rekeying them if necessary.

Common padlocks include combination locks, keyed padlocks, and high-security variants with hardened shackles. Locksmiths can advise on the best padlock types for specific needs and provide key duplication services.

7. Cam Locks – Small but Essential Security Locks

Used primarily on cabinets, mailboxes, and small safes, cam locks are less complex but vital for securing access to valuables or confidential materials.

Locksmiths can repair broken cams, replace tumblers or keys, and even upgrade cam locks to higher-security versions on request.

Why Professional Locksmith Services Are Critical

Attempting DIY repairs or lock picking without expertise can cause damage or reduce security. Trained locksmiths utilize a blend of traditional lock picking techniques and modern tools tailored for different door lock types. Their service portfolio includes emergency lockouts, security lock solutions for upgrades, and key duplication services to prevent inconveniences.

Regional considerations such as climate and building codes also affect lock performance. For example, humid environments may accelerate rust in metal locks, and locksmiths must recommend corrosion-resistant hardware accordingly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the different types of residential locks a locksmith services?

Locksmiths commonly service pin tumbler locks, deadbolts, and knob locks in residential settings. They handle lock repairs, rekeying, and key duplication services to maintain secure and functional home entry points. Electronic and smart locks are also increasingly popular in residential security solutions.

Can locksmiths fix electronic locks and smart locks?

Yes, locksmiths with specialized training can service electronic locks, including smart locks. They provide installations, programming, battery replacements, troubleshooting, and secure lock repair services that integrate with home automation systems.

What commercial lock systems do locksmiths specialize in?

Locksmiths frequently work with mortise locks, deadbolts, master key systems, and high-security commercial locks. Their expertise includes installing robust lock solutions, rekeying, emergency lockouts, and tailored security upgrades for businesses.

Are locksmiths skilled in lock picking techniques for emergency access?

Absolutely. Trained locksmiths use professional lock picking techniques and non-destructive tools to gain entry when keys are lost or locks malfunction. This skill avoids damaging the lock and ensures the quickest possible resolution in emergencies.
