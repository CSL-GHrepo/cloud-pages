Key Security Tips for Parkland Residents

In my experience, the best way to prevent lockouts and break-ins is to stay proactive with your home’s security. Regularly inspect your locks for signs of wear or damage, especially after storms or vandalism. If you notice any issues, call a professional immediately before an incident occurs.

Avoid hiding spare keys outside your home — burglars know all the common spots. Instead, give a spare key to a trusted neighbor or use a secure lockbox. This reduces the chance of unauthorized access while providing you peace of mind.

Upgrade outdated locks, especially on entry points that see frequent use. Modern deadbolts and smart locks offer better resistance against forced entry and picking, common techniques used by intruders.

Ensure all exterior doors and windows are secured with high-quality locks. Reinforce vulnerable areas like sliding glass doors with additional security bars or sensors.

Consider installing security cameras or alarm systems for added protection. Many modern systems integrate with smartphones, allowing real-time monitoring and alerts.

Lastly, educate family members about security practices. Simple habits like locking doors and not sharing access codes can significantly improve your safety.

Staying vigilant and investing in quality locks makes your home less appealing to intruders and more secure for everyone inside.
