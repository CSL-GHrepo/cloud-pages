Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door Between Professional Visits

While regular professional maintenance is essential, homeowners can take simple steps to help keep their garage doors in good working condition between service visits.

  • Visual Inspections: Routinely check for signs of wear, rust, or damage on springs, cables, and rollers.
  • Clean Tracks and Remove Debris: Ensure tracks are clear of dirt, leaves, and obstacles to keep the door running smoothly.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply garage door lubricant on hinges, rollers, and springs every few months—avoiding grease that attracts dust.
  • Test Safety Features: Verify photo-eye sensors and auto-reverse functions periodically by placing objects in the door's path.
  • Balance the Door: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway to see if it stays in place. If it moves up or down, professional adjustment may be needed.

By following these proactive practices, Coral Springs residents can extend the lifespan of their garage doors and reduce emergency repair needs.
