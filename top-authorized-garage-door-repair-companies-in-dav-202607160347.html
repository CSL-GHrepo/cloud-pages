Comprehensive Safety Features Ensured by Authorized Services

Garage doors are heavy, complex mechanical systems with the potential for injury or property damage if not properly maintained or installed. Certified repair companies in Davie emphasize safety to protect families and businesses alike.

Key Safety Components and Checks

  • Auto-Reverse Mechanism: Modern garage doors are equipped with sensors that detect obstructions and immediately reverse the door to prevent crushing accidents. Authorized technicians routinely test and recalibrate these sensors during service visits.
  • Photoelectric Eye Alignment: These safety eyes ensure the door closes only when the pathway is clear; misalignment or dirty lenses can disable this feature, increasing risk.
  • Torsion Spring Tension Adjustment: Springs store tremendous energy and improper tension or wear can cause dangerous failures. Professionals inspect and replace springs following strict safety guidelines.
  • Emergency Release Cords: In case of power failure or malfunction, emergency cords allow manual opening of garage doors. Authorized installers ensure these cords are accessible and functional.

As highlighted by the Consumer Product Safety Commission, improper garage door repairs contribute to thousands of injuries annually—working with certified technicians minimizes these risks.
