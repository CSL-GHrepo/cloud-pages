Clear title is a must for any real estate transaction. Cash buyers in Las Vegas usually conduct thorough title searches to uncover any liens, unpaid taxes, or legal encumbrances that could potentially delay or derail the sale.

If these issues are uncovered, skilled cash buyers may offer creative solutions such as price adjustments or credit back to the seller. Having a Nevada real estate attorney assist you through title resolution can ensure a smooth closing process.
