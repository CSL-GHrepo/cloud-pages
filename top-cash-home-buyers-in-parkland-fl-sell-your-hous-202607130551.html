Reputable cash buyers will conduct title searches to ensure clear ownership and uncover any liens or encumbrances. Sellers should confirm that buyers have a process to handle these issues smoothly, protecting all parties from legal disputes.

Understanding Closing Costs

While many cash sales reduce traditional fees, sellers should clarify if any closing costs, escrow fees, or commissions apply in their transactions. Transparent communication about costs up front prevents surprises at signing.

Tax Implications

Sellers should consider capital gains taxes or potential tax consequences when selling their property. Consulting a tax professional is advisable, especially for inherited or investment properties.

Contract Review

Always have cash sale contracts reviewed by a qualified real estate attorney or trusted advisor to verify terms, contingencies, and obligations before signing.

Beware of Scams

Although Parkland has many legitimate cash buyers, sellers should be wary of fraudulent offers or pressure tactics. Vet companies thoroughly, avoid upfront fees, and require written offers and contracts.
