The Role of Title Companies in Cash Home Sales in Parkland

Title companies play a pivotal, often unseen role in guaranteeing smooth and secure cash home sales. Their involvement protects both buyers and sellers, especially for quick transactions.

What Does a Title Company Do?

  • Title Search: Confirms the property's ownership history and checks for liens, claims, or encumbrances.
  • Title Insurance: Protects the buyer (and sometimes the seller) from future claims or disputes over ownership.
  • Escrow Services: Facilitates the safe transfer of funds and documents between parties, ensuring each side meets their contractual obligations.
  • Closing Coordination: Arranges document signing, records the title transfer with the county, and disburses funds appropriately.
