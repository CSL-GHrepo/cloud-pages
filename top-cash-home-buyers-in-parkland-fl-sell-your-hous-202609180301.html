Legal and Tax Considerations When Selling to Cash Buyers in Parkland

Cash buyers expect clear and marketable title before closing. Sellers should work with a title company or attorney to verify that the property does not have any liens, outstanding mortgages, or legal disputes. Resolving these issues prior to the sale can prevent delays.

Homeowners in Parkland should consider potential capital gains taxes on profits from the sale. If the property was your primary residence for at least two of the last five years, you may qualify for exemptions up to $250,000 for single filers or $500,000 for married couples filing jointly.

Cash sales often involve slightly different contracts than traditional sales. It’s vital to have legal counsel review all documents before signing to confirm that the terms protect your rights and outline contingencies such as earnest money deposits, inspection periods, and closing costs.
