Long-term Garage Door Maintenance Tips

Maintaining your garage door is less complicated than most think. Regular upkeep keeps components in check and extends the lifespan of your system. In Parkland’s humid climate, routine maintenance is especially important.

Lubricate moving parts like rollers, hinges, and springs every six months to prevent rust and ensure smooth operation. Use a silicone-based lubricant for the best results.

Inspect the tracks for misalignment or debris. Clean them regularly and make adjustments if needed to prevent uneven wear or jamming.

Test the safety features of your garage door, such as auto-reverse mechanisms and sensors, to ensure they work correctly. This is crucial for family safety and code compliance.

Check the weather seals around the door for damage or leaks. Replacing worn seals helps keep out moisture and pests, improving insulation.

Schedule professional tune-up services annually. Our technicians can identify potential issues early, saving money and reducing emergency repairs.
