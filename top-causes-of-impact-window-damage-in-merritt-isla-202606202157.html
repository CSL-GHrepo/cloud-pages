The Top Causes of Impact Window Damage in Merritt Island, FL

1. Hurricane and Tropical Storm Winds

Merritt Island is no stranger to powerful hurricanes and tropical storms. Even the most robust wind-resistant windows can experience stress under hurricane-force winds that often reach speeds over 100 mph. According to FEMA, impact-resistant windows reduce window failure by as much as 75% during high-wind events, but damage is still possible from prolonged wind pressure or sudden gusts.

  • Effect: Stress fractures, seal failure, and glazing damage.
  • Why it happens: Continuous pounding of wind pressure can weaken window frames and compromise the laminated glass over time.
  • Preventive Measure: Investing in professional impact window installation Merritt Island ensures proper sealing and anchoring to withstand high winds.

2. Debris Impact During Storms

Flying debris, one of the most common causes of impact window damage, poses an omnipresent threat during heavy storms in coastal Florida. The impact glass is designed to resist penetration, but sharp or large debris such as tree limbs, roofing materials, or trash can chip, crack, or dislodge window components.

  • Effect: Visible cracks, broken frames, or compromised seals allowing water intrusion.
  • Why it happens: When surrounding landscaping, construction, or other property materials become projectiles, even the strongest glass can be challenged.
  • Preventive Measure: Clear potential debris before storms and reinforce property with durable perimeter protections.

3. Coastal Weather Effects on Windows

The salty, humid coastal environment of Merritt Island accelerates the degradation of impact window components such as metal frames and sealing gaskets. According to studies by the American Architectural Manufacturers Association, salt spray corrosion is a leading cause of window seal failure in coastal areas.

  • Effect: Corroded frames, fogging between glass layers, and weakened seals.
  • Why it happens: Salt deposits attract moisture, promoting rust and the breakdown of materials when combined with Florida’s intense sun exposure.
  • Preventive Measure: Regular cleaning with non-corrosive products and applying anti-corrosion coatings extend window life.

4. Lack of Regular Maintenance

A surprisingly frequent cause of damage is neglecting regular impact window maintenance tips. Like any home system, windows require periodic inspection for tiny cracks, operational issues, or seal degradation that, if ignored, can escalate into full repair needs.

  • Effect: Gradual weakening of window integrity and higher vulnerability during storms.
  • Why it happens: Homeowners skip maintenance due to perceived durability or invulnerability myths around impact windows.
  • Preventive Measure: Schedule professional inspections annually and promptly address minor repairs through dedicated impact window repair Merritt Island services.
