Glossary of Key Terms in Commercial Copier Leasing

Lease Term: The duration of the leasing agreement, typically ranging from 24 to 60 months, depending on your needs.

Residual Value: The estimated value of the copier at the end of the lease, influencing the monthly payment amount.

Maintenance Agreement: A contract including repairs, supplies, and servicing to keep the copier operational.

Upgrade Clause: A provision allowing the lessee to upgrade to newer equipment before the lease ends, often included in flexible leasing plans.

Cost per Copy: The fee charged for each printed or copied page, typically included in maintenance packages to control expenses.
