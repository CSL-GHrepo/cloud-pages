How to Transition Smoothly from Owned Equipment to a Leasing Model

Moving from owning copiers to leasing can be an operational and cultural shift for many Boca Raton organizations. Proper planning mitigates disruptions and optimizes the value of the leasing arrangement.

Conducting an Equipment Audit

Document current copier models, usage patterns, maintenance histories, and associated costs. This baseline helps to evaluate lease offers and identify potential savings or upgrades.

Evaluating Contract Terms and Exit Clauses

Understand the length of the lease, renewal options, early termination penalties, and upgrade possibilities. Leasing companies with transparent, flexible contracts earn greater trust and long-term partnerships.

Staff Training and Change Management

Introduce leased equipment gradually with training sessions to acquaint employees with the new systems, especially if transitioning to multifunction or cloud-connected copiers. Leasing providers often offer onboarding support and resources.

Planning for Equipment Disposal or Return

Coordinate the end-of-life handling of owned equipment, whether selling, recycling, or donating, and ensure leased equipment is returned or upgraded per contract. Providers frequently assist with disposal to ease administrative burdens.
