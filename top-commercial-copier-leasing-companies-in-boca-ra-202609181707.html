Financial Considerations and Lease Structuring Strategies for Boca Raton Businesses

Operating Lease vs. Capital Lease

Commercial copier leases generally fall into two categories:

  • Operating Lease: Classified as a rental expense, it typically offers shorter terms and no ownership at the end. This is favored by businesses prioritizing cash flow flexibility and technological upgrades.
  • Capital Lease: Treated like a financed purchase, these agreements often lead to ownership upon completion, helping capitalize assets on the balance sheet but usually involving higher monthly payments.

Choosing the right lease type depends on budgeting preferences, tax strategy, and how long your business intends to use the equipment.
