Essential Industry Terms for Hollywood Businesses

Understanding key terms can make your copier leasing experience more straightforward. 'End of lease options' refer to the choices you have once your lease expires—return, buy, or upgrade. 'Page volume' indicates the number of pages a copier can handle monthly and influences your leasing plan. 'Service level agreements (SLAs)' define the scope and response times for maintenance support. Lastly, 'lease buyout options' allow you to purchase the equipment at the end of your lease term, often at a reduced price.

Knowing these terms helps you negotiate smarter and ensures your leasing agreement aligns with your business needs.
