Understanding Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) in Copier Leasing

While monthly lease payments may seem straightforward, the true cost of copier leasing includes several hidden factors that influence the total cost of ownership. Businesses in Hollywood should evaluate all components before signing an agreement.

  • Supply Costs: Toner, paper, staples, and other consumables can add significant expenses. Review if supplies are included or billed separately.
  • Service Fees: Clarify what maintenance services are covered and which repairs might incur additional charges.
  • Usage Charges: Lease contracts often contain page volume limits with fees for overages; understanding typical office output helps avoid surprises.
  • End-of-Lease Obligations: Potential costs for excessive wear, damage, or missing accessories at lease termination.

Calculating TCO helps Hollywood businesses budget accurately and compare different leasing proposals effectively.
