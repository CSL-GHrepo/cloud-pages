Comparing Leasing Models: Operating Lease vs. Capital Lease in Miami

Understanding different leasing structures can help Miami businesses align copier leasing with financial and operational goals. The two primary types are operating leases and capital leases, each with distinct implications.

Operating Lease

  • Typically short- to medium-term agreements (12-36 months).
  • Leased equipment remains the property of the leasing company throughout the contract.
  • Lease payments are treated as operating expenses, potentially advantageous for tax purposes.
  • Often includes comprehensive maintenance and service.

Capital Lease (Finance Lease)

  • Usually longer-term leases with an option to purchase the asset at the end of the term.
  • Lessee assumes most of the risks and benefits of ownership.
  • Lease payments are treated as asset depreciation and liabilities on the balance sheet.
  • Often used when businesses plan to keep equipment beyond the lease period.

Miami businesses should consult with financial advisors to determine the most beneficial lease type based on cash flow, tax strategy, and growth projections.
