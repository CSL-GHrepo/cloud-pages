Hidden Fees and Unexpected Costs

Some leasing agreements contain hidden service fees or restrictive clauses that inflate total costs beyond the monthly payments disclosed initially. Common issues include:

  • Excessive charges for exceeding monthly print volume limits
  • Costs for consumables like toner or paper not covered by the lease
  • Early termination penalties or exorbitant upgrade fees

Tip: Always review the lease contract carefully and clarify all potential fees with your leasing representative to avoid surprises.

Poor Maintenance Response Times

Downtime can cripple business productivity, particularly in fast-paced Miami industries. Some companies struggle with slow or unreliable service from copier maintenance providers.

Operating Lease vs. Capital Lease: What’s Best for Your Business?

Understanding the difference between operating and capital leases can impact your accounting and tax treatment:

  • Operating Lease: Treated as an expense on the income statement; no asset or liability recorded on the balance sheet, offering off-balance-sheet financing advantages.
  • Capital Lease: Treated as an asset purchase and debt financing; asset and liability recorded, suitable for businesses intending to own the copier after the lease.

Consult your accountant or financial advisor to determine the most beneficial lease structure based on your business’s financial goals and compliance requirements.
