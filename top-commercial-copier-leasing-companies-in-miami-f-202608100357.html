Leasing and Tax Advantages Specific to Florida Businesses

Florida tax codes and federal regulations often favor leasing as a business expense. Miami’s commercial entities can capitalize on these breaks to optimize tax planning:

  • Lease Payments as Operating Expenses: Payments are deductible as a business operating expense, reducing taxable income in the year incurred.
