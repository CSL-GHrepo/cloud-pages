Evaluating Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) in Copier Leasing

A key consideration often overlooked in copier leasing decisions is Total Cost of Ownership (TCO). Beyond the monthly lease payment, TCO includes all related costs incurred through the copier’s lifecycle. Assessing TCO helps South Florida businesses avoid unexpected expenses and choose the best leasing strategy.

Components of TCO in Leasing

  • Monthly Lease Payment: The base cost covering equipment use.
  • Maintenance and Service: Routine servicing, repairs, and tech support—often included but verify coverage details.
  • Consumables: Toner, drums, and other supplies, which may or may not be part of the lease agreement.
  • Overage Charges: Fees if monthly copy volume exceeds contract limits.
  • Installation and Setup: Initial costs to integrate copiers with existing office systems.
  • End-of-Lease Fees: Charges for excessive wear, damages, or failure to return equipment.

Leasing companies that provide transparent cost breakdowns enable better TCO management. South Florida businesses should request detailed quotations and compare them on TCO rather than monthly payments alone to ensure financial clarity.
