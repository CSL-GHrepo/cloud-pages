Tips for Maintaining Your Leased Commercial Copier in Boca Raton

Even with maintenance included in most leasing contracts, proactive day-to-day care of your copier can extend its life and improve performance, especially in Boca Raton’s unique climate.

  • Keep the Copier Clean: Regularly clean paper trays, glass scanning surfaces, and external panels to prevent dust buildup that can cause jams or image quality issues.
  • Use Recommended Supplies: Always source toner, drums, and parts approved by the leasing provider to ensure compatibility and avoid voiding service agreements.
  • Control Humidity: Keep copiers in well-ventilated, air-conditioned rooms to reduce problems caused by excess moisture, such as paper curling or toner clumping.
  • Train Staff: Educate employees on proper copier use and basic troubleshooting to minimize operational errors and unnecessary service calls.
  • Monitor Performance: Use built-in diagnostic tools or service reports provided by your leasing company to identify issues early and schedule timely maintenance.
