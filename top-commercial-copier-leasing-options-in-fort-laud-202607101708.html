How to Evaluate Lease Agreements and Avoid Common Pitfalls

Entering into a copier lease contract requires careful review to avoid hidden fees or restrictive terms that could impact your business operations negatively. Consider these important points during lease evaluation:

  • Understand All Fees: Ask explicitly about installation charges, supply costs (like toner), service call fees, and penalties for exceeding page limits.
  • Review Maintenance Obligations: Confirm the scope of onsite services, turnaround times for repairs, and who bears responsibility for consumable replacement.
  • Check End-of-Lease Conditions: Determine your options for purchase, lease renewal, or equipment return, plus any associated fees or requirements.
  • Assess Flexibility Clauses: Make sure the lease allows upgrades or downgrades as your business needs evolve without punitive costs.
  • Clarify Data Security Policies: Ensure providers guarantee secure data deletion and compliance with regulatory standards when equipment is returned.

When in doubt, seek input from legal or financial advisors familiar with commercial equipment leases to safeguard your company’s interests.
