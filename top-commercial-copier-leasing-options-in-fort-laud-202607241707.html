Evaluating Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) in Copier Leasing

While leasing helps spread out equipment expenses, understanding the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) is crucial for Fort Lauderdale businesses to make informed decisions. TCO encompasses not only lease payments but also all associated costs over the lease term, including supplies, maintenance, repairs, and energy consumption.

Factors influencing TCO include:

  • Page yield and supplies costs: Comparing toner replacement costs and expected pages per cartridge helps anticipate running expenses.
  • Energy efficiency: Modern commercial copiers compliant with ENERGY STAR standards reduce electricity bills, a vital consideration given Florida’s climate and utility rates.
  • Maintenance fees and service agreements: Inclusive maintenance can lower unexpected repair costs but may increase monthly lease payments.
  • Downtime cost: The financial impact of equipment failure or slow print jobs affecting business productivity.

Companies often find that a slightly higher monthly lease fee can result in lower overall costs by avoiding expensive repairs and minimizing lease-related surprises.

"We always advise clients to look beyond the initial lease rate and evaluate all cost components to avoid budget shocks down the line," explains James Peterson, a financial analyst specializing in office technology procurement.
