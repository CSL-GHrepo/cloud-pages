Comparing Operating Leases vs. Capital Leases for Fort Lauderdale Businesses

Beyond the typical lease structures mentioned earlier, it’s important for businesses to differentiate between operating leases and capital leases, as each affects financial reporting and tax treatment differently:

  • Operating Lease: Treated more like a rental agreement, operating leases keep leased copiers off the company’s balance sheet, preserving borrowing capacity and often allowing easier upgrades. Monthly payments are treated as operational expenses.
  • Capital Lease: Functions more like a financed purchase where the asset may appear on the balance sheet as property. This can offer depreciation benefits and possible ownership at lease-end but can impact debt ratios.

Many Fort Lauderdale leasing companies educate their clients on these distinctions and partner with financial advisors to choose lease structures aligned with business goals and accounting strategies.
