Understanding Copier Lease Contract Fine Print: Potential Pitfalls and Tips

Before signing any copier lease agreement, Hollywood businesses should be aware of several contract details that can affect long-term satisfaction and costs.

Common Contract Clauses to Review

  • Lease Term Length: Typical leases range from 24 to 60 months; choosing the right term impacts upgrade flexibility and monthly payments.
  • End-of-Lease Options: Confirm buyout prices, renewal terms, or return conditions to avoid unexpected charges.
  • Maintenance Scope: Clarify whether service covers parts, labor, and supplies; some contracts exclude essential items.
  • Usage Limits and Overage Charges: Understand page limits on color vs. black-and-white prints to prevent costly overage fees.
  • Early Termination Fees: Ask about penalties if your business outgrows or changes its copier needs mid-contract.

Tips for Negotiating Favorable Lease Contracts

  • Request a clear and thorough breakdown of all costs, including hidden fees
  • Negotiate for flexible upgrade clauses allowing equipment refresh before term ends
  • Ensure the provider commits to response time guarantees for service calls
  • Seek an option for lease deferment if unexpected business disruptions arise

Due diligence during contract review not only prevents surprises but can improve your leasing experience and total cost of ownership.
