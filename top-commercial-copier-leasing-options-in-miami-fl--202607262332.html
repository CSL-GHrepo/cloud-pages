Comparing Lease Structures: Everything You Need to Know

Understanding your lease options is critical to choosing the best commercial copier leasing solution for your Miami business. Common lease structures include:

  1. Fair Market Value Lease (FMV): Allows businesses to lease a copier for a term, then return or buy the equipment at market value, offering flexibility.
  2. $1 Buyout Lease: Here, the business owns the equipment for $1 at the end of the lease, ideal if you want ownership.
  3. Fixed Term Lease: A fixed monthly payment and term with no buyout, often used for newer models.

Miami companies favor FMV leases for their flexibility, given the fast pace of technological advances in commercial printing equipment.
