Understanding the Fine Print: Common Lease Agreement Clauses to Watch

Before signing a commercial copier lease in Miami, closely review contract terms and watch for clauses that could impact your total cost and flexibility.

Usage Limits and Overages

Many leases set maximum monthly copy or print volume allowances. Exceeding these caps often results in per-page overage charges that can quickly add up. Understand your typical usage patterns and negotiate higher thresholds or favorable overage pricing.

Maintenance and Service Responsibilities

Clarify what the lease covers regarding parts, labor, toner, and consumables. Some agreements might exclude certain supplies or repairs after a period, shifting unexpected costs back to you. Ensure prompt service response times and escalation procedures are documented.

Early Termination and Renewal Options

Leasing contracts commonly last 36-60 months, but your business needs may change. Check penalties for breaking a lease early and whether there are automatic renewals that could extend obligations beyond your desired timeline.

End-of-Lease Equipment Disposition

Know your options for returning, purchasing, or upgrading equipment at lease end. Some providers offer trade-in incentives or lease-to-own paths that can save money over time. Also, confirm responsibilities for data wiping and equipment removal.
