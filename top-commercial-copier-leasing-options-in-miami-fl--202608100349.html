Common Pitfalls to Avoid When Leasing Commercial Copiers in Miami

Even with thorough research, some businesses encounter avoidable mistakes during copier lease selection. Knowing common pitfalls helps Miami companies safeguard their investment:

  • Overestimating Needs: Leasing the highest capacity copier “just in case” can lead to paying for unused features; accurate volume projections are essential.
  • Ignoring Service Level Agreements (SLA): Not confirming guaranteed response times for repairs can result in costly downtime.
  • Hidden Fees: Watch for charges related to installation, removal, shipping, or excess meter clicks often omitted from initial quotes.
  • Neglecting End-of-Lease Terms: Understand buyout options, return conditions, and penalties to avoid surprises at contract expiration.
