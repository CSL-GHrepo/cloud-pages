Understanding Commercial Copier Financing Options

How to Negotiate Optimal Copier Leasing Terms in South Florida

Leasing agreements can vary greatly in structure and cost. South Florida businesses that approach negotiations strategically gain favorable terms fostering long-term benefits.

Key Negotiation Tips

  • Clarify Total Cost of Ownership: Ensure transparency regarding all costs including overage fees, service call charges, and end-of-lease buyout options.
  • Request Flexible Upgrade Clauses: Negotiate upgrade terms that allow equipment refreshes as technology advances without excessive penalties.
  • Establish Defined Service Level Agreements (SLAs): Commitments on response times, uptime guarantees, and penalties for service failures prevent surprises.
  • Explore Early Termination Options: Inquire about conditions and fees associated with early lease termination to avoid long-term lock-ins during uncertain market conditions.
  • Bundle Services for Discounts: Combining leasing with managed print services or supply contracts can yield cost savings.

Example: A Coral Gables financial firm successfully negotiated a pilot lease agreement with flexible upgrade and early termination provisions, enabling a trial period that led to an expanded fleet under favorable terms.
