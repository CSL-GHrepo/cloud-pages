South Florida’s rapidly evolving business conditions require adaptable contracts. Request clauses permitting upgrades when technology improves or early termination without steep penalties if your office footprint or staffing changes.

Leasing providers often offer discounts for integrating copier equipment with managed print services (MPS), software licenses, and supply deliveries. Bundling multiple services leads to streamlined vendor management and significant expense reduction.

Ensure lease agreements distinctly separate monthly fixed payments from variable costs such as per-page charges and consumables. This clarity aids in budgeting and avoiding surprise fees.
