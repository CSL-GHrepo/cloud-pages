Quick Answer: What Are the Common Copier Types Available for Lease?

Copier leasing options primarily fall into four common categories:

  • Multifunction Copier Machines (MFPs): Combine printing, scanning, copying, and faxing in one device, ideal for streamlined office tasks.
  • Single-Function Office Copier Models: Focus on high-volume copying with straightforward features for businesses that don’t require extras.
  • Digital Copiers: Utilize advanced digital copier technology offering crisp outputs and network connectivity.
  • Printer and Copier Combos: Perfect for small to medium-sized businesses needing versatile printer and copier functions in a single device.

Understanding Copier Types and Their Business Applications

1. Multifunction Copier Machines (MFPs)

Multifunction copier machines are among the most sought-after leased office copier models. They integrate several business copier features—copying, printing, scanning, and faxing—into one system, reducing clutter and increasing productivity.

  • Suitable for: Medium to large offices requiring versatile document handling.
  • Benefits: Reduced equipment costs, enhanced workflow integration, and easy centralized leased copier maintenance.
  • Example: A marketing firm leasing an MFP can scan client contracts, print color proposals, and fax documents all from one secure device.

2. Single-Function Office Copier Models

These copiers are designed purely for copying tasks, often offering high-speed, high-volume capacities. While they lack multifunction capabilities, their simplicity may offer a cost-effective solution for businesses primarily focused on copying.

  • Suitable for: Organizations with heavy copying needs but minimal scanning or printing requirements.
  • Example: A print shop leasing a robust single-function copier to meet daily bulk copying demands.

3. Digital Copier Technology

Digital copiers represent the modern standard by replacing old analog machines. These devices improve output quality, speed, and offer network connectivity, remote management, and sometimes cloud integration. Most multifunction copiers today are digital.

According to IDC, digital copier technology has decreased operational costs by up to 30% for businesses leveraging advanced features in leased copiers.
  • Key Features: High-resolution output, digital scanning, toner-saving modes, and integration with business software.
  • Business advantage: Enhanced document security and streamlined workflows.

4. Printer and Copier Combos

Especially valuable for small offices or startups, printer and copier combos provide multifunctional capabilities at a lower overall footprint. These hybrid machines reduce capital costs associated with maintaining separate devices.

  • Use case: Small law firms or boutique agencies that need versatile printing and copying but have limited space.
  • Copier Leasing Benefit: Leasing options often include bundled maintenance, keeping devices running smoothly without unexpected downtime.

How to Choose the Right Copier to Lease for Your Business

When selecting the best copier leasing option, consider the following business copier features and needs:

  1. Volume Requirements: Estimate monthly copy and print volumes to pick an appropriate machine that won’t slow your workflow.
  2. Functionality: Do you need only copying, or also scanning, faxing, and printing capabilities?
  3. Space Constraints: Ensure the copier fits your office layout, especially with multifunction machines and printer/copier combos.
  4. Budget: Balance leasing costs against value-added features and service agreements.
  5. Maintenance Services: Confirm if the lease covers repair, toner replacement, and routine maintenance to avoid hidden costs.
