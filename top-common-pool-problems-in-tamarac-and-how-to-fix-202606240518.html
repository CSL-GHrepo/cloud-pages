1. Cloudy or Green Pool Water: The Most Frequent Tamarac Pool Headache

Many Tamarac pool owners notice their pool water turning cloudy or green — a sure sign of algae or bacteria invasion, often caused by insufficient sanitation and poor water circulation.

  • Root Causes: Inadequate chlorine levels, poor filtration, or high phosphates/nitrates from local lawn runoff.
  • Fast Fix: Shock the pool immediately with a high dose of chlorine or a non-chlorine shock product designed for algae removal.
  • Preventive Measures: Maintain consistent chlorine levels (2-4 ppm), regularly clean filters, and skim debris daily.

Expert Tip: “Pools in Tamarac require more frequent shocking in the summer months due to higher UV exposure and rainfall, which introduce contaminants,” advises Laura Simmons, a Tamarac pool maintenance specialist.
