Understanding Tax Implications of Selling Your Home for Cash in Nevada

Selling a home for cash can have tax consequences that vary based on your individual situation. It’s important to be informed and seek professional advice:

  • Capital Gains Tax. If the property is your primary residence and you meet the IRS requirements, you may exclude up to $250,000 ($500,000 for married couples) of capital gains. Investment or rental properties are typically subject to capital gains tax.
  • Reporting Sale Proceeds. The sale must be reported on your tax return; your cash buyer should provide IRS Form 1099-S if required.
  • 1031 Exchange Opportunities. For investment properties, consider a 1031 Exchange to defer capital gains by reinvesting sale proceeds into a like-kind property, which may be relevant for cash sales.
  • Consult a Tax Advisor. Laws change frequently, and personal circumstances vary, so professional advice ensures compliance and efficient tax planning.
