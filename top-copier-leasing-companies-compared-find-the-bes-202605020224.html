Copier Lease vs Buy in 2024: What You Should Know

Deciding whether to lease or buy depends on your business’s financial flexibility and usage expectations. Consider these factors:

  • Upfront Costs: Leasing requires little to no upfront investment compared to purchasing outright.

  • Tax Benefits: Leasing payments may be fully deductible as a business expense, while purchasing depreciates over time.
  • Maintenance Costs: Leases often include maintenance, while ownership means budgeting for repairs separately.
