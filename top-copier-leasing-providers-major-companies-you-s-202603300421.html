What to Look for When Choosing a Copier Leasing Provider

Choosing the right copier_leasing company involves more than just comparing prices. Consider these key factors:

  1. Lease Agreement Flexibility: Look for rental contracts that allow upgrades or early termination without penalties.
  2. Comprehensive Maintenance: Ensure service and parts replacement are built into the agreement to avoid downtime.
  3. Technology Integration: Providers should offer multifunction copiers with current software and security capabilities.
  4. Financial Options: Transparent copier financing options such as fair market value leases vs. dollar buyouts.
  5. Managed Print Services: Providers that bundle managed print services help optimize print volume and reduce supply waste.
