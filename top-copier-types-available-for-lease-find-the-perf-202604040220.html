1. Multifunction Copier Machines (MFPs)

Multifunction copier machines, commonly known as MFPs, combine printing, scanning, faxing, and copying into a single device. They are an excellent choice for most offices due to their versatility and space-saving design.

  • Features: Print, scan, copy, fax, sometimes email and cloud services integration.
  • Ideal for: Small to medium-sized offices with varied document handling needs.
  • Benefits: Reduces the need for multiple devices, lowers office clutter, and streamlines workflows.
