What does regular Coral Springs pool maintenance typically include?

Regular pool maintenance in Coral Springs involves balancing water chemistry (pH, chlorine levels), cleaning filters and pumps, skimming debris, vacuuming the pool floor, brushing walls, and inspecting equipment for wear or damage.
