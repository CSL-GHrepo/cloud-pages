Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door Between Professional Visits

While trusting experts like The Garage Door Pros for comprehensive maintenance is ideal, homeowners can take simple actions to preserve their garage doors in good working condition:

  • Visual Inspection: Look for signs of wear, cracks, or unusual noises monthly.
  • Clean Tracks Regularly: Remove dirt, debris, and spider webs to keep door movement smooth.
  • Test Safety Features: Check auto-reverse sensors by placing an object in the door’s path during closing.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Use manufacturer-recommended lubricants on rollers, hinges, and springs every 6 months.
  • Check Weatherstripping: Replace damaged seals to prevent drafts and pests.

Following these simple tips complements professional maintenance, enhances safety, and extends hardware longevity.
