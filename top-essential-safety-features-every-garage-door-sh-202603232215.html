Preventative Maintenance Tips for Parkland Homeowners

Regular maintenance can significantly extend the life of your garage door while preventing costly repairs. Start by inspecting the moving parts and hardware for signs of wear or corrosion. Tighten loose bolts and screws to keep everything secure.

Lubricate rollers, hinges, tracks, and springs at least twice a year using a suitable garage door lubricant. Proper lubrication reduces noise and ensures smooth operation, especially in the humid Parkland climate.

Check the balance of your garage door by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door halfway. If it doesn't stay in place or feels heavy, call us for an inspection. Proper balance prevents strain on the opener and hardware.

Test the safety features of your garage door, such as the auto-reverse mechanism and sensor alignment. These features protect pets and children from injury and must work correctly at all times.

Schedule professional inspections annually—our team can identify issues early and perform necessary adjustments or replacements before they turn into emergencies.
