Preventing Mold Recurrence in Fort Lauderdale: What Experts Recommend

Once mold is removed, preventing future growth is critical. Because Fort Lauderdale’s humidity averages above 70% year-round, ongoing mold prevention must include:

  • Maintaining indoor humidity between 30-50% using dehumidifiers or air conditioners
  • Promptly repairing leaks or water intrusion
  • Ensuring proper ventilation in bathrooms, kitchens, and basements
  • Using mold-resistant paints and materials for renovations
  • Scheduling regular Fort Lauderdale mold inspections to catch issues early
