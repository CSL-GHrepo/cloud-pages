Tips to Prevent Mold Growth in Fort Lauderdale Homes and Businesses

While professional mold remediation is essential once mold is detected, prevention is always better. Fort Lauderdale residents can reduce mold risk by following these actionable tips:

  • Maintain Indoor Humidity Below 60%: Use dehumidifiers especially during humid summer months.
  • Fix Leaks Promptly: Check roofs, windows, and plumbing regularly.
  • Improve Ventilation: Use exhaust fans in kitchens and bathrooms.
  • Clean and Dry After Water Intrusion: Dry wet areas within 24-48 hours.
  • Regular Mold Inspections: Annual or biannual professional inspections recommended for Fort Lauderdale homeowners due to the subtropical environment.
