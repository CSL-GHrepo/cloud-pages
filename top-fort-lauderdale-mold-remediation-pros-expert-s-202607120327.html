Preparing for Mold Remediation: What Clients Should Know

Understanding what to expect before, during, and after mold remediation helps Fort Lauderdale residents and businesses plan accordingly and ensure smooth processes.

Pre-Remediation Preparations

  • Clear access to affected areas by removing furniture, personal items, and valuables.
  • Make arrangements for temporary relocation if remediation involves extensive demolition or strong odors.
  • Turn off HVAC systems as recommended to prevent spore spread.
  • Communicate special health needs to remediation providers, such as asthma or allergy sensitivities.

During Remediation

  • Licensed professionals set up containment, use negative pressure airflow, and apply HEPA filtration.
  • Removal of mold-affected materials follows strict safety and disposal protocols.
  • Regular updates often provided to clients on remediation progress.

Post-Remediation Steps

  • Clearance testing by third-party labs to confirm mold levels are safe
