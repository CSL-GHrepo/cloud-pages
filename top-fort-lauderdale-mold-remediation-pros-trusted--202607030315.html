Protecting Your Fort Lauderdale Home or Business from Mold

Prevention is as important as remediation. Here are actionable steps recommended by local indoor air quality experts:

  • Maintain indoor relative humidity between 30-50% using dehumidifiers and air conditioning.
  • Promptly repair plumbing leaks and roof damage.
  • Ensure proper ventilation in bathrooms, kitchens, and crawlspaces.
  • Regularly schedule professional Fort Lauderdale mold inspection if you live in high-risk areas, especially during the rainy season.

Routine inspections can catch mold problems early, saving costs and health risks down the line.
