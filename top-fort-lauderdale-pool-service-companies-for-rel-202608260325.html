Replacing single-speed pumps with energy-saving variable speed models reduces electrical usage while maintaining superior water circulation. These pumps are compliant with Florida’s energy regulations and increase filtration effectiveness.

  • Customizable speed settings optimize operation according to pool activity levels.
  • Quieter operation enhances outdoor enjoyment.
  • Potential energy savings of up to 80% compared to traditional pumps.

Energy-Efficient Heating Solutions

Modern pool heaters, including heat pumps and solar heaters, extend the swimming season with greater environmental and cost efficiency than older gas or electric models. Fort Lauderdale’s abundant sunshine makes solar heating particularly attractive.
