Tips for Maintaining Your Pool Between Professional Services

Even with professional maintenance, your pool benefits from basic homeowner care to extend the service provider’s efforts and reduce costs:

  • Regular Skimming: Remove leaves and debris daily using a pool net to prevent filter clogs.
  • Monitor Water Chemistry: Use a handheld test kit weekly to check chlorine and pH levels.
  • Maintain Water Level: Keep water at the mid-skimmer height to ensure proper filtration.
  • Run Filters Daily: Ensure pool pumps operate 8-12 hours daily during peak season to circulate chemicals and clean water.
  • Check Pool Equipment: Inspect pumps, heaters and timers regularly for unusual sounds or leaks.

Combined with routine professional visits, these steps help preserve a sparkling, inviting pool year-round.
