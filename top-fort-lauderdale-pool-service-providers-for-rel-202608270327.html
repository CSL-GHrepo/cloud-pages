Hurricane Preparedness for Fort Lauderdale Pools

Given Fort Lauderdale’s vulnerability to tropical storms and hurricanes, specialized pool service providers incorporate tailored hurricane readiness programs to minimize damage and downtime during storm seasons.

Pre-Storm Pool Care Best Practices

  • Lowering Water Levels: Reducing the pool water level prevents overflow and potential damage to the pool shell and surrounding deck during heavy rains.
  • Securing Equipment: Pumps, heaters, and filters should be disconnected and relocated to protected areas or properly anchored to withstand high winds.
  • Removing Loose Items: Everything from pool furniture to automatic covers must be cleared to prevent debris from becoming hazardous projectiles.
  • Chemical Stabilization: Balancing chemical levels prior to a storm reduces contamination risks from rainwater and debris influx.

Professional pool services in Fort Lauderdale typically provide storm prep checklists and emergency plans customized to individual pool setups.
