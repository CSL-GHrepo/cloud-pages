Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Parkland

Regular maintenance keeps your garage door operating smoothly and can prevent costly repairs down the line. We recommend inspecting your door and hardware at least twice a year, especially after Florida storms or heavy rain. — The Garage Door Pros serves the greater Parkland area.

Lubricate moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and tracks with a silicone-based lubricant. This reduces wear and prevents squeaking. Avoid using grease, which can attract dirt and debris.

Check the balance of your garage door by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting it halfway. The door should stay in place. If it doesn’t, call us for adjustment.

Test safety features like the auto-reverse and sensors monthly. Properly functioning safety features prevent injuries and property damage.

Keep the area around the garage door clear of debris, tools, and obstructions. A clean workspace helps prevent accidents and ensures easy access for repairs.

  • Inspect and lubricate moving parts biannually.
  • Test the auto-reverse and sensors monthly.
  • Check door balance by manual testing.
  • Clear clutter from around the garage door area.
  • Schedule professional inspections annually.
