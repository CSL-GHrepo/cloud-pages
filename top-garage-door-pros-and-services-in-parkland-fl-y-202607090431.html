How to Maintain Your Garage Door Between Professional Visits

While The Garage Door Pros provide thorough professional maintenance services, Parkland homeowners can take practical steps to care for their garage doors daily or weekly, extending the intervals between repairs and inspections.

  • Keep the tracks clean: Remove debris, cobwebs, and dust buildup that could impair smooth door movement.
  • Visually inspect springs and cables: Look for visible wear or rust, but never attempt DIY repairs as these components are under high tension.
  • Test auto-reverse safety features monthly: Place a lightweight object under the door and attempt to close it to ensure sensors react properly.
  • Lubricate moving parts periodically: Use silicone-based spray lubricants; avoid heavy grease that attracts dirt.
  • Check weather stripping: Ensure it is flexible and fully attached to prevent water infiltration and pest entry.

By integrating these simple yet effective habits, Parkland residents can reduce the risk of unexpected breakdowns and maintain optimal door function.
