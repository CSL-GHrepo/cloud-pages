Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Parkland Homeowners

Routine maintenance is crucial to keep your garage door functioning reliably and safely, especially in Parkland’s unique climate. While The Garage Door Pros offer professional inspections and tune-ups, there are simple DIY steps homeowners can take between professional visits to extend the lifespan of their garage doors.

Monthly Visual Inspection

Regularly inspect your garage door and components for visible wear or damage. Focus on:

  • Checking for loose or missing hardware such as nuts, bolts, and screws.
  • Inspecting cables and springs for fraying or rust.
  • Looking for dents or warping on door panels, particularly after storms.
  • Ensuring safety sensors are clear of dirt and aligned correctly.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Use a silicone-based lubricant or garage door-specific lubricant to reduce friction on springs, rollers, hinges, and tracks. Avoid using grease or oil-based products which can attract dirt and cause clogs over time.

Test Auto-Reverse Mechanism

Every month, place a small object like a piece of wood in the door’s path and close it to test the auto-reverse feature. The door should automatically lift upon contact with the obstruction. If it fails, contact The Garage Door Pros immediately for a safety inspection.

Balance Check

Disconnect your opener by pulling the release handle and manually lift the door halfway. If it doesn’t stay in place or is difficult to lift, the springs may need adjustment or replacement. Always leave spring repairs to professionals—as they are under high tension and can be dangerous.
