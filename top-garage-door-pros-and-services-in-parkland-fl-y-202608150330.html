Safety Tips Every Parkland Homeowner Should Know

Garage doors are heavy machinery with powerful components, and misuse or neglect can lead to injuries. The Garage Door Pros emphasize safety education to complement their professional services.

  • Children’s Safety: Teach children to stay clear during garage door operation and never to play near the door or opener controls.
  • Regular Testing of Safety Sensors: Ensure photo eyes and auto-reverse mechanisms work correctly to prevent accidents.
  • Avoid DIY Repairs: Springs and cables are under extreme tension; improper handling can cause serious injury.
  • Keep Remote Controls Secure: Prevent unauthorized access by keeping garage door remotes inside the home and limiting duplication.
  • Visual Inspections: Periodically check for visible signs of wear like frayed cables, bent tracks, or misaligned panels and schedule professional inspection before problems escalate.
