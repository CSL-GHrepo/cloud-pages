Garage Door Safety and Compliance in Parkland, FL

Safety is paramount when dealing with garage doors, which contain components under high tension and automated systems that can pose hazards. The Garage Door Pros prioritize compliance with the latest local codes and national safety standards, such as those developed by the Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) and Underwriters Laboratories (UL).

Key Safety Features and Regular Testing

  • Auto-Reverse Mechanism: Doors automatically reverse if an obstacle is detected during closing to prevent injury or property damage.
  • Photo-Eye Sensors: Installed at foot level to detect children, pets, or objects beneath the door.
  • Manual Emergency Release: Allows you to disengage the door from the opener and operate it manually during power outages or emergencies.
  • Spring Containment: Safety cables inside extension springs or enclosed spring systems that limit flying debris if a spring breaks.
  • Periodic Safety Inspections: Professional technicians check the integrity of these systems during routine maintenance visits.
“Ignoring garage door safety can lead to serious injuries or costly property damage. Expert inspection and adherence to safety standards are non-negotiable,” warns Michelle Bryant, Safety Inspector at Parkland Building Authority.
