Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Parkland Residents

Homeowners and businesses alike can extend the lifespan of their garage doors by following simple maintenance tips recommended by The Garage Door Pros:

  1. Lubricate all moving parts, including rollers and tracks, every six months to reduce wear.
  2. Inspect springs and cables quarterly for signs of fatigue or rust.
  3. Test safety sensors monthly to ensure automatic reversing is working correctly.
  4. Keep tracks free of debris and dirt to prevent binding and misalignment.
  5. Schedule professional spring tension adjustments annually to maintain balance and prevent unexpected breaks.

Implementing these garage door maintenance tips not only ensures safety and convenience but also optimizes door performance in Parkland’s challenging climate.
