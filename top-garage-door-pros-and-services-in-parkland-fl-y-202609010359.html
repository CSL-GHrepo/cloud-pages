Tips for Extending the Life of Your Garage Door in Parkland’s Climate

Aside from professional maintenance, there are several proactive steps homeowners can take to protect their garage doors and enhance performance in Parkland’s specific climate:

  • Regularly clean tracks and rollers: Remove dirt, debris, and salt buildup to prevent rust and ensure smooth movement.
  • Lubricate parts every 6 months: Use appropriate garage door lubricants on hinges, springs, and rollers to reduce wear and tear.
  • Inspect weather seals: Replace cracked or hardened seals at the bottom and sides to keep out moisture, pests, and drafts.
  • Test safety sensors monthly: Verify auto-reverse functionality to reduce injury risk and avoid accidents.
  • Balance the door: A properly balanced door prevents strain on openers and reduces mechanical failure.

Adopting these habits not only preserves the door’s mechanical health but also enhances overall safety and home comfort.
