Maintenance Tips to Prolong Your Garage Door’s Life

Preventive maintenance is the key to avoiding unexpected breakdowns. Industry professionals recommend regular checks every six months to maintain peak garage door performance. Here are essential garage door maintenance tips from The Garage Door Pros:

  1. Lubricate moving parts: Use silicone spray or garage-door lubricant on springs, rollers, and hinges to reduce friction.
  2. Test safety sensors: Ensure sensors are aligned and clean to prevent accidental closures.
  3. Inspect cables and springs: Look for signs of wear or rust and schedule replacement before a failure occurs.
  4. Tighten hardware: Vibration can cause bolts and nuts to loosen over time; regular tightening helps avoid mechanical issues.
  5. Balance the door: A properly balanced door reduces strain on the opener and prevents uneven wear.

Following these tips not only enhances safety but also extends the garage door’s operational lifespan, saving Parkland residents and businesses money while ensuring convenience.
