Seasonal Garage Door Care: Preparing for Weather Changes in Parkland

Parkland’s climate presents seasonal challenges that can affect garage door performance. Preparing your garage door before and after seasonal shifts can extend its lifespan and prevent costly repairs.

Pre-Hurricane Season Preparation

Given Florida’s susceptibility to hurricanes and tropical storms, reinforcing your garage door is crucial to protect your property. Recommended steps include:

  • Installing hurricane-rated doors or panels
  • Checking and tightening all hardware and brackets
  • Ensuring the door’s locking mechanism is secure
  • Verifying the backup power options for electronic openers

Summer Maintenance Tips

High heat and humidity during Florida summers can accelerate wear on rubber seals and metal components. It’s advisable to:

  • Lubricate moving parts more frequently
  • Inspect weatherstripping for cracks or mold
  • Test sensors and automatic reversal mechanisms regularly

Winter and Dry Season Considerations

Although milder, the drier months can cause materials to contract and weather stripping to lose flexibility. Winter checks include:

  • Ensuring smooth operation of springs and cables
  • Checking for gaps that could allow dust or pests
  • Verifying opener functions after temperature drops
