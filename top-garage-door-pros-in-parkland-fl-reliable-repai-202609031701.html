Common Garage Door Problems and How The Garage Door Pros Address Them

Garage doors involve multiple components working in harmony. When one part malfunctions, it can jeopardize the entire system’s function. Parkland residents frequently encounter the following issues, which The Garage Door Pros expertly diagnose and remedy:

Broken Springs and Cables

Garage door springs operate under high tension, counterbalancing door weight to allow smooth lifting and lowering. Over time or with repeated use, springs can snap or cables can fray, causing the door to become heavy and difficult to operate—or even dangerous to use.

The Garage Door Pros use industry-leading techniques to replace springs and cables safely, ensuring balanced and reliable door function. Because of the risk involved with high-tension components, attempting DIY repair is strongly discouraged.

Motor & Opener Failures

Garage door openers can fail due to electrical issues, worn-out motors, or sensor malfunctions. Symptoms include:

  • Door failing to open or close completely
  • Intermittent operation or no response to remote controls
  • Loud or unusual noises from the motor mechanism

The Garage Door Pros perform thorough diagnostics and repairs or replacement of openers, ensuring optimal performance. They also assist in selecting energy-efficient, quiet, and smart-capable motors.

Misaligned Tracks and Rollers

Tracks guide the door’s movement. Over time, impacts, ground settling, or rust can cause misalignment that leads to jerky motions, grinding noises, or the door becoming stuck.

The technicians expertly realign tracks and replace damaged rollers to restore smooth operation, often preventing more costly repairs down the road.
