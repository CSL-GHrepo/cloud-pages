Detailed Guide to Garage Door Spring Repairs and Safety Considerations

Garage door springs are one of the most critical components of your garage door system, responsible for counterbalancing the door’s weight for smooth operation. Unfortunately, spring repairs and replacements are intricate tasks that require expertise and safety precautions.

Types of Garage Door Springs

  • Torsion Springs: Mounted above the door, these springs wind and unwind to lift and lower the door smoothly.
  • Extension Springs: Located on either side of the door, these springs extend to provide the necessary counterbalance.

The Garage Door Pros specialize in both torsion and extension spring repairs with precision equipment and industry-standard safety measures.

Why DIY Spring Replacement Is Risky

Attempting to repair garage door springs without professional knowledge can result in serious injury or death. Springs are under extreme tension; improper handling can cause them to snap violently, potentially causing property damage or bodily harm.

Trained technicians from The Garage Door Pros use specialized tools such as winding bars and follow strict safety protocols, ensuring the replacement or adjustment is performed securely with no risk to homeowners or staff.

Signs Your Garage Door Springs Need Attention

  • Garage door becomes difficult to lift manually
  • Loud banging or snapping sounds during operation
  • Visible gaps or corrosion on springs
  • Door fails to stay open or closes too quickly

Addressing these issues promptly with The Garage Door Pros can prevent complete system failure and extend your garage door’s operational life.
