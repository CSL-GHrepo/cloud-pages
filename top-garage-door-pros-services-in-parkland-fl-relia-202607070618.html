Advanced Safety Features

Modern garage doors incorporate advanced safety features to protect users and prevent costly accidents. These include:

  • Auto-reverse mechanism: Sensors detect obstructions and automatically reverse the door’s movement if an object or person is detected underneath the closing door.
  • Infrared safety beams: Positioned along the door’s threshold, these beams trigger the door to stop or reverse if interrupted.

  • Battery backup systems: Guarantee continuous operation of automatic doors even during electrical failures, a useful feature during Florida storms and outages.
