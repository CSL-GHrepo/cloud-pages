Understanding Garage Door Safety: Protecting Your Family and Property

Garage doors are heavy, mechanically complex systems that pose significant safety risks if installed or maintained improperly. The Garage Door Pros emphasize comprehensive safety protocols for every service.

Key Safety Features and Best Practices

Ensuring the safety of your garage door involves more than just mechanical repair—it includes upgrading and verifying essential safety components:

  • Automatic Reversal Mechanisms: Sensors that detect obstructions beneath the door to prevent accidents
  • Properly Rated Springs and Cables: Using manufacturer-recommended parts to prevent unexpected breakage
  • Regular Professional Inspections: Quick identification of worn parts before failure occurs

Seasonal Garage Door Tips: Preparing for Parkland’s Weather Patterns

Parkland’s subtropical climate creates unique conditions that affect garage door performance throughout the year. The Garage Door Pros recommend seasonal maintenance and care tips to keep your door optimally functioning.

Spring and Summer

  • Inspect for rust or corrosion caused by salt air and constant humidity
  • Check and lubricate moving parts to reduce wear in hot temperatures
  • Ensure weather stripping is intact to minimize heat gain and insect intrusion

Fall and Winter

  • Test auto-reverse sensors after higher rain and storm frequency
  • Look for signs of water infiltration and seal gaps promptly
  • Perform balance checks to adjust tension affected by cooler temperatures
