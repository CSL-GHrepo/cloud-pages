Comprehensive Garage Door Safety Inspections: Beyond Basic Maintenance

Safety inspections conducted by certified technicians go deeper than routine maintenance, identifying hidden risks before they materialize into expensive damages or hazards.

Detailed Safety Inspection Checklist

  • Spring and Cable Integrity: Examining wear patterns, corrosion, and stress fractures beyond surface appearance.
  • Opener Auto-Reverse Testing: Verifying that sensors trigger stops or reversals correctly to prevent injuries.
  • Track Condition Assessment: Checking for warped, cracked, or improperly fastened tracks that could cause malfunctions or door drops.
  • Hardware Tightness and Alignment: Ensuring hinges, bolts, and brackets are secure to prevent sagging or misalignment.
  • Electrical System Safety: Inspecting wiring, motor function, and grounding to avoid shorts or fire risks.

Annual or biannual safety inspections are recommended for all Parkland property owners, with detailed reports and action plans offered by The Garage Door Pros to maintain continuous safety compliance.
