Common Garage Door Myths Debunked

Misconceptions about garage door maintenance and repair can lead to neglect or costly mistakes. Here are some common myths debunked by industry professionals at The Garage Door Pros.

Myth 1: “Garage doors don’t require regular maintenance.”

Reality: Regular maintenance is essential for safety and longevity. Ignoring routine inspections and lubrication often leads to premature failure and expensive repairs.

Myth 2: “You can fix garage door springs yourself.”

Reality: Garage door springs are under high tension and can cause serious injury if handled improperly. Spring repair or replacement should always be performed by licensed professionals.

Myth 3: “All garage door openers are the same.”

Reality: There are various types of openers (chain-drive, belt-drive, screw-drive), each with different noise levels, durability, and maintenance needs. Choosing the right opener enhances performance and longevity.

Myth 4: “Replacing a panel is always cheaper than a full door.”

Reality: While panel replacement might appear cheaper short-term, mismatched colors or materials and hidden structural issues could mean replacing the entire door is more cost-effective in the long run.

Safety Features and Regulations for Garage Doors in Parkland, FL

Compliance with local safety regulations and adopting advanced safety features is paramount. Parkland’s building codes require specific guidelines to safeguard residents and properties.

Mandatory Safety Features

  • Auto-Reverse Mechanism: Doors must reverse automatically if an obstruction is detected during closing to prevent injuries or damage.
  • Photo-Eye Sensors: Positioned near the floor, these sensors stop the door from closing if something crosses the beam.
  • Manual Release Handle: Allows the door to be disengaged from the opener during power outages or emergencies.
  • Force Settings: Ensures the door exerts minimal force during closing to reduce risk of injury.

Preparing Your Garage Door for Storm Season in Parkland

Parkland’s location in South Florida means hurricane and storm seasons bring increased risk of wind damage and debris impact. Proper preparation can mitigate damage and improve safety.

Storm-Ready Garage Door Tips

  • Reinforcement: Consider reinforcing garage doors with storm panels or upgrade to hurricane-rated doors designed to withstand high winds.
  • Regular Inspection: Check and tighten all hardware to reduce the risk of parts loosening under stress.
  • Weather Seals: Verify and replace weather stripping to keep out rain and wind-driven debris.
  • Emergency Release Accessibility: Ensure manual release mechanisms are functional and accessible in case of power outages during storms.
