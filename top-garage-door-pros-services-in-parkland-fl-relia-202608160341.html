Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for Parkland Residents

Parkland’s subtropical climate means your garage door faces unique challenges like heat, humidity, and occasional tropical storms. Proactively adapting your door care routine to seasonal changes can prevent premature wear and maintain smooth operation year-round.

Spring and Summer Care

  • Inspect and lubricate moving parts regularly to prevent heat-related expansion and friction.
  • Check weather stripping and seals to keep humidity and heat from entering your garage, improving energy efficiency.
  • Schedule a professional inspection after heavy rain or storms to detect any damage to tracks, springs, or sensors.

Fall and Winter Preparation

  • Although mild, winter storms can still cause damage—ensure door balance and performance are checked before cooler months start.
  • Test the auto-reverse feature on the door opener to ensure compliance with safety standards and the protection of family and pets.
  • Clear debris from tracks and surrounding areas to avoid obstruction and water accumulation.

By aligning maintenance routines with seasonal demands, Parkland homeowners can extend the life of their garage doors and reduce the risk of unexpected failures.
