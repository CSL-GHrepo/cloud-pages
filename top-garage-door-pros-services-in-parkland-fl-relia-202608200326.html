Maintenance Tips for Prolonged Garage Door Lifespan in Parkland

Understanding routine maintenance can empower Parkland residents to keep their garage doors operating smoothly and safely between professional service visits. Below are practical maintenance tips that homeowners can perform:

Monthly Checks

  • Inspect door balance by manually opening the door halfway; a balanced door should stay in place
  • Test auto-reverse safety features by placing an object beneath the closing door to ensure it reverses
  • Clean tracks and rollers, removing debris and dirt to prevent jams
  • Check and tighten loose bolts, nuts, and screws on hardware
  • Listen for unusual noises indicating wear on rollers or opener components

Biannual Maintenance

  • Lubricate moving parts such as hinges, rollers, and springs using appropriate lubricants recommended by manufacturers or professionals
  • Inspect weatherstripping and seals for cracks or gaps and replace as necessary
  • Clean sensors with a soft cloth to ensure accurate operation

Understanding Garage Door Springs: Critical Components and Care

Garage door springs are among the most essential but often overlooked parts of the system. They bear the load of hundreds of pounds, balancing the door’s weight to facilitate easy opening and closing.

Types of Garage Door Springs

  • Torsion Springs: Mounted above the door opening, these springs twist as the door opens and store energy to counterbalance the door’s weight
  • Extension Springs: Located alongside the upper tracks, they extend as the door closes and contract when it opens

Understanding which spring type your door uses helps with proper maintenance and identifying wear signs. Torsion springs generally last longer but require expert handling during replacement due to high tension.

Signs Your Garage Door Springs Need Attention

  • Difficulty lifting the door manually or door being heavier than usual
  • Visible gaps in the springs or corrosion caused by Parkland’s humid environment
  • Loud noises such as banging or snapping sounds when operating the door
  • Door failing to stay open or closing too quickly

Because springs store tremendous amounts of tension, repairs and replacements must be performed by trained professionals like The Garage Door Pros to ensure user safety and system integrity.
