Garage Door Maintenance Tips: Keep Your Door Running Smoothly

Routine maintenance is essential to avoid unexpected failure and costly repairs. The Garage Door Pros offers these top garage door maintenance tips for Parkland residents:

  1. Lubricate moving parts: Use a silicone-based lubricant on rollers, hinges, and springs every six months.
  2. Test safety sensors: Ensure sensors are clean and aligned for automatic reverse functions.
  3. Inspect weather stripping: Replace any cracked or missing seals to improve insulation and energy efficiency.
  4. Check door balance: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door to ensure smooth operation.
  5. Keep tracks clean and clear: Remove debris regularly and tighten loose bolts or screws.

Implementing these tips not only extends the lifetime of your garage door but also improves security and operational safety. Partnering with The Garage Door Pros for professional tune-ups guarantees optimal performance throughout Parkland’s variable seasons.
