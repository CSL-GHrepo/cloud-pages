Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Coral Springs

Routine care can extend your garage door’s lifespan considerably, avoid emergency repairs, and keep your home secure. Here are expert-recommended maintenance tips:

  • Inspect hardware monthly: Check springs, cables, rollers, and pulleys for signs of wear or rust.
  • Lubricate moving parts: Use silicone-based lubricant to prevent corrosion in Coral Springs’ humid environment.
  • Test automatic openers: Ensure safety sensors are aligned and functioning correctly.
  • Keep tracks clean: Remove debris regularly to prevent door misalignment or strain on the motor.
  • Schedule professional inspections: Have a certified local garage door technician inspect your system twice per year.
