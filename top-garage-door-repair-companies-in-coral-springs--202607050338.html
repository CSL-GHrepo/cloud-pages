Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Coral Springs

Regular Coral Springs garage door maintenance can prevent most emergency repairs and extend the lifespan of your equipment. Industry studies show that well-maintained garage doors reduce repair costs by up to 30% annually (Source: National Association of the Remodeling Industry).

  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply silicone-based lubricants to springs, rollers, and hinges at least twice a year.
  • Inspect Weather Seals: Replace worn-out seals to prevent moisture and pests.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Features: Ensure sensors work properly for family safety.
  • Check Balance and Alignment: Unbalanced doors strain openers and hardware.
