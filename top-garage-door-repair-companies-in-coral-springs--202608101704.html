How to Maintain Your Garage Door Between Professional Services

While expert maintenance is important, homeowners can take simple steps to keep their garage doors functioning smoothly between professional visits.

  • Visual Inspection: Check for signs of wear on springs, cables, rollers, and hinges monthly.
  • Lubrication: Apply garage door-specific lubricant to moving parts every 3-6 months to reduce friction and noise.
  • Balance Test: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway. A properly balanced door should stay in place without falling or rising.
  • Clean Tracks and Sensors: Remove debris and dirt that could obstruct smooth operation or sensor detection.
  • Test Safety Features: Regularly test auto-reverse mechanisms by placing an object under the door to ensure it reverses properly when obstructed.
  • Remote and Keypad Batteries: Replace batteries annually to maintain uninterrupted access.

Document your observations and share them with your garage door technician during maintenance visits to enable targeted inspections and timely repairs.
