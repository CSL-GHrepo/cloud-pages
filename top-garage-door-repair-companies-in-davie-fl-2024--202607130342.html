How Seasonal Changes Affect Your Garage Door and What You Can Do

While Florida’s climate remains warm year-round, slight seasonal variations bring specific challenges that impact garage door functionality and durability.

Spring and Summer: Moisture and Heat Challenges

  • High humidity encourages rust and mildew buildup requiring frequent cleaning and lubrication;
  • Frequent rainstorms can damage weather seals, necessitating inspection and replacement;
  • High temperatures cause metal parts to expand, sometimes affecting door alignment and opener sensitivity.

Fall and Winter: Cooler Temperatures and Storm Preparedness

  • Cooler nights may cause contraction of metal components, contributing to noisy operation;
  • Storm season demands robust readiness, including verifying hurricane-rated door integrity and backup power for openers;
  • Debris from fall winds can obstruct tracks or sensors, highlighting the need for regular cleaning.

Scheduling bi-annual maintenance before peak summer and hurricane seasons can help Davie residents minimize risks and avoid emergency repairs.
