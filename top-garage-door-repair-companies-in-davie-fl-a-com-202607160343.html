Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door Between Professional Services

Regular attention to your garage door can prevent many common problems and extend its operational life. Here are some expert-recommended maintenance tips for Davie residents:

  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply silicone-based lubricants to rollers, hinges, and springs every six months to reduce wear and noise.
  • Check Balance: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway; it should stay in place without assistance. Imbalance indicates spring or cable issues.
  • Inspect Weather Stripping: Replace cracked or deteriorated seals to maintain energy efficiency and keep out pests.
  • Keep Tracks Clean: Remove debris or buildup along the tracks to prevent misalignment.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Function: Place a block under the door and close it; the door should reverse upon contact, ensuring sensor functionality.
  • Monitor Noise Levels: Squeaks or grinding sounds may signal the need for timely professional inspection.

Performing these routine checks and minor upkeep can reduce emergency service calls and prolong your garage door’s lifespan.
