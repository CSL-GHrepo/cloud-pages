Recommendations for DIY Garage Door Troubleshooting Before Calling a Pro

Although many garage door repairs require professional expertise, some minor issues can be easily checked or temporarily resolved by homeowners while awaiting service. Here are some troubleshooting tips recommended by industry professionals:

  • Check Power Supply: If your garage door opener isn’t responding, confirm that it’s plugged in and that circuit breakers or GFCI outlets haven’t tripped.
  • Inspect Remote Batteries: Weak or dead batteries are a common cause of unresponsive remotes.
  • Clear Obstructions: Make sure nothing is blocking the door’s path or obstructing sensors, as safety mechanisms will prevent door operation.
  • Manual Release: Use the emergency release cord to manually open and close the door if the motor malfunctions.
  • Listen for Noises: Unusual grinding or squeaking may point to lubrication needs or hardware misalignment.

Remember that working with garage door springs and cables can be hazardous. If you’re unsure, it’s always safer to rely on trained Davie professionals.
