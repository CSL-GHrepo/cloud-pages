How Seasonal Changes Affect Garage Door Performance in Davie

Davie’s subtropical climate subjects garage doors to unique challenges requiring seasonal awareness and adjustment. Garage door experts recommend the following seasonal tips to maintain optimal operation:

Summer Heat and Humidity

  • Metal Expansion: High temperatures can cause metal parts to expand, potentially leading to sticking or misalignment of door tracks and rollers.
  • Lubrication: Applying weather-resistant lubricants regularly helps reduce friction and prevents rust caused by humidity.
  • Opener Overheating: Ensure proper ventilation around electric openers to avoid overheating and performance decline.

Winter and Rainy Season

  • Contracting Components: Cooler temperatures can tighten springs and cables, causing stiffer operation.
  • Water Damage Prevention: Routine inspection and replacement of weather seals prevent water ingress that could damage electrical systems or wood doors.
  • Drainage Checks: Clearing debris from drainage paths near the garage door prevents pooling water and erosion.

Professional maintenance visits scheduled seasonally can catch issues early, limiting downtime and expensive repairs.
