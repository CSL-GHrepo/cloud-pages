Tips to Maintain Your Garage Door Between Repairs

Even the best repair companies will tell you that ongoing maintenance is key to a long-lasting garage door. Here are some simple steps every Davie resident can take:

  1. Lubricate Moving Parts: Use a silicone spray on rollers, hinges, and springs every six months.
  2. Check Door Balance: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway. It should stay in place without drifting.
  3. Inspect Weather Seals: Harsh weather in Florida demands good sealing to keep water and debris out.
  4. Test Garage Door Opener Safety Features: Regularly test auto-reverse mechanisms to prevent accidents.
  5. Schedule Professional Garage Door Maintenance Davie: Twice-yearly inspections by experts can identify issues before they escalate.
