Seasonal Garage Door Care: Tailoring Maintenance for Davie’s Climate

The subtropical humidity and heat cycles in Davie, FL require unique maintenance strategies to protect your garage door investment. Considering seasonal care can significantly reduce wear and prolong service life.

Spring & Summer Maintenance

  • Lubricate All Moving Parts: Heat increases friction and wear; high-quality silicone or lithium-based lubricants help components move smoothly.
  • Check for Corrosion and Rust: Metals are particularly vulnerable during the wet season; early detection prevents extensive damage.
  • Inspect Weather Seals: Replace damaged strips to prevent moisture intrusion and insect entry, especially important during rainy months.

Fall & Winter Maintenance

Although Florida’s winters are mild, temperature fluctuations can still stiffen door mechanisms.

  • Test Auto-Reverse Sensors: Cold mornings can affect sensor sensitivity; ensure they function properly for safety.
  • Examine Springs and Cables: Cooler temperatures can exacerbate metal fatigue; early spring replacement may be necessary.
  • Clean Tracks and Rollers: Debris accumulated during the fall can cause misalignment; cleaning helps preserve smooth door operation.
