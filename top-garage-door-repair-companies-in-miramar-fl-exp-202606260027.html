Preventing garage door failures begins with regular maintenance, especially in Miramar’s humid, coastal environment where metal components can rust and weather seals degrade faster. Industry experts recommend the following:

  • Schedule bi-annual inspections for springs, cables, and rollers.

  • Test the door’s balance and automatic reversal features quarterly for safety.
