Maintaining Your Garage Door in Miramar: Tips from the Experts

Regular maintenance extends your door’s life and prevents unexpected breakdowns. Miramar garage door maintenance specialists recommend:

  • Lubricate moving parts every 3-6 months to prevent rust.
  • Inspect and tighten hardware annually, especially due to humidity-induced looseness.
  • Test safety sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms monthly.
  • Schedule professional inspections bi-annually, particularly before hurricane season.
