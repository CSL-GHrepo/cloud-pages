Maintaining Your Garage Door in Pembroke Pines: Pro Tips

Regular maintenance can prolong the life of your garage door and prevent emergencies. Here are some maintenance tips tailored for Pembroke Pines homeowners:

  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Use silicone-based lubricants to reduce friction on rollers, hinges, and springs.
  • Inspect Weather Seals: Protect against rain and humidity by replacing worn weather stripping promptly.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Function: Ensure the door reverses safely upon hitting an object to prevent injuries and damage.
  • Balance Check: An unbalanced door strains the opener and hardware—hire local technicians for professional assessments.
  • Schedule Annual Professional Maintenance: According to a study by the International Door Association, professional garage door maintenance reduces emergency repair incidents by 25% per year.
