How Seasonal Changes Affect Garage Doors in Pembroke Pines and How to Prepare

Although Pembroke Pines enjoys a fairly consistent climate, seasonal variations still impact garage door condition and operation. Understanding these influences helps with timely maintenance and repair planning.

Summer Challenges: Heat and Humidity

High temperatures and humidity can cause:

  • Material expansion leading to warping or misalignment.
  • Increased metal corrosion from salt air near coastal areas.
  • Opener motor overheating.

Technicians recommend lubrication of moving parts and inspection of seals to prevent heat ingress during summer months. Regular maintenance visits before the peak heat season mitigate these effects.

Storm Season Precautions

From June to November, South Florida faces tropical storms and hurricanes. These weather events can inflict damage such as:

  • Dents or impact damage from flying debris.
  • Warped tracks or bent frames from strong winds.
  • Electrical failures due to power surges or water intrusion.
