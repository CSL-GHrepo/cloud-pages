2. Misaligned Tracks and Rollers

Tracks can shift slightly over time because of daily wear or external impacts, causing rollers to jam or produce grinding noises. This misalignment can escalate into further damage if ignored.

  • Signs include uneven door movement and loud scraping sounds

Understanding Garage Door Safety Features for Plantation Homes

Modern garage doors incorporate numerous safety features to protect users and property. When selecting or upgrading your Plantation garage door, be sure to consider these components:

Auto-Reverse Mechanism

This feature detects obstacles under the closing door and automatically reverses the motion to prevent injury or damage. Regular testing of auto-reverse sensors is critical, especially in family homes with children or pets.

Photoelectric Sensors

Installed near the floor on either side of the door frame, these sensors emit invisible beams that stop the door if interrupted. Plantation technicians recommend routine cleaning and alignment to maintain accuracy in these humid conditions.

Manual Release Handles

Located inside the garage, manual release cords allow homeowners to disconnect the door from the opener system, enabling manual operation during power outages—a common occurrence during Florida storms.

Pinch-Resistant Panels and Finger Guards

New doors often feature designs aimed at preventing fingers from getting caught between panels—an especially important safety consideration for homes with young children.
