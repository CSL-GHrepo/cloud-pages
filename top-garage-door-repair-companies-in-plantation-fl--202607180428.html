Common Garage Door Problems and How Plantation Experts Fix Them

Understanding common issues helps homeowners know when to call professionals rather than attempt risky DIY repairs.

  • Broken or Worn Springs: Springs bear most of the door’s weight and often break from wear and tear. Technicians safely remove and replace springs using specialized tools to avoid injury.
  • Malfunctioning Openers: Issues can stem from electrical faults, gears, or remote misalignment. Experts diagnose opener malfunctions, perform mechanical repairs, or install replacements if needed.
  • Off-Track Doors: Tracks can become bent or misaligned due to impact or debris. Skilled technicians realign and clean tracks to restore smooth operation.
  • Noisy Garage Doors: Squeaks and grinding noises usually indicate worn rollers or lack of lubrication. Maintenance includes cleaning, lubrication, and component replacement if necessary.
  • Sensor and Safety Equipment Failures: Sensors often get misaligned or dirty, triggering door malfunctions. Technicians clean, align, or replace sensors to ensure safety protocols function correctly.

Timely repairs by Plantation garage door experts ensure longevity and safety of your door system, avoiding more costly and dangerous malfunctions.
