Understanding Garage Door Safety Features: Protecting Your Family and Property

Safety remains paramount when dealing with garage doors, which have heavy moving parts and powerful mechanisms. Plantation garage door repair companies emphasize the importance of keeping safety features functional, including:

Safety Sensors and Auto-Reverse Mechanisms

Modern garage doors are equipped with photoelectric sensors installed near the floor on either side of the door opening. These sensors detect obstructions and automatically reverse the door if an object or person is detected during closing, preventing injuries and damage.

Regular maintenance should include sensor alignment checks and functionality tests because misaligned or dirty sensors can cause the door to malfunction, posing a safety hazard.

Manual Release Handles

Emergency manual release handles permit users to disengage the garage door from its automatic opener system, allowing manual operation during power outages or mechanical failures. Plantation technicians verify that these handles are accessible and operable during service calls to avoid entrapment scenarios.

Spring Containment and Maintenance

Garage door springs store significant tension and can be dangerous if they snap or fail. Professionals in Plantation ensure springs are correctly balanced and replaced before they become a safety risk. Some companies also install spring containment cables, which help prevent springs from flying off if broken, protecting family members and property.

Preparing Your Garage Door for Seasonal Changes in Plantation

Rainy Season Precautions

  • Check and Replace Weatherstripping: Ensures effective waterproof sealing to prevent leakages inside the garage.
