DIY Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Plantation Homeowners

While professional maintenance should be scheduled annually, there are simple checks and upkeep tasks Plantation residents can perform to keep their garage door system in good condition between service visits:

  • Visual Inspection: Look for any obvious signs of damage such as bent tracks, frayed cables, or cracked panels.
  • Clean Tracks: Remove debris and dirt from the tracks using a dry cloth to prevent jams.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply garage door-specific lubricant (avoid WD-40) on rollers, springs, and hinges every 6 months.
  • Test Safety Sensors: Make sure the auto-reverse safety function is working by placing an object under the door and trying to close it.
  • Tighten Hardware: Check bolts and screws, tightening any loose components to reduce wear.

Despite these helpful tips, any signs of malfunction or broken components should always prompt immediate professional service to avoid injury or further damage.
