Common Garage Door Problems and the Repair Process Explained

Understanding typical garage door issues can empower homeowners in Plantation, FL to recognize when professional help is needed and what to expect from repairs.

Frequent Garage Door Problems

  • Broken Springs: Springs counterbalance the door’s weight to aid smooth movement. A broken spring can cause the door to become stuck or dangerously fall.
  • Malfunctioning Openers: Wiring, sensors, or motor problems can prevent the door from opening or closing properly.
  • Misaligned Tracks: Tracks that are bent or misaligned interfere with door movement and cause grinding noises.
  • Worn Cables: Steel cables lift the door in conjunction with springs; frayed or snapped cables create unsafe conditions.
  • Weather Seal Damage: Cracks or gaps in seals lead to draft, pests, and water leaks.
