Understanding Garage Door Components and Common Repair Issues

Having a basic understanding of garage door components can help homeowners communicate effectively with technicians and identify when repairs are needed.

Key Components to Know

  • Springs: Torsion or extension springs counterbalance the weight of the door, making lifting manageable.
  • Rollers and Tracks: Rollers glide the door along tracks, ensuring smooth movement.
  • Openers and Remotes: Electric garage door openers provide automated door lifting with remote controls or wall switches.
  • Safety Sensors: Infrared sensors prevent a door from closing if an object or person blocks the path.
  • Panels and Weatherstripping: Panels form the door’s structure, and weatherstripping provides insulation and protection against the elements.

Common Garage Door Problems in Weston, FL

  • Broken Springs: Springs wear out with use and are typically the most common cause of garage door failure. Experts warn against DIY spring repairs due to the high tension involved.
  • Misaligned Tracks: Tracks can bend or shift, causing the door to jam or operate noisily.
  • Worn Rollers: Old or damaged rollers create uneven movement and excessive noise.
  • Sensor Malfunctions: Dirt or misaligned sensors cause the door to stop or reverse unexpectedly.
  • Opener Electrical Issues: Power surges or wiring problems can affect remote control and automation features.
  • Corrosion and Rust: Salt air and humidity in South Florida accelerate corrosion of metal parts, necessitating more frequent replacement or maintenance.

Timely identification and service of these issues by experienced professionals can prolong the life of your garage door and minimize safety risks.
