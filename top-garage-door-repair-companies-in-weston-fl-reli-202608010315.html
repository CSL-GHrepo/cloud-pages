Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance Strategies for Weston's Climate

Weston's subtropical climate with distinct wet and dry seasons demands proactive garage door maintenance adapted to seasonal challenges. Planning maintenance around these seasonal cycles can extend the door’s lifespan and reduce sudden repair needs.

Pre-Summer Maintenance

  • Inspect for Humidity-Related Wear: High humidity promotes rust and corrosion, especially on metal springs, rollers, and tracks.
  • Lubricate Metal Components: Using corrosion-resistant lubricants helps mitigate rust development and ensures smoother operation during the hot months.
  • Check Door Opener Electronics: Elevated summer temperatures can cause overheating in motors.
  • Seal and Weatherstrip: Ensure weather strips are intact to maintain cooler garage temperatures and prevent moisture ingress.

Post-Hurricane or Storm Maintenance

  • Track Alignment Inspection: Strong winds may cause track bending or loosening.
  • Panel Damage Assessment: Impact from debris or hail requires quick repair or replacement to maintain security.
  • Sensor Realignment: Storm vibrations can misalign safety sensors, leading to door malfunctions.

Winter Season Tips

Although Weston's winters are mild, cooler temperatures can affect garage door rubber seals, making them brittle and less effective.

  • Replace Weather Stripping: Annually inspect rubber seals and replace if cracked or hardened.
  • Test Door Balance: Improper door balance in cooler weather may cause unnecessary strain on opener motors.
