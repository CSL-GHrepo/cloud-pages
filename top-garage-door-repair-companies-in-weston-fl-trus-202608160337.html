DIY Garage Door Maintenance Tips to Reduce Repairs

While complex repairs should be left to professionals, regular homeowner maintenance can prevent many issues and prolong the life of your garage door system. Here are expert-recommended tasks that Weston residents can perform safely.

  • Regular Visual Inspections: Look for signs of wear on springs, cables, rollers, and tracks. Early detection can prevent major failures.
  • Lubrication: Applying garage door-specific lubricant to moving parts every 3-6 months minimizes friction and noise.
  • Balance Testing: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway. It should stay in place without assistance—if not, the springs may need adjustment.
  • Sensor Cleaning: Wipe down photo-eye sensors to ensure unobstructed signals and proper functioning.
  • Track Cleaning: Remove debris and ensure tracks are free from dents or bends to allow smooth door movement.

Note: Homeowners should never attempt spring replacements or complex electrical repairs due to safety risks.
