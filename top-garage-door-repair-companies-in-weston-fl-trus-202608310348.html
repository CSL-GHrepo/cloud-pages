How to Maintain Your Garage Door for Longevity in Weston’s Climate

Regular maintenance is key to avoiding costly repairs and ensuring your garage door functions smoothly despite South Florida’s challenging weather conditions. Professional garage door companies typically offer maintenance packages, but homeowners can also perform simple checks and upkeep tasks between service appointments.

Essential Maintenance Tips

  • Lubricate moving parts: Springs, rollers, hinges, and tracks should be lubricated every six months with a high-quality silicone or lithium-based lubricant to minimize friction and prevent rust.
  • Inspect weather seals: Check for cracks, gaps, or brittleness in bottom and side seals; replace promptly to maintain energy efficiency and keep out pests.
  • Clean and clear tracks: Remove debris, dirt, or leaves from tracks to prevent binding or misalignment.
  • Test garage door balance: Disconnect the opener and manually move the door halfway up; a balanced door should hold position without sagging or jerking.
  • Check safety sensors: Most modern garage doors have photo-eye sensors that stop closing if an obstacle is detected. Ensure these are clean and aligned for optimal safety.
  • Replace worn hardware: Regularly inspect cables and springs for signs of wear; early replacement avoids dangerous breakdowns.

"Routine maintenance extends the life of your garage door system by at least 30%, saving homeowners hundreds in repairs or premature replacements," advises Lisa Martinez, a garage door technician from Weston.
