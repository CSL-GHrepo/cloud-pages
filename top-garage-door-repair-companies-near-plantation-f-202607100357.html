Preventive Maintenance Tips to Extend Your Garage Door’s Lifespan

Regular preventive maintenance is one of the best ways to avoid costly garage door repairs and replacements. Plantation’s humid climate and frequent use make it essential to perform routine inspections and upkeep.

  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Applying garage door lubricant to springs, hinges, rollers, and tracks at least twice a year reduces friction, prevents rust, and keeps the door operating smoothly.
  • Inspect and Tighten Hardware: Regularly check bolts, screws, and brackets for looseness or wear. Vibrations from door movement can loosen hardware over time, leading to unsafe conditions.
  • Test the Balance of Your Door: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway. A properly balanced garage door should stay in place. If it moves up or down, it may need spring adjustment, which requires professional service.
  • Check Safety Sensors: Ensure that the photo-eye sensors on either side of the door are clean and aligned. Tampered or dirty sensors can impede proper door operation and create safety risks.
  • Clean Tracks and Remove Debris: Dirt, leaves, or buildup on tracks can obstruct the door's path. Wiping the tracks clean and ensuring no obstacles exist prevents jams and roller damage.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Feature: Periodically test the door’s auto-reverse by placing an object in its path while closing. Automatic reversal upon contact is critical to avoid accidents, especially with children and pets.

Scheduling seasonal maintenance with a local Plantation garage door company can also help identify small problems early. Most providers offer maintenance plans that combine inspection, lubrication, and minor adjustments for a fixed fee, reducing emergency repair needs.
