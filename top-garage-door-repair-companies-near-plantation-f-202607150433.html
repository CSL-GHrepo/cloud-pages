Safety Tips for Plantation Homeowners: Garage Door Usage and Maintenance

Beyond professional repairs and maintenance, homeowners can play a crucial role in ensuring their garage doors remain safe and functional. Plantation garage door specialists recommend:

  • Regular Visual Inspections: Look for signs of wear such as frayed cables, sagging doors, and rusted springs.
  • Testing Automatic Reverse Features: Ensure that photoelectric eyes and reverse sensors respond promptly to obstacles, as required by safety regulations.
  • Avoid DIY Repairs on High-Tension Components: Springs and cables are under extreme tension and should only be handled by licensed professionals to avoid injury.
  • Keep Remote Controls Secure: Prevent unauthorized garage door access by regularly changing codes and safely storing remotes.
  • Routine Lubrication: Apply manufacturer-recommended lubricants to rollers, tracks, and hinges to reduce friction and prolong lifespan.

Emphasizing safety awareness helps reduce emergency trips to garage door repair services and protects families from accidents.
