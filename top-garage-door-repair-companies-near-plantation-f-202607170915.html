Understanding Garage Door Spring Types and Their Importance

The spring system is a critical component of your garage door mechanism, counterbalancing the door’s weight to allow smooth operation. In Plantation, where humidity and salt air can accelerate corrosion, knowing the types of springs and their specific pros and cons can help you make better maintenance and repair decisions:

  • Torsion Springs: Mounted above the door, torsion springs provide efficient counterbalance and are typically longer-lasting under proper maintenance. They offer smoother door operation and are preferred by most professionals for residential and commercial doors.
  • Extension Springs: Located on either side of the door tracks, extension springs stretch and contract as the door moves. While often less expensive, these springs require more frequent replacement and precise safety cables to avoid accidents.
  • High-Cycle Springs: Designed for increased durability, these springs can handle up to 25,000 to 100,000 cycles, ideal for high-traffic garage doors in commercial settings or busy households.
“Choosing the right spring type and maintaining it properly can extend the life of your garage door system and prevent unexpected failures,” notes John Martinez, Plantation garage door technician with extensive experience in spring repair and replacement.

Garage door repair companies in Plantation often provide spring assessments and recommend upgrades that best suit your door’s usage and environmental exposure.
