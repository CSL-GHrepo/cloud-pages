Common Garage Door Problems and How Plantation Technicians Diagnose Them

Understanding typical garage door issues helps homeowners communicate effectively with repair professionals and make informed service decisions. Here are some frequently encountered problems and diagnostic steps:

Broken Springs

Garage door springs bear significant tension and naturally degrade over time. Technicians typically:

  • Inspect spring condition and note corrosion, gaps, or breaks
  • Determine suitable spring type (torsion or extension) for replacement
  • Test door balance and counterweight to ensure proper operation post-replacement

Garage Door Won’t Open or Close

Common causes include motor failure, sensor misalignment, or track obstructions. Diagnostic steps include:

  • Running diagnostic tests on the motor and opener unit
  • Checking photo-eye sensors for debris, damage, or improper alignment
  • Examining tracks for bends, dents, or foreign objects

Unusual Noises During Operation

Grinding, squeaking, or popping sounds usually signal lubrication issues, worn rollers, or misaligned hardware. Professionals will:

  • Visually inspect rollers and hinges for wear or damage
  • Apply manufacturer-recommended lubricants at key points
  • Check and tighten loose components

Door Opens or Closes Partially

This behavior typically results from broken springs, opener sensitivity settings, or sensor issues. Repair specialists diminish this problem by:

  • Adjusting force and limit settings on the garage door opener
  • Replacing or repairing springs or cables as needed
  • Verifying that sensors are unobstructed and properly aligned
