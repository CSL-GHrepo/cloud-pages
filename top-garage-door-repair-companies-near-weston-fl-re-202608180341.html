Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance: Preparing for Weston’s Weather

Weston’s subtropical climate requires homeowners to be proactive about garage door maintenance to prevent weather-related issues. Understanding seasonal concerns can help extend the functional life of your garage door.

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Inspect for Rust and Corrosion: Spring humidity can accelerate rust on metal components. Regularly applying rust inhibitors and replacing rusty parts can prevent failure.
  • Clean and Lubricate Moving Parts: Heat and moisture can cause dirt build-up and wear. Use silicone-based lubricants on tracks, hinges, bearings, and springs every 3-4 months during these seasons.
  • Check Weather Stripping and Seals: Ensure seals are intact to prevent humid air and pests from entering the garage, which can damage stored belongings.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Test Auto-Reverse and Safety Sensors: Evening shorter daylight hours mean increased garage use in low light. Ensure sensors are clean and well-aligned to avoid accidents.
  • Inspect Door Balance: Temperature fluctuations can affect spring tension. Proper balance ensures smooth operation and reduces motor strain.
  • Prepare for Storm Season: Investigate reinforcing options for garage doors to withstand high winds—an important consideration during hurricane season.

Hiring a local garage door professional for comprehensive seasonal checkups provides tailored advice and services to mitigate region-specific risks, saving money on costly repairs later.
