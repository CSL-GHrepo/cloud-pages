Safety Considerations and Compliance in Garage Door Repairs

Safety is paramount when dealing with garage door repairs, especially for high-tension components and electrical systems. Plantation repair professionals follow strict safety protocols to protect homeowners and technicians.

Key Safety Guidelines

  • Spring Handling: Torsion springs store immense energy; improper handling can cause severe injury. Experts use specialized tools to safely release and install springs.
  • Sensor Calibration: Safety sensors must be precisely aligned to detect obstacles and reverse door motion, preventing accidents.
  • Electrical Safety: Repair on openers and wiring is conducted following local electrical codes to avoid fire risks.
  • Load Testing: Post-repair tests ensure door balance and smooth operation, avoiding unexpected failures.
