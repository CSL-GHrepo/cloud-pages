Aluminum Garage Doors: Lightweight and Corrosion-Resistant

Aluminum doors offer excellent resistance to rust and corrosion, making them ideal for Florida’s salty air. They are lightweight, which reduces wear on springs and opener mechanisms.

  • Disadvantages: Less impact resistant compared to steel, can dent under heavy forces

Tips for Homeowners: Extending the Life of Your Garage Door System

Beyond professional services, homeowners in Pembroke Pines can take proactive steps that contribute to the longevity of their garage door system.

  • Regular Cleaning: Wash door surfaces and tracks to remove salt deposits, dirt, and mold
  • Lubrication: Apply garage door lubricant to rollers, hinges, springs, and tracks twice a year, especially before hurricane season
  • Avoid DIY Spring or Cable Repairs: These components store dangerous tension and should only be serviced by certified professionals
  • Balance Testing: Occasionally disconnect the opener and manually lift the door to test spring balance; it should stay open about 3-4 feet above closed without falling or rising rapidly
  • Sensor Cleaning: Wipe garage door safety sensors clean monthly to avoid obstructions caused by dust or spider webs

Consistant monitoring and gentle care delay premature failures and reduce emergency repair needs.
