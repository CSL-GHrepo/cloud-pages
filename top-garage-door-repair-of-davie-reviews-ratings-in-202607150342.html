Preventive Maintenance Tips: Maximize the Life of Your Garage Door

Alongside professional maintenance services, Garage Door Repair of Davie educates customers on simple preventative steps homeowners can take to extend the life of their garage doors and avoid costly repairs.

DIY Inspection Checklist

  • Visual Inspection: Check tracks, springs, cables, and rollers for signs of wear, rust, or damage monthly.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Feature: Place an object under the door to ensure it automatically reverses upon contact.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Use recommended garage door lubricant on rollers, hinges, springs, and tracks at least twice a year.
  • Clean Sensors: Wipe photo-eye sensors clean to prevent sensor misalignment or blockage.
  • Weather-Seal Maintenance: Inspect and replace worn weather stripping to prevent water and pest infiltration.
