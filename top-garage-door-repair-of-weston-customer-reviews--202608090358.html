Customer Education and DIY Tips Backed by Garage Door Repair Professionals

Safety First: Avoiding Common DIY Pitfalls

Garage doors are heavy and involve complex spring mechanisms that store significant tension. Untrained attempts to repair or adjust springs, cables, or openers can cause severe injury or equipment damage. Garage Door Repair of Weston strongly advises:

  • Never attempting to adjust or remove springs without professional tools and training.
  • Turning off power to automatic openers before performing any cleaning or minor troubleshooting.
  • Keeping children and pets away from the door area when operating or performing DIY inspections.

Regular Simple Tasks Homeowners Can Perform

Beyond safety concerns, there are many maintenance tasks within the scope of the average homeowner, including:

  • Visual Inspection: Checking for visible wear, dents, or misalignment on door panels and tracks at least quarterly.
  • Track Cleaning: Removing debris such as dirt, leaves, or cobwebs that could hinder door motion.
  • Lubricating Moving Parts: Applying manufacturer-recommended lubricant to hinges, rollers, and tracks once or twice yearly to reduce friction.
  • Testing Safety Sensors: Ensuring photo-eye sensors are properly aligned and functional by placing an object in the door path during closure tests.
