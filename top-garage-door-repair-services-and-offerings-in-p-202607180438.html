Understanding Common Garage Door Problems and How Experts Address Them

While many garage door issues may seem straightforward, attempting DIY repairs can be risky and inefficient. Garage Door Repair of Plantation educates customers on common problems and explains why professional intervention is often the best choice.

Typical Garage Door Problems

  • Broken springs: Springs endure significant stress and can snap, causing sudden door failure and potential injury if handled improperly.
  • Malfunctioning openers: Issues may arise from worn-out motors, faulty sensors, or electrical problems.
  • Noisy operation: Squealing or grinding noises often indicate worn rollers, lack of lubrication, or misaligned tracks.
  • Door misalignment or imbalance: Results in uneven movement, excessive strain on components, and safety risks.
  • Damaged panels or weather seals: Affect security, insulation, and overall aesthetics.
