Seasonal Tips: Preparing Your Garage Door for Plantation’s Climate

Garage doors in Plantation face distinct seasonal challenges due to the subtropical environment. Homeowners can extend door lifespan and maintain performance by following tailored seasonal maintenance tips:

Spring and Summer

  • Inspect weather stripping for wear or gaps to keep out humidity and pests.
  • Lubricate metal parts more frequently to counteract corrosion accelerated by humidity and salt air.
  • Check automatic opener batteries and backup systems in case of summer thunderstorms or power outages.
  • Test sensors and safety features thoroughly to prevent malfunctions during high use.

Fall and Winter

  • Remove debris around tracks to reduce obstruction and ice buildup.
  • Examine springs and cables for signs of fatigue from temperature fluctuations.
  • Consider upgrading to insulated doors to preserve indoor temperature during cooler months.
  • Schedule professional maintenance before hurricane season to secure the door against high winds and heavy rains.
