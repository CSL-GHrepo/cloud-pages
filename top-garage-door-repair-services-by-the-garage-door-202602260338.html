Local Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Parkland Residents

Keeping your garage door in top shape requires more than just annual inspections. Simple, routine maintenance can prevent costly repairs. Here are some tips based on my experience working with Parkland homes.

First, visually inspect your door regularly for signs of wear or damage. Look for frayed cables, rust, or misaligned tracks. Catching these early saves money and hassle.

Second, lubricate moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and tracks every few months. Use a silicone-based lubricant to prevent squeaks and reduce friction. This keeps the door operating smoothly and quietly.

Third, test safety features monthly. Place an object in the door’s path to ensure automatic reversal functions properly. This is vital for family safety and complies with industry standards.

Fourth, keep the area around your garage door clear of debris, leaves, or ice, especially in the rainy season. Obstructions can interfere with sensors and hardware, causing malfunctions.

Finally, consider scheduling professional tune-ups annually. Our team can perform comprehensive checks, adjust spring tension, and replace worn parts—saving you money and extending your system’s life.
