Tips for Preventive Garage Door Maintenance in Coral Springs, FL

Prevention remains the best way to avoid emergency repairs. Coral Springs homeowners can extend the life of their garage doors by following these maintenance tips:

  • Regular Lubrication: Spray moving parts with silicone-based lubricant every six months.
  • Inspect Springs and Cables: Look for rust, wear, or fraying that indicates the need for spring replacement.
  • Test Safety Features: Monthly checks of auto-reverse sensors ensure safe operation.
  • Clean Tracks and Rollers: Remove debris to prevent binding and uneven movement.
  • Schedule Professional Inspections: Annual tune-ups by Coral Springs overhead door service specialists.
