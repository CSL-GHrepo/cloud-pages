Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Coral Springs

To minimize unexpected repairs, consider the following maintenance tips recommended by local experts:

  1. Regularly lubricate moving parts such as hinges, rollers, and springs with suitable garage door oil.
  2. Inspect door balance by disconnecting the opener and manually moving the door halfway; it should stay in place.
  3. Test safety sensors monthly to ensure the door reverses when obstructions are detected.
  4. Schedule annual professional Coral Springs garage door maintenance to catch small issues before they become costly repairs.
  5. Replace worn weather seals to protect interior spaces from Florida storms and pests.
