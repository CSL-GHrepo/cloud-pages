Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Coral Springs Homeowners

Maintaining your garage door goes beyond occasional repairs. Incorporate these key maintenance tips recommended by Garage Door Repair of Coral Springs to enhance performance and ensure safety:

  1. Lubricate Moving Parts: Use quality garage door lubricants on rollers, bearings, and tracks every six months to reduce friction and wear.
  2. Inspect Springs and Cables: Look for signs of rust, fraying, or imbalance, especially given Coral Springs’ humid weather.
  3. Test Auto-Reverse Feature: Industry safety standards recommend monthly testing of your door opener’s reverse system to protect children and pets.
  4. Clean Tracks and Remove Debris: Dirt buildup can disrupt door movement, so keep tracks clear and aligned.
  5. Check Weather Seals: Replace worn-out weatherstripping to prevent water damage and improve energy efficiency.
