How to Maintain Your Garage Door in Coral Springs

Simple maintenance steps can extend your garage door’s functionality and reduce repair needs:

  1. Regular lubrication: Apply grease to hinges, rollers, and springs every six months.
  2. Inspect weather stripping: Replace cracked or damaged seals to keep moisture and pests out.
  3. Test safety sensors: Clean and align to prevent accidental closures.
  4. Balance the door: Ensure proper tension to avoid unnecessary strain on openers.
  5. Schedule annual professional inspections: Use trusted Coral Springs service providers to catch issues early.
