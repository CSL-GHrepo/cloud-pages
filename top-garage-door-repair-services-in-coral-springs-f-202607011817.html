Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Coral Springs

Proactive maintenance is key to avoiding inconvenient breakdowns. Here are practical steps Coral Springs homeowners can follow:

  1. Regular Inspection: Check for visible wear on springs, cables, rollers, and hinges every 3-6 months.
  2. Lubricate Moving Parts: Use silicone or lithium-based lubricants on rollers, tracks, and springs to minimize friction and noise.
  3. Test the Opener: Make sure the garage door opener responds correctly and safety sensors are in proper alignment.
  4. Schedule Professional Maintenance: Affordable garage door maintenance plans from trusted local garage door companies can save money and prevent emergencies.
