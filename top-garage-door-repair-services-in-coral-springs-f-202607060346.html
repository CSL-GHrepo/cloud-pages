How to Maintain Your Garage Door in Coral Springs

Many homeowners overlook simple maintenance tasks that extend the life of their garage doors. Follow these essential tips recommended by experts at Garage Door Repair of Coral Springs:

  1. Perform monthly visual inspections: Look for signs of wear such as rust, cracks, or frayed cables.
  2. Lubricate moving parts: Use appropriate garage door lubricants on rollers, hinges, and springs every six months.
  3. Test safety sensors: Ensure sensors reverse the door when an obstruction is detected.
  4. Check door balance: A properly balanced door eases strain on the opener and reduces wear.
  5. Schedule professional tune-ups: A certified technician can identify hidden problems and prevent costlier repairs.

Consistent garage door maintenance in Coral Springs not only preserves the door’s functionality but also adds value to your home.
