Expert Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Coral Springs

Regular maintenance prevents most garage door failures. Here’s a checklist recommended by local Coral Springs technicians to keep your door in prime condition:

  1. Inspect springs and cables for rust and wear every three months.
  2. Lubricate hinges, rollers, and tracks with silicone-based lubricant or garage door grease every six months.
  3. Test safety sensors regularly to ensure automatic reverse functions are working.
  4. Check the balance of the door by disconnecting the opener and manually moving the door halfway—if it doesn’t stay, the springs likely need adjustment.
  5. Clean the exterior surfaces and repaint if necessary to protect from Coral Springs’ sun and moisture exposure.

Follow these steps alongside professional yearly inspections to minimize costly repairs.
