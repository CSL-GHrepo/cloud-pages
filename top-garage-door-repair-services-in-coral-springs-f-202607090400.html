Advanced Safety Features: Protecting Your Family and Employees

New garage door installations and retrofits incorporate enhanced safety mechanisms to prevent injury and property damage. These features include:

  • Photoelectric Sensors: Infrared beams detect objects or people in the door’s path and automatically stop or reverse door movement.
  • Force Settings: Opener motors are programmed to reverse if they encounter unusual resistance, preventing crushing hazards.
  • Manual Release Mechanisms: In case of power failure, users can quickly disengage the opener for safe manual operation.
  • Battery Backup Systems: Guarantee garage door functionality during outages, especially important during Florida storms and hurricanes.
