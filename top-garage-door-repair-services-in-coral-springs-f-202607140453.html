Common Garage Door Problems and How Experts Address Them

Understanding frequent garage door issues can help homeowners recognize symptoms early and seek professional help before problems worsen. Garage Door Repair of Coral Springs specializes in identifying and fixing a wide range of common malfunctions.

Spring Breakage and Wear

Garage door springs endure significant tension and wear over time, eventually breaking or losing strength. A broken spring can make it impossible to lift the door manually or with an opener and may cause the door to slam shut unexpectedly.

Expert technicians carefully measure and replace springs to manufacturer specifications, ensuring balanced door movement and longevity.

Sensor Misalignment or Failure

Safety sensors prevent garage doors from closing on objects, pets, or people. Misaligned sensors can result in doors refusing to close or reversing unexpectedly.

The technicians at Garage Door Repair of Coral Springs recalibrate and test these sensors during routine maintenance or emergency visits to maintain optimal safety operation.

Track Obstructions and Damage

Dents, bends, or accumulated debris in garage door tracks can impede smooth movement and cause the door to get stuck or make grinding noises.

Professional repair involves realigning or replacing damaged sections and thorough cleaning to restore effortless motion.

Opener Malfunctions

Garage door openers are complex electronic devices prone to mechanical and electrical issues such as motor failure, wiring damage, or remote control problems.

Garage Door Repair of Coral Springs technicians provide diagnostics and repairs tailored to all major brands and models, often restoring function without full opener replacement.
