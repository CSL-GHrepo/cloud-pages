Safety Features and Compliance in Garage Door Systems

Safety is a paramount concern not just during repair but in the overall design and operation of garage doors. Recent regulatory changes and technological advancements have introduced mandatory safety features and best practices.

Auto-Reverse Mechanism

Since 1993, all residential garage door openers sold in the U.S. must include an automatic reverse feature. This mechanism detects obstructions and reverses door movement to prevent injury or damage.

Garage Door Repair of Coral Springs rigorously tests this functionality during every service to ensure compliance and protect families.

Photoelectric Sensors

Photoelectric eyes are installed at the base of garage door tracks to sense objects or people in the door's path. When the sensor beam is interrupted, the door stops and reverses.

Technicians routinely inspect and calibrate these sensors, as dust or misalignment can cause malfunctions or false alarms.

Manual Release and Emergency Access

In cases of power outages or mechanical failure, a manual release lever allows users to disengage the opener and operate the door manually. Proper installation and labeling are essential for quick emergency access.

Garage Door Repair of Coral Springs advises homeowners and business managers on proper emergency procedures and sets up backup battery systems when necessary.
