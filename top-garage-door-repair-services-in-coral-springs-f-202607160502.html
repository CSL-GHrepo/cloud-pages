Photoelectric Eye Sensors and Auto-Reverse Mechanisms

Modern garage doors incorporate photoelectric eye sensors—infrared beams placed near the bottom of the door frame—that detect any obstruction in the door’s path. If these sensors register an object or person, the door automatically stops closing and reverses to avoid injury or damage.

Garage Door Repair of Coral Springs routinely tests and calibrates these sensors during maintenance visits to ensure:

  • Accurate detection of small and large objects
  • Proper alignment between sensor pairs
  • Instantaneous door response to sensor triggers

Failure of these components can pose serious risks, especially in homes with children or pets, making professional checks essential.

Emergency Manual Release and Backup Systems

Power outages are common in South Florida during stormy seasons. To guarantee garage door accessibility even without electricity, technicians install and maintain emergency manual release cords with easy-to-operate mechanisms.

Additionally, advanced openers often include battery backup systems enabling doors to open and close multiple times without power. Garage Door Repair of Coral Springs evaluates battery health and replacement needs to ensure uninterrupted operation during emergencies.
