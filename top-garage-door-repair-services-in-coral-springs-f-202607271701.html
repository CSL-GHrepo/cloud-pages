Seasonal Garage Door Care: Preparing Your Coral Springs Garage for Weather Changes

The subtropical climate of Coral Springs poses unique challenges, with hot summers, heavy rains, and occasional storms. Season-specific garage door maintenance and preparation help mitigate weather-related damage and prolong door life.

Spring and Summer Maintenance Tips

  • Inspect and lubricate moving parts: High temperatures can dry out lubricants, leading to squeaks and strains.
  • Check weatherstripping: Replace cracked or brittle seals to prevent moisture intrusion during summer thunderstorms.
  • Test opener battery backups: Storms can cause power outages, so ensuring backup systems function properly is essential.
  • Clean door surfaces: Frequent washing removes debris, pollen, and salt build-up from nearby roads.

Fall and Winter Considerations

  • Examine tracks for rust: While Coral Springs winters are mild, occasional cold snaps can cause condensation and corrosion.
  • Adjust tension springs: Temperature changes affect spring elasticity, so timing adjustments may be necessary to avoid malfunction.
  • Seal cracks or gaps: Prevent cooler air infiltration and pest entry by maintaining door seals.
  • Schedule professional inspections: Address latent issues before the holiday season when garage door use increases.
