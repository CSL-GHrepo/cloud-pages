Maintaining a tight seal between the garage door and floor prevents drafts, dust, pests, and water intrusion. Weather stripping and robust bottom seals are especially important during rainy seasons and hurricanes.

Corrosion-resistant coatings and galvanized or stainless-steel hardware help combat the corrosive effects of coastal air, prolonging the door’s lifespan and reducing maintenance needs.
