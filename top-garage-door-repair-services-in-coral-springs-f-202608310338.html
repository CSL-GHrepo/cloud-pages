Preventive Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Coral Springs Homeowners

Industry professionals recommend maintaining your garage door at least twice a year to avoid emergency repairs. Here are easy tips tailored for Coral Springs residents:

  • Inspect and lubricate moving parts: Oil hinges, rollers, and springs to prevent rust from the humid Florida air.
  • Check balance and alignment: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway — it should stay in place without sliding up or down.
  • Test safety sensors: Ensure the photoelectric sensors reverse the door if an object is detected.
  • Examine door seals and weather stripping: Replace worn seals to prevent rain and pests from entering.
  • Schedule a professional inspection: Certified technicians can detect issues invisible to untrained eyes.

Regular garage door maintenance Coral Springs not only prolongs door life but also boosts operational safety, avoiding costly repairs.
