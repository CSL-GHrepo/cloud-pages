Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Davie, FL

Preventive maintenance is vital to avoiding costly repairs and prolonging your garage door’s lifespan. Here are expert-recommended tips for Davie homeowners:

  1. Schedule Biannual Inspections: Check cables, springs, rollers, and the opener system every six months.
  2. Lubricate Moving Parts: Use silicone-based lubricants on hinges and rollers to prevent rust and squeaking.
  3. Test Safety Sensors: Ensure automatic reverse mechanisms work reliably to prevent accidents.
  4. Replace Weather Seals: Keep seals intact to block humidity, pests, and debris from entering your garage.
  5. Call Professionals for Repairs: Avoid DIY fixes on springs and cables—these components are under high tension and dangerous to handle.
