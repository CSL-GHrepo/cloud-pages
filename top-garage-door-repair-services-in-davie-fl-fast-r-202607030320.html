Maintaining Your Garage Door: Tips from Professional Technicians

Garage door safety and longevity depend on proper upkeep. Garage Door Repair of Davie recommends these routine maintenance tips:

  1. Lubricate moving parts: Springs, rollers, and tracks should be lubricated every six months to reduce wear.
  2. Inspect safety sensors: Ensure sensors are clean and aligned, especially after storms.
  3. Check for balance: An unbalanced door strains your opener and springs—seek professional help if it feels heavy or jerky.
  4. Test your door’s auto-reverse: This important safety feature should reverse the door if it detects an obstacle.

Regular professional inspections can reduce emergency repairs and extend your door’s service life.
