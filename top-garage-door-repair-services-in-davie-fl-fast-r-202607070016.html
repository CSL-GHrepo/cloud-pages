Detailed Look at Garage Door Spring Repair and Replacement

Garage door springs are among the most critical yet vulnerable components of any garage door system. Understanding the different types and repair approaches provides homeowners in Davie with valuable insights into this common repair.

Types of Garage Door Springs

  • Torsion Springs: Mounted above the door, these springs store mechanical energy by twisting. They are preferred for their durability and smoother operation.
  • Extension Springs: Located on either side of the door, extension springs stretch and contract. They are typically used on lighter garage doors but require precise balancing.

Signs Your Garage Door Springs Need Repair or Replacement

  • Garage door becomes heavy or difficult to open manually
  • Loud noises such as banging or snapping during operation
  • Visible gaps or deformation in springs
  • Door fails to stay open or closes abruptly

Attempting to repair or replace springs without professional knowledge can be dangerous due to the high tension involved. Garage Door Repair of Davie’s trained technicians follow stringent safety protocols to handle these repairs safely and efficiently.
