DIY Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Davie Residents

While professional maintenance is crucial for safety and longevity, there are several simple tasks homeowners can perform regularly to keep their garage doors in good shape. Below are practical tips suitable for Davie’s climate:

  • Visual Inspection: Monthly walkthroughs to check for visible damage such as dents, rust spots, or loose hardware.
  • Lubrication: Applying garage door lubricant (specific for rollers, hinges, and springs) every 3-6 months helps reduce noise and prevent wear.
  • Track Cleaning: Clearing debris and dirt from tracks improves door operation and prevents misalignment.
  • Weather Seal Check: Inspect and replace worn bottom seals to keep moisture, pests, and hot air out.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Function: Place an object under the door and close it to ensure the door reverses promptly for safety.

It’s important to avoid attempting repairs involving springs or electrical components, as these require professional expertise and specialized tools.
