Maintenance Tips to Avoid Common Garage Door Problems

Regular maintenance can prevent many common garage door issues, saving time and money. Here are simple steps any Davie resident can follow:

  1. Inspect and Lubricate Moving Parts: Use a suitable lubricant on springs, rollers, and tracks at least twice a year.
  2. Check Door Balance: Disconnect the opener and manually raise the door—if it falls quickly or is uneven, call professionals.
  3. Test Safety Features: Ensure sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms work correctly.
  4. Remove Debris from Tracks: Clean out dirt and grease that could hinder smooth operation.

Following these guidelines will complement professional services and extend the lifespan of your garage door system.

Seasonal Considerations: Preparing Your Garage Door for South Florida Weather

Davie’s climate demands proactive measures to protect your garage door from the effects of heat, humidity, storms, and hurricanes.

Pre-Hurricane Season Inspections

Before the hurricane season (June to November), a thorough inspection identifies vulnerabilities such as weakened springs, loose hardware, or failing weatherstripping that could lead to damage during high winds.

  • Reinforcement: Installing wind-rated garage doors or adding bracing kits can improve resistance.
  • Seal Integrity: Check seals and replace worn-out weatherstripping to prevent water intrusion.
  • Emergency Preparedness: Ensure emergency manual release functions smoothly in case of power loss.

Managing Humidity and Corrosion

High humidity accelerates corrosion of metal components, potentially shortening door lifespan. Annual lubrication and corrosion inhibitor treatments are recommended.

Storm Damage Repairs

After storm events, prompt damage assessments and repairs prevent further complications like water damage, mold, and security risks.
