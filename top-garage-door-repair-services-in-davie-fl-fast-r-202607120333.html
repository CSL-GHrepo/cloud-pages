Safety Considerations: How to Protect Your Family and Property

Garage doors are among the largest moving parts in your home and can present serious safety hazards if neglected or improperly maintained. Beyond functionality, prioritizing safety safeguards your family and prevents costly liability.

Regular Safety Inspections

Professionals recommend routine inspections to check for:

  • Auto-Reverse Functionality: Modern doors should include sensors that stop and reverse the door when an object or person is detected beneath it.
  • Spring Tension and Condition: Springs under high tension can be dangerous if broken. Ensuring proper adjustment and integrity is critical.
  • Pinch Points and Moving Parts: Exposed mechanisms should be inspected to avoid finger or hand injuries.
  • Manual Release Mechanism: Verify that the emergency release is accessible and functioning, allowing manual operation during power outages or failures.
