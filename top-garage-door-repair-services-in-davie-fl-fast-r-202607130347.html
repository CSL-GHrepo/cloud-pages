How to Choose the Right Garage Door Material for Your Davie Property

Selecting a garage door material suited to Davie's environment, aesthetics, and functionality ensures durability and value. Each material type offers distinct benefits and considerations:

Steel Garage Doors

Steel is the most common material and offers strength, security, and affordability. Steel doors can be insulated for energy efficiency and coated with rust-resistant finishes suitable for humid, salty air environments like Davie.

Aluminum Garage Doors

Aluminum doors are lightweight and resistant to corrosion, making them excellent choices for coastal areas. Their modern look pairs well with contemporary homes but they may dent more easily than steel.

Wood Garage Doors

Wood provides a classic, attractive appearance and excellent customization options. However, wood requires more maintenance—including sealing and staining—to prevent weather damage and warping in South Florida’s climate.

Fiberglass Garage Doors

Fiberglass doors are lightweight, resistant to dents, and good insulators. They handle moisture well but can yellow over time in direct sunlight.
