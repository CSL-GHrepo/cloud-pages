Seasonal Garage Door Tips for Davie Residents

Seasonal weather changes, although less dramatic in Davie than in other parts of the country, still require proactive garage door care to optimize performance and longevity. Garage Door Repair of Davie recommends the following tips to keep your garage door in top condition throughout the year.

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Inspect and clean weather seals to prevent moisture ingress and insect intrusion
  • Lubricate rollers, hinges, and springs regularly to minimize friction and wear
  • Check electronic sensors and circuit boards for any moisture-related damage
  • Wash door panels to remove salt deposits and other corrosive elements from coastal breezes

Fall and Winter Preparation

  • Test door balance and adjust springs to account for cooler temperatures affecting metal tension
  • Ensure proper insulation to prevent cold drafts if garage spaces are temperature sensitive
  • Verify that automatic openers have functioning battery backups in case of storms or power outages
  • Examine and replace any worn weather stripping to maintain an effective seal against wind and rain

Seasonal upkeep minimizes unexpected malfunctions and costly repairs, while also extending the life of your garage system.
