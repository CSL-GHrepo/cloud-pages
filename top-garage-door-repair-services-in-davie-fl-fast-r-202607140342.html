Addressing Common Garage Door Problems with Expert Solutions

Garage doors comprise many parts that can experience wear or failure over time. Understanding common issues can expedite diagnosis and repair. Garage Door Repair of Davie’s technicians are trained to troubleshoot and resolve all typical problems efficiently, often in a single service call:

Broken Springs and Cables

Garage door springs bear immense tension to balance the door’s weight. Breakage can cause the door to suddenly drop and become unsafe. Similarly, worn or snapped cables affect door lifting and can damage other components. Technicians replace springs and cables using proper tools, ensuring balanced operation and safety compliance.

Misaligned or Damaged Tracks

Tracks guide the door’s movement; dents or bending from impact or debris cause the door to malfunction, jam, or derail. Garage Door Repair of Davie inspects alignment and condition, performing repairs or full track replacement to restore smooth operation.

Faulty Sensors and Safety Mechanisms

Modern garage doors include infrared sensors that detect objects or people in the door’s path to prevent accidents. Dirty, misaligned, or damaged sensors cause frequent reversals or failure to close. Thorough inspection, cleaning, and adjustment are standard protocols in sensor repair.

Worn Rollers and Hinges

Rollers and hinges enable smooth rolling of the door. Over time, they may corrode or become noisy, impeding door movement. Upgrading to nylon rollers or heavy-duty hinges can extend lifespan and reduce noise, a popular solution offered by Garage Door Repair of Davie.

Opener Malfunctions

From electrical issues to worn gears and circuit board failures, opener problems require technical expertise for safe and prompt resolution. The team performs detailed diagnostics and recommends repair versus replacement based on cost-effectiveness and reliability.
