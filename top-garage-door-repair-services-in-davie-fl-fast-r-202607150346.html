Preparing for Seasonal Garage Door Challenges in Davie

Davie experiences a mix of weather challenges throughout the year that affect garage door performance. Proactive seasonal care helps prevent costly damage and ensures uninterrupted door function.

Summer and Hurricane Season Precautions

  • Inspect and reinforce doors: Ensure all panels and hardware are securely fastened to withstand high winds and storms.
  • Clear drainage paths: Prevent water buildup around door tracks and thresholds to avoid corrosion and swelling.
  • Check weather seals: Replace worn seals to minimize moisture intrusion during heavy rains.
  • Test emergency manual release: Ensure operability during power outages common in severe weather.

Winter and Cooler Months Tips

  • Lubricate moving parts: Prevent freeze-up and reduce wear in cooler, sometimes damp conditions.
  • Inspect torsion springs: Cold weather can make springs brittle; early detection of weaknesses is critical.
  • Address condensation: Check for any signs of moisture inside the garage and improve ventilation to prevent mold and rust.
