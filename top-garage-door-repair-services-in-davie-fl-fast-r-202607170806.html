Maintenance Tips for Homeowners to Extend Garage Door Lifespan

While professional maintenance is crucial, regular homeowner care can prevent some common issues and extend the life of your garage door. Garage Door Repair of Davie recommends the following:

  • Regular Cleaning: Wash the door and tracks with mild detergent and water to prevent buildup of dirt and debris that may cause wear.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Use appropriate lubricants on springs, hinges, rollers, and tracks every 6 months to reduce friction and noise.
  • Check Door Balance: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway; it should stay in place. If it does not, springs may need adjustment.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Mechanism: Place a small object in the door’s path to ensure it reverses upon contact, maintaining safety.
  • Inspect Weatherstripping: Replace cracked or damaged seals to prevent moisture and pests from entering the garage.
  • Monitor Noise and Movement: Address grinding, squeaking, or jerky door movement early by scheduling professional inspection.

Following these tips can lower repair costs, reduce downtime, and keep garage doors functioning reliably between professional service appointments.
