Tips for DIY Garage Door Care: What You Can Do to Maintain Your Door

While professional service is essential for repairs and complex maintenance, homeowners can perform simple tasks to prolong the life of their garage doors effectively.

  • Visual inspection: Regularly check for visible signs of wear such as frayed cables, chipped paint, or excessive rust.
  • Clean tracks and rollers: Remove debris to prevent jams or noisy operation, using a damp cloth without heavy solvents.
  • Lubricate moving parts: Apply a silicone-based lubricant to rollers, hinges, and springs at least twice a year.
  • Test safety sensors: Wave an object in the sensor beam to ensure the door reverses as expected, contacting your technician if it does not.
  • Balance check: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway—it should stay in place without drifting up or down.

If any abnormalities are detected during these checks, it is advisable to contact a professional rather than attempting complicated repairs.
