5. Weather Seal Damage

Safety Tips for Garage Door Owners in Davie, FL

While professional repair and maintenance are crucial, homeowners and business owners in Davie should also practice safety measures to prevent accidents and prolong door lifespan. Here are expert recommendations from Garage Door Repair of Davie:

  • Never attempt to repair springs or cables yourself: These components are under extreme tension and can cause serious injury.
  • Regularly test the auto-reverse feature: Place an object in the door’s path and observe if the door reverses upon contact.
  • Keep sensors clean and properly aligned: This ensures reliable operation of the safety mechanisms.
  • Keep hands and tools away from moving parts: Especially the tracks, rollers, and springs.
  • Disconnect power before performing minor DIY tasks: Such as cleaning or lubricating door surfaces.
  • Schedule professional maintenance at least once a year: Especially important in Floridian weather conditions that can hasten component wear.
