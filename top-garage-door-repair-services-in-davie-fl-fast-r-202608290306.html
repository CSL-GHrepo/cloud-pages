Tips for Garage Door Safety and Troubleshooting at Home

While professional service is essential for repairs, homeowners can take simple precautions to prolong the life of their garage doors and avoid common issues:

  • Regular visual inspections: Look for signs of wear such as frayed cables, rust, or loose hardware.
  • Listen for unusual noises: Grinding or squeaking sounds often indicate lubrication or alignment problems.
  • Keep tracks clean: Remove debris and ensure tracks are free from dents or damage.
  • Test safety features monthly: Use the door’s auto-reverse function by placing an object in the path; the door should reverse immediately.
  • Avoid DIY repairs: Handling springs and cables is dangerous; always hire a professional for repairs.
