Tips for Prolonging the Life of Your Garage Door System in Davie

Beyond professional maintenance, homeowners and business owners can take simple steps to extend the life of their garage doors and reduce the likelihood of emergency repairs. Here are practical recommendations:

  • Keep Tracks Clean and Aligned: Remove debris like leaves or dirt from the door tracks regularly to prevent jamming or uneven wear.
  • Inspect Weatherstripping: Check seals at the bottom and sides of the door for cracks or gaps and replace them as needed to maintain insulation.
  • Listen for Unusual Sounds: Creaking, grinding, or popping noises can indicate worn springs or rollers – prompt attention can avoid bigger issues.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Mechanism Monthly: Place an object under the door to ensure it reverses promptly on contact, protecting children and pets.
  • Maintain Balanced Door Movement: Manually lift the door to test if it stays in place at halfway; if it falls or rises, professional rebalancing is needed.
  • Avoid DIY Adjustments on Springs: Springs are dangerous to adjust without tools and training – call a pro for safety.
  • Consider Seasonal Lubrication: Use silicone-based or lithium grease lubricants on moving metal parts twice per year, ideally in spring and fall.
  • Secure Remote Controls and Keypads: Change access codes regularly and monitor for any lost or stolen remotes.
