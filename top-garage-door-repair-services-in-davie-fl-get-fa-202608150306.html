Preventive Garage Door Care Tips for Davie Homeowners

In addition to professional maintenance and repairs, homeowners can take simple steps to prolong garage door life and reduce emergency situations.

Regular Visual Inspection

  • Check for visible wear or damage on springs, cables, rollers, and tracks.
  • Look for gaps or warping in door panels caused by moisture exposure.
  • Test the door balance by disconnecting the opener and manually lowering the door halfway; it should stay in place without drifting.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Apply garage door lubricant to springs, rollers, hinges, and tracks every six months to reduce friction and prevent rust buildup. Avoid using heavy grease, which can attract dirt and gum up moving parts.

Test Safety Features Monthly

Test auto-reverse sensors and the door’s resistance to closing on objects by placing a sturdy item under the door’s path. If the door does not reverse promptly, schedule professional service immediately.

Monitor Opener Remote Batteries and Signals

Weak remote signals or failing batteries can cause intermittent garage door operation. Replace remote batteries yearly and keep the area around sensors clean and free from obstructions.
