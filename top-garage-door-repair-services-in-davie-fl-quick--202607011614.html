Expert Tips for Maintaining Garage Doors in Davie’s Climate

Davie’s warm, humid environment and occasional thunderstorms demand extra care for garage doors. Here are expert recommendations from seasoned technicians:

  1. Regular Lubrication: Use a silicone-based lubricant on hinges, springs, and rollers every 3-6 months to prevent rust and ensure smooth operation.
  2. Inspect for Wear and Tear: Check cables, springs, and tracks for fraying, dents, or corrosion frequently, especially post-storm.
  3. Test Safety Features: Monitor automatic reverse and photo-eye sensors monthly to maintain safety compliance.
  4. Balance the Door: Improperly balanced garage doors can strain motors and springs; professional adjustment every year is advisable.
  5. Address Repairs Promptly: Broken garage door fix jobs should never wait to avoid injury or further damage.
