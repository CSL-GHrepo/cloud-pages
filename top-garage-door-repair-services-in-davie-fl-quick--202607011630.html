Tips for Affordable Garage Door Maintenance

Extending the life of your garage door and avoiding costly emergency repairs is possible with simple maintenance steps recommended by Garage Door Repair of Davie:

  1. Regularly lubricate springs, rollers, and hinges with garage door-safe lubricants.
  2. Check and tighten hardware to prevent loosening from vibrations.
  3. Test auto-reverse safety features monthly to ensure proper operation.
  4. Keep tracks clear of debris and inspect for dents or bends.
  5. Schedule professional inspections annually to catch early signs of wear.
