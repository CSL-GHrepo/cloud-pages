How to Recognize When You Need Garage Door Repair in Davie

Not all garage door problems are obvious, but some red flags include:

  1. Garage door moves unevenly or hesitates when opening/closing.
  2. Unusual noises such as grinding or squeaking.
  3. Remote or keypad opener stops working or has intermittent response.
  4. Door won’t close completely or reverses unexpectedly.
  5. Visible damage to springs, cables, or panels.

Ignoring these signs can result in permanent damage or safety hazards. According to the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission, thousands of injuries happen annually due to malfunctioning garage doors, underscoring the need for professional repairs.
