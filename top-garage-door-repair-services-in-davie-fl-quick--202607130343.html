Understanding Common Garage Door Problems: Causes & Prevention

Besides fixing urgent issues, understanding the root causes behind common garage door problems can help homeowners prevent future breakdowns. Here is a detailed look at typical problems and preventive tips:

1. Noisy Garage Doors

Loud screeching or grinding noises often signal the need for lubrication or indicate worn rollers and hinges. Regular lubrication of moving parts every 6-12 months can prevent excessive noise and reduce wear.

2. Door Doesn’t Open or Close Fully

This issue can result from misaligned sensors, damaged tracks, or faulty openers. Homeowners should periodically check sensor alignment and clear obstructions in tracks to avoid functionality loss.

3. Slow or Jerky Door Movement

Resistance from debris buildup or deteriorated rollers and cables can cause jerky operation. Routine inspection and cleaning of tracks combined with timely replacement of worn parts address this effectively.

4. Broken Springs or Cables

Because springs and cables carry the door’s full weight, breaks pose serious hazards. Awareness of wear signs such as visible gaps in springs or frayed cables and prompt professional replacement prevent dangerous accidents.
