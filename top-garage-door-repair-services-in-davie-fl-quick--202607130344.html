Garage doors come with different types and levels of insulation, which influence their ability to resist heat flow:

  • Polystyrene insulation: Lightweight and cost-effective, offering basic insulation value
  • Polyurethane insulation: Denser and providing higher R-values (thermal resistance), better suited to Davie’s climate
  • Double- or triple-layered doors: Enhanced insulation through multiple material layers, improving temperature control and soundproofing

Choosing the right insulation not only keeps your garage cooler in summer but can also reduce strain on adjoining living areas’ air conditioning.
