DIY Garage Door Maintenance Tips to Extend the Life of Your Door

While professional inspections and repairs are critical, homeowners can also take proactive steps to maintain their garage doors between service visits.

  • Monthly Visual Inspections: Check for visible signs of wear, rust, frayed cables, or misaligned tracks.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply silicone or lithium-based lubricants to rollers, hinges, and springs to reduce friction and prevent rust.
  • Test Safety Features: Regularly test auto-reverse functions by placing an object in the door’s path to ensure sensors are functioning properly.
  • Clean Door Surfaces: Wash door panels with mild detergent and water to remove dirt and salt deposits, especially after storms.
  • Tighten Hardware: Use a wrench to tighten loose bolts or screws that can cause rattling or misalignment.
  • Balance the Door: Disconnect the opener and manually raise the door halfway. It should hold position without drifting; if not, the springs need adjustment by a professional.

Following these simple maintenance tips can help detect early issues and maintain smooth operation until professional service is needed.
