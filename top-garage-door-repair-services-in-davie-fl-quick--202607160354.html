Preventing Common Garage Door Issues: Tips from Davie Experts

Prevention is as valuable as repair when it comes to maintaining the longevity and optimal performance of your garage door. Here are some pro tips recommended by Garage Door Repair of Davie’s seasoned technicians:

  • Regular Cleaning: Wash garage door surfaces and tracks to remove dirt, debris, and salt buildup caused by coastal proximity.
  • Seasonal Lubrication: Apply silicone or lithium-based lubricants to rollers, hinges, and springs every 6 months to minimize wear and friction.
  • Balance Testing: Manually raise the door halfway and release—it should hold position firmly without drifting. If it does not, the springs may require adjustment.
  • Sensor Alignment Checks: Clean and visually inspect safety sensors to ensure no obstacles or misalignments interfere with automatic closing.
  • Battery Replacement: Replace backup batteries annually in smart openers to avoid outages during power failures.

Routine attention based on local climate conditions can prevent emergency repairs, saving time and money while keeping your garage door operating smoothly year-round.
