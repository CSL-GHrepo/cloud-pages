Common Garage Door Problems Specific to Davie’s Climate and How to Prevent Them

Davie’s hot, humid environment and occasional heavy rainstorms create unique challenges for garage door systems. Understanding these issues can help you take proactive measures.

Rust and Corrosion on Metal Components

High humidity and salt air lead to accelerated rusting on garage door springs, tracks, hinges, and rollers, which may cause noisy operation and mechanical failures.

"Routine inspections and lubricating metal parts with weather-resistant products are key to preventing early corrosion," advises Maria Lopez, senior technician at Garage Door Repair of Davie.
  • Use corrosion-resistant hardware when replacing parts
  • Consider stainless steel or aluminum options for exposed components
  • Implement regular cleaning to remove salt deposit buildup

Warping and Swelling of Wood Garage Doors

Wood doors exposed to persistent moisture may warp or swell, leading to binding and uneven operation.

  • Apply proper weather sealing and repainting every few years
  • Ensure proper drainage around the garage entrance to avoid water pooling
  • Schedule annual inspections focused on door alignment and surface conditions

Sensor and Opener Malfunctions Due to Storm Damage

Heavy rain and electrical storms may damage electronic components like safety sensors and openers.

  • Surge protection devices help prevent electrical damage
  • Moisture-resistant shielding on wire connections improves longevity
  • Routine testing of safety sensors ensures reliable operation after storms
