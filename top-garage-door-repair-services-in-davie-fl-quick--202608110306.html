Common Garage Door Problems in Davie & How to Troubleshoot

Understanding frequent garage door issues can empower Davie residents to recognize problems early and call for expert assistance promptly.

Garage Door Won’t Open or Close Fully

  • Possible Causes: Obstructed photo-eye sensors, misaligned tracks, malfunctioning opener, or broken springs.
  • Simple Checks: Clear debris in sensors’ line of sight, visually inspect tracks for dents or bends, attempt manual operation to gauge spring tension.
  • Professional Action: Repairs should be conducted by professionals to address mechanical or electrical failures safely.

Door Reverses After Touching Ground

  • Possible Causes: Safety sensors misaligned or dirty, the force setting on the opener too low, or track obstructions.
  • Troubleshooting: Clean sensors gently with a soft cloth, realign sensors until the indicator lights are steady, and clear track.
  • Expert Help: Adjust opener settings and inspect mechanical parts for wear or damage requiring replacement.

Loud Noises During Operation

  • Possible Causes: Worn rollers, dry or rusted hardware, loose bolts, or unbalanced door.
  • DIY Steps: Lubricate moving parts with garage door-specific spray and tighten visible hardware.
  • When to Call Pros: Persistent noise often indicates worn components needing expert diagnosis.

Slow Garage Door Movement

  • Potential Reasons: Old opener motor, worn springs, or track problems causing excessive resistance.
  • Initial Checks: Clean and lubricate tracks; test opener response time.
  • Replacing Parts: Often necessary to restore smooth and timely operation.
