Tips for DIY Garage Door Troubleshooting Before Calling a Technician

While professional service is essential for safe and effective repairs, some minor issues can be identified or temporarily addressed by homeowners. Garage Door Repair of Davie offers these simple troubleshooting tips to help recognize common problems:

  • Check Power Supply: Verify that the opener is plugged in and circuit breakers are not tripped.
  • Inspect Remote Batteries: Replace batteries if the remote control is unresponsive.
  • Clear Track Obstructions: Remove debris or objects blocking the door path.
  • Reset Safety Sensors: Align the photo-eye sensors located near the floor on either side of the door.
  • Manual Operation Test: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door to check for smooth movement and balance.

If these steps don’t resolve the issue or you notice broken springs, frayed cables, or unusual noises, it’s best to contact experienced garage door technicians to avoid injury or further damage.
