Understanding Garage Door Safety Features in Davie, Florida

Safety is paramount in garage door design and repair. With heavy moving parts and electronic components, poorly maintained or improperly installed doors can pose hazards to residents and employees alike.

Essential Safety Mechanisms

  • Auto-Reverse Function: Modern automatic doors include sensors that detect obstructions and reverse the door to prevent crushing injuries.
  • Infrared Safety Sensors: Positioned near the base of the door tracks, these beams ensure the door operates only when the path is clear.
  • Manual Release Handles: Provide the ability to disengage the automatic opener for manual operation during emergencies or power outages.
  • Spring Containment Systems: Guards protect against spring breakage, which can cause door failure and injuries.
