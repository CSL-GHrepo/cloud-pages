  • Securing Weather Seals: Maintain or replace seals to keep water, wind, and debris out during severe weather.
  • Emergency Preparedness: Know who to contact for urgent garage door repairs during or immediately after a storm—Garage Door Repair of Davie offers prioritized emergency service to assist in such situations.

Managing Humidity-Related Issues in the Summer

High humidity affects metal parts through rust and causes wooden doors to warp, creating operational challenges. To minimize these effects:

  • Perform biannual lubrication on springs, rollers, and hinges using corrosion-resistant products recommended by professionals.
  • Have technicians inspect wooden components for swelling or cracks and apply protective finishes if needed.
  • Use dehumidifiers inside attached garages to limit moisture accumulation on electric openers and control panels.

Winter and Dry Season Maintenance

Although winters are mild, the drier air can cause weather stripping to crack or shrink, compromising insulation and allowing dust and pests to enter. Recommended actions include:

  • Replacing worn or brittle weather seals annually
  • Cleaning and inspecting tracks for dirt buildup
  • Checking opener sensitivity and adjusting settings for cooler, denser air if necessary
