Safety Considerations for Garage Door Ownership in Davie, FL

Garage doors are heavy, mechanically complex systems with potential hazards if not maintained or operated correctly. Ensuring safety for your family, employees, and property is paramount.

Common Garage Door Hazards

  • Spring Failures: Torsion and extension springs store significant tension; improper handling can cause severe injury.
  • Pinched Fingers: Moving parts and edges pose risks especially for children who play near garage doors.
  • Malfunctioning Auto-Reverse Sensors: If sensors fail, the door might close on objects or people.
  • Improperly Balanced Doors: Unbalanced doors may slam shut or fail to open fully.

Safety Maintenance Tips

  • Test the auto-reverse function monthly by placing a solid object under the door’s path.
  • Visually inspect cables, springs, and rollers for signs of wear or damage.
  • Avoid attempting DIY repairs on springs or cables; always contact licensed professionals.
  • Keep remote controls and wall switches out of reach of children.

"Regular safety inspections can prevent accidents and costly damages. Ten minutes of routine checks can save lives and money," emphasizes John Martinez.
