Common Garage Door Problems and How Professionals Address Them

Understanding common garage door issues can help you identify problems early and understand the value of professional repairs performed by Garage Door Repair of Davie’s certified technicians.

Broken or Overstretched Springs

Garage door springs absorb the majority of the door’s weight to enable smooth opening and closing. When these springs break or lose tension, the door may become difficult to lift, close improperly, or get stuck.

Attempting self-repair is dangerous as springs are under high tension. Professionals use specialized tools to safely replace or adjust springs, restoring balance and preventing damage to the door or opener.

Misaligned or Damaged Tracks

Tracks guide the door’s movement and must be perfectly aligned for smooth operation. Impact damage, debris, or loose mounts can cause the door to grind, jump tracks, or become stuck.

Garage Door Repair of Davie technicians inspect tracks thoroughly and perform realignment, cleaning, and replacement as necessary, ensuring safe and quiet operation.

Faulty Opener Mechanisms

Mechanical or electronic failures in the garage door opener—motors, gears, circuit boards, or wiring—can prevent the door from operating altogether. Garage Door Repair of Davie services all major opener brands, diagnosing issues quickly and performing repairs or recommending replacements.

Deteriorating Weather Seals

Cracked or missing weather seals allow water, dust, pests, and excessive heat into your garage, potentially damaging stored items and affecting climate control.

Garage Door Repair of Davie evaluates and replaces weather seals to restore protection and improve energy efficiency.
