Tips for Extending the Lifespan of Your Garage Door in Davie, FL

Beyond regular professional maintenance, property owners can take several practical steps to prolong the life and optimal functionality of garage doors:

  • Monthly Visual Inspections: Check for obvious signs of wear such as frayed cables, rusty springs, and loose bolts.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Use manufacturer-recommended lubricants on rollers, hinges, and springs to reduce friction and wear.
  • Keep Tracks Clear: Regularly remove debris like leaves, dirt, and stones from tracks to prevent misalignment.
  • Test Safety Features: Periodically test auto-reverse mechanisms and photo-eye sensors to ensure they stop and reverse the door if an obstruction is detected.
  • Avoid Excessive Force: Never force the door manually if it is stuck—this can cause more damage and increase repair costs.
  • Protect from Weather: Use weather seals and door sweeps to prevent moisture ingress and pest damage.

Taking these simple actions, combined with periodic professional inspections by Garage Door Repair of Davie, can result in fewer breakdowns and extended service life.
