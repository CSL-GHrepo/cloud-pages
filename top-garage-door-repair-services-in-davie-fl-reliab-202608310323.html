Common Garage Door Problems and DIY Vs. Professional Repair Advice

Understanding typical garage door problems can help homeowners recognize when a simple fix is possible and when professional intervention is necessary. This knowledge also supports better communication with repair technicians.

Common Issues

  • Broken Springs: Springs bear the weight of the door and assist in opening/closing. Breakage requires immediate professional repair due to dangerous tension.
  • Malfunctioning Opener: Problems with electrical components or sensors can sometimes be resolved by resetting or replacing batteries.
  • Misaligned Sensors: Sensors might become blocked or misaligned, causing the door to stop closing.
  • Damaged Panels or Tracks: Physical damage can disrupt door movement and compromise safety.
  • Strange Noises: Squeaking or grinding often indicates lubrication needs or worn parts.

When to DIY and When to Call a Professional

While some maintenance tasks are safe for DIY, such as lubricating moving parts or clearing sensor paths, others involve safety risks:

  • Safe DIY Tasks: Cleaning tracks, tightening loose hardware, replacing batteries, lubricating rollers.
  • Call a Professional For: Spring replacement, cable repair, opener motor issues, panel or track realignment, sensor replacement.

Making the right choice prevents accidents and costly errors. Garage Door Repair of Davie offers consultations to help customers assess their situation and recommend appropriate action.
