Safety First: Essential Garage Door Safety Tips for Davie Residents

Garage doors may seem like simple mechanical systems, but they can be dangerous if mishandled or malfunctioning. Ensuring safety protects your family, pets, and property.

Daily Safety Checks Anyone Can Perform

While professionals conduct detailed inspections, homeowners should regularly perform simple checks, such as:

  • Ensuring the auto-reverse safety sensor at the door’s base is clear of obstructions and aligned correctly.
  • Testing the door’s auto-reverse by placing an object underneath the door and closing it; the door should reverse upon contact.
  • Listening for unusual noises that can signal worn springs or rollers needing attention.
  • Checking the door balance manually by disconnecting the opener and lifting the door halfway; it should hold position without falling or rising.

Understanding Garage Door Spring Systems: Torsion vs. Extension Springs

One of the most critical but often overlooked components of garage doors are the spring systems, which bear the door’s weight and enable smooth operation.

Torsion Springs

Torsion springs are mounted horizontally above the garage door opening. They twist to store mechanical energy and are known for their durability and balance precision. Benefits include:

  • Longer lifespan, with typical cycles between 10,000 and 20,000
  • Smoother and quieter operation
  • Greater safety due to placement above the door

Extension Springs

Extension springs run parallel along the track and extend when the door opens. While initially less expensive, they have some drawbacks:

  • Shorter lifespan, generally around 10,000 cycles
  • More prone to snapping and causing damage if safety cables aren’t installed
  • Typically louder during operation

Technicians at Garage Door Repair of Davie help homeowners evaluate the right spring system based on usage frequency, door weight, and safety preferences.
