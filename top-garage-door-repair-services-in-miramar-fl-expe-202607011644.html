Preventive Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Miramar Homeowners

To extend the life and enhance the safety of your garage door, regular maintenance is key. Here are expert-recommended steps you can follow or have your technician perform:

  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Hinges, rollers, and springs need lubrication every 6 months to prevent rust and noise.
  • Check Balance and Alignment: Imbalanced doors strain the opener and hardware, risking damage. Professional adjustment recommended.
  • Inspect Weatherstripping: Replace damaged seals to keep humidity and pests out, important for Miramar’s humid climate.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Safety Sensor: Ensures the door reverses when obstructed, preventing accidents.
  • Schedule Annual Professional Inspections: A certified technician can detect and fix small issues before they become costly repairs.
