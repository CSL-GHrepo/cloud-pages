Essential Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Miramar Residents

Preventive maintenance is the key to avoiding costly emergency repairs. Miramar’s weather conditions mean regular upkeep is even more important. Here are actionable maintenance tips recommended by Miramar garage door technicians:

  1. Lubricate moving components such as springs, rollers, and hinges every six months to reduce wear and noise.
  2. Inspect weather seals to prevent water and debris intrusion, especially important during hurricane season.
  3. Test the garage door balance and automatic reversal monthly to ensure safety features are operational.
  4. Clean and clear tracks to avoid jams and improve door movement.
  5. Schedule professional maintenance annually to catch early signs of wear and extend door lifespan.

According to the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission, malfunctioning garage doors cause over 20,000 injuries annually, emphasizing the importance of regular professional inspections.
