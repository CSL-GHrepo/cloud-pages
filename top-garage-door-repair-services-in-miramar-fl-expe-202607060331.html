Pro Tips: Maintaining Your Garage Door Between Repairs

While professional service is essential, homeowners can take simple measures to prevent common issues:

  • Lubricate moving parts every six months with garage door–specific lubricants to reduce wear.
  • Keep sensors clean and aligned to avoid automatic closing failures.
  • Test balance monthly by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door.
  • Check weather seals for damage, which helps maintain energy efficiency.

Following these tips reduces the frequency and cost of emergency repairs.
