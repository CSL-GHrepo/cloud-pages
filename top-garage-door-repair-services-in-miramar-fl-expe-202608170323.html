Advanced Safety Features in Modern Garage Doors

Modern garage door systems include numerous safety enhancements designed to prevent accidents and injuries. These features are especially important in a family-friendly community like Miramar, where children and pets are part of daily life.

  • Auto-Reverse Mechanism: Detects obstructions and reverses the door automatically to prevent crushing incidents.
  • Photo-Eye Sensors: Infrared beams at the bottom of the door stop it from closing if something crosses the threshold.
  • Manual Emergency Release: Allows quick manual operation during power outages or system failures to ensure access or prevent lock-ins.
  • Rolling Code Technology: Prevents code grabbing by changing the access codes automatically each time the door is used.
  • Battery Backup Systems: Guarantee that doors operate safely even during power failure, crucial during Miramar’s stormy seasons.
