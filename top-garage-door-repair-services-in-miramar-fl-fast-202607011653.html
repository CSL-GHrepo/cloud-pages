Expert Tips to Maintain Your Garage Door in Miramar

Industry studies indicate that regular maintenance can extend the life of your garage door by up to 10 years and reduce emergency repairs by 25% (Source: International Door Association).

Here are key maintenance tips every Miramar resident should consider:

  1. Regularly Inspect Springs and Cables: Look for signs of wear, rust, or gaps in springs and fraying cables. These need professional attention immediately.
  2. Lubricate Moving Parts: Use silicone-based lubricant on rollers, hinges, and tracks every six months to reduce friction and noise.
  3. Test Auto-Reverse Features: Safety sensors must reverse the door when detecting obstacles to avoid accidents.
  4. Schedule Professional Overhead Door Maintenance Miramar Experts Recommend: A technician can spot underlying issues before they escalate, saving you money and hassle.
