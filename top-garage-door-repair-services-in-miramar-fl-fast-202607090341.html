Auto-Reverse Mechanism

The auto-reverse feature automatically stops and reverses the door if an object or person interrupts its path during closing. This safety function is crucial for preventing injuries and damage, especially in households with children or pets. Technicians regularly inspect and calibrate this feature to ensure it works flawlessly according to manufacturer specifications and safety codes.

Photo-Eye Sensors

Photo-eye sensors are installed near the bottom of a garage door frame to create an invisible beam across the doorway. If this beam is broken by an obstacle, the door will immediately halt and reverse its motion. This is one of the most effective safety measures to prevent entrapment and collisions, which Garage Door Repair of Miramar routinely tests and replaces if defective or misaligned.

Manual Release Locks with Safety Enhancements

In the event of power outages or mechanical failure, a manual release lock allows users to open the garage door manually. Understanding Garage Door Opener Types and Choosing the Right One

Not all garage door openers are created equal. With several options available, selecting the right opener is key to balancing convenience, noise level, security, and compatibility with your garage door type.

  • Chain-Drive Openers: Durable and cost-effective but tend to be noisier. Ideal for detached garages or locations where noise is less of a concern.
  • Belt-Drive Openers: Quieter and smoother operation, making them preferable for attached garages and residential areas where noise reduction is important.
  • Screw-Drive Openers: Require less maintenance and provide reliable performance but can be noisier and less forgiving of extreme temperature variations.
  • Direct-Drive Openers: The quietest and most durable, with the motor mounted on the track itself. Typically the most expensive but well-suited for homes prioritizing silent operation.

Garage Door Repair of Miramar’s experts provide personalized consultations to help customers select and install the most appropriate opener type based on their budget, garage design, and personal preferences.
