Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for Miramar’s Climate

The subtropical climate of Miramar presents unique challenges, and adapting garage door care routines seasonally can help maintain optimal operation year-round.

Spring and Summer: Combatting Humidity and Heat

  • Check for Rust and Corrosion: Inspect metal components and use rust-resistant sprays as needed to protect exposed parts.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts More Frequently: Due to increased humidity, lubricants can wash away quicker—reapply every 3-4 months.
  • Inspect Weather Seals: High temperatures can cause seals to dry out and crack; replace seals to maintain insulation.
  • Test Opener Batteries and Sensors: Heat can affect battery life and sensor accuracy, so test and replace batteries if necessary.

Fall and Winter: Preparing for Storm Season

  • Reinforce Door Panels: Ensure door panels are securely fastened to withstand high wind gusts and avoid storm damage.
  • Inspect and Upgrade Locks: Verify locking mechanisms are functioning properly to protect against potential break-ins during storm evacuations.
  • Clear Debris: Remove leaves and dirt from tracks and gutters to prevent jams caused by seasonal debris accumulation.
  • Schedule Professional Inspection: A thorough check before hurricane season can uncover weaknesses small enough to address before major storms.
“Proper seasonal care reduces emergency call-outs by 40% during South Florida’s storm season,” states Lisa Martinez, a local home maintenance advisor.
