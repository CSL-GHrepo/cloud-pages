Preventive Maintenance Strategies for Commercial Doors

Given the high frequency of use and heavier components involved, commercial garage doors benefit tremendously from preventive upkeep. Recommended strategies include:

  • Monthly operational testing to detect irregular noises or sluggish movement.
  • Quarterly lubrication of rollers, hinges, and tracks to reduce wear.
  • Regular inspection of counterbalance systems, including springs and cables, by certified technicians.
  • Verification of automatic safety sensors and photo-eye devices to ensure OSHA compliance.
  • Emergency readiness drills with in-house staff to familiarize them with manual release systems.

Implementing these steps prolongs equipment life, reduces expensive emergency repairs, and maintains workplace safety.
