Common Myths About Garage Door Repairs Debunked

The garage door industry has its share of misconceptions that can delay necessary repairs or lead to improper DIY attempts. Understanding these myths helps Miramar homeowners make informed decisions.

Myth 1: "A Garage Door Spring Can Be Repaired Instead of Replaced"

While it may seem cost-effective to repair a broken spring, in reality, springs are under extreme tension and wear out over time. Attempting to fix a damaged spring is unsafe and unreliable. Professional replacement is the only safe and lasting solution.

Myth 2: "Lubrication Is Optional and Doesn’t Affect Performance Much"

Ignoring lubrication leads to increased friction, premature wear on rollers and hinges, and noisy operation. Regular lubrication is vital to smooth mechanical function and extends the life of parts significantly.

Myth 3: "You Can Ignore Safety Features Like Auto-Reverse Since They Rarely Fail"

Auto-reverse and safety sensors are life-saving features designed to prevent injuries. Failure in these systems can result in serious accidents. Regular testing and maintenance are critical and cannot be skipped based on false confidence.

Myth 4: "Any Garage Door Technician Can Handle Smart Opener Issues"

Smart technology issues require specialized knowledge in both mechanical repairs and IT-based troubleshooting. Not all technicians have the skills or tools to properly service Wi-Fi connected or app-controlled openers.
