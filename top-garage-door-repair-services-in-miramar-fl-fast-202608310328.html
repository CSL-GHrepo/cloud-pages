Preventing Future Issues: Expert Tips from Garage Door Repair of Miramar

Beyond professional maintenance appointments, there are simple steps homeowners can take to prolong the life of their garage doors:

  • Monthly Visual Inspections: Check for visible damage or wear such as frayed cables, rusted springs, or cracked panels.
  • Keep Tracks Clean: Remove debris and dirt from rails to avoid obstructions and uneven movement.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Use garage-door-specific lubricants on rollers, hinges, and springs at least twice a year.
  • Test Safety Features: Regularly test auto-reverse and photo-eye sensors by placing an object in the door’s path and confirming it stops and reverses correctly.
  • Balance the Door: With the door closed, manually lift it halfway and release—if it falls or rises, springs likely require adjustment by experts.
