DIY Garage Door Repairs: When to Avoid and When It’s Safe

While some minor garage door maintenance tasks can be handled by homeowners, many repairs require professional expertise to avoid injury and ensure durability.

  • Lubricating rollers, hinges, and springs with the appropriate garage door lubricant.
  • Cleaning tracks to prevent debris buildup.
  • Checking weatherstripping and replacing if visibly cracked or damaged.

Tasks to Avoid DIY

  • Spring Replacement: Garage door springs are under extreme tension and can cause serious injury if mishandled.
  • Opener Repairs: Electrical components require specialized tools and knowledge.
  • Track Realignment: Misadjustment can cause door binding or unsafe operation.
