Garage door tracks can become bent, clogged, or misaligned due to impacts, debris, or natural settling of the house. This leads to noisy operation, the door becoming stuck, or uneven movement.

Professional technicians assess track alignment using precision tools, realign or replace damaged segments, and remove obstructions to ensure smooth and consistent door travel. Attempting DIY fixes often results in further damage or unsafe conditions, reinforcing the value of expert intervention.

Broken Springs: A Hazardous Emergency

Garage door springs bear the heavy load of the door and are under extreme tension. They can break without warning, causing the door to become inoperable or even fall suddenly, posing serious safety risks.
