DIY Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Miramar Homeowners

While professional maintenance is critical, there are simple upkeep practices homeowners can do to extend the life of their garage doors between service visits.

Basic Garage Door Maintenance Tasks

  • Visual Inspection: Check for visible signs of wear, rust, or damage to panels, springs, cables, and tracks monthly.
  • Lubrication: Apply garage door lubricant to moving parts like hinges, rollers, and springs to reduce friction and noise (avoid WD-40 which can attract dirt).
  • Test Auto-Reverse: Place an object in the door's path and close the door to ensure it reverses upon contact.
  • Clean Tracks: Wipe tracks with a damp cloth and clear debris to prevent obstructions (do not use grease on tracks).
  • Check Balance: Disconnect the opener and manually raise the door to mid-position; it should stay in place without assistance.

Regular simple care can prevent minor issues from escalating into costly emergencies.
