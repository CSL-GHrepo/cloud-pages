Seasonal Garage Door Care: Tailoring Maintenance to Miramar’s Climate

Miramar’s weather patterns necessitate specific seasonal maintenance strategies to prolong your garage door’s lifespan and performance:

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Inspect for Moisture Damage: High humidity can lead to rust and corrosion. Check metal components and treat any early signs of oxidation promptly.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Heat and humidity increase wear on springs and rollers. Applying silicone-based lubricants during these months reduces friction and noise.
  • Check Weatherstripping: Replace any deteriorated seals that can let in rain or humid air, preventing interior damage and mold growth.
  • Test Opener Sensors: Ensure rain and moisture have not misaligned safety sensors or damaged electrical components.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Examine Thermal Insulation: Cooler months put strain on uninsulated doors, resulting in uncomfortable garage conditions and higher heating costs.
  • Clear Track Obstructions: Leaves and debris accumulate more in fall, potentially jamming door mechanisms. Regular cleaning during this season prevents issues.
  • Evaluate Door Balance: Temperature fluctuations can affect spring tension, causing uneven door movement; adjustments by a professional are recommended.
  • Inspect Electrical Components: Shorter daylight hours and storms warrant checking opener wiring and connections for moisture-related damage.
