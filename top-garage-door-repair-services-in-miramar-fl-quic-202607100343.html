Extending Garage Door Life Through Seasonal Maintenance Tips

Miramar’s climate means garage doors undergo seasonal stress variations—from intense summer heat to occasional rainfall and humidity swings. Homeowners who perform regular DIY checks can identify small issues before they escalate.

  • Spring: Clean tracks and rollers of debris accumulated over winter; lubricate moving parts to ensure smooth operation; inspect weather seals for cracks
  • Summer: Increase inspection frequency for rust or corrosion influenced by humidity and coastal air; verify opener sensor alignment; test automatic reverse functions
  • Fall: Check tension of springs and cables before cooler months; replace worn weather stripping to seal against debris and moisture; clean exterior panels to prevent staining
  • Winter: Although Miramar experiences mild winters, monitor for dampness-related components deterioration; operate door weekly to prevent sticking or freezing
