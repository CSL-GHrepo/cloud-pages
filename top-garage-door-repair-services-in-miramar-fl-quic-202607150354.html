Common Garage Door Problems and How Miramar Experts Resolve Them

While some garage door issues are visible, others can be subtle yet have serious operational impacts. Experienced technicians in Miramar identify root causes quickly and apply effective solutions based on the problem's nature.

Broken Springs

Broken torsion or extension springs are one of the most common issues affecting garage doors. These springs counterbalance the door’s weight to enable smooth opening and closing.

  • Symptoms: Door fails to open or closes abruptly; unusual noises like snapping.
  • Repair: Springs must be replaced by trained professionals due to tension risks.
  • Preventive Tip: Regular maintenance and inspection can detect early wear signs.

Misaligned or Damaged Tracks

Garage doors rely on precisely aligned tracks for smooth movement. Tracks can bend or become obstructed due to impacts or debris accumulation.

  • Symptoms: Door jerks, gets stuck, or makes grinding noises while opening/closing.
  • Repair: Technicians straighten tracks, remove obstructions, or replace damaged sections.
  • Preventive Tip: Periodic cleaning and visual inspections help maintain track integrity.

Faulty Garage Door Openers

Automatic doors depend on electric openers that may suffer from motor or sensor failures, wiring issues, or remote control malfunctions.

  • Symptoms: Door won’t respond to controls, opens partially, or reverses unexpectedly.
  • Repair: Diagnostics identify faulty components for repair or replacement.
  • Preventive Tip: Timely software updates and battery replacement in remotes maintain functionality.
