Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for Miramar Residents

Miramar’s weather varies throughout the year, and seasonal care can prevent many common garage door issues. Here are practical tips to keep your door in peak condition year-round:

Spring Maintenance Focus

  • Clean and lubricate moving parts: Remove winter grime and apply silicone-based lubricants to springs and rollers.
  • Inspect weather stripping: Replace any worn seals to keep out rain and prevent humidity damage.
  • Test sensors and safety features: Ensure electronic eyes and emergency reverse mechanisms function properly after winter exposure.

Summer Preparation

  • Check door balance: Heat can cause door expansion; ensure it opens and closes smoothly without strain.
  • Examine opener components: High use in summer months may stress motors and gears—preemptive checks reduce failure risk.

Fall and Winter Readiness

  • Verify insulation integrity: Seal air leaks to improve energy efficiency during cooler months.
  • Trim nearby foliage: Minimizes debris getting into tracks during periodic storms common to the season.
  • Schedule professional inspection: Address humidity-induced warping or corrosion before weather worsens.
