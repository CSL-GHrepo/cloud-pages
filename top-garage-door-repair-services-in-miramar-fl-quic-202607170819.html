Common Garage Door Problems in Miramar and How Professionals Address Them

Garage doors, subjected to heavy daily use and Florida’s harsh weather, often develop familiar issues. Understanding these common problems helps homeowners and businesses seek prompt solutions.

Typical Garage Door Issues

  • Broken or Worn Springs: Springs are critical for lifting the door; wear can cause the door to become heavy or unbalanced.
  • Misaligned or Damaged Tracks: Tracks can bend or become clogged with debris, causing doors to jam or derail.
  • Faulty Openers or Remote Controls: Electrical problems, dead batteries, or damaged sensors often interfere with remote operation.
  • Noisy Operation: Squeaking, grinding, or banging noises may indicate lubrication issues or failing hardware.
  • Sensor Malfunctions: Photo-eye sensors, if dirty or misaligned, can stop the door from closing properly.

Professional Troubleshooting and Repairs
