Preparing Your Garage Door for Miramar’s Hurricane Season

Miramar’s geographic location makes it vulnerable to hurricanes, posing significant risk to garage doors and underlying structures. Proper preparation can mitigate damages and enhance safety.

Key Steps Before Hurricane Season

  • Inspect and Reinforce Doors: Install wind-resistant garage doors or reinforce existing doors with bracing kits certified for high-wind conditions.
  • Check and Secure Track and Hardware: Tighten loose bolts and upgrade rollers to heavy-duty models designed to withstand storms.
  • Test Automatic Openers and Manual Release: Ensure opener battery backups work in case of power outages, and know how to operate doors manually.
  • Trim Surrounding Vegetation: Clear tree limbs and debris to reduce risk of impact damage.

During and After the Storm

Post-storm inspections should be conducted promptly. Engage professional garage door repair services to assess structural integrity, realign any shifts, and replace damaged components. Early action can prevent secondary problems such as water intrusion and security vulnerabilities.
