Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Miramar Homeowners

Miramar’s climate means garage doors endure year-round exposure to heat, humidity, and storms. Preventative maintenance tailored to seasonal changes can significantly extend door lifespan and reduce emergency repair needs.

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Inspect weather stripping for cracks or gaps to maintain energy efficiency and keep out pests.
  • Lubricate rollers, hinges, and springs using synthetic garage door lubricants to resist humidity-related corrosion.
  • Check automatic sensors and opener functions to ensure they are free of dust and moisture buildup.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Test the balance of the door; improper balance can cause opener strain as temperatures drop.
  • Remove debris from tracks and clean the door surface to prevent mold and mildew growth during wet seasons.
  • Inspect seals and weather stripping again, replacing any brittle or cracked components before cooler temperatures.

Scheduling professional maintenance visits twice yearly can catch small problems early and provide peace of mind against unexpected failures brought on by Miramar’s climate extremes.
