Expert Residential Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Miramar Homeowners

Regular maintenance not only prevents breakdowns but also enhances the safety and energy efficiency of your garage door. Miramar homeowners are encouraged to incorporate these tips in between professional visits:

  1. Lubricate moving parts: Use a silicone-based lubricant on hinges, springs, and rollers every six months.
  2. Test safety sensors: Ensure automatic reversal systems activate correctly to prevent accidents.
  3. Inspect weather stripping: Replace cracked or brittle seals to keep out humidity and pests.
  4. Check balance and alignment: Manually lift the door halfway and observe if it holds steady; uneven movement is a repair sign.

Consistent care extends your door’s lifespan, saving money on costly replacements common in the Miramar real estate market.
