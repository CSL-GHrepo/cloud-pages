Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Miramar Climates

Miramar’s unique weather calendar—hot summers, mild winters, and a hurricane season from June through November—demands seasonal care routines for garage doors to maximize longevity and safety.

Spring and Summer Considerations

  • Humidity management: Inspect and replace weather seals to prevent moisture intrusion which can corrode hardware and damage electrical components.
  • Lubrication checks: High temperatures can cause lubricants to dry out; re-lubricate moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and springs to ensure smooth operation.
  • Sun damage prevention: Inspect painted surfaces and finishes for fading or cracking, and apply protective coatings if necessary to extend appearance and durability.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Storm preparedness: Schedule an inspection ahead of hurricane season to reinforce door hardware, tighten loose bolts, and verify emergency release mechanisms function properly.
  • Temperature fluctuations: Cooler temperatures can cause metal parts to contract, resulting in potential misalignment. Check tracks, rollers, and springs for signs of wear or looseness.
  • Humidity control: Continue monitoring weather stripping for cracks or brittleness as mornings become cooler and humidity fluctuates.

Adhering to these seasonal maintenance tips can minimize emergency repair calls and ensure your garage door is ready for Miramar’s weather’s challenges.
