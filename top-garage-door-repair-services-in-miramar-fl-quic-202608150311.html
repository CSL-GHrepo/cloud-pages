Maintenance Tips to Extend Your Garage Door’s Lifespan in Miramar's Climate

Routine maintenance is crucial to prevent premature failure due to Miramar’s humid, coastal environment. Here are expert tips for homeowners and commercial property managers to keep garage doors in top shape year-round.

Monthly Visual Inspection

  • Check tracks and rollers for debris, rust, or wear.
  • Look for signs of frayed cables or damaged springs.
  • Verify that weather seals are intact and free of cracks.
  • Test safety sensors by placing an object in the door’s path to ensure automatic reversal.

Lubrication and Cleaning Schedule

Apply a silicone-based lubricant to moving parts like hinges, rollers, and springs every 3-4 months to reduce friction and prevent rust. Avoid petroleum-based lubricants, which can attract dirt and cause buildup. Clean the surfaces regularly with mild soap and water to remove grime and environmental residue.

Check Balance and Alignment Quarterly

A garage door that is out of balance strains the opener motor and accelerates hardware wear. To test balance, disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway—if it slips up or down, professional adjustment is needed.

Schedule Professional Maintenance Annually

Even with routine DIY care, annual professional inspections catch issues that may go unnoticed, such as hidden corrosion or subtle mechanical weaknesses. Technicians can adjust tension, replace worn components, and update safety features to comply with current regulations.
