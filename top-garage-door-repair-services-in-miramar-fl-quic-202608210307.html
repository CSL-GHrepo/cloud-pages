DIY Garage Door Care Tips for Miramar Homeowners

While professional maintenance is crucial, regular homeowner inspections and basic care practices can prevent minor issues from escalating—a good practice tailored to Miramar’s environmental challenges.

Monthly Visual Inspection

  • Check for visible signs of rust, dents, or warping on your garage door panels and hardware.
  • Ensure weather seals are intact and free from gaps or cracks.
  • Observe whether the door moves smoothly and quietly during operation.

Lubrication of Moving Parts

Applying garage door lubricant to springs, rollers, hinges, and tracks every 3-6 months keeps parts moving efficiently and reduces wear caused by humidity-induced corrosion.

Safety Sensor Testing

Test the door’s automatic reversing mechanism by placing an object (like a broomstick) in the door’s path. The door should reverse upon contact. If it doesn’t, schedule a professional inspection immediately.

Cleaning and Debris Removal

Regularly remove dirt, leaves, and debris from tracks and bottom seals to prevent blockages that can impede operation or cause door misalignment.

"Spending just a few minutes each month inspecting and lubricating your garage door can save hundreds in repair bills and increase overall safety, especially here in Miramar," advises Maria Gomez, a local home maintenance expert.
