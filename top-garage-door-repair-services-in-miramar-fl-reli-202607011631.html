Preventative Garage Door Maintenance Miramar

Routine maintenance significantly reduces risks of unexpected failures. Experts recommend keeping your door lubricated, inspecting springs and cables, and testing safety sensors regularly. Key maintenance tips include:

  • Lubricate moving parts with silicone-based lubricants every 6 months.
  • Check and tighten hardware including bolts and mounts.
  • Test auto-reverse functionality to ensure safety compliance.
  • Clean tracks and rollers to avoid debris accumulation.
