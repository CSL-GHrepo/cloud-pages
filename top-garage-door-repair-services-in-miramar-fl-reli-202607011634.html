Essential Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Miramar Homeowners

Regular maintenance is key to avoiding emergency repairs. Experts recommend scheduling professional maintenance at least once a year to keep your door in optimal condition.

  • Lubricate moving parts and hinges to minimize friction and noise.
  • Inspect and tighten hardware regularly to prevent loose or broken components.
  • Check balance by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door halfway; if it doesn’t stay in place, call a professional.
  • Test safety sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms to protect families and belongings.
  • Replace weather-stripping to keep out humidity and debris, which is critical in humid climates like Miramar’s.
