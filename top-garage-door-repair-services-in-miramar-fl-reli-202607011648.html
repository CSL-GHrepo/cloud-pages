Practical Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Miramar

Preventative maintenance is critical for avoiding emergency repairs and extending your garage door’s lifespan. Here are actionable tips recommended by Miramar’s garage door experts:

  • Conduct monthly visual inspections to check for wear on springs, cables, and rollers.
  • Lubricate moving parts with garage door-specific lubricants to reduce friction and noise.
  • Test the door’s balance at least twice a year by disconnecting the opener and manually moving the door—it should move smoothly.
  • Inspect and test safety sensors regularly to ensure automatic reversal works correctly.
  • Schedule annual professional tune-ups with a local company like Garage Door Repair of Miramar to identify hidden issues.

Adopting these simple maintenance routines can lead to significant savings and prevent disruptions to your daily routine.
