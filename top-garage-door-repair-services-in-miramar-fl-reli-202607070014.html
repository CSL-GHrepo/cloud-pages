Choosing the Right Garage Door Material for Your Miramar Home

Steel Garage Doors: Durable and Low Maintenance

Steel is a popular option for its strength and resistance to cracking or warping. It can be insulated for energy efficiency and treated with weather-resistant coatings to combat corrosion.

  • Advantages: High durability, low upkeep, wide design options.
  • Considerations: Potential for rust if coatings are damaged; inspections recommended.

Aluminum Garage Doors: Lightweight and Modern

Aluminum doors resist corrosion even better than steel, making them suitable for Miramar’s salty ocean air. They often feature glass panels for increased natural light.

  • Advantages: Rust-resistant, lightweight, stylish designs.
  • Considerations: Less impact-resistant than steel; dents possible with heavy impact.

Wood Garage Doors: Classic Appeal with Higher Maintenance

Wood offers timeless aesthetics and customization but requires periodic refinishing to protect from moisture and sun damage.

  • Advantages: Authentic look, customizable finishes and styles.
  • Considerations: Higher maintenance, susceptibility to warping and pests.
