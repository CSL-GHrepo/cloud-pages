Seasonal Garage Door Tips for Miramar Homeowners

Miramar’s climate poses unique seasonal challenges, requiring homeowners to adapt garage door care accordingly. Here are tips to keep your door working optimally year-round:

Spring and Summer

  • Inspect for rust and corrosion: Salt air and humidity can accelerate rust, particularly on metal hardware and tracks. Regular cleaning and protective coatings help.
  • Lubricate moving parts: Warm weather can cause parts to dry out faster; using silicone-based lubricants prevents squeaking and friction.
  • Check sensors and openers: Heat can affect electronic components, so verifying proper sensor alignment and functionality reduces opener issues.

Fall and Winter

  • Seal weatherstripping: Even in mild winters, air leaks waste energy. Inspect and replace weatherstripping to improve insulation.
  • Test automatic reverse features: Cooler temperatures impact the mechanical response; regular testing ensures safety mechanisms perform correctly.
  • Clear debris from tracks and hinges: Fallen leaves and dirt can cause misalignment or jamming during rainy days common in winter months.

Following these seasonal routines can prevent many common issues and extend the life of your garage door system.
