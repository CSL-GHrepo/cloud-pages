Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Miramar

To reduce unexpected repairs and extend the life of your garage door, follow these expert-recommended maintenance steps:

  1. Schedule bi-annual inspections: Have a professional technician check moving parts each spring and fall.
  2. Keep tracks clean and lubricated: Use silicone-based lubricants to avoid rust buildup in the humid Miramar climate.
  3. Test safety features monthly: Ensure photo eyes and auto-reversing mechanisms respond correctly.
  4. Listen for unusual noises: Creaking or grinding can signal the need for component servicing or replacement.
  5. Replace weather seals: Proper sealing helps protect your garage from rain and improves energy efficiency.
