Understanding Garage Door Spring Systems: Torsion vs. Extension

Garage springs bear much of the mechanical load in opening and closing doors, making them a focal point in repair and maintenance. Miramar residents benefit from understanding the types of springs involved when discussing issues with service professionals.

Torsion Springs

Torsion springs are mounted horizontally above the garage door opening and work by twisting to apply torque and counterbalance the door’s weight. These springs offer smoother operation and greater durability and are common in residential and commercial applications in Miramar.

  • Typically last longer than extension springs due to more balanced stress distribution.
  • Require professional expertise for replacement due to potential dangers of high tension.
  • Provide more precise control over door movement, reducing wear on other components.

Extension Springs

Extension springs run along the sides of the garage door and stretch when the door closes. They are easier to install but tend to wear out faster and offer less reliable performance under heavy usage.

  • Common in older or budget door installations.
  • Often noisier during door operation compared to torsion spring systems.
  • Require careful inspection and frequent maintenance in humid climates like Miramar’s.

Professional garage door technicians in Miramar recommend torsion spring upgrades for enhanced safety and longevity when replacing older extension springs, offering tailored advice based on door type and usage patterns.
