How Regular Garage Door Maintenance Protects Your Investment

Garage doors, despite their robust construction, are mechanical systems subject to wear and tear. Proactive maintenance not only enhances reliability but also extends the service life of the entire door system, saving homeowners from costly emergency repairs or premature replacements.

Components Inspected During Routine Maintenance

  • Springs and Cables: Checked for signs of fatigue, rust, or fraying.
  • Rollers and Hinges: Lubricated to ensure smooth, quiet operation and prevent premature breakdown.
  • Tracks: Inspected for alignment and free of debris to prevent jamming.
  • Weather Stripping: Replaced or repaired to maintain energy efficiency and prevent moisture entry.
  • Openers and Sensors: Tested for sensitivity, responsiveness, and safety compliance.
