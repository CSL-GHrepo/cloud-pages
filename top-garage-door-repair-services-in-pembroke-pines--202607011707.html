Tips for Pembroke Pines Homeowners: Maintaining Your Garage Door

Even the best garage doors require proactive care. Here are expert-recommended maintenance tips specifically for Pembroke Pines residents:

  • Inspect door springs and cables for rust or damage annually.
  • Lubricate moving parts every six months with weather-resistant lubricants.
  • Test safety sensors monthly to ensure automatic reversal functions work.
  • Clean garage door panels regularly to prevent mold and mildew buildup in humid conditions.
  • Check opener batteries and remote controls to avoid unexpected failures.

Routine upkeep reduces the likelihood of emergency situations and extends the overall lifespan of your garage door system.
