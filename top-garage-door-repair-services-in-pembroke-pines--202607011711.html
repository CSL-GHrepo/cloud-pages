How to Maintain Your Garage Door in Pembroke Pines for Longevity

Proactive maintenance reduces breakdowns and extends the life of your garage door. Industry experts recommend the following tips tailored for the Pembroke Pines environment:

  • Regular Lubrication: Apply lubricant to springs, rollers, and hinges every six months to prevent rust from humid coastal air.
  • Check Weatherstripping: Inspect and replace weather seals damaged by sun or rain to keep your garage insulated and dry.
  • Test Safety Features: Ensure auto-reverse and photo-eye sensors function correctly to protect family and property.
  • Tighten Hardware: Constant door vibrations can loosen bolts and screws over time—check monthly and tighten as needed.
  • Schedule Professional Inspections: Annual service from local garage door technicians can spot potential issues before they cause failures.
