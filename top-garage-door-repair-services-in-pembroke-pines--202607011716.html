Maintaining Your Garage Door in Pembroke Pines: Tips from Local Experts

Prevention is key to avoiding unexpected breakdowns. Here are actionable garage door maintenance tips recommended by local technicians:

  • Schedule biannual inspections—spring and fall—to adjust springs and rollers.
  • Lubricate moving parts with silicone-based lubricants every six months.
  • Test auto-reverse safety features monthly to ensure proper function.
  • Keep tracks clean from debris and wipe down door surfaces to prevent corrosion.
  • Replace weather seals as needed to protect against rain and humidity.

Regular maintenance not only safeguards your family’s security but also reduces the total cost of ownership over time.
