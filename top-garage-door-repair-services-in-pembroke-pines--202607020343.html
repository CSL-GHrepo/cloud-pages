Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Pembroke Pines Residents

Industry experts agree that regular preventive maintenance can cut garage door repair costs by up to 30% annually (National Association of Home Builders, 2022). Here are practical tips to keep your garage door in peak condition:

  • Monthly Visual Inspection: Check for bent tracks, frayed cables, and loose hardware.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Use silicone-based lubricants on hinges, springs, and rollers every 3-6 months.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Function: Place an object under the door and close it to ensure the safety sensors work properly.
  • Clean Tracks and Sensors: Remove debris and dirt to avoid operational issues due to clogged components.
  • Schedule Professional Maintenance: Twice-yearly inspections and tune-ups from Garage Door Repair of Pembroke Pines help catch issues early.
