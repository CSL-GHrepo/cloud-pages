Ensuring Long-Term Reliability with Regular Garage Door Maintenance

Many garage door problems are preventable through routine maintenance. Expert specialists in Pembroke Pines recommend the following maintenance tips to avoid costly repairs:

  • Lubricate moving parts every 6 months to reduce wear and noise.
  • Inspect and tighten hardware, bolts, and rollers seasonally.
  • Test balance and auto-reverse safety features quarterly.
  • Clean and remove debris from tracks and sensors regularly.
  • Schedule professional maintenance inspections annually or bi-annually.
