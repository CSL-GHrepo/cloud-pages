How to Maintain Your Garage Door Between Repairs

While professional maintenance is crucial, homeowners and business owners can take simple steps to extend the life of their garage doors:

  • Regularly lubricate hinges, rollers, and springs using silicone-based lubricants.
  • Inspect door panels and weather stripping for damage or warping.
  • Test garage door balance and auto-reverse functions monthly.
  • Keep tracks clean and free of debris.
  • Schedule professional overhead door maintenance Pembroke Pines at least once a year.

Following these practices can prevent many common failures and keep your garage door operating smoothly until your next scheduled service.
