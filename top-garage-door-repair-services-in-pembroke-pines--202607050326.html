Expert Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Pembroke Pines

Prevention is always better than repair. Here are some actionable tips for ongoing garage door maintenance Pembroke Pines homeowners can perform to avoid emergencies:

  • Regularly Inspect Moving Parts: Check rollers, springs, and cables for signs of rust or wear and replace as needed.
  • Lubricate the Door: Use silicone or lithium-based lubricants on tracks and hinges every six months.
  • Test the Safety Features: Ensure sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms work properly to avoid injuries.
  • Tighten Hardware: Use a wrench to tighten bolts and screws that loosen over time.
  • Check Door Balance: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door to see if it stays open halfway; imbalance signals a problem with springs or cables.
