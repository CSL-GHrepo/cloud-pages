How to Maintain Your Garage Door for Long-Term Performance

Preventative maintenance is the key to extending your garage door’s lifespan and avoiding emergency repairs. Pembroke Pines residents should follow these maintenance steps every six months to a year:

  • Visual inspection: Look for worn springs, cables, rollers, and hinges.
  • Lubrication: Use a silicone-based spray on moving components to reduce wear.
  • Test safety sensors: Ensure the photo-eye sensors reverse the door when obstructed.
  • Check balance and alignment: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway; it should stay put.
  • Tighten hardware: Vibrations can loosen bolts and screws over time.
  • Clean tracks: Remove debris and ensure tracks are properly aligned.

Following these simple steps, combined with professional inspections by Pembroke Pines overhead door services, will help you avoid costly garage door replacement in Pembroke Pines.
