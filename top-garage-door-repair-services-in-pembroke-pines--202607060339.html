Common Garage Door Problems and Solutions in Pembroke Pines

Understanding common issues can help homeowners identify when professional service is needed:

  1. Broken or worn garage door springs: These are under high tension and pose safety risks. Replacing springs ensures balanced door operation and prevents accidents.
  2. Malfunctioning automatic openers: Electronic faults or sensor misalignment can cause the door to stop or reverse unexpectedly.
  3. Damaged panels or tracks: Cracked or bent door panels and misaligned tracks hinder smooth door movement and affect security.
  4. Off-track garage doors: Often caused by debris or impact, this requires professional realignment to prevent further damage.
  5. Weather seal failure: Worn seals let in moisture and pests, reducing energy efficiency and creating potential hazards.
