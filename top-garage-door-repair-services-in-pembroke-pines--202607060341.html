Expert Tips to Extend the Life of Your Garage Door

Beyond professional repairs, there are proactive steps homeowners and business owners can follow to maintain their garage doors:

  1. Regular lubrication: Apply silicone-based lubricants to rollers, hinges, and tracks every six months to prevent squeaking and rust.
  2. Inspect weather seals: Replace cracked or damaged weatherstripping to improve insulation and protect against South Florida’s moisture.
  3. Test safety features: Monthly testing of auto-reverse and sensor systems ensures your door stops and reverses to prevent accidents.
  4. Keep tracks clean and aligned: Remove debris and ensure tracks are free from dents to promote smooth operation.
  5. Schedule professional maintenance: A licensed technician should perform a detailed inspection annually to catch emerging issues early.
