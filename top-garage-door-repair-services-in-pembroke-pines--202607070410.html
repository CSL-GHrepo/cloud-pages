Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for Pembroke Pines Homeowners

While professional maintenance is key, simple seasonal care steps can prevent many common issues and extend the life of your garage door system.

Spring and Summer

  • Clean tracks and rollers to remove dust and debris accumulated during winter.
  • Check and lubricate all moving parts with a silicone-based garage door lubricant.
  • Inspect weather seals and replace any damaged or brittle sections caused by summer heat.
  • Test balance and alignment to avoid strain on openers during frequent use.

Fall and Winter

  • Ensure adequate sealing to prevent drafts and minimize energy loss as temperatures drop.
  • Remove leaves or dirt around the door bottom to avoid water accumulation and rust risk.
  • Check springs and cables for signs of wear intensified by colder weather.
  • Test auto-reverse safety sensors regularly as fog or condensation may impact sensor efficacy.

Incorporating these DIY tips alongside annual professional tune-ups can significantly improve garage door reliability in Pembroke Pines’ subtropical climate.
