Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for Pembroke Pines Residents

Pembroke Pines' subtropical climate, with its heat, humidity, and rainy season, affects garage doors differently throughout the year. Tailoring care to each season can prolong your door’s lifespan and maintain reliable operation.

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Inspect for Rust and Corrosion: Humidity and rain can accelerate rust on metal components. Applying weather-resistant lubricants and protective coatings helps mitigate damage.
  • Check Weather Seals: Ensure rubber seals at the bottom and sides are intact to prevent water ingress during Florida’s frequent summer storms.
  • Test Safety Sensors Regularly: Summer dust and pollen buildup can affect sensor function; cleaning and adjustment are necessary to avoid operation failures.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Examine Springs and Cables: Cooler temperatures can increase metal brittleness. Schedule professional inspections to detect early wear signs.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Despite milder winters, lubrication prevents stiffness and noise caused by temperature fluctuations.
  • Check Door Balance: Properly balanced doors reduce strain on opener motors and hardware, ensuring smooth functioning during fluctuating weather conditions.

General Tips Year-Round

  • Never attempt DIY repair on springs or cables due to high tension risks.
  • Schedule bi-annual professional maintenance visits for comprehensive checks.
  • Keep garage door tracks clean and free from debris to prevent operational jams.
