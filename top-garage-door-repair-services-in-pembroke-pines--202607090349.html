The Role of Professional Garage Door Inspections in Accident Prevention

Garage door malfunctions are not only inconvenient but can pose serious safety risks. Regular inspections by certified garage door technicians significantly reduce the likelihood of accidents involving family members, pets, or employees.

Common Garage Door Hazards Addressed by Inspections

  • Spring Failures: Garage door springs are under high tension and can cause injuries if they snap unexpectedly. Professional inspections identify worn or fatigued springs before failure.
  • Cable Malfunctions: Frayed or slack cables can cause doors to fall or hang unevenly, presenting crushing hazards.
  • Sensor Misalignments: Safety sensors that fail to detect obstructions may not trigger door reversals, leading to potential trapping accidents.
  • Improper Balance: Doors out of balance strain the opener and other components, increasing breakdown risks.

Inspection Checklist for Certified Garage Door Professionals

When scheduling an inspection, technicians typically evaluate:

  • Condition of springs, cables, rollers, and pulleys
  • Track alignment and structural integrity
  • Functionality of the opener and remote controls
  • Performance of safety features such as auto-reverse and photoelectric sensors
  • Signs of wear on door panels, seals, and hardware
