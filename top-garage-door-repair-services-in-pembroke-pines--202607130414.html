Seasonal Garage Door Care: Adapting Maintenance to Pembroke Pines’ Climate

The subtropical climate of Pembroke Pines poses unique challenges that impact garage door performance throughout the year. Tailoring maintenance routines according to seasonal demands can prevent premature wear and improve reliability.

  • Frequent Lubrication: High temperatures increase evaporation, causing lubricants to dry out faster. Applying silicone-based lubricants monthly during summer helps maintain smooth operation of moving parts.
  • Check Weather Seals: Heat and sun exposure may cause seals to crack or shrink; replacing them before peak summer prevents pest intrusion and energy loss.
