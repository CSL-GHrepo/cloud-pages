Corrosion and Rust

Salt air from the nearby Atlantic Ocean can accelerate corrosion, especially on metal components such as springs, rollers, and tracks. Rust weakens structural parts and affects smooth operation, leading to premature failure.

  • Choosing galvanized or stainless steel components can prolong life in coastal environments.
  • Regular lubrication and cleaning are critical to reducing buildup of salt and moisture.

Warping and Material Degradation

Wood and composite garage doors can absorb moisture in high-humidity conditions, resulting in warping, swelling, or delamination. This not only affects appearance but also door operation and seal integrity.

  • Applying specialized weatherproof coatings enhances resistance to humidity and UV damage.
  • Vinyl and aluminum doors are preferred in Pembroke Pines for their resistance to moisture-related issues.

Storm Damage and Wind Load Concerns

Hurricane season puts heavy wind and debris pressure on garage doors, which can lead to physical damage or seal failure. Building codes in Florida require garage doors to meet specific wind load ratings, making compliance essential.

  • Installing reinforced garage doors or wind-rated doors can protect property and enhance safety.

How to Prepare Your Garage Door for Florida’s Hurricane Season

Preparing your garage door before hurricane season is a crucial step in safeguarding your home and minimizing costly repairs. Experts recommend a proactive maintenance and inspection routine tailored to the heightened risks during these months.

  • Inspect Weatherstripping and Seals: Replace worn weatherstripping to prevent water intrusion and draft.
  • Reinforce Door Structure: Add braces or upgrade to wind-resistant doors certified for local hurricane codes.
  • Secure Hardware: Tighten bolts, screws, and brackets to withstand high winds and shaking.
  • Test Automatic Openers: Ensure backup battery systems are operational in case of power outages.
  • Trim Surrounding Trees and Debris: Remove potential projectiles that could damage the door during a storm.

Taking these measures can dramatically reduce the risk of garage door failure during severe weather, protecting valuables stored inside and maintaining home security.
