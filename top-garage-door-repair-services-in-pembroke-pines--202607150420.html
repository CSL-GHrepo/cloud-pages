Seasonal Garage Door Care: Addressing Pembroke Pines’ Climate Challenges

Pembroke Pines experiences a humid subtropical climate with hot summers, mild winters, and a hurricane season that can last from June through November. These environmental factors place unique demands on garage doors, making seasonal care an essential part of maintenance.

Effects of Humidity and Heat

High humidity can cause metal parts to rust and wooden doors to warp or swell. Heat can strain opener motors and degrade rubber seals.

  • Recommended Actions: Regular lubrication of metal components with rust-resistant products and timely replacement of weather stripping to prevent moisture infiltration.
  • Use of UV-resistant paint or sealants on wooden or fiberglass doors to prevent fading and cracking.

Preparing for Hurricane Season

Strong winds and storms pose a significant threat to garage doors, which are often the most vulnerable entry points during hurricanes. Damage to garage doors can lead to structural integrity problems and water intrusion.

  • Installation of hurricane-rated garage doors and wind-resistant reinforcement kits.
  • Ensuring proper anchoring of tracks and springs to withstand high wind pressures.
  • Post-storm inspections to assess and promptly repair any damage such as dents, misalignments, or weakened hardware.

Professional garage door repair companies in Pembroke Pines often offer specialized storm preparation and repair services, helping property owners safeguard their homes and businesses.
