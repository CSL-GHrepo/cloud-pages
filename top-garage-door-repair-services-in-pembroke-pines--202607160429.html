Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Pembroke Pines Residents

Given the distinct weather patterns in Pembroke Pines, including hot summers, a wet hurricane season, and mild winters, seasonally tailored maintenance helps extend your garage door’s lifespan.

Spring Maintenance

  • Inspect and clean the door and tracks to remove debris accumulated during winter.
  • Test all safety features to ensure full function in preparation for increased usage.
  • Lubricate moving parts to prevent squeaks and wear during the warmer months.

Summer Care

  • Check weather seals to prevent hot air from infiltrating and raising indoor temperatures.
  • Test opener motors for overheating signs due to frequent use.
  • Schedule professional inspections before hurricane season begins.

Hurricane Season Preparedness

  • Upgrade impact-resistant garage doors to meet or exceed local building codes.
  • Reinforce door locks and fasteners to prevent wind-driven damage.
  • Secure or remove potential flying debris near garages.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Clear gutters and downspouts near the garage area to avoid water pooling.
  • Inspect door alignment and adjust to address any warping or contraction due to temperature changes.
  • Lubricate and test openers to ensure reliable operation during cooler months.

Following a seasonally proactive maintenance schedule can save Pembroke Pines residents significant repair costs and downtime.
