Seasonal Care Tips for Pembroke Pines Garage Doors

Preparation and maintenance tailored to South Florida’s climate can drastically extend the life and reliability of garage doors. Here are valuable seasonal tips:

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Inspect and lubricate moving parts more frequently due to increased humidity and rain exposure.
  • Check weather seals for cracks or gaps that could let in moisture, pests, or hot air.
  • Test sensors frequently—they can be affected by rain or debris buildup.
  • Trim nearby trees or vegetation to prevent damage from storms or fallen limbs.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Although South Florida winters are mild, cooler temperatures can affect spring tension; testing door balance helps avoid strain on the opener.
  • Review your garage door opener battery backup or manual release system ahead of hurricane season.
  • Arrange a professional inspection before hurricane season to reinforce your door against high winds.

Following manufacturer recommendations and scheduling professional tune-ups twice a year keeps Pembroke Pines garage doors in prime condition through all seasons.
