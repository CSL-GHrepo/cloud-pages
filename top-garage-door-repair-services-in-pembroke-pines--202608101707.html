DIY Garage Door Maintenance Tips to Complement Professional Service

While certified professionals should handle repairs and installations, homeowners can take several steps to maintain their garage doors between service visits:

  • Visual Inspections: Regularly check for obvious signs of wear such as cracked panels, loose bolts, and debris buildup.
  • Track Cleaning: Keep tracks free of dirt and grease that could impede smooth operation.
  • Lubrication: Apply manufacturer-approved lubricants to rollers, hinges, and springs every 3-6 months to reduce friction.
  • Test Auto-Reverse: Place a small object like a block in the door’s path and close the door. It should automatically reverse when hitting the object—if not, call a professional immediately.
  • Remote Battery Replacement: Swap out remote batteries before they die to avoid lockouts or failed operations.

These simple practices can extend the life of your garage door and prevent minor issues from escalating.
