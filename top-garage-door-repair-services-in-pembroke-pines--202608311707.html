DIY Garage Door Maintenance Tips: What You Can Safely Do

While professional servicing is crucial, homeowners in Pembroke Pines can take everyday steps to keep their garage doors in good shape and avoid minor issues escalating.

  • Visual Inspection: Regularly check door panels, tracks, springs, and cables for apparent wear or damage.
  • Clean Tracks and Rollers: Remove dirt, debris, and spider webs that can impede movement.
  • Lubrication: Use silicone-based lubricant on rollers, hinges, and springs every 3-6 months – avoid oil-based lubricants which attract dirt.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Feature: Place an object under the closing door to ensure it reverses promptly for safety.
  • Check Weather Seals: Inspect bottom and side seals for cracks or gaps to maintain energy efficiency.
  • Tighten Hardware: Periodically tighten bolts and screws to prevent loosening from vibration and movement.

These simple tasks reduce common problems but never attempt spring or cable repair yourself. Contact Garage Door Repair of Pembroke Pines for all professional service needs.
