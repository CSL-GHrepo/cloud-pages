Preventative Residential Garage Door Maintenance Tips

Even with expert repair availability, homeowners can take proactive steps to extend their garage door’s operational life:

  1. Lubricate Moving Parts Regularly: Use a high-quality silicone spray or garage door lubricant on rollers, hinges, and springs.
  2. Inspect Safety Features: Test auto-reverse mechanisms and photo-eye sensors monthly to prevent accidents.
  3. Keep Tracks Clean and Aligned: Remove debris and ensure tracks are straight to avoid jerky or stalled door movement.
  4. Schedule Professional Inspections: At least once a year, local technicians can identify early signs of wear or hazards.

These simple measures can prevent emergency garage door repair requests and improve home security.
