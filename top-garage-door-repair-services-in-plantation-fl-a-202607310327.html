Safety Tips and Precautions for Plantation Garage Door Owners

Although garage doors seem simple, they are complex mechanical systems with powerful springs and motors that can pose serious safety risks if improperly handled. The Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation urges homeowners to follow safety guidelines to prevent accidents and injuries.

Do's and Don'ts for Safe Garage Door Use

  • Do keep remote controls out of reach of children to prevent unintended operation.
  • Do regularly test and maintain the auto-reverse safety feature to ensure the door stops or reverses upon encountering an obstacle.
  • Do schedule yearly professional inspections to identify worn components before failure.
  • Don't attempt DIY spring repairs or replacements—garage door springs are under high tension and can cause severe injury.
  • Don't leave the garage door partially open for extended periods, which can attract intruders or pests.
  • Don't ignore unusual sounds or sluggish door operation; these signs often precede breakdowns.

Following these precautions helps Plantation residents avoid costly repairs and keeps families safe.
