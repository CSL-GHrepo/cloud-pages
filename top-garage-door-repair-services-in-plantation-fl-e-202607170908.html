Understanding Garage Door Safety Features and Compliance in Plantation, FL

Safety is paramount when it comes to garage doors as they involve heavy moving parts and electrical mechanisms. Local codes and industry standards require specific safety features to protect users.

Essential Safety Features Installed by Professionals

  • Auto-Reverse Mechanism: Doors must have sensors that detect obstructions and automatically reverse to prevent injury or damage.
  • Photo Eye Sensors: Positioned near the floor to alert the opener system if an object or person crosses the threshold.
  • Manual Emergency Release: Enables users to manually operate the door during power outages or emergencies.
  • Spring Containment Systems: High-tension springs are enclosed or balanced to reduce the risk of sudden mechanical failure.
