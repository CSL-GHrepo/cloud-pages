Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for Plantation’s Climate

Maintaining a garage door to withstand Plantation’s unique weather patterns requires attention to seasonal changes. Here are some expert tips tailored to local conditions:

Summer Preparation

  • Inspect and lubricate: High temperatures can dry out springs and rollers. Use silicone-based lubricants recommended by professionals.
  • Check weather stripping: Replace any cracked or missing seals to keep humidity and insects out.
  • Test opener functions: Heat can affect motor efficiency, so verify the opener operates smoothly.

Rainy Season Readiness

  • Clear drainage: Ensure gutters around garage and door threshold are free of debris to prevent water pooling.
  • Inspect door panels: Look for any warping or damage caused by moisture exposure.
  • Perform electrical safety checks: Since humidity heightens the risk of short circuits, make sure wiring and sensors are well-sealed and functioning.

Hurricane and Storm Preparedness

  • Reinforce doors: Consider installing hurricane-rated doors or adding reinforcement kits for extra protection.
  • Secure loose parts: Tighten bolts and brackets to prevent damage during high winds.
  • Plan for power outages: Invest in a manual release mechanism or battery backup opener to ensure access during storms.

Following these season-specific tips with professional guidance helps extend your garage door's life and performance, avoiding surprises during Florida’s unpredictable weather shifts.
