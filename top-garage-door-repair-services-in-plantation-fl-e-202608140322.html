Dealing with Common Garage Door Problems: Troubleshooting Tips for Plantation Residents

While professional repairs are recommended for safety and longevity, understanding common garage door problems and basic troubleshooting techniques can help Plantation homeowners address minor issues and decide when to call an expert.

Garage Door Won’t Open or Close

  • Check Power Supply: Ensure the opener is plugged in and the circuit breaker has not tripped.
  • Remote and Wall Control Batteries: Replace batteries regularly, especially if the door responds sporadically.
  • Obstruction Detection: Clean sensors and ensure they are aligned correctly; even small debris or spider webs can block signals.

Unusual Noises During Operation

  • Lubrication Needed: Springs, rollers, and hinges require periodic lubrication to reduce squeaks and grinding noises.
  • Loose Hardware: Tighten bolts and screws periodically to minimize rattling.
  • Damaged Rollers or Tracks: Signs of wear or damage should prompt professional inspection to prevent sudden failure.

Slow or Uneven Door Movement

  • Balance and Springs: Doors out of balance often indicate spring issues that need professional adjustment or replacement.
  • Track Obstructions: Clear any debris, and ensure tracks are properly aligned.
