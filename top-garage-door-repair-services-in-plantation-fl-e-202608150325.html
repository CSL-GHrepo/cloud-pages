Maintaining Garage Door Longevity: Best Practices and Seasonal Tips for Plantation Residents

Ensuring your garage door lasts as long as possible requires ongoing care tailored to Plantation’s environment. Here are some expert-recommended best practices:

Routine Cleaning to Prevent Corrosion and Mold

Regularly washing your garage door with mild detergent and water helps remove salt residue, dirt, and mildew that thrive in humid climates. Rinse thoroughly and inspect for chipped paint or damaged seals that could expose the door to moisture damage.

Seasonal Inspection and Tune-Ups

Plantation experiences both hot summers and stormy rainy seasons. Schedule professional inspections in early spring and fall to check for:

  • Spring: Damage from winter moisture accumulation, sensor alignment, and lubrication of moving parts.
  • Fall: Preparation for hurricane season by verifying door anchors, tightening hardware, and testing emergency release mechanisms.

Storm Preparedness and Garage Door Reinforcement

Given Florida’s exposure to hurricanes, Plantation homeowners should consider:

  • Installing hurricane-rated garage doors that comply with Florida Building Code requirements.
  • Reinforcing existing doors with hurricane straps, additional bracing, or secondary locking systems.
  • Maintaining storm shutters or temporary protective panels to prevent damage from debris impact.

The right preparation can save thousands in repair costs and keep your family safe during severe weather.
