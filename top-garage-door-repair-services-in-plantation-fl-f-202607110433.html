Common Garage Door Problems Specific to Plantation Climate

Plantation’s coastal environment presents unique challenges that can impact garage door performance and lifespan. Understanding these common issues helps homeowners recognize early warning signs and seek timely repairs.

  • Corrosion and Rust: The salty air accelerates metal parts’ rusting, particularly on springs, rollers, and tracks, which impairs smooth operation and safety.
  • Warping and Swelling: High humidity can cause wooden door panels to warp or swell, leading to misalignment, sticking, and gaps that invite pests or drafts.
  • Sensor Malfunctions: Frequent exposure to moisture can affect electrical components like safety sensors or openers, resulting in intermittent or complete failures.
