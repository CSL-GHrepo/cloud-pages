Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Plantation Homeowners

Florida’s diverse weather—with hot summers, heavy rain, and occasional strong storms—necessitates seasonal garage door maintenance to ensure longevity and flawless operation.

Spring and Summer Maintenance Checklist

  • Inspect Weather Seals: Heat and humidity can degrade rubber seals; check and replace worn seals to prevent water entry and pests.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply silicone-based lubricants to springs, rollers, and tracks to reduce friction and noise.
  • Test Safety Sensors: Ensure sensors are clean and properly aligned to avoid malfunctions.
  • Check Panels and Frames: Look for warping or damage caused by sun exposure and address promptly.

Fall and Winter Maintenance Checklist

  • Examine Door Balance: Temperature fluctuations can affect tension; test door balance to prevent motor strain.
  • Inspect for Rust: High humidity and salt air can cause corrosion; clean and treat affected parts.
  • Test Opener Backup Battery: With storm season approaching, verify backup systems are functional.
  • Clear Debris Around Tracks: Remove leaves and dirt to ensure smooth operation.

Following these seasonal tips, or scheduling professional maintenance with Garage Door Repair of Plantation, helps avoid unexpected failures and costly repairs.
