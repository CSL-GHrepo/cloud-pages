Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips Tailored for Plantation’s Climate

Plantation’s unique subtropical climate presents specific challenges that can affect garage doors throughout the year. Tackling seasonal maintenance proactively helps extend the system’s life and prevents weather-related damages.

Spring and Summer

  • Inspect for Moisture Damage: Frequent thunderstorms increase the risk of rust and corrosion on metal components. Regularly check tracks, springs, and hinges for signs of rust and apply rust inhibitors as necessary.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Heat and humidity can dry out lubricant, causing friction and wear. Use weather-resistant lubricant formulations for durability.
  • Check Weather Seals: Inspect door seals and replace cracked or brittle seals to prevent water intrusion during heavy rains and retain interior temperatures.

Fall and Winter

  • Adjust Tension and Balance: Cooler temperatures can affect spring tension and door balance. A well-balanced door prevents premature component failure.
  • Maintain Opener Electronics: Humidity may affect opener circuits; ensure protective casings are intact and sensors are clean and aligned.
  • Clear Debris: Remove fallen leaves and accumulated dirt from tracks and around the door to avoid operational interference.

Professional garage door maintenance services from Garage Door Repair of Plantation include seasonal tune-ups tailored to these climate considerations, improving reliability through all seasons.
