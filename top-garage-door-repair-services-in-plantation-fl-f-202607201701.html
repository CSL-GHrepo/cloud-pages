Common Garage Door Problems and How Professional Services Address Them

While some minor issues might seem manageable, many common garage door problems require expert diagnosis and repair to ensure long-term safety and performance. Here’s a look at typical problems and how Garage Door Repair of Plantation approaches them:

Broken Garage Door Springs

Garage door springs bear the tension required to lift heavy doors safely. Broken torsion or extension springs can render the door inoperable and pose injury risks if improperly handled.

  • Inspection: Technicians examine spring condition and tension to identify wear points or metal fatigue.
  • Replacement: Using properly rated springs for door weight and size, installers replace damaged components safely.
  • Adjustment: Springs are calibrated to balance the door, preventing strain on the opener and hardware.

Malfunctioning Garage Door Sensors

Safety sensors prevent doors from closing on objects or people. Dirt, misalignment, or wiring issues can cause sensors to malfunction, stopping the door or reversing operation unexpectedly.

  • Cleaning and Aligning: Technicians clean lenses and adjust sensor positions for optimal detection.
  • Testing: Sensors undergo operational testing to verify response accuracy.
  • Replacement: Faulty sensors and wiring are replaced to restore dependable safety functions.

Noisy Garage Doors

Unusual sounds such as grinding, squeaking, or banging often indicate worn hardware or lack of lubrication. Ignoring these noises can lead to more serious mechanical failures.

  • Lubrication: Application of premium garage door lubricants reduces friction in bearings, rollers, and hinges.
  • Hardware Replacement: Worn rollers, hinges, or tracks are replaced with durable parts to restore smooth operation.
  • Alignment and Leveling: Ensuring door components are aligned eliminates impacts and reduces vibration noise.
