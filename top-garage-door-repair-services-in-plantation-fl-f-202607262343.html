Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for Plantation Residents

While professional maintenance is key, proactive homeowners can take several simple steps throughout the year to enhance garage door performance and longevity, especially given Plantation’s climate challenges.

Spring Maintenance Checklist

  • Inspect weather stripping and replace cracked or torn seals to prevent water intrusion during the rainy season.
  • Check for rust on exposed metal components caused by winter humidity; apply rust-inhibiting spray if needed.
  • Lubricate hinges, rollers, springs, and tracks to prepare for increased summer use.
  • Test the automatic reverse function to ensure safety sensors align after winter settling.

Summer Maintenance Checklist

  • Monitor door balance periodically—heat and sun exposure can affect spring tension and tracking.
  • Verify opener remotes and keypad functionality, considering heat-related battery drain.
  • Clean door panels with mild detergent to remove salt buildup and UV-induced residue.
  • Schedule a professional inspection before hurricane season for reinforcement advice.

Autumn Maintenance Checklist

  • Clear any accumulated leaves, twigs, or debris from the door tracks and around the door base to avoid obstruction.
  • Inspect and tighten loose hardware caused by frequent thermal expansion and contraction.
  • Confirm that weather seals remain flexible and intact ahead of cooler and wetter months.
  • Test garage lighting fixtures to ensure proper operation as daylight hours diminish.

Winter Maintenance Checklist

  • Check for condensation or moisture buildup inside the garage, which can accelerate corrosion.
  • Operate the door slowly to reduce motor strain in cooler temperatures.
  • Inspect garage door opener batteries and replace if low to maintain reliable function.
  • Schedule professional preventative maintenance to address any seasonal wear or damage promptly.
