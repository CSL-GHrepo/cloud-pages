Garage Door Troubleshooting: DIY Tips and When to Call the Experts

While professional garage door repair is often necessary for complex issues, some minor problems can be temporarily managed by homeowners while awaiting expert service. Here are some common issues and in-home troubleshooting advice:

Garage Door Won’t Open or Close

  • Check the remote battery: Replace to rule out power failure.
  • Inspect sensors: Clean and align photo-eye sensors; obstructions can prevent the door from operating.
  • Manual release: Use the emergency pull cord to operate the door manually if the power opener fails.

Door is Noisy or Jerky

  • Lubricate moving parts: Apply silicone-based lubricant to rollers, hinges, and springs to reduce friction.
  • Tighten hardware: Loose bolts or screws can cause rattling and uneven motion.

Door is Misaligned or Off Track

  • Inspect visible tracks for dents or obstructions.
  • Do not attempt realigning the tracks yourself if springs or cables are involved—these components carry dangerous tension.

Always prioritize safety and contact Garage Door Repair of Plantation for complex problems requiring specialized tools and expertise.
