Understanding Common Garage Door Problems and Their Solutions

To better appreciate the value of professional repairs, it’s helpful to understand frequent garage door issues encountered in Plantation homes and businesses, along with typical solutions.

Garage Door Won’t Open or Close

  • Causes: Dead remote batteries, misaligned safety sensors, broken springs, or power supply problems.
  • Repairs: Replacing batteries, realigning sensors, spring replacement, or electrical diagnostics and repairs.

Unusual Noises During Operation

  • Causes: Worn rollers, loose hardware, dry hinges, or failing opener components.
  • Repairs: Lubrication, tightening bolts, roller replacement, or opener motor repairs.

Door Opens or Closes Partially

  • Causes: Track obstructions, spring tension issues, or malfunctioning limit switches.
  • Repairs: Cleaning tracks, adjusting spring tension, or resetting limit switches for proper travel limits.

Door Is Off Track or Misaligned

  • Causes: Accidental impacts, worn rollers, or track damage.
  • Repairs: Re-aligning or replacing tracks and rollers, inspecting for structural damage.

Remote or Keypad Not Working

  • Causes: Signal interference, dead batteries, or receiver issues.
  • Repairs: Reprogramming devices, battery replacement, or installing signal boosters.
