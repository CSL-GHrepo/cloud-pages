DIY Garage Door Maintenance Tips from the Experts

While professional maintenance is crucial, there are simple steps Plantation homeowners can take to extend the life of their garage doors between professional visits.

  • Visual inspections: Regularly check for loose or broken hardware, frayed cables, or worn weather seals.
  • Lubrication: Apply garage door lubricant to hinges, rollers, and springs every few months to reduce friction and wear.
  • Sensor cleaning: Wipe down safety sensors to keep them free from dirt or spider webs that could obstruct their functioning.
  • Balance testing: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway; it should hold its position if balanced correctly.
  • Check opener remote batteries: Replace batteries regularly to avoid communication failures.
