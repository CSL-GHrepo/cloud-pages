Common Garage Door Problems and How Professional Repair Solves Them

Broken Garage Door Springs

Garage door springs bear significant stress and can break due to fatigue or weather-related corrosion. Attempting DIY repair is dangerous due to the high tension involved. Professional technicians use specialized tools and safety protocols to safely replace springs and restore smooth operation.

Misaligned or Damaged Tracks

If your garage door struggles to open or closes unevenly, bent or misaligned tracks could be the cause. Skilled technicians precisely realign or replace tracks, ensuring the door glides properly without jams or strain on other components.

Malfunctioning Garage Door Opener Motors

Opener motors can fail due to electrical issues, worn gears, or mechanical obstructions. Garage Door Repair of Plantation offers comprehensive diagnostic services to identify the root cause and either repair or replace faulty motors, restoring quiet and reliable operation.

Sensor and Remote Control Issues

Safety sensors prevent garage doors from closing on objects or people. Dirt, misalignment, or electrical failure can cause sensors to malfunction. Likewise, remote controls may lose programming or face interference. Experienced technicians quickly rectify these problems, ensuring your door’s safety features function impeccably.

Customer Education: Tips for Safe Garage Door Usage

Proper usage of your garage door helps extend its lifespan and keeps you and your family safe. Here are some expert-recommended best practices:

  • Never attempt to manually force the door open or shut—use the opener or contact a professional if the door is stuck.
  • Keep clear of the door’s path when it is operating to prevent injury.
  • Test the auto-reverse feature monthly to ensure it stops and reverses when encountering an obstacle.
  • Educate children about the dangers of playing near or with garage doors.
  • Maintain a clear area around the garage door sensors to avoid false triggers.
  • Schedule professional maintenance even if the door appears to be functioning normally; early detection prevents larger problems.
