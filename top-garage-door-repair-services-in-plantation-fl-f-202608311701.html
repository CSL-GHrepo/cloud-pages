Tips for DIY Garage Door Maintenance: What You Can Do Safely

While professional service is recommended for most repairs, homeowners can perform several maintenance tasks to reduce wear and prolong their garage door’s life.

  • Visual Inspections: Regularly check for visible signs of wear, damage, or rust on springs, cables, and panels.
  • Lubrication: Use recommended lubricants on rollers, hinges, and tracks to ensure smooth operation.
  • Test Safety Features: Periodically test auto-reverse safety sensors on automatic doors by placing an object in the path.
  • Keep Tracks Clean: Clear debris from tracks and ensure they remain aligned without attempting major adjustments.
  • Tighten Hardware: Check and tighten loose bolts or screws on the garage door assembly.
