Maintaining Your Garage Door: Tips from Plantation’s Experts

Preventive residential garage door maintenance is critical to avoiding emergency repairs and costly replacements. Experts recommend these simple yet effective steps:

  • Monthly visual inspections: Look for wear on springs, cables, rollers, and tracks.
  • Lubricate moving parts: Use a silicone-based lubricant on hinges and springs every six months.
  • Test door balance: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway. It should stay in place.
  • Clean weather stripping: Remove dirt and debris that can interfere with door sealing.
  • Schedule professional maintenance: Annual tune-ups by licensed technicians catch issues early.

Following these measures can extend garage door lifespan significantly, saving you both inconvenience and money. Plantation’s garage door specialists, like Garage Door Repair of Plantation, offer maintenance plans customized to regional conditions for maximum reliability.
