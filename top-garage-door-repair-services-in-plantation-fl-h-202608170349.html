Maintenance Tips to Extend the Life of Your Garage Door in Plantation

  • Safety Features Check: Confirm sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms work properly to protect family and property.
  • Professional Tune-Ups: Schedule annual maintenance with local technicians to air out problems before they escalate.

DIY Garage Door Repair: When to Try and When to Call the Pros

Many homeowners may feel inclined to attempt simple garage door repairs themselves to save money. While some tasks are manageable without professional help, safety and expertise concerns often make it wiser to hire qualified technicians.

Simple Repairs You Can Handle Safely

  • Lubrication: Applying lubricant to rollers, hinges, and springs to reduce noise and wear.
  • Replacing Weather Seals: Installing new bottom or side seals to improve insulation and block drafts.
  • Manual Balance Check: Testing door balance by disconnecting the opener and raising the door manually.
  • Tightening Hardware: Checking and tightening loose bolts or screws to prevent rattling and misalignment.

Repairs Best Left to Professionals

  • Spring Replacement: Garage door springs are under extreme tension and can cause serious injury if mishandled.
  • Opener Motor Repairs: Electrical components require skilled diagnostics and safe wiring practices.
  • Cable Replacement: Like springs, cables are dangerous to replace without proper tools and training.
  • Door Panel Replacement and Alignment: Requires specialized knowledge and equipment to ensure smooth and safe operation.
“Attempting to repair high-tension springs or cables without experience is extremely risky,” cautions Jake Ramirez, lead technician at Garage Door Repair of Plantation. “We encourage homeowners to call experts immediately when problems go beyond simple upkeep.”
