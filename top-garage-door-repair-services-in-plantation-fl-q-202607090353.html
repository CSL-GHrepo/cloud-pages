Understanding Garage Door Safety: Critical Precautions For Plantation Residents

Garage doors are large, heavy mechanical systems requiring strict safety protocols to prevent injury or property damage. Homeowners must understand these precautions:

  • Never Attempt Spring Repairs Yourself: Springs store immense tension and can cause severe injury if mishandled. Always contact certified professionals for spring issues.
  • Keep Hands Clear of Moving Parts: Avoid placing fingers near hinges, rollers, or tracks while the door is operating.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Features Regularly: Verify that sensors stop and reverse the door if an object interrupts its path, complying with safety regulations.
  • Secure Remote Controls and Keypads: Prevent unauthorized access by controlling who has remote devices and regularly updating keypad entry codes.
  • Educate All Household Members: Teach everyone how to operate the garage door safely, including manual release procedures during power failures.
