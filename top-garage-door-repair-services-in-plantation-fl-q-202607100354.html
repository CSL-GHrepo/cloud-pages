Tips for DIY Garage Door Troubleshooting: When to Call the Professionals

While some minor issues can be addressed by homeowners safely, many garage door repairs require professional expertise. Here are a few DIY tips paired with warnings to know when to stop and seek expert help:

  • Check Power Supply: If your garage door opener doesn’t respond, verify power and remote batteries before calling for service.
  • Inspect Safety Sensors: Clean and align sensors if the door won’t close. Misalignment is a common reason for non-closing doors.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply silicone spray or recommended lubricant to hinges and rollers to reduce noise and friction.
  • Avoid Attempting Spring Repairs: Garage door springs are under extreme tension and can cause injury. Always contact licensed technicians for spring-related problems.
  • Don’t Force the Door: If the door sticks or jams, avoid using excessive force which can worsen damage.
