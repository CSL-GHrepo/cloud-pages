Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Plantation, FL

To avoid costly repairs, regular maintenance is essential. Here are proactive steps you can take:

  1. Inspect and lubricate: Check springs, rollers, and hinges every six months and apply appropriate lubricant.
  2. Balance test: Disconnect the opener and manually raise the door halfway to see if it stays in place, indicating proper spring tension.
  3. Check weather seals: Ensure seals around the door are intact to keep moisture and pests out.
  4. Test safety features: Regularly test auto-reverse and photo-eye sensors for opener safety compliance.
  5. Schedule professional maintenance: Engage affordable garage door technicians for yearly professional checkups.

How Weather and Seasonal Changes Affect Garage Door Performance in Plantation

Although Plantation enjoys a relatively consistent subtropical climate, subtle seasonal variations can impact your garage door’s performance and lifespan.

Spring and Summer Considerations

  • Heat Expansion: Metal parts may expand causing harder movement or sticking sections.
  • Humidity: Can corrode metal components and degrade rubber seals, requiring timely lubrication and part replacement.
  • Storm Preparation: Tropical storms necessitate preemptive inspections and reinforces doors to withstand strong winds.

Fall and Winter Adjustments

  • Cooler Temperatures: Springs and torsion bars can become brittle, increasing the risk of breakage.
  • Morning Dew and Moisture: Promote rust formation and freezing of seals or tracks, potentially jamming doors.
  • Reduced Daylight: Encourages issues with opener sensor alignment that may require recalibration.
