Tips for Seasonal Garage Door Care in Plantation’s Climate

Plantation’s warm, humid subtropical climate exposes garage doors to specific wear factors that can affect performance and longevity. Here are expert seasonal care tips:

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Inspect and clean tracks regularly to prevent debris buildup caused by pollen and plant matter.
  • Lubricate moving parts every three months to combat increased friction caused by humidity.
  • Check weather seals for cracks or gaps to prevent water and insects from entering the garage.

Fall and Winter Care

  • Even with mild winters, check door balance and springs, as temperature fluctuations can affect metal tension.
  • Test safety sensors before potential heavy rain or storms and replace batteries in any keyless controls.
  • Trim nearby trees and shrubs to avoid damage from branches during windstorms common in Florida’s hurricane season.

Following season-specific maintenance schedules minimizes the impact of Plantation’s climate on garage door systems and enhances reliability.
