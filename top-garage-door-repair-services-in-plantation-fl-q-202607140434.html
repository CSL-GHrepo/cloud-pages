Understanding Garage Door Components: A Guide for Plantation Homeowners

Knowing the essential parts of a garage door system helps homeowners quickly identify issues and communicate effectively when requesting repairs or replacements.

Key Components Explained

  • Springs: Torsion or extension springs counterbalance the weight of the door and facilitate smooth opening and closing.
  • Panels: The large door sections that make up the overall door; can be steel, wood, aluminum, or composite.
  • Tracks: Metal rails that guide the door as it opens and closes.
  • Rollers: Small wheels attached to the door that run along the tracks.
  • Opener: The electric motor system that automates door movement, including remotes and wall controls.
  • Cables: Steel cables connected to springs that support door weight.
  • Weather Seals: Strips around the door edges and bottom that protect against weather intrusion.

Recognizing these parts helps Plantation residents understand technician recommendations and take proactive measures for maintenance.
