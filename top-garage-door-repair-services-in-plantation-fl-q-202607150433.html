Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door Between Professional Services

While professional maintenance is crucial, Plantation homeowners and business owners can extend the life of their garage doors by performing simple upkeep tasks regularly. These include:

  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply garage door lubricant to rollers, hinges, and springs every six months to reduce friction and noise.
  • Check Balance: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway; it should stay in place without sliding up or down. An unbalanced door stresses the opener and components.
  • Inspect Weather Seals: Look for gaps, tears, or hardened seals at the edges and bottom to maintain insulation and pest resistance.
  • Test Auto-Reverse: Place a small object in the door’s path and close it; it should immediately reverse when it touches the obstacle.
  • Clear Debris: Keep tracks and nearby areas free from dirt, leaves, or ice buildup to avoid mechanical blockages.

Following these practices can prevent small issues from becoming urgent repairs and save Plantation property owners money over time.
