Understanding Garage Door Parts: A Closer Look

For Plantation homeowners and business operators, familiarizing yourself with the basic components of your garage door system can help you communicate more effectively with repair professionals and better understand necessary repairs.

Key Components of Residential Garage Doors

  • Door Panels: The main door sections, which can be made from steel, aluminum, wood, or composite materials.
  • Springs: Torsion or extension springs counterbalance the door’s weight, making it easy to lift and lower.
  • Tracks: Guide the door as it opens and closes; properly aligned tracks reduce wear and ensure smooth operation.
  • Rollers: Small wheels that move along the tracks to support and guide the door.
  • Cables: Assist the springs in lifting the door and maintain tension to prevent erratic movement.
  • Opener: The motorized mechanism that powers the door’s movement, connected to a remote control or wall panel.
  • Safety Sensors: Infrared beams near the ground ensure the door reverses if an object or person crosses its path.

Common Failure Points and Signs to Watch For

  • Worn Springs: Look for gaps in torsion springs or broken cables; a stuck door often signals spring problems.
  • Misaligned Tracks: Doors that get stuck or jump noisily on closing/opening often indicate track damage or shifts.
  • Damaged Rollers: Noisy operation and uneven door movement can result from cracked or rusted rollers.
  • Sensor Malfunctions: If the door won’t close and the safety eyes indicate a blockage, sensor cleaning or realignment may be necessary.
