How to Prepare Your Garage Door for Plantation’s Storm Season

Plantation, FL, is vulnerable to tropical storms and hurricanes during certain times of the year. Preparing your garage door for storm conditions is essential to protect your property and ensure operational readiness afterward.

Reinforcement and Inspection

Storm-force winds and debris can severely damage garage doors not designed or maintained for extreme weather:

  • Install Wind-Resistant Doors: Consider impact-rated garage doors built to withstand hurricane-force winds, especially if your home is in a high-risk zone.
  • Inspect Hardware and Tracks: Secure loose bolts, brackets, and rollers to prevent door failure during high winds.
  • Check Weatherstripping and Seals: Ensure all seals are intact to prevent water intrusion during storms.

Emergency Preparedness Tips

  • Mechanical Locks: Use manual locks or braces on sectional doors to enhance stability during power outages.
  • Backup Power: Invest in battery backup systems for automatic openers to enable door access when grid power is down.
  • Post-Storm Inspection: After storms, have a professional inspect for warping, track bends, or motor damage before use.
