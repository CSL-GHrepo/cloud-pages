Salt-laden air, typical near coastal areas like Plantation, accelerates metal corrosion. Garage door components such as springs, hinges, and tracks are especially vulnerable.

  • Signs to Watch For: Visible rust spots, stiffness in moving parts, or unusual noises during operation.
  • Preventive Measures: Applying anti-corrosion sprays during routine maintenance and opting for galvanized or stainless steel parts.

Tips for DIY Garage Door Care Between Professional Services

While professional maintenance is indispensable, simple DIY routines can prolong your garage door’s life and reduce unexpected breakdowns.

  • Visual Inspection: Regularly check for signs of wear such as frayed cables, loose bolts, or cracked panels.
  • Lubrication: Apply silicone-based or lithium grease to springs, rollers, and hinges every six months to maintain smooth operation.
  • Keep Tracks Clean: Remove debris and dirt from tracks using a dry cloth and ensure the alignment is straight.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Feature: Place an object under the door and close it to make sure the door reverses upon detection.
  • Replace Weatherstripping: Check and replace worn weatherstripping to prevent drafts and pest intrusion.

These simple steps minimize wear and help identify issues early. However, any detected malfunction beyond basic care warrants immediate professional evaluation to avoid accidents or expensive repairs.
