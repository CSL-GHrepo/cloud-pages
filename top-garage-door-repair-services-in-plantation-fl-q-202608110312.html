DIY Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Plantation Residents

While expert repair is essential for serious issues, routine maintenance tasks done by homeowners can prolong the life of a garage door and reduce repair costs. Here are some safe, simple DIY tips recommended by Plantation garage door professionals:

  • Visual Inspection: Monthly checks of door panels, springs, cables, and tracks for obvious wear or damage.
  • Cleaning: Wipe down door surfaces and tracks to remove dirt, spider webs, and debris that impair movement.
  • Lubrication: Apply lubricant to rollers, hinges, and springs twice a year using a silicone-based spray or grease recommended by experts.
  • Balance Test: Disconnect the door opener and manually lift the door halfway. A balanced door should stay in place without falling or rising.
  • Test Safety Sensors: Periodically verify the auto-reverse sensors block the door from closing when obstructed.

Always avoid DIY attempts on springs and openers if unfamiliar, as these components can be dangerous. Scheduling professional inspections in addition to regular DIY care is the best approach.
