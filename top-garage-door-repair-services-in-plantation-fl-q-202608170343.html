Practical Tips for Plantation Homeowners: Maintaining Your Garage Door Between Repairs

Beyond professional services, homeowners in Plantation can take several simple steps to keep their garage doors running smoothly:

  1. Lubricate Moving Parts: Use silicone-based lubricant on rollers, hinges, and springs to reduce friction and wear.
  2. Check Door Balance: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway—if it doesn’t stay in place, it may need adjustment.
  3. Test Auto-Reverse: Place an object under the door to ensure it reverses when closing—a vital safety feature.
  4. Clean Tracks: Remove debris and ensure tracks are properly aligned.
  5. Schedule Annual Maintenance: Professional inspections catch small problems early.

Following these practices can reduce unexpected breakdowns and extend the service intervals for your garage door system.
