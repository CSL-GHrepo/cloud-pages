Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Plantation Residents

Plantation’s subtropical climate means homeowners should adapt garage door maintenance practices to seasonal changes. Proper seasonal care extends the lifespan of your garage door system and prevents unexpected failures.

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Heat and humidity can accelerate wear on springs, rollers, and hinges. Use manufacturer-approved lubricants to reduce friction and noise.
  • Inspect Weatherstripping: The warm months bring heavy rains and storms. Check seals and weatherstripping to prevent water intrusion and pest entry.
  • Test Balance and Alignment: Heat can cause metal components to expand, altering door alignment. Regular testing prevents binding or uneven wear on parts.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Check for Moisture Damage: Cooler temperatures and storms can cause condensation buildup—inspect for rust and corrosion on springs and tracks.
  • Examine the Opener Battery and Electrical Components: Ensure backup battery units are functional for power outages during storms common in the season.
  • Clear Debris: Remove leaves and dirt from door tracks and exterior to avoid obstruction or damage from freezing conditions overnight.

Scheduling professional seasonal tune-ups in Plantation ensures that minor issues are detected early and resolved before they become costly repairs.
