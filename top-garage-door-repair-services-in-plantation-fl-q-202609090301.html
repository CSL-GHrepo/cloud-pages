Common Garage Door Problems Specific to Plantation and How to Avoid Them

Understanding the typical garage door issues caused by local environmental conditions can help Plantation residents take preventive measures. Garage Door Repair of Plantation shares insights drawn from years of service in the area.

Rust and Corrosion Due to Coastal Air

Plantation’s proximity to the ocean exposes metal door components to salty air, which accelerates corrosion. Preventive measures include:

  • Choosing galvanized or stainless steel hardware
  • Applying protective coatings and lubricants regularly
  • Scheduling semi-annual inspections to detect early signs of rust

Warping and Swelling from Humidity

High humidity levels can cause wooden garage doors to warp or swell, affecting operation and appearance. Homeowners can mitigate this by:

  • Using quality exterior paint or sealants designed for humid climates
  • Opting for composite or metal doors when replacement is necessary
  • Ensuring proper ventilation within the garage space

Opener Malfunctions from Power Surges During Storms

Frequent storms in Plantation can cause electrical fluctuations that damage garage door openers and sensors. Strategies to prevent these issues include:

  • Installing surge protectors specifically rated for garage door openers
  • Conducting routine electrical system checks
  • Implementing battery backup systems for uninterrupted operation
