Comprehensive Garage Door Spring Repair and Replacement: Ensuring Safety and Performance

Garage door springs are critical components that bear the door’s weight and facilitate smooth, balanced movement. They are also among the most dangerous parts to repair or replace without professional experience. Here's what you need to know:

Types of Garage Door Springs

  • Torsion springs: Mounted above the door, these springs use torque and are preferred for durability and balanced lifting of heavy doors.
  • Extension springs: Positioned on either side of the door tracks, these stretch and contract to counterbalance the door’s weight, common in older or lighter doors.

Signs You Need Spring Repair or Replacement

  • Garage door opens unevenly or slowly, suggesting uneven spring tension.
  • Loud, unusual noises like snapping or banging when operating the door.
  • Visible gaps or broken coils in the spring mechanism.
  • Door won’t stay open or is difficult to raise manually.

The Risk Factor: Why DIY Spring Repairs Are Dangerous
