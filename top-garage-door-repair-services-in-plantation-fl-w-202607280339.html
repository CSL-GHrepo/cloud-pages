Maintenance Tips DIYers Can Do at Home Safely in Plantation

While professional upkeep is essential, there are several maintenance tasks Plantation homeowners can perform to prolong their garage door’s life and improve safety between technician visits:

  • Visual Inspection: Look for signs of wear, rust, cracks, and unusual noises periodically.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply garage door grease or silicone spray to hinges, rollers, and springs every six months (avoid over-lubrication).
  • Test Auto-Reverse Function: Place an object under the door and close it to ensure it reverses on contact.
  • Clear Tracks: Remove debris, dirt, or cobwebs to prevent obstruction of door movement.
  • Keep the Area Dry: Prevent standing water near the door and keep drainage paths clear.

Warning: Never attempt to repair garage door springs or cables yourself due to the risk of serious injury.
