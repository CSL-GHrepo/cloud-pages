How Weather Affects Garage Doors in Plantation and Preventative Measures

Plantation’s subtropical climate introduces unique challenges to garage door durability and function. Understanding these impacts enables proactive care and timely repairs.

Humidity and Corrosion

High humidity levels accelerate rusting on metal components such as springs, hinges, and tracks. Coastal salt air exacerbates corrosion, particularly on steel parts. Regular inspections and corrosion-resistant coatings mitigate damage.

  • Protective lubricants: Use silicone-based or other non-water-based lubricants selectively to prevent moisture retention.
  • Galvanized or stainless steel: Opt for parts made from corrosion-resistant metals when replacing components.
  • Routine cleaning: Periodic washing of the door exterior removes salt deposits that cause surface degradation.

Heat and Expansion

Florida’s intense heat causes materials like aluminum and wood to expand and contract, potentially leading to misalignment or warping of garage doors. Trained technicians understand these dynamics and adjust installation or repairs accordingly.

  • Expansion gaps: Ensuring sufficient gaps during installation accommodates material movement without straining the door mechanism.
  • Material selection: Composite materials or treated wood options resist warping better than untreated wood.
  • Regular adjustment: Periodic resets of track and spring tensions counterbalance the effects of temperature fluctuations.

Storm Preparedness and Impact Damage

Plantation’s susceptibility to tropical storms and hurricanes necessitates reinforced garage doors and emergency preparedness:

  • Wind-rated doors: Doors tested and rated for high-wind resistance help protect your property during severe weather.
  • Impact-resistant panels: Laminated glass or reinforced steel panels reduce damage from flying debris.
  • Storm shutters and backup power: Consider supplementary storm protection and generator-powered openers in case of outages.
  • Post-storm inspections: Prompt assessment of door damage after storms prevents long-term issues and safety hazards.
