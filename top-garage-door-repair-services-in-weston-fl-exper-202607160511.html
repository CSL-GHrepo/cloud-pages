Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance Tips Specific to Weston's Climate

Weston’s subtropical climate, with distinct wet and dry seasons, requires homeowners to follow specific maintenance practices to keep garage doors operating optimally year-round.

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Check for Rust and Corrosion: High humidity can cause metal components to rust; lubricate and replace parts as needed.
  • Inspect Weather Seals: Ensure bottom seals and side weatherstripping are intact to prevent water intrusion during summer storms.
  • Test Automatic Sensors: Rain, moisture, or dirt can misalign or obstruct sensors, so regular cleaning and alignment checks are crucial.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Inspect and Tighten Hardware: Temperature swings can cause expansion and contraction, loosening bolts and screws.
  • Verify Door Balance: Cooler temperatures affect spring tension; an unbalanced door strains openers and can be dangerous.
  • Clear Debris from Tracks: Falling leaves and debris accumulate in tracks, potentially causing jams or damage.

Garage Door Repair of Weston offers seasonal inspection services to proactively address these climate-specific issues before they cause breakdowns.
