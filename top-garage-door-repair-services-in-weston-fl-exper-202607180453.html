Common Garage Door Problems and How Specialists Address Them

Understanding frequent garage door issues helps homeowners recognize when to seek professional intervention. Garage Door Repair of Weston’s technicians diagnose and fix problems including:

1. Noisy Garage Doors

Common causes of squeaks and grinding sounds include worn rollers, dry hinges, or misaligned tracks. The team conducts thorough inspections, lubricates components with high-quality garage door grease, replaces damaged parts, and adjusts hardware to restore whisper-quiet operation.

2. Slow or Uneven Movement

A garage door that opens or closes sluggishly or at uneven speeds might signal spring fatigue, opener motor issues, or binding tracks. Accurate balancing and opener calibration performed by licensed technicians restore smooth, safe motion and prevent premature wear on the system.

3. Door Reversing Mid-Cycle

If a garage door reverses unexpectedly after starting to close, obstruction sensors might be misaligned or malfunctioning. Technicians carry specialized tools to realign sensors, replace faulty wiring or components, and verify compliance with safety standards set by the Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC).

4. Remote Control or Keypad Malfunctions

Wireless entry devices can fail due to dead batteries, signal interference, or programming glitches. Garage Door Repair of Weston offers prompt troubleshooting, replacement of remotes, keypads, and security system updates, ensuring seamless access control.
