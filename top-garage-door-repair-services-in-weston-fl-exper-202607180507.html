DIY Garage Door Maintenance vs. Professional Service: What You Should Know

Many homeowners consider performing basic garage door maintenance themselves. While some tasks are safe and straightforward, others require professional expertise to avoid injury or costly errors. Below is an overview to help you decide when to call in the experts.

Safe DIY Maintenance Tasks

  • Lubrication: Applying silicone spray or garage door lubricant to rollers, hinges, and springs (using proper safety precautions).
  • Visual Inspections: Checking for obvious signs of wear, loose bolts, damaged weather stripping, or frayed cables without touching dangerous components.
  • Cleaning: Washing door panels and removing debris from tracks to prevent obstruction.

Tasks Requiring Professional Intervention

  • Spring Replacement: Garage door springs are under extreme tension and can cause serious injury if handled improperly.
  • Opener Repairs: Motor, sensor, or electrical issues should be diagnosed with specialized equipment.
  • Track Alignment: Misaligned tracks can cause door malfunctions and require precise adjustment.
  • Safety Sensor Calibration: Precise sensor alignment is needed to comply with safety regulations and prevent accidents.

Hiring licensed technicians, like those at Garage Door Repair of Weston, ensures your repairs meet safety standards and extend the longevity of your garage door system.
