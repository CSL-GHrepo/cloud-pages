Maintaining Your Garage Door: Tips for Weston Residents

Preventative care is key to avoiding costly repairs and prolonging your garage door’s lifespan. Here are expert recommendations for residential garage door maintenance tailored to Weston’s environment:

  • Regular Lubrication: Use silicone spray on rollers, hinges, and springs every 6 months to prevent rust caused by Weston’s humid conditions.
  • Balance Testing: Disconnect your opener and manually lift the door midway. A balanced door should stay in place without falling or rising.
  • Check Weather Seals: Inspect and replace worn bottom seals to prevent pest entry and improve energy efficiency.
  • Inspect Cables and Springs: Look for signs of wear like fraying or corrosion; report issues to your garage door technician immediately.
  • Schedule Annual Professional Inspections: Hire Garage Door Repair of Weston for annual checkups to catch small problems before they become emergencies.
