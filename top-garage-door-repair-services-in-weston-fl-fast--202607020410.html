Tips for Extending the Life of Your Garage Door in Weston, FL

Good maintenance practices help avoid breakdowns and improve safety. Follow these actionable tips:

  • Schedule professional maintenance at least once per year.
  • Lubricate moving parts with manufacturer-recommended products every 6 months.
  • Keep sensors clear of dirt and debris to maintain auto-reverse functionality.
  • Monitor door balance by manually lifting halfway; if it drops, call a professional immediately.
  • Address odd noises, jerky movements, or slow operation promptly with expert diagnosis.

Taking these steps helps minimize emergency repairs and saves you money long-term.
