Common Garage Door Problems in Weston and Their Root Causes

Understanding the typical issues that afflict garage doors in Weston can help homeowners recognize problems early and seek appropriate repairs or maintenance.

Frequent Problem 1: Garage Door Springs Breaking

Garage door springs bear intense stress as they counterbalance the weight of the door. In Weston’s humid climate, springs may rust or experience metal fatigue more quickly, leading to breaks or snaps, which can cause the door to become inoperable or unsafe.

Symptoms of failing springs include noisy operation, difficulty opening or closing, and uneven door movement. Timely spring repair or replacement is critical to maintaining balanced door movement and preventing damage to openers.

Frequent Problem 2: Misaligned or Damaged Tracks

Garage door tracks are prone to bending or misalignment from impacts, such as vehicle collisions or debris accumulation caused by seasonal storms common in South Florida. Misaligned tracks cause the door to jam or produce grinding noises and hasten wear on rollers and springs.

Garage Door Repair of Weston offers precise track realignment and replacement services, using specialized tools to ensure the tracks maintain correct geometry for smooth door travel.

Frequent Problem 3: Faulty Sensors and Safety Mechanisms

Safety sensors prevent garage doors from closing on objects, pets, or people. These sensors can become misaligned, dirty, or fail electronically, resulting in unresponsive doors or safety hazards.

Regular inspection and adjustment of sensors are part of routine maintenance, and Garage Door Repair of Weston provides sensor diagnostics and replacements to keep your home and family safe.
