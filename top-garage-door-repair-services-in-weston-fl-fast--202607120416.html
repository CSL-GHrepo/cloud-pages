Signs Your Garage Door Needs Immediate Repair

Understanding the warning signs of a failing garage door can prevent emergencies and costly damage. Experts advise paying close attention to these common indicators:

Loud Noises During Operation

Squeaks, grinding, or banging sounds often signal worn rollers, loose hardware, or damaged springs that need immediate inspection before they cause a breakdown.

Slow or Uneven Movement

If your garage door moves slower than usual or appears crooked when opening or closing, this can indicate track misalignment, cable issues, or motor problems that should be addressed promptly.

Door Doesn’t Open or Close Fully

This is a classic sign of sensor malfunction or obstruction, which poses a safety risk, especially if the door closes unexpectedly or doesn't detect objects properly.

Visible Damage or Rust on Components

Since Weston’s humid and coastal environment accelerates corrosion, visual inspection of springs, cables, tracks, and panels is crucial. Rust compromises structural integrity and requires professional replacement or treatment.

Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for Weston Residents

Regular care adapted to seasonal changes can extend the life of your garage door and prevent unexpected failures. Here’s how to keep your garage door in top shape throughout the year:

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Check and clean weather seals to prevent moisture intrusion during rainy days.
  • Lubricate rollers, hinges, and springs monthly to reduce friction caused by heat and humidity.
  • Inspect for rust spots and schedule professional treatment if needed.
  • Test safety sensors frequently to ensure they function correctly amid lasting sunlight glare.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Clear leaves and debris from tracks that may obstruct door movement.
  • Tighten loose bolts and hardware loosened during temperature fluctuations.
  • Check the door balance to reduce strain on the opener as cooler temperatures stiffen parts.
  • Ensure insulation and weatherstripping are intact to maintain indoor warmth.
