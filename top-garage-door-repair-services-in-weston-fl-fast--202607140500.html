Garage Door Safety Inspections: What Weston Residents Should Know

Garage doors are heavy and potentially dangerous to operate if components fail. Annual or semi-annual safety inspections are a critical, often-overlooked aspect of garage door care.

Key Safety Components Inspected

  • Auto-Reverse Sensors: Detect obstructions and prevent the door from closing on people, pets, or objects. These are tested for alignment and operational reliability.
  • Spring Tension and Condition: Problems with torsion or extension springs can cause violent door movement and injuries if they snap unexpectedly.
  • Cables and Pulleys: Frayed or loose cables increase the risk of door malfunctions and accidents.
  • Manual Release Mechanism: Ensures the door can be safely disengaged from the opener for manual operation during power outages or emergencies.
The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) estimates over 20,000 injuries related to garage doors annually, many of which could be prevented through proper maintenance and safety inspections.
