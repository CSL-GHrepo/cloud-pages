Understanding Garage Door Safety Features and Their Importance

Garage doors are heavy and operate under tension, making safety an essential concern. Many accidents result from malfunctioning parts or improper maintenance. Knowing the safety features and ensuring they are functioning properly can prevent injury and property damage.

Auto-Reverse and Sensors

One of the most critical safety features on modern automatic garage doors is the auto-reverse mechanism. This feature detects obstructions in the door’s path and reverses the door to prevent crushing accidents.

  • Photoelectric Sensors: Installed near the floor on either side of the door, these sensors use infrared beams to detect objects or people crossing beneath the door.
  • Force Detection: The opener system senses resistance if the door hits an obstacle and immediately reverses direction.
  • Regular Testing: Manufacturers recommend monthly tests to ensure sensors are clean, aligned, and operational.

Garage Door Repair of Weston technicians provide thorough safety inspections and can retrofit older doors with updated sensor technologies to meet current safety standards.

Emergency Manual Release

Power outages or mechanical failures could trap vehicles or people inside the garage. An emergency manual release cord and mechanism allow you to disconnect the door from the automatic opener and operate it manually.

Technicians educate homeowners on locating and correctly using the manual release, and they ensure the mechanism is accessible and functioning properly during maintenance visits.
