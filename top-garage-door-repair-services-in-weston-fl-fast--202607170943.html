Common Garage Door Hazards and How to Prevent Them

  • Broken Torsion Springs: These springs are under extreme tension and can cause severe injuries if they snap unexpectedly. Never attempt DIY spring repairs—always contact certified technicians.
  • Faulty Auto-Reverse Mechanisms: If the door does not reverse on contact with an obstacle, it can cause crushing accidents. Regular sensor testing and alignment are essential.
  • Worn Out Cables and Pulleys: Damaged cables can snap suddenly or cause the door to become unbalanced, creating a risk of collapse or jamming.
  • Improper Manual Release Usage: Misuse of the emergency release can cause the door to fall freely. Proper education on operating this feature is crucial.
