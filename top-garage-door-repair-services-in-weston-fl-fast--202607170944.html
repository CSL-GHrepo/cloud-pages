FAQs About Garage Door Repair and Maintenance in Weston, FL

How often should I schedule garage door maintenance?

Experts recommend at least one comprehensive inspection and servicing per year. However, in Weston's humid climate, bi-annual checks may be beneficial to combat accelerated wear caused by moisture and salt air.

What are signs my garage door spring needs replacement?

Listen for loud bangs, difficulty opening, uneven door movement, or visible gaps or breakage in the spring. Never attempt to repair springs yourself, as they are under high tension and pose serious injury risks.

Can I repair a garage door myself to save costs?

Minor tasks like lubricating moving parts or cleaning tracks can be DIY, but most repairs—especially those involving springs, cables, or electronics—should be handled by trained professionals to ensure safety and proper functionality.

How long does a garage door typically last?

With regular maintenance, a quality garage door can last 15–30 years. Harsh weather and lack of upkeep may shorten this lifespan considerably.
