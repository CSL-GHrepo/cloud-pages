Safety Features and Upgrades to Consider for Your Weston Garage Door

Safety remains paramount when it comes to garage doors. Many accidents occur due to mechanical failures, outdated components, or improper use. Staying current with safety regulations and installing modern features can protect your family and property.

Auto-Reverse Sensors and Photo Eye Technology

All garage doors sold and installed today must include anti-entrapment devices, such as auto-reverse sensors and photo eyes. These safety features detect obstructions under the door during closing and reverse the door’s motion to prevent injuries or damage.

  • Photo eyes: Infrared beams that trigger the door to open if an object or person interrupts the signal.
  • Force sensors: Hardware that senses unusual resistance, stopping or reversing door movement.

Weston homeowners should ensure these safety devices are regularly tested and maintained. Garage Door Repair of Weston includes safety inspections as part of their maintenance plans and promptly replaces faulty sensors.

How to Detect Early Signs of Garage Door Problems

Early detection of issues can save Westonians from costly repairs and emergencies. Homeowners should watch for the following warning signs:

  • Unusual noises: Grinding, squeaking, or banging during operation
  • Slow or jerky movement: Door doesn’t move smoothly or hesitates
  • Door imbalance: Door slants or doesn’t stay in place when manually opened halfway
  • Visible wear: Rust, cracks, or frayed cables
  • Remote or keypad failure: Openers not responding consistently
