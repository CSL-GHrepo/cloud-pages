Preventative Maintenance Tips for Weston Homeowners

Preventative maintenance is the best way to extend the life of your garage door and minimize emergency repairs. Regular inspections can catch issues early before they become costly problems.

In Weston, FL, the humid environment can cause metal components to rust or corrode. Applying a protective lubricant to moving parts every few months helps reduce friction and wear.

Check the balance of your garage door monthly by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door halfway. If it doesn’t stay in place, a spring adjustment might be needed.

Ensure safety features like sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms are functioning properly. Test these regularly to prevent accidents and injuries.

Keep the tracks clean and free of debris. Use a damp cloth to wipe away dirt or dust, and lubricate the rollers and hinges to keep the door operating quietly.
