Sectional Garage Doors

These doors consist of multiple horizontal panels hinged together, allowing smooth vertical movement along tracks. Sectional doors are popular due to their space efficiency and versatility. Materials vary from steel and aluminum to wood and fiberglass, with options for insulation to improve energy efficiency.

  • Pros: Durable, customizable, and good thermal insulation options.
  • Cons: Requires regular track maintenance to avoid misalignment.

Roll-Up Garage Doors

Commonly used in commercial settings, roll-up doors consist of metal slats that roll back into a coil above the opening, saving overhead space. They are ideal for warehouses and storage facilities, providing robust security and easy operation.

  • Pros: Space-saving, strong against forced entry.
  • Cons: Can be noisy without proper lubrication and maintenance.

Carriage-Style Garage Doors

Mimicking historic carriage house doors, these swing-open doors add a charming aesthetic to many Weston homes. Modern versions have automated openers for convenience, combining style with functionality.

DIY Garage Door Repair: Risks and When to Call Professionals

While minor maintenance tasks like lubricating hinges and cleaning tracks are feasible for most homeowners, attempting major garage door repairs poses significant risks. Springs, cables, and openers are under high tension and can cause serious injuries if mishandled.

Common DIY Tasks Safe for Homeowners

  • Lubricating rollers, hinges, and tracks with appropriate garage door lubricants.
  • Cleaning dirt and debris from tracks to ensure smooth operation.
  • Inspecting weather seals and replacing them if visibly worn.
  • Testing the auto-reverse mechanism with simple obstruction tests.

When to Contact Garage Door Repair Professionals

  • Broken or worn springs that must be replaced under torsion tension.
  • Frayed or snapped cables requiring immediate replacement for door stability.
  • Malfunctioning openers with electrical or mechanical issues.
  • Misaligned tracks causing the door to bind or derail.
  • Damaged panels requiring structural repairs or replacement.

Hiring certified technicians not only ensures the repair is done correctly but also reduces liability and safety hazards. Professional repairs often come with guarantees, providing additional assurance.
