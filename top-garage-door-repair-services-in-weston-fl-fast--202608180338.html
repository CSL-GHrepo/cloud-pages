Preparing for Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance in Weston, FL

Seasonal changes in Weston call for proactive garage door adjustment and upkeep to maintain smooth operation and safety.

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Inspect and Lubricate Moving Parts: Warmer weather can cause components to expand and wear; lubrication reduces friction.
  • Check Weather Seals: Replace worn or cracked seals to prevent moisture intrusion during summer rains.
  • Test Safety Sensors: Clean and calibrate sensors to ensure reliable obstruction detection.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Tighten Hardware: Colder temperatures can cause contraction and loosening of bolts and screws.
  • Inspect Springs and Cables: Early detection of tension wear helps avoid winter breakdowns.
