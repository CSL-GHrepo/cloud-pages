Ensuring Garage Door Safety: Key Considerations for Weston Residents

Garage doors are large, heavy mechanical devices that can pose safety risks if improperly maintained or repaired. Weston homeowners and business operators must prioritize safety to prevent injury and property damage.

Essential Safety Features

  • Photo-Eye Sensors: Installed at the door's base, these sensors detect objects or people in the door’s path and automatically stop or reverse closing movement.
  • Auto-Reverse Mechanism: Ensures the door reverses direction if resistance is met while closing, a critical feature to prevent accidents.
  • Manual Release Handles: Allow the door to be disengaged and operated manually during power outages or emergencies.
  • Force Settings: Proper adjustment regulates how much force the door’s motor exerts to open or close the door, reducing risks of entrapment or damage.
