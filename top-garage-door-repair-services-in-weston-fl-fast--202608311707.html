Essential Garage Door Safety Tips

  • Keep Clear of Moving Parts: Always ensure children and pets remain away from the door while it is operating.
  • Regular Professional Inspections: Schedule expert inspections at least once a year to identify and address issues like worn cables, loose hardware, or unbalanced doors.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Features: Test the automatic reversal safety mechanism monthly to make sure the door retracts when it encounters an obstacle.

Preparing Your Garage Door for Seasonal Changes in Weston

Florida’s weather can be unpredictable, with high humidity, occasional storms, and intense heat affecting garage door longevity. Seasonal maintenance helps prepare doors for these conditions.

Pre-Summer Garage Door Checklist

  • Inspect and replace worn weather stripping to prevent heat and moisture ingress.
  • Lubricate springs, rollers, and tracks with weather-resistant grease to prevent rust and wear.
  • Test opener sensors and auto-reverse functions to ensure proper operation during storm season.
  • Secure all bolts and brackets tightened to avoid loosening caused by storm vibrations or wind.

Post-Storm Garage Door Care

  • Check for dents, cracks, or alignment issues caused by debris impact.
  • Dry out weather seals that may have absorbed moisture to prevent mold and deterioration.
  • Schedule professional inspections if the door was exposed to flooding or heavy winds.

Taking seasonal precautions can avoid costly repairs and maintain optimal door function year-round.
