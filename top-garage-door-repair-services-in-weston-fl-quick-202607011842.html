Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Weston Year-Round

To avoid sudden malfunctions, follow these expert maintenance tips:

  • Inspect springs, cables, and rollers every 3-6 months for signs of wear.
  • Lubricate moving parts with silicone-based lubricant during seasonal maintenance.
  • Test safety sensors monthly to ensure they stop the door if an object is detected.
  • Keep the tracks clean and remove debris to prevent jamming.
  • Schedule professional inspections annually to catch hidden problems.
