How to Maintain Your Garage Door in Weston for Longevity

Preventive maintenance can save time, money, and stress. Here are expert tips tailored to Weston’s climate and garage door models:

  1. Regular Lubrication: Use silicone-based lubricants on hinges, rollers, and springs every 6 months to prevent rust and friction.
  2. Visual Inspections: Check for dents, worn cables, and balance issues monthly; unbalanced doors strain openers and safety sensors.
  3. Test Safety Features: Ensure auto-reverse and photo sensors respond properly to prevent injury.
  4. Clean the Tracks: Dirt and debris accumulate faster in Florida’s humid environment, potentially inhibiting functionality.
  5. Schedule Professional Tune-Ups: Annual maintenance by trained technicians can catch problems before they cause breakdowns.

Adhering to these steps can extend the life of your garage door system and reduce the need for emergency garage door repair.
