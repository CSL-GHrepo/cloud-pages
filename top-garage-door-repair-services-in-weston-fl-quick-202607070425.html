Tips for Extending the Life of Your Garage Door

Prolonging the functionality and appearance of your garage door requires simple yet effective actions:

  • Regular lubrication: Apply proper lubricants to hinges, rollers, and springs to reduce friction and wear.
  • Clean tracks: Remove dirt, leaves, and debris periodically for smooth door operation.
  • Balance testing: Check balance by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door; uneven weight can signal spring or cable problems.
  • Sensor alignment: Ensure safety sensors are free from obstructions and properly positioned.
  • Weather seal upkeep: Replace damaged seals to keep moisture and pests out.
  • Professional maintenance visits: Schedule twice-yearly checks by certified technicians to identify and address issues promptly.

Following these tips helps Weston residents avoid emergency repairs, extend door life, and maintain safety.
