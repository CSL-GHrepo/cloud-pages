Industry Terms Explained for Weston Residents

Torsion springs: These are heavy-duty springs mounted above the garage door, balancing its weight for smooth operation. Proper tension is crucial for safety and efficiency.

Track alignment: The process of ensuring the steel tracks are straight and level, allowing the door to open and close safely without binding or derailing. Misaligned tracks cause noise and malfunction.

Remote programming: Setting or reprogramming the remote control or smart app to communicate with your garage door opener. It’s a simple step that improves convenience and security.

Cable replacement: Replacing the steel cables that lift and lower the door. Worn or frayed cables pose safety hazards and should be addressed promptly.

Spring tension adjustment: Fine-tuning the tension of garage door springs to ensure the door balances correctly. Proper tension prevents strain on the opener and extends component life.
