Essential Tips for Extending the Life of Your Garage Door

While Garage Door Repair of Weston offers expert repair and replacement, homeowners can adopt several proactive measures to help their garage doors last longer and operate smoothly.

1. Conduct Routine Visual Inspections

Regularly check for visible signs of wear, such as frayed cables, bent tracks, cracked panels, or rusted springs. Early detection allows for timely repair or preventive maintenance before costly failures occur.

2. Keep Tracks and Rollers Clean

Remove debris, dirt, and cobwebs from tracks and rollers to avoid obstruction and premature wear. Simple monthly cleaning during routine maintenance can prevent many common issues.

3. Test Auto-Reverse Function Monthly

Safety sensors and auto-reverse features reduce injury risks by stopping the door if an object is detected underneath. Testing these features regularly ensures they are functioning correctly and comply with safety standards.

4. Balance the Door

Periodically disconnect the opener and manually move the door halfway up. If it does not stay in place or is difficult to move, the door may be unbalanced, indicating worn springs or hardware needing professional attention.

5. Avoid DIY Repairs for Critical Components

Always consult professionals for inspecting or repairing springs, cables, or electrical components. These parts are under high tension and can cause injury if handled improperly.
