Common Garage Door Problems Beyond Springs and Openers

While springs and openers are frequent repair points, garage doors can suffer from a variety of other issues that homeowners should be aware of.

Damaged or Misaligned Tracks

Garage door tracks guide the door’s movement smoothly up and down. When tracks become bent, dented, or clogged with debris, the door may jerk, stick, or fail to open entirely. Track problems can also cause extra strain on springs and openers, accelerating wear.

  • Signs to watch for: Grinding noises, visible track warps, or a door that jumps off track during operation.
  • Professional care: Technicians can realign tracks, remove obstructions, and replace damaged sections to restore smooth movement.

Worn Rollers and Hinges

Rollers and hinges are critical to the door’s quiet and balanced function. Over time, rollers can become worn, corroded, or seized, while hinges may loosen or bend, resulting in noisy operation and uneven door movement.

  • Symptoms: Squeaking, uneven door movement, or difficulty opening the door manually.
  • Repair approach: Roller replacement with nylon or steel rollers, hinge tightening or replacement, and lubrication to reduce friction.

Broken Cables

Garage door cables work alongside springs to safely lift and lower the door. Broken or frayed cables are hazardous and can cause the door to fall unexpectedly.

  • Warning signs: Visible cable damage, uneven door hanging, or failure to remain open.
  • Safety-first repairs: Due to high tension, cable repairs require professional handling with proper tools and safety gear, as provided by Garage Door Repair of Weston.
