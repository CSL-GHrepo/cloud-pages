DIY Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Weston Homeowners

While professional maintenance is crucial, homeowners can take simple steps to keep their garage doors in good shape between service visits. Here are practical tips customized for the Weston environment:

  • Clean and lubricate tracks and moving parts: Use a silicone spray lubricant every 3-6 months to prevent rust and squeaking noises, especially important in humid conditions.
  • Inspect weather stripping: Replace cracked or worn seals to keep out moisture and pests, preserving garage interior condition.
  • Test auto-reverse mechanisms: Place a block beneath the door and close it slowly to ensure it reverses on contact, a key safety feature.
  • Keep the area around sensors clear: Remove cobwebs, dust, or debris that might disrupt the sensors’ signal and interfere with smooth door operation.
  • Tighten loose bolts and screws: Vibrations can loosen hardware, so check and tighten them periodically to avoid bigger problems.

Note: Always disconnect power sources and use caution during any maintenance activities. If unsure, contact a professional technician to avoid injury or further damage.
