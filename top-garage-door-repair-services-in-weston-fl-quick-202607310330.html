Hidden Dangers of Garage Door Springs and Cables

Garage door springs are under tremendous tension. Without specialized knowledge and tools, attempting repairs can result in serious injury or damage. Cables also bear heavy loads and must be handled precisely.

  • Improper spring handling can cause violent releases, snapping components dangerously.
  • Incorrect cable re-tensioning leads to door misalignment or sudden failure.
  • Faulty repairs may void manufacturer warranties or insurance coverage.

Professional technicians in Weston invest in ongoing training and maintain industry certifications to safely handle these high-tension components.

Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for Weston Homeowners

Weston’s subtropical climate necessitates particular attention to garage door care during seasonal changes. Regular adapted maintenance keeps doors functioning smoothly year-round.

Spring and Summer Care

  • Inspect and lubricate moving parts to counteract humidity-induced rust.
  • Clear debris around tracks to prevent blockage from heavy rains or windblown leaves.
  • Check weather seals and replace if compromised to keep pests and moisture out.
  • Test auto-reverse and safety sensors more frequently as storm season approaches.

Fall and Winter Care

  • Examine door balance and tension, as cooler temperatures can stiffen springs and cables.
  • Remove ice buildup on exterior door surfaces to avoid damage during operation.
  • Confirm backup battery functionality for openers in the event of power outages during storms.
  • Secure any loose hardware or panels susceptible to wind stress.

Professional tune-ups timed with these seasonal shifts optimize system longevity and reduce emergency repair risk in Weston homes.
