Tips for Extending the Lifespan of Your Garage Door in Weston, FL

Your garage door is an essential investment. Extending its lifespan protects your property and saves money on premature replacements. Beyond professional maintenance, simple routine care can make a big difference.

  • Clean the door surface regularly: Remove dirt, debris, and salt buildup that accelerate corrosion.
  • Inspect weather seals: Replace cracked or missing seals to prevent moisture infiltration and improve insulation.
  • Balance checks: Test the door’s balance to ensure the springs and opener aren’t overworked.
  • Secure hardware: Tighten loose bolts and hinges to reduce wear and rattling noise.

These small, easy actions combined with annual professional check-ups can help keep your garage door operating smoothly for years.
