Preparing Your Garage Door for Florida Seasons: Seasonal Maintenance Tips

Weston residents experience a humid subtropical climate with hot summers, mild winters, and a hurricane season that demands specific garage door care. Beyond routine maintenance, seasonal preparation is crucial.

Spring and Summer: Combatting Humidity and Storm Damage

  • Inspect seals and weather stripping for gaps caused by expansion and contraction.
  • Lubricate moving components with weather-resistant oils to prevent rust from humidity and salt air.
  • Clear debris from tracks and sensors, especially after storms or heavy winds.
  • Schedule professional inspections before hurricane season to ensure door integrity and emergency functionality.

Fall and Winter: Preventing Cold Weather Wear

  • Check for and repair any cracks or warps caused by temperature swings.
  • Test the auto-reverse safety feature and sensor alignment regularly, as colder weather can affect responsiveness.
  • Maintain opener batteries and electrical components to ensure reliable operation during any power outages.
