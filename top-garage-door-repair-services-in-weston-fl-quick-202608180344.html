Common Garage Door Problems and How Professionals Fix Them

Many garage door issues require specialized knowledge to diagnose and repair efficiently. The team at Garage Door Repair of Weston routinely addresses the following common problems with proven solutions:

1. Noisy Garage Doors

Causes can include worn rollers, loose hardware, or insufficient lubrication. Technicians perform thorough inspections, replace damaged rollers or hinges, and apply high-quality lubricants to restore quiet operation.

2. Door Doesn’t Open or Close Fully

This can result from misaligned tracks, faulty sensors, or broken springs. Professionals realign tracks using precise measurements and calibrate the safety sensors to ensure accurate door movement and secure closing.

3. Opener Remote Not Functioning

Electrical issues, weak batteries, or interference can disrupt remote signals. Garage Door Repair of Weston tests remotes, replaces batteries, reprograms devices, and checks wiring connections for a dependable fix.

4. Garage Door Reverses Before Closing

This safety feature activates if an obstruction or sensor malfunction is detected. Technicians clean sensor lenses, adjust sensor alignment, and evaluate force settings on the opener to remedy nuisance reversals.

5. Broken or Loose Garage Door Springs

Attempting spring replacement is dangerous due to extreme tension. Expert technicians from Garage Door Repair of Weston employ certified tools and safety precautions to safely replace springs, restoring balance and functionality.
