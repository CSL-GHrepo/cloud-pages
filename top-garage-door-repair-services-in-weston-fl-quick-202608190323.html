Tips on Maintaining Your Garage Door in Weston, FL

Owners in Weston can extend the life of their garage doors by following these key maintenance tips:

  • Schedule semi-annual inspections: Check springs, tracks, cables, and rollers for wear or damage.
  • Lubricate moving parts: Use appropriate lubricants on springs, rollers, and hinges to prevent corrosion, especially important in humid climates.
  • Test the balance: Disconnect the opener and manually raise and lower the door to ensure smooth operation.
  • Clean weather seals: Regularly clean and inspect to keep the garage insulated and protected from pests.
  • Monitor opener performance: Test sensors, remotes, and safety features monthly to prevent malfunction or accidents.
