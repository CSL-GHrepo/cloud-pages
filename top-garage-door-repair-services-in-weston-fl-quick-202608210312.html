Comprehensive Garage Door Safety Inspections: Protecting Your Family and Assets

Garage door systems contain high-tension springs and heavy panels that pose safety risks if not maintained or repaired properly. Beyond emergency fixes, routine safety inspections are a vital service offered by local specialists to prevent accidents before they occur.

What a Professional Safety Inspection Includes

  • Spring Balance Check: Testing the tension and condition of torsion or extension springs to prevent sudden failures.
  • Roller and Track Examination: Ensuring that rollers are smooth and tracks are aligned, reducing risk of derailment or jamming.
  • Sensor and Opener Safety Test: Verifying that auto-reverse and obstruction detection features are working to protect children, pets, and vehicles.
  • Hardware Tightening and Lubrication: Tightening loose bolts and lubricating moving parts to minimize wear and extend component life.
  • Visual Inspection of Door Panels: Checking for dents, cracks, and rust spots that could compromise structural integrity.

Weston residents benefit from scheduling regular safety inspections with Garage Door Repair of Weston, which helps identify problems that could lead to injury or costly repairs.
