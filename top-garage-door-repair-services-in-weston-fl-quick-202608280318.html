Common Garage Door Problems and Expert Solutions Explained

Understanding common garage door issues helps homeowners and businesses recognize when it’s time for professional help and what to expect from repairs. Garage Door Repair of Weston frequently addresses the following problems with proven solutions.

Garage Door Won't Open or Close

This is one of the most frustrating issues and can result from several causes, such as:

  • Dead remote batteries or malfunctioning remotes
  • Obstructed safety sensors or misalignment
  • Broken torsion springs or cables
  • Electrical issues with the opener motor or wiring

Technicians start with a systematic diagnosis to pinpoint the root cause and fix it, whether that means sensor realignment, spring replacement, or motor repair.

Loud or Jerky Operation

Excessive noise or jerky movements often indicate worn rollers, lack of lubrication, or track misalignment. Ignoring this can accelerate wear on other components and cause complete breakdowns.

Service includes thorough cleaning and lubrication of moving parts, roller replacement, and precise track adjustment to restore smooth, quiet operation.

Garage Door Slams Shut or Reverses

This behavior is usually a safety feature responding to sensor triggers or resistance. Causes may include:

  • Obstructions in the door path
  • Faulty or misaligned safety sensors
  • Incorrect force settings on the opener

Technicians carefully test sensor alignment, clear door pathways, and recalibrate force settings to ensure the door operates smoothly and reliably without posing a hazard.

Broken Springs and Cables

Torsion and extension springs counterbalance the heavy weight of the door for safe operation. Over time, springs fatigue and risk breaking unexpectedly, which is dangerous for DIY repairs.

"Garage door springs are under high tension and can cause serious injury. Professional replacement is essential to restore balance and prevent accidents."

Garage Door Repair of Weston technicians have specialized tools and training to safely remove and replace springs and cables, restoring proper operation and door weight balance.

Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Weston's Climate

The subtropical climate of Weston with hot summers, frequent rain, and occasional storms poses unique challenges to garage doors. Tailored seasonal maintenance safeguards your investment and ensures consistent performance year-round.

Spring and Summer: Moisture and Heat Preparations

  • Inspect and replace weather seals to prevent water intrusion during heavy rain or hurricanes.
  • Lubricate metal parts to resist rust and corrosion accelerated by humidity.
  • Check and tighten bolts and screws loosening from temperature expansion.
  • Evaluate balance and sensor function to avoid strain during high-frequency use.

Fall and Winter: Cooling and Storm Readiness

  • Test insulation integrity to reduce heat loss if your garage is conditioned or connected to living spaces.
  • Clear debris that can accumulate during fall and block drain paths or tracks.
  • Examine opener batteries and backup power sources—power outages can be common during storms.
  • Inspect door alignment for freeze-related shifting or warping, even though freezing is uncommon.
