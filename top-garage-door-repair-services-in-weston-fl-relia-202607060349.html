Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Weston, FL

To extend the life of your garage door and reduce the need for costly repairs, follow these simple maintenance tips recommended by Garage Door Repair of Weston’s experts:

  • Lubricate moving parts: Apply lubricant to hinges, rollers, and springs at least twice a year.
  • Check balance and alignment: Test the door’s balance by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door halfway. It should stay in place.
  • Inspect weather seals: Replace worn or cracked seals to protect against Weston’s humid climate.
  • Test safety features: Ensure auto-reverse and photo-eye sensors are working properly to avoid accidents.
  • Schedule annual professional maintenance: A licensed technician can spot potential issues and fix them before they escalate.
