Common Garage Door Problems: Troubleshooting Tips Before Calling a Professional

While some garage door issues require expert repair, a few common problems can be diagnosed or even temporarily resolved with simple troubleshooting. Being proactive can save time and prevent unnecessary service calls.

Garage Door Won’t Open or Close

  • Check the remote battery: Dead or weak batteries are a frequent cause of unresponsive remotes.
  • Inspect safety sensors: Ensure nothing is blocking or misaligning the infrared sensors near the door tracks. Sensors must “see” each other to operate.
  • Verify power source: Confirm that the garage door opener is plugged in and circuit breakers are not tripped.

Garage Door Makes Loud Noises

  • Lubricate moving parts: Hinges, rollers, and springs often produce noise when dry or rusty. Use a silicone-based lubricant approved for garage doors.
  • Tighten hardware: Loose bolts and screws can cause rattling or creaking sounds.
  • Check rollers for damage: Worn or bent rollers increase noise and can affect smoothness.

Door Moves Unevenly or Is Off-Track

  • Inspect tracks: Look for bent or clogged tracks and remove debris.
  • Check rollers and hinges: Damaged rollers or worn hinges can cause uneven operation.
  • Avoid DIY realignment: Off-track doors can be hazardous. If you see significant misalignment, it’s safest to call a professional immediately.

Garage Door Opener Remote Not Working Consistently

  • Reset or reprogram the remote: Sometimes a system reset fixes connectivity glitches.
  • Check for signal interference: Nearby electronic devices or Wi-Fi routers can disrupt signals.

These tips can often restore temporary function or clarify whether professional service is needed. Always prioritize safety and avoid DIY repairs involving springs or cables, as these parts store a high amount of tension and can cause serious injury.

Steel is the most common choice due to its strength and cost efficiency. Benefits include:

  • Excellent durability against weather impacts and daily usage
  • Low maintenance compared to wood
  • Available with insulation cores for energy efficiency
  • Wide variety of paint and finish options

Aluminum Garage Doors

Aluminum doors are lightweight, corrosion-resistant, and ideal for wider garage openings. Characteristics include:

  • Lower weight reduces stress on opener and springs
  • Minimal corrosion potential, perfect for humid coastal areas
  • Sleek, contemporary appearance often with glass panels
  • Less insulation compared to steel or wood

Wood Garage Doors

Wood doors offer timeless beauty and customization but require more upkeep. Considerations include:

  • Rich natural look that complements many architectural styles
  • Requires periodic staining or painting to protect from moisture
  • Heavier construction requiring stronger hardware
  • Prone to warping or termite damage if not properly maintained

Fiberglass Garage Doors

Fiberglass combines durability with low maintenance, mimicking wood grain without its vulnerability. It features:
