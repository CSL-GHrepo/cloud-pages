Maintenance Tips to Maximize Garage Door Longevity in Weston

While professional maintenance is essential, homeowners can take simple preventive measures to extend the service life of their garage doors.

Monthly Garage Door Checks

  • Visual inspection: Look for signs of wear such as rust, cracked panels, and loose bolts or screws.
  • Balance test: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway. If it does not stay in place, springs may need adjustment or replacement.
  • Safety sensor test: Ensure the sensors reverse the door when an object is detected in the path.

Seasonal Lubrication Routine

Using a high-quality silicone-based lubricant, homeowners should lubricate moving parts such as hinges, rollers, tracks, and springs at least twice a year. Avoid grease or oil-based lubricants that can attract dirt and cause gumming.

Clearing Drainage and Ensuring Proper Sealing

Weston’s rainy season can cause water accumulation near the garage threshold. Routinely clear debris from drainage paths and check the bottom weather seal to prevent water intrusion and damage.
