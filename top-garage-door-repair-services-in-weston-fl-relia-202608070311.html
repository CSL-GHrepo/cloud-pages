DIY Garage Door Troubleshooting Tips Before Calling a Professional

While professional service is critical for complex or dangerous repairs, many small issues can be diagnosed and sometimes resolved by homeowners, saving time and money.

Common Garage Door Problems You Can Check

  • Garage Door Won’t Open or Close: Check the batteries in remotes first. Ensure sensors are aligned and not obstructed.
  • Door Reverses Before Closing: Clean the photo-eye sensors with a soft cloth; debris or spider webs often cause sensor malfunction.
  • Unusual Noises: Inspect rollers and hinges for visible debris or rust and apply silicone lubricant as needed.
  • Door Stuck in Position: Look for visible track obstruction or misalignment, but avoid forcing the door.
  • Slow or Uneven Closing: Check for balance by disconnecting the opener and manually moving the door halfway. Uneven movement signals mechanical issues.
