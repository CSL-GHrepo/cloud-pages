Expert Tips for Garage Door Maintenance in Weston’s Climate

Regular maintenance can significantly extend a garage door’s lifespan and prevent inconvenient breakdowns. Considering Weston’s subtropical climate impact, here are expert tips recommended by Garage Door Repair of Weston to keep your door in prime condition:

  • Periodic Lubrication: Apply garage-specific lubricant to springs, rollers, hinges, tracks, and openers every 3 to 6 months to reduce wear and corrosion.
  • Inspect Weatherstripping: Check and replace weatherstripping along the door’s bottom and sides to prevent moisture, pests, and dust intrusion.
  • Clean and Tighten Hardware: Frequent exposure to humidity can loosen bolts and screws; tightening them regularly prevents structural stress.
  • Balance Testing: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway; if it falls or rises unevenly, schedule a professional adjustment.
  • Check Safety Sensors: Clean lens covers and test auto-reverse sensors monthly to ensure they stop the door when an object is detected to avoid accidents.
  • Spring and Cable Inspections: Springs and cables endure tremendous tension; any signs of rust, fraying, or wear warrant immediate professional service.

“Consistent upkeep tailored to Weston’s environmental factors not only saves money long term but also contributes to family safety,” explains Michael Reynolds, a senior technician with Garage Door Repair of Weston.
