Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Weston Homeowners

Preventative care is the cornerstone of avoiding costly repairs. Here are expert-recommended maintenance tips for garage doors in Weston, FL:

  • Inspect springs, cables, rollers, and pulleys every 6 months for wear or damage.
  • Lubricate moving parts with garage-specific lubricants to reduce friction and noise.
  • Test the auto-reverse safety feature monthly to protect children and pets.
  • Check door balance manually: doors off-balance strain springs and openers excessively.
  • Clean and clear tracks from debris to prevent jamming or misalignment.

Following these tips helps maintain smooth operation, reduces emergency calls, and enhances safety. Partnering with a local company like Garage Door Repair of Weston for scheduled maintenance ensures professional inspections and service.
