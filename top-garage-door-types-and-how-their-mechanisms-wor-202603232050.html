Maintaining Your Garage Door in Parkland

Regular maintenance is the best way to prevent emergency repairs and extend the life of your garage door. In Parkland, the humid climate can cause metal parts to rust and lubricants to degrade faster, making routine checks vital.

Here's what you should do periodically: inspect hardware for tightness, lubricate moving parts, clean tracks, and test safety features like sensors. If you notice anything unusual, call a professional immediately.

Checking alignment of photo eye sensors is also crucial. Misaligned sensors can cause doors to malfunction or refuse to close, creating safety hazards.
