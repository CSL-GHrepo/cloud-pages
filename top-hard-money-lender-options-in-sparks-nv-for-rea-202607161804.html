Glossary of Key Terms for Sparks Hard Money Loan Investors

  • Loan-to-Value (LTV): The ratio of the loan amount to the appraised value of the property, expressed as a percentage.
  • Hard Money Loan: A short-term loan secured by real estate, provided by private investors or companies rather than banks.
  • Bridge Loan: A temporary loan used to bridge the gap between purchasing a new property and selling an existing one.
  • Points: Upfront fees equal to a percentage of the loan amount, paid to the lender at closing.
  • Draw Schedule: The planned disbursement of funds during rehab projects based on completion milestones.
  • Exit Strategy: The method by which the borrower intends to repay the loan, such as property sale or refinancing.
  • Appraisal: An objective estimate of a property's value conducted by a licensed appraiser.
  • Interest-Only Payments: Loan payments that cover only the interest accrued each month, with principal repaid at loan maturity.
