Understanding Common Terms and Conditions in Cape Coral Hard Money Loans

Being familiar with standard loan terms ensures investors can negotiate better deals and avoid surprises. Key conditions include:

Loan-to-Value (LTV) Caps

Lenders typically cap loans at 65-75% of either the purchase price or the ARV — whichever is lower. Investors should calculate how much capital they need upfront and factor in closing costs to avoid funding gaps.

Interest-Only Payments vs. Principal and Interest

Many Cape Coral hard money loans allow interest-only payments during the rehab phase, reducing initial cash outlays. However, loans may convert to principal and interest payments once the rehab is complete, requiring investors to have cash flow plans.

Points and Origination Fees

Commonly, lenders charge 2-5 points (1 point = 1% of loan amount) upfront as origination fees. Understanding these fees’ impact on overall cost helps investors evaluate loan affordability.

Prepayment Penalties

Some lenders impose penalties for early loan payoff to protect their interest revenue. Negotiating low or no prepayment penalties provides flexibility to refinance or sell sooner without added costs.

Draw Schedules and Inspections

For rehab loans, funds are typically released in draws based on work completed. Lenders often require inspections or contractor affidavits before releasing each disbursement, so planning rehab phases accordingly can prevent funding delays.
