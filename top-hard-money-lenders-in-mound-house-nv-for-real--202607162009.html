Evaluating Loan Terms: What To Look For in a Mound House Hard Money Loan Agreement

Before signing any loan contract, it’s crucial to conduct thorough due diligence on the terms offered by hard money lenders serving Mound House. Consider the following factors carefully:

  • Interest Rates and Fees: Understand the total cost of borrowing, including upfront origination fees, monthly interest charges, and any prepayment penalties.
  • Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratios: Higher LTVs increase leverage but may come at a cost of stricter repayment terms or higher rates. Typical LTVs range from 60% to 75% of the property’s after-repair value.
  • Repayment Terms: Review duration, amortization schedule, balloon payments, and flexibility for early repayment or extensions.
  • Draw Schedules and Disbursements: For rehab or construction loans, determine how and when funds will be disbursed — often in stages tied to project milestones or inspections.
  • Default Clauses and Penalties: Understand consequences for late payments or breach of contract to avoid surprises during project challenges.
  • Lender Requirements: Some lenders may require escrows for taxes and insurance or proofs of contractor licensure and permits before loan approval.

It is recommended to consult with a real estate attorney or financial advisor experienced in hard money lending to review loan agreements before committing.
