10. Detailed Breakdown of Costs Associated With Hard Money Loans

Understanding the full cost picture of hard money financing empowers investors to accurately forecast returns and avoid unpleasant surprises. Below is a detailed explanation of the typical cost components:

  • Interest Rates: Commonly range from 8% to 15%, usually calculated monthly or annually on the outstanding loan balance. Rates tend to be higher than traditional mortgages but reflect the short-term nature and added risk of these loans.
  • Origination Fees: Typically 1-3% of the loan amount, these upfront fees cover lender administrative costs for reviewing and processing the loan application.
  • Points: Some lenders charge “points,” each equal to 1% of the loan amount, which can be paid upfront or rolled into the loan. This is especially common in lower credit score or higher-risk scenarios.
  • Closing Costs: Fees like title insurance, escrow fees, and recording fees may be required and can add an additional 1-3% to the total borrowing cost.
  • Prepayment Penalties: Certain loans may include penalties if you repay the loan early, a factor to consider when planning your exit strategy.

By carefully mapping out these expenses in your investment pro forma, you ensure realistic profit margin assessment and prevent cash flow issues during the project.
