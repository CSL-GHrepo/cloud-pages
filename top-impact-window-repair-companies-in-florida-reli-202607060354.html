Impact Window Replacement Florida: When to Repair vs. Replace

One common dilemma is deciding if a damaged impact window needs repair or full replacement. Skilled impact glass repair experts will assess the extent of the damage to inform your decision. Minor cracks, chips, and frame damage can often be repaired cost-effectively without compromising safety.

However, industry studies show that replacing windows older than 10 years or those with extensive frame damage could provide better long-term protection and energy efficiency. The Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety (IBHS) recommends timely impact window replacement to maintain storm preparedness.

  • Repair: Suitable for minor impact damage such as small chips, cracks, or frame dents.
  • Replacement: Advisable for shattered panes, compromised frames, or severely aged installations.

A licensed contractor will guide you through this process, typically offering both repair and replacement services to fit your budget and timeline.
