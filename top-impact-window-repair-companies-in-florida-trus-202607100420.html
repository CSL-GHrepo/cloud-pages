Many leading repair companies now work with advanced laminated glass products that go beyond standard impact resistance. These include:

  • Smart Tinting Glass: Glass that can dynamically change tint levels to reduce glare and heat gain during peak sun hours, lowering cooling costs.

For Florida homeowners, adopting these advanced glass options during repair or replacement means long-term savings and higher durability, especially in coastal or high-wind areas.

Maintenance Tips for Extending the Life of Your Impact Windows

Regular maintenance is crucial to preserving the integrity and performance of your impact windows. Here are practical tips recommended by Florida’s top repair experts:

  • Frequent Inspection: Check windows quarterly for cracks, seal failures, or hardware issues, especially after storms.
  • Clean with Care: Use mild detergent and soft cloths to avoid damaging glass coatings or frame finishes, especially in salty coastal air.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply lubricant to hinges, locks, and rollers to keep windows operating smoothly.
  • Replace Worn Seals Promptly: Damaged seals allow moisture intrusion and reduce energy efficiency, undermining impact resistance.
  • Professional Tune-Up: Schedule periodic professional inspections and minor repairs to address issues before they escalate.
