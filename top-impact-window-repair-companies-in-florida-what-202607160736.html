Impact Window Repair and Replacement: Key Differences

Understanding when to repair versus when to replace your impact windows is crucial for both safety and budget management. Repairing is ideal for issues like:

  • Seal failure or fogging between glass panes
  • Minor cracks or chips that do not compromise structural integrity
  • Broken locks or hardware on impact window frames

However, impact window replacement in Florida is necessary when:

  • The glass or frame has sustained significant damage from a hurricane or accidental impact
  • Windows no longer comply with updated Florida building codes
  • Energy efficiency is compromised due to worn or outdated materials

Industry professionals recommend periodic inspections to determine the best course of action. “A timely repair can extend the life of impact windows, but complete replacements are sometimes the most cost-effective in areas with repeated storm exposure,” says Michael Torres, a certified technician with over 15 years in Florida window repair.

Understanding Florida's Building Code Requirements for Impact Windows

Compliance with Florida’s building codes is mandatory for all impact window repairs and installations. These regulations are designed to protect occupants and property by requiring windows to withstand severe weather event forces.

Key Code Provisions Homeowners Should Know

  • Miami-Dade County Large Missile Test: Windows must resist impact from a 9-pound 2x4 lumber propelled at approximately 50 feet per second.
  • Wind Load Ratings: Windows must maintain structural integrity under very high wind speeds, often up to 150 mph in many coastal areas.
  • Installation Requirements: Strict guidelines on fastener types, frame anchoring, and flashing to prevent water intrusion.
  • Energy Efficiency: Compliance with the Florida Energy Code (FEC) necessitates windows to meet minimum U-factors and Solar Heat Gain Coefficients (SHGC).

Failing to meet these codes can result in failed inspections, null insurance claims, and costly corrections after installation. Thus, working with knowledgeable and certified companies is crucial.

How to Prepare Your Impact Windows Before Hurricane Season

Proactive preparation can make a significant difference when hurricane season approaches. Here are practical steps every homeowner should take:

  • Schedule a Pre-Season Inspection: Certified technicians can verify window integrity and repair minor issues before storms hit.
  • Clear Debris and Vegetation: Trim trees and remove loose objects near windows that could become hazardous projectiles.
  • Check Weather Stripping and Seals: Ensure all caulking and seals are intact to prevent water intrusion.
  • Test Window Locks and Hardware: Confirm all moving components work smoothly to maximize strength during high winds.
  • Stock Up on Emergency Supplies: Keep tarps and plastic sheeting on hand to cover windows temporarily if damage occurs.

Preparing now adds an additional layer of protection to your home and can reduce the stress of last-minute repairs when storms approach.
