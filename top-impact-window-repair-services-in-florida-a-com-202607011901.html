Signs You Need Impact Window Repair or Replacement

Identifying when to call on impact glass repair specialists can save thousands in costly repairs later. Some common indicators include:

  • Visible chips, cracks, or spider webbing in the laminated glass that compromise durability
  • Water leakage or condensation between glass layers suggesting seal failure
  • Difficulty opening or closing windows due to frame warping or hardware damage
  • Noise infiltration increasing — an indicator the seal integrity has declined
  • Physical damage to window frames after storms, such as dents or warping

Ignoring these signs means risking structural damage and endangering your family during hurricanes or tropical storms. Those in Florida should get routine residential impact window maintenance inspections at least annually, ideally before hurricane season.
