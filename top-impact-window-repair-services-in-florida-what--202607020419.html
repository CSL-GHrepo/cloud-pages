Impact Window Maintenance Tips to Extend Lifespan

Proper maintenance can significantly extend the durability and performance of impact windows. Florida homeowners should adhere to the following tips recommended by industry experts:

  • Regular Cleaning: Clean glass and frames monthly with mild soaps; avoid abrasive chemicals that can degrade seals.
  • Inspect for Damage: After storms, inspect windows for cracks, seal failures, or frame warping immediately.
  • Check Weatherstripping: Replace worn weatherstripping to maintain impact-resistant performance and energy efficiency.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Hinges and locks should be lubricated annually to ensure smooth operation.
  • Schedule Professional Inspections: Annual professional checkups help detect subtle issues and perform preventative repairs.

Failing to maintain impact windows may void manufacturer warranties and reduce their effectiveness against storms.

When to Opt for Impact-Resistant Window Replacement

Not every window issue requires a repair. In some cases, full replacement is the safer and more cost-effective option—especially after severe hurricane or storm damage when foundational elements like frame integrity are compromised.

Signs that point to replacement instead of repair include:

  • Shattered or extensively cracked laminated glass
  • Frame deformation or separation from the wall structure
  • Repeated seal failure causing persistent fogging or moisture buildup
  • Outdated windows not meeting current Florida building codes or energy-efficiency standards
