Maintaining Florida Impact-Resistant Windows for Longevity and Performance

Regular maintenance plays a vital role in maximizing your windows’ durability and effectiveness. Industry studies show that “homes that perform regular impact window maintenance in Florida reduce the likelihood of costly storm damage repair by up to 40%.” (Source: Florida Building Commission Report, 2022).

Simple but essential steps include:

  • Periodic glass and frame inspection for cracks, chips, or seal damage.
  • Cleaning glass with manufacturer-approved cleaners to avoid scratches that can weaken impact resistance.
  • Ensuring weather stripping and seals remain intact to prevent air and water intrusion.
  • Scheduling professional inspections before hurricane season.

Proactive maintenance not only preserves your windows’ protective abilities but also can significantly reduce insurance premiums, making it an investment in your home’s overall financial health.
