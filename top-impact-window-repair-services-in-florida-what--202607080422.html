Types of Impact Window Damage and When to Repair

Impact windows are sturdy, but never invincible. Understanding common damage types will help you identify when to seek urgent repair or replacement.

Common Damage Types

  • Cracks or Chips on Impact Glass: Resulting from debris strikes or pressure stress.
  • Seal Failure and Fogging: Moisture infiltration between glass layers often indicates seal failure requiring glass replacement rather than repair.
  • Frame Distortion or Warping: Frames may bend or crack after extreme wind or impact, affecting window performance.
  • Glass Breakage: Complete shattering requires full pane replacement.
  • Hardware Malfunction: Locks, hinges, and sash components may become damaged and need professional servicing.

Florida’s high winds and exposure to UV rays can accelerate deterioration, emphasizing the importance of regular residential impact window maintenance and timely repairs.
