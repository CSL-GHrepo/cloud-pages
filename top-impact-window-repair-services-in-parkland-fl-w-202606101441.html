Impact Window Maintenance Tips for Parkland Homeowners

Even the best impact windows require regular care to maintain optimal performance. Here are actionable maintenance steps for residential impact window upkeep:

  • Inspect seals and weather stripping biannually to prevent water leaks.
  • Clean glass and frames with non-abrasive cleaners to avoid scratching or weakening the glass layer.
  • Check for chips, cracks, or discolorations that could indicate underlying damage needing professional repair.
  • Schedule an annual professional assessment, especially before hurricane season, to identify potential vulnerabilities.
  • Immediately address any hardware or locking mechanism issues for security assurances.
