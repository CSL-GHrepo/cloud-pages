Qualification and Underwriting

  • Hard Money Loans: Emphasis placed on the asset’s value rather than borrower credit scores or income history—particularly valuable for investors with non-traditional income streams or past credit challenges.

Use Case Suitability

  • Hard Money Loans: Best for fix-and-flip projects, rehab funding, bridge loans, or land purchases where speed and flexibility are paramount.
  • Conventional Loans: More appropriate for buy-and-hold investors planning longer-term holdings or owner-occupied properties.
