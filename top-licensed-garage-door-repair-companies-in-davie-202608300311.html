Common Garage Door Problems and How Licensed Professionals Address Them

Garage doors are complex mechanical systems subject to wear and tear over time. Understanding common issues can help you recognize when to call a licensed garage door repair company in Davie, FL.

Broken or Worn Springs

Garage door springs handle the weight and counterbalance the door for smooth operation. When springs wear out or break, doors become heavy and difficult or impossible to lift manually or with an opener.

  • Signs: Loud bangs, door stuck halfway, or slamming shut unexpectedly.
  • Licensed repair approach: Professionals replace springs with ones rated precisely for the door’s weight and usage patterns, ensuring safety and longevity.

Misaligned or Damaged Tracks

If garage door tracks are bent or misaligned, the door may bind, make grinding noises, or suddenly derail.

  • Signs: Door moves unevenly, jerks, or pops out of track.
  • Licensed repair approach: Technicians inspect track alignment with precise tools and make needed adjustments or replacements using manufacturer-grade parts.

Faulty Garage Door Opener

Mechanical or electrical failures in the opener disrupt automation. Older units may experience worn gears, failing remotes, or motor burnout.

  • Signs: Door not responding to remote, slow operation, or erratic behavior.
  • Licensed repair approach: Professionals diagnose electrical components and offer repairs or replace openers with energy-efficient modern models compliant with safety standards.

Sensor and Safety Mechanism Failures

Modern garage doors have safety sensors to prevent closing on objects or people.

  • Signs: Door reverses unexpectedly, doesn’t close fully, or safety light blinks.
  • Licensed repair approach: Experts align sensors correctly, replace faulty wiring or units, and test safety interlocks to comply with UL325 safety standards.
“Attempting to repair springs or sensors yourself can be dangerous. Licensed technicians bring the tools and training necessary to avoid injury and ensure compliance with safety regulations.” – Davie Garage Safety Council
