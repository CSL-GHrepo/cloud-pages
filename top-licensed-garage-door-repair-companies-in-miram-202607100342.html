Safety Tips Every Miramar Homeowner Should Know About Garage Doors

Beyond hiring licensed professionals, homeowners can take proactive steps to maintain garage door safety between service visits:

  • Regularly Check Safety Sensors: Ensure that photo-eye sensors near the ground are clean and aligned to prevent accidental closures.
  • Avoid DIY Spring Repairs: Garage door springs store massive tension and repairing them requires professional training to prevent injuries.
  • Keep Remote Controls Secure: Protect your garage door opener from unauthorized use by controlling access to remotes and keypad codes.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Mechanism: Periodically place an object in the door’s path during closing to verify it automatically reverses upon contact.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply manufacturer-approved lubricants to hinges, rollers, and springs every six months to reduce wear.
“Garage doors are the largest moving mechanical object in a home — treating them with regular care and caution ensures safety for families and pets.” — Consumer Product Safety Commission
