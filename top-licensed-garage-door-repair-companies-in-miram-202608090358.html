Common Garage Door Problems: What Licensed Technicians Diagnose and Fix

Experienced and licensed garage door repair companies in Miramar are equipped to address a wide range of problems swiftly and correctly. Here are some frequent issues and how professionals manage them:

Broken Torsion Springs

Torsion springs bear much of the garage door’s weight, controlling the lift mechanism. When they break, the door becomes extremely heavy and unsafe to operate manually.

"Attempting to replace torsion springs without proper tools and training is highly dangerous — licensed technicians have the knowledge and equipment to handle these repairs safely," warns Hector Rivera.

Licensed repair services use calibrated machines to unwind and install new springs, ensuring correct tension and balance for smooth operation.

Misaligned or Damaged Tracks

Tracks guide the movement of the garage door and must be perfectly aligned to prevent jerking or “off-track” conditions that could damage the door or opener motor.

  • Patching bent sections of track
  • Removing obstructions or debris
  • Adjusting or realigning components to manufacturer specifications

Professionals also inspect mounting brackets and hardware for wear and replace them as needed.

Faulty Garage Door Openers

Opener failures are common and may stem from electrical issues, worn gears, sensor malfunctions, or remote control problems.

Licensed technicians perform comprehensive diagnostics to isolate the cause and recommend repair or replacement options aligned with budget and usage needs. They ensure that safety features such as photoelectric sensors and auto-reverse mechanisms function correctly to prevent injuries or property damage.
