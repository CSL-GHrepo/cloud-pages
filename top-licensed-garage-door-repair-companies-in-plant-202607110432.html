Maintenance Tips from Plantation Experts to Extend Garage Door Lifespan

Regular maintenance can drastically reduce the frequency and cost of major repairs. Local licensed professionals often recommend the following routine practices, especially vital in Plantation’s humid and stormy climate:

  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply garage door lubricant or silicone spray to rollers, hinges, springs, and tracks every 6 months to prevent rust and squeaking.
  • Inspect Weather Seals: Check bottom seals and door perimeter weatherstripping for cracks or damage to prevent water intrusion and pest entry.
  • Test Balance and Alignment: Imbalanced doors strain the opener and pose safety risks. Professional adjustments ensure smooth, safe operation.
  • Clean Tracks and Remove Debris: Clear leaves, dirt, and debris regularly to avoid jamming and component wear.
  • Schedule Annual Professional Tune-Ups: Certified technicians can identify early signs of wear or damage invisible to the untrained eye.

Following these maintenance tips reduces emergency repair needs and prolongs the effective lifespan of your garage door system.
