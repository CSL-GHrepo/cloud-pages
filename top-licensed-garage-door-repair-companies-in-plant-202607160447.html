Common Garage Door Problems and Their Expert Solutions

Understanding frequent garage door issues helps Plantation homeowners recognize when professional repair is necessary. Here are some common problems with explanations of how licensed technicians address them:

Broken or Worn Springs

The torsion and extension springs on a garage door counterbalance its weight. Springs endure significant tension and eventually break from wear.

  • Signs: Loud sudden noises during operation, door falls rapidly when closing or opening, visible gaps in springs.
  • Expert Solution: Licensed technicians replace springs using specialized tools, ensuring correct sizing and tension to restore safe door balance.

Malfunctioning Garage Door Openers

Electric openers can fail due to motor problems, wiring faults, or sensor misalignment.

  • Signs: Door does not respond to button press or remote, opener runs but door does not move, intermittent operation.
  • Expert Solution: Professionals perform electrical diagnostics, realign photo-eye sensors, replace capacitors or circuit boards, and program remotes for reliable performance.

Off-Track or Misaligned Doors

Garage doors operate on rails; damage or obstructions cause the door to derail.

  • Signs: Door becomes stuck halfway, uneven movement, rails visibly bent or damaged.
  • Expert Solution: Technicians straighten or replace tracks, lubricate moving parts, and inspect rollers to ensure smooth, safe operation.

Excessive Noise During Operation

Grinding, squeaking, or banging noises can indicate loose hardware or worn components.

  • Signs: Noises every time the door opens or closes, vibrations felt near hinges or rollers.
  • Expert Solution: Service includes tightening bolts and screws, replacing worn rollers or hinges, and lubrication of all moving parts.

Remote Control or Keypad Issues

Problems with opening controls can stem from electrical faults or battery failure.

  • Signs: Remote fails to activate door, keypad does not respond, intermittent functionality.
  • Expert Solution: Reprogramming accessories, replacing batteries, repairing or replacing damaged wiring or buttons.
