Understanding Garage Door Safety Features and Compliance in Plantation

Garage doors are heavy, mechanically complex equipment that pose serious safety concerns if improperly maintained or installed. In Plantation, licensed companies rigorously adhere to local safety codes and recommend critical safety features to protect residents and property.

Key Safety Features to Look For

  • Auto-Reverse Mechanisms: Sensors that detect obstacles in the door’s path and immediately stop or reverse closing for child and pet safety.
  • Photo-Eye Sensors: Infrared beams at the base of the door that trigger openings if tripped.
  • Manual Emergency Release: Allows the door to be disengaged from mechanical openers during power failures or emergencies.
  • Pinch-Resistant Panels: Door panel edges designed to prevent finger injuries during operation.
  • Spring Containment Systems: Enclosures or safety cables that prevent runaway springs from causing damage or injury in case of failure.
