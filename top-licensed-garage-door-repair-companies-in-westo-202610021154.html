Energy Efficiency Considerations for Garage Doors in Weston

Common insulation options include:

  • Polystyrene Panels: Lightweight foam boards that fit inside door panels, providing basic insulation and sound dampening

Sealing and Weatherstripping

In addition to panel insulation, proper sealing around the garage door frame is critical. Weatherstripping and threshold seals prevent drafts, water intrusion during heavy rain, and pest entry. Technicians check and replace these components during maintenance or repairs to ensure a tight fit.
