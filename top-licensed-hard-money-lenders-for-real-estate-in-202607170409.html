Understanding Different Types of Hard Money Loans Available in Cutler Bay

Licensed hard money lenders in Cutler Bay typically offer several loan types tailored to distinct investment scenarios. Familiarity with these options helps investors select the best match for their needs.

  • Fix and Flip Loans: Designed for investors purchasing properties in need of renovation. These loans often provide upfront capital plus renovation funds via draw schedules and typically carry terms from 6 to 18 months.
  • Bridge Loans: Short-term loans used to bridge timing gaps between property sales or financing events. These enable investors to enter deals quickly while waiting for long-term financing or liquidation.

Each loan type carries unique underwriting requirements and payoff expectations, so clear communication with your lender is key to aligning terms with your project timeline and exit strategy.

Typical Costs and Fees Associated with Hard Money Loans

Beyond interest rates, borrowers should be aware of other fees that may apply when working with hard money lenders in Cutler Bay. Understanding the full cost structure prevents surprises and aids budgeting.

  • Origination Fees: Usually ranging from 1% to 5% of the loan amount, these fees cover lender administrative and underwriting expenses.

  • Points: Sometimes lenders charge points upfront in exchange for lowered interest rates or as part of the funding process.
  • Inspection and Draw Fees: For renovation loans, lenders may charge fees for site inspections related to disbursing renovation funds incrementally.
  • Prepayment Penalties: Although many Florida hard money lenders do not impose these, it’s essential to confirm if early payoff fees are applicable.
