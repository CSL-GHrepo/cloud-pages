Impact Window Maintenance Tips for Florida Homeowners

Maintaining impact windows is essential to protect your investment and ensure they're ready when the next storm arrives. Here are key tips from certified window contractors:

  • Regular Cleaning: Use mild soap and water to clean the glass and frames—avoid abrasive materials that might degrade impact-resistant coatings.
  • Inspect Seals and Frames: Look for cracks, chips, or warping that compromise the window’s protective qualities.
  • Lubricate Locks and Hinges: Ensure all moving parts operate smoothly for quick emergency boarding or window locking during storms.
  • Trim Nearby Trees: Avoid branches that could break and cause window damage during high winds.
  • Professional Annual Inspection: Have licensed impact window contractors inspect glazing and structural integrity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I verify if an impact window repair contractor is licensed in Florida?

To verify licensing, visit the Florida Department of Business & Professional Regulation website and enter the contractor’s name or license number. Licensed impact window contractors will hold a Certified General or Building Contractor license with specific endorsements for window installation and repair. Always ask contractors for their license details and proof of certification before beginning any work.
