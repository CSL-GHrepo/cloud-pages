Preventing Mold Growth in Your Miami Home

Beyond remediation, ongoing mold prevention in Miami requires vigilance. Here are actionable tips recommended by experts:

  • Maintain indoor humidity between 30-50% using dehumidifiers.
  • Fix leaks promptly, especially in bathrooms, kitchens, and basements.
  • Keep gutters and drainage systems clean to prevent water buildup near the foundation.
  • Ensure proper ventilation in attics, crawl spaces, and laundry areas.
  • Use mold-resistant building materials in renovations and new construction.

Integrating these practices with annual professional mold inspections can significantly reduce the risk of mold recurrence in Miami homes and businesses.
