How to Prepare for Mold Remediation in Your West Palm Beach Home

Before remediation begins:

  1. Document Mold Presence: Take photos and note visible mold areas or musty odors to share with the mold removal company.
  2. Address Moisture Sources: Identify leaks or water intrusion problems alongside experts to prevent mold from returning.
  3. Ventilate the Area: If safe, open windows to reduce humidity and improve air circulation before professionals arrive.
  4. Plan Temporary Relocation if Needed: Some remediation activities require vacating the property for health and safety reasons.

Following these steps ensures remediation proceeds smoothly and effectively.
