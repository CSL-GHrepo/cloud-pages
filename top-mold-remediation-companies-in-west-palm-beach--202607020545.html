Can mold remediation companies assist with water damage restoration West Palm Beach?

How do I verify if a mold remediation company has certified mold experts FL?

Verify certifications by requesting proof of credentials such as IICRC (Institute of Inspection Cleaning and Restoration Certification) for mold remediation or AMRT (Applied Microbial Remediation Technician). Reputable companies will gladly provide this information along with references and evidence of insurance coverage.
