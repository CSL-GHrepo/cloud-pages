Practical Mold Prevention Tips for West Palm Beach Homeowners

Preventing mold growth is just as important as remediation. Here are some actionable mold prevention tips FL residents can apply:

  1. Maintain indoor humidity below 60% using dehumidifiers and air conditioning.
  2. Fix leaks and water intrusion immediately after storms or plumbing failures.
  3. Increase ventilation in bathrooms, kitchens, and crawl spaces.
  4. Use mold-resistant building materials during renovations or repairs.
  5. Regularly inspect with professional mold inspection services especially after hurricanes.
  6. Keep gutters clean and ensure proper drainage away from your home.

Following these tips not only reduces the risk of harmful mold but can also decrease the need for expensive professional remediation later on.
