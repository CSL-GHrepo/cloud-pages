How to Maintain Your Pool Between Professional Visits

Even with expert help, some day-to-day pool care is essential. Here are actionable steps Hallandale Beach homeowners can take:

  1. Skim debris daily: Use a pool net to remove leaves, bugs, and dirt particles from the surface.
  2. Check water chemistry weekly: Maintain proper pH (7.4-7.6), chlorine (1-3 ppm), and alkalinity levels to avoid algae and bacteria growth.
  3. Run the pump for 8-12 hours daily: Proper circulation prevents stagnation and chemical imbalances.
  4. Inspect filters and clean monthly: Dirty filters reduce flow and sanitation efficiency, necessitating professional-scale pool filter cleaning FL services periodically.
  5. Schedule seasonal service: Before summer and after winterization, professional inspection can prevent major problems.

Combining your own efforts with professional pool cleaning Hallandale ensures consistent water quality and equipment longevity.
