Tips for Maintaining Your Pool Between Professional Visits

While professional service is essential, homeowners can extend their pool’s health by:

  1. Testing water chemistry weekly using kits or digital testers
  2. Skimming leaves and debris daily, especially during fall
  3. Running filtration systems for 8-12 hours per day to ensure circulation
  4. Checking pool equipment for any unusual noises or leaks
  5. Shocking the pool according to manufacturer recommendations or after heavy use
