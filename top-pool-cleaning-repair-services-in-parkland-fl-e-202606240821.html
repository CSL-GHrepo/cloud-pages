Expert Tips for Pool Maintenance in Parkland, FL

Maintaining your pool between professional visits is vital. Here are some weekly pool cleaning tips recommended by expert pool technicians:

  1. Test Pool Water Chemistry: Use a reliable test kit to check pH, alkalinity, and chlorine levels every 3-4 days.
  2. Skim and Brush: Remove leaves and debris daily; brush tiles and walls weekly to prevent algae buildup.
  3. Maintain Pool Equipment: Clean pump baskets weekly and check for leaks or unusual noises.
  4. Keep Deck Area Clean: Reduces debris falling into the water and minimizes pest attraction.

Following these tips reduces the need for costly swimming pool repair services and extends the life of your pool.
