What does typical pool maintenance in Sunrise FL include?

Typical pool maintenance in Sunrise FL involves regular cleaning (skimming, brushing, vacuuming), water testing and balancing, filter cleaning or replacement, and routine inspections of pumps and heaters. Maintenance schedules generally happen every 1-2 weeks depending on pool use and weather conditions to prevent algae buildup that thrives in the local humid climate.

Signs you may need pool repair services in Sunrise include frequent cloudy water despite regular cleaning, unusual noises from pumps or filters, leaks, uneven water levels, or equipment failing to operate.
