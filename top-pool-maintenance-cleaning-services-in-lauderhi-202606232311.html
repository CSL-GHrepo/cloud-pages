Top Pool Maintenance Tips for Lauderhill Pool Owners

Beyond professional service, there are effective pool maintenance tips Lauderhill residents can use to keep their pools healthy between professional visits:

  1. Test Water Weekly: Use reliable test strips or kits to monitor pH and chlorine levels. Recommended pH is 7.2-7.6, with chlorine levels between 1-3 ppm.
  2. Clean Pool Filters: Backwash or rinse sand and DE filters regularly, while replacing cartridge filters as needed to maintain optimal flow.
  3. Skim Daily: Remove leaves and debris from the surface to prevent clogging and algae growth.
  4. Maintain Proper Water Level: Ensure the water level remains halfway up the skimmer opening for best circulation.
  5. Schedule Regular Inspections: Catch cracks, leaks, or malfunctioning equipment early to avoid expensive repairs.
