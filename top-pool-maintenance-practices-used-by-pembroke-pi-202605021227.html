2. Precise Pool Chemical Balancing FL

Swimming pool water treatment requires accurate chemical management to ensure water is both safe and clear. Typical practices in Pembroke Pines include:

  • Maintaining pH levels between 7.2 and 7.8 to protect pool surfaces and swimmer comfort
  • Monitoring chlorine levels to keep sanitizer concentrations optimum for killing bacteria and algae
  • Regular testing for alkalinity, calcium hardness, and stabilizer to prevent corrosion or scaling
