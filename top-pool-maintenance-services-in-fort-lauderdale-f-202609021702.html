Tips for Pool Owners: DIY Maintenance Between Professional Visits

Even with routine professional care, pool owners can take practical steps to maintain water quality and equipment health between visits:

  • Regular Skimming: Remove leaves, insects, and surface debris daily to reduce strain on filters.
  • Monitor Water Levels: Maintain proper water level to ensure effective pump operation and prevent air intake.
  • Quick Spot Cleaning: Brush pool walls and steps weekly to prevent algae patches.
  • Test Chemical Levels: Use home test kits 2-3 times per week to watch pH and chlorine levels, adjusting if possible.
  • Check Equipment Visually: Inspect pumps, heaters, and filters for obvious leaks, noise, or wear.
  • Use a Pool Cover: Minimize debris and evaporation, protecting chemicals from rapidly dissipating in the sun.

While these tasks help keep pools in good shape, they should not replace professional chemical balancing, equipment maintenance, or deep cleaning services that require specialized tools and expertise.
