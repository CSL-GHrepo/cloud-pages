9. Green Pool Cleanup: Restoring Your Pool to Sparkling Condition

Dealing with a green, algae-infested pool is one of the most challenging scenarios Fort Lauderdale pool owners may face, particularly after heavy rains or prolonged neglect. Green pool cleanup requires specialized knowledge and aggressive treatment approaches to restore water clarity and safety efficiently.

Common Causes of Green Pools in Fort Lauderdale

  • Excessive rainwater introducing nutrients that feed algae growth.
  • Inconsistent chemical treatment or sanitizer depletion due to evaporation and sun exposure.
  • Poor filtration or circulation impaired by debris or equipment malfunction.
  • Warm temperatures and high humidity creating an ideal environment for algae proliferation.

Steps in Professional Green Pool Cleanup Services

  • Shock treatment: High doses of chlorine or non-chlorine oxidizers are used to kill algae cells quickly.
  • Algaecide application: Specialized chemicals target resistant algae forms to prevent regrowth.
  • Thorough brushing and vacuuming: Physical removal of dead algae and debris from pool surfaces and bottom.
  • Filter cleaning or replacement: Filters clogged with algae require backwashing, chemical cleaning, or replacement.
  • Water balancing: Re-establishing correct pH, alkalinity, and sanitizer levels after algae removal.

Professional green pool cleanup in Fort Lauderdale often involves follow-up visits and monitoring to ensure the algae is fully eradicated and water conditions remain stable. Attempting DIY fixes without proper expertise can exacerbate the problem, leading to longer recovery times and increased costs.
