9. Saltwater Pool Conversion and Maintenance Services

Saltwater pools have gained popularity in Fort Lauderdale for their gentler water chemistry and lower chlorine requirements. Specialized saltwater pool service involves unique maintenance needs that differ from traditional chlorine pools.

Saltwater pool services include:

  • Installation of salt chlorinators that convert salt into chlorine safely and efficiently
  • Monitoring salt concentration levels to optimize sanitation without corrosion risks
  • Regular cleaning and inspection of corrosion-prone components like metal fittings and pumps
  • Troubleshooting and repairing chlorinator cells and control systems
  • Balancing pool water chemistry to prevent scale buildup and maintain swimmer comfort

John Martinez explains, “While salt pools offer fewer pungent chemical odors and a softer feel, they do require expert upkeep to protect equipment from accelerated salt corrosion, particularly in coastal climates like Fort Lauderdale.”

10. Green Pool Cleanup and Restoration Services

Occasionally, pool owners in Fort Lauderdale face severe algae blooms or chemical failures leading to a ‘green pool,’ where algae growth and organic debris render the water murky and unsafe. Specialized green pool cleanup services provide comprehensive restoration to bring pools back to sparkling condition.

Green pool restoration typically involves:

  • Shock treatments with high doses of chlorine or alternative sanitizers to eliminate algae
  • Thorough brushing and vacuuming to remove biofilm and organic residues
  • Water testing and chemical balancing over multiple days to stabilize parameters
  • Filter cleaning or replacement to remove trapped contaminants
  • Addressing underlying causes such as circulation issues or equipment malfunctions to prevent recurrence

For instance, a local gym with a publicly accessible pool faced a post-storm algae outbreak that turned the water green and forced closure. Professional green pool cleanup services restored water clarity and sanitized the pool within 72 hours, allowing rapid reopening in compliance with health regulations.

Preventing Green Pools: Maintenance Tips

  • Regular monitoring and adjustment of sanitizer levels
  • Ensuring proper water circulation and filtration function
  • Prompt removal of debris to reduce nutrient availability for algae
  • Scheduled professional inspections to detect and warn of early algae growth
