9. Pool Leak Detection Services: Protecting Your Investment

Hidden leaks can silently damage your pool and surrounding property, leading to costly water bills and structural issues. In Fort Lauderdale, where the water table is high and soils can shift, professional leak detection is a critical service.

How Professional Leak Detection Works

  • Electronic Leak Detection: Utilizes pressure testing and electronic listening devices to pinpoint leaks in pipes and fittings.
  • Dye Testing: Dye is introduced near suspected leak areas to visually identify escaping water.
  • Infrared Thermography: Detects temperature differences in pool surfaces, highlighting water escaping from the pool shell.

Early leak detection helps pool owners avoid significant damage to decks, landscaping, and even home foundations, which are vulnerable in Fort Lauderdale’s sandy soil conditions.

Signs You May Need Leak Detection

  • Unexpected pool water level drops despite no heavy use or evaporation
  • Wet spots or pooling water around the pool area
  • Algae blooms despite balanced chemicals, indicating potential water replacement issues
  • Visible cracks or deterioration of pool shell or deck

Saltwater pools are popular for their softer water feel and lower use of chlorine. However, they require distinct maintenance due to salt corrosion potential and equipment impact.

  • Regular salt level monitoring and adjustment
  • Inspection and maintenance of salt generators

Over time, pool surfaces degrade due to sun, chemicals, and use. Resurfacing restores the pool’s structural integrity and aesthetic appeal.
