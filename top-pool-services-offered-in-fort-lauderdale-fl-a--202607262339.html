Specialized Services: Pool Leak Detection and Waterproofing

Leak detection is a highly specialized service critical to maintaining pool integrity, particularly in Fort Lauderdale where soil conditions and salt air can exacerbate structural vulnerabilities.

Detecting leaks early can prevent significant damage such as foundation erosion, decking instability, and high water bills. Professional technicians use advanced methods such as:

  • Pressure testing of plumbing lines to identify hidden cracks
  • Electronic leak detection equipment utilizing acoustic sensors
  • Dye testing for visual identification of water escapes
  • Infrared thermography to detect moisture behind pool surfaces and walls

Once a leak is located, various waterproofing options can be applied to fortify your pool, such as:

  • Application of waterproof membranes beneath plaster finishes
  • Specialized sealants compatible with saltwater environments
  • Crack injections using epoxy or polyurethane formulas designed for wet and submerged conditions

Engaging companies with local expertise ensures that waterproofing solutions are suited to Fort Lauderdale’s unique climate and geographical soil factors, maximizing the lifespan of repairs.
