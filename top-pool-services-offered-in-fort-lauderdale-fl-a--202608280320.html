1. Pool Cleaning Fort Lauderdale: The Foundation of Pool Care

Pool cleaning is the most common and visible service offered. It involves physical removal of debris, skimming, vacuuming, brushing pool walls and floors, and emptying skimmer baskets. In Fort Lauderdale, frequent cleaning is essential year-round due to landscapes laden with palms, flowering trees, and coastal winds that deposit leaves and dirt.

A typical professional pool cleaning service includes:

  • Surface skimming to remove leaves and insects

7. Saltwater Pool Service FL: Specialized Maintenance for Salt Chlorination Systems
