Regular Spring and Cable Inspections

The torsion and extension springs, as well as the supporting cables, hold tremendous tension and must be inspected regularly. A snapped spring or frayed cable can cause doors to fall suddenly, resulting in severe injury or damage.

  • Certified professionals use specialized tools to assess spring condition and measure tension.
  • They replace worn cables and springs with industry-standard components rated for the specific door weight.
  • Technicians also educate homeowners on warning signs such as sagging doors, loud noises, or uneven movement.

In Davie, where everyday humidity can accelerate rust and corrosion, regular spring and cable checks are especially crucial.
