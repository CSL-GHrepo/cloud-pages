Red Flags to Watch For

  • Unsolicited Offers: Beware of knock-on-door contractors claiming urgent repairs are needed after storms.
  • Demand for Full Payment Upfront: Legitimate companies typically require payment upon completion or offer phased billing.
  • No Written Estimate or Contract: Lack of documentation increases risks of miscommunication and unexpected charges.
  • Pressure Tactics: High-pressure sales to rush acceptance of repairs or upgrades can indicate scam activity.

Best Practices to Avoid Scams

  1. Research companies through online reviews, BBB ratings, and local referrals.
  2. Ask for proof of licensing, insurance, and bonding before agreeing to service.
  3. Request itemized written estimates covering parts, labor, and any additional fees.
  4. Verify emergency service credentials, especially for last-minute calls.
  5. Trust your instincts; reputable providers maintain transparency and professionalism.

Preparing Your Garage Door for Hurricane Season in Davie: Best Practices

Given Davie’s location in a hurricane-prone region, preparing your garage door ahead of storm season is essential to protect your home and ensure structural integrity. Garage doors are often the most vulnerable entry points during high winds, and their failure can trigger extensive damage to the entire property.

Key Preparatory Measures

  • Schedule a Professional Inspection: Have a certified technician assess the door for weaknesses, damaged weather seals, or worn hardware.
  • Install Wind-Load Reinforcements: Stronger brackets, reinforced panels, or additional bracing improve the door’s ability to resist hurricane-force winds.
  • Test Auto-Reverse and Safety Sensors: Ensure all safety features function correctly to avoid accidents during an emergency closure.
  • Secure Remote Controls and Backup Systems: Keep extra batteries and test battery backup systems to maintain operability during power outages.
  • Clear Surrounding Debris: Remove leaves, branches, and other potential projectiles that could impact or jam the door.
