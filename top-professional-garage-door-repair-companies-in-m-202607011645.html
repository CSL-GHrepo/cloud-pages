Maintaining Your Garage Door: Tips From Miramar’s Top Technicians

Preventing garage door issues is often easier and less costly than repairing major damage. Here are some professional maintenance tips:

  1. Schedule routine inspections every 6-12 months to assess springs, cables, tracks, and opener function.
  2. Lubricate moving parts with appropriate garage door lubricants to reduce wear caused by Miramar’s salty coastal air.
  3. Test your garage door's balance by manually lifting it halfway—if it doesn’t stay, call a professional immediately.
  4. Replace weather stripping regularly to maintain energy efficiency and prevent water intrusion.
  5. If your opener is older than 10 years, consider a timely garage door opener installation upgrade for better technology and safety features.

These small steps can drastically extend the lifespan of your door and reduce the need for emergency garage door repair services.
