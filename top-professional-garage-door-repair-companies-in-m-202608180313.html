Safety Considerations and Compliance in Garage Door Repairs

Safety is paramount when repairing or installing garage doors. Licensed Miramar technicians adhere strictly to safety codes and standards to protect clients and technicians alike.

Key Safety Standards and Regulations

  • ANSI/BHMA A156.10 and A156.19: Standards covering the safe operation and installation of residential and commercial garage doors.
  • OSHA Guidelines: Ensuring workplace safety for commercial garage door installations and repairs.
  • Florida Building Code Compliance: Mandated adherence to statewide construction and safety codes, including wind load requirements critical in storm-prone areas.

Common Safety Hazards Addressed by Professionals

  • Spring tension risks: Improper handling of torsion or extension springs can cause serious injury.
  • Electrical hazards: Correct wiring and grounding of motorized openers prevent shocks and fire risks.
  • Door imbalance and sudden closures: Regular adjustment prevents accidental slamming, minimizing injuries and property damage.
