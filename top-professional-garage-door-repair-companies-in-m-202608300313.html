11. Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Miramar’s Climate Year-Round

Miramar’s unique weather patterns—hot, humid summers combined with seasonal storms—require thoughtful garage door care practices to prolong service life and maintain safety standards.

Seasonal Protective Measures

  • Spring inspection: After heavy rains and storms, check for water intrusion, rust, and damage to tracks and door panels.
  • Summer lubrication: Apply weather-resistant lubricants to moving parts to reduce overheating risks.
  • Fall sealing: Ensure all weather-stripping and bottom seals are intact prior to cooler, wetter months.
  • Winter assessment: Although winters are mild, humidity remains high, so look for signs of mold or mildew near the garage entrance.

Routine Visual Inspections

Set monthly reminders to:

  • Listen for unusual noises such as grinding or squeaking which can indicate bearing or spring issues
  • Test auto-reverse function to ensure child and pet safety
  • Check balance by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door halfway—if it doesn’t stay in place, springs may require adjustment
  • Observe tracks for dirt accumulation, debris, or misalignment
