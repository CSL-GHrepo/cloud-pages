Seasonal Garage Door Care Tips for Miramar Residents

Miramar’s subtropical climate means garage doors undergo unique seasonal challenges. Tailoring care practices by season can help maintain optimal performance:

Spring and Summer

  • Inspect door seals and weather stripping to prevent moisture intrusion during heavy rains and hurricanes.
  • Check and clear tracks from debris brought by seasonal storms.
  • Regularly lubricate metal parts to counteract humidity-induced corrosion.
  • Test opener battery backup systems in preparation for potential power outages.

Fall and Winter

  • Examine insulation quality to improve energy efficiency during cooler months.
  • Verify door balance and operation to avoid spring fatigue issues later in the year.
  • Clean and treat moving parts to prevent rust buildup from residual salt exposure.

Adhering to these seasonal care steps can greatly reduce the risk of unexpected garage door failures and costly repairs for Miramar homeowners.
