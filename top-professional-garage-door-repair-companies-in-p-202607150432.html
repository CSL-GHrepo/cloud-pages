Maintenance Tips from Plantation’s Garage Door Professionals

While professional inspections are essential, there are steps homeowners and property managers can take regularly to maintain their garage doors and signal when professional help is needed.

  • Monthly Visual Inspections: Check for visible signs of wear such as frayed cables, cracked panels, and rust spots.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Use silicone-based lubricants on springs, hinges, rollers, and tracks every 3-6 months to reduce friction and noise.
  • Test Safety Mechanisms: Make sure the auto-reverse sensor reacts properly by placing an object in the door’s path during closing to verify functionality.
  • Tighten Hardware: Seasonally check bolts and screws to prevent loosening caused by door vibrations.
  • Clean Tracks: Remove debris and buildup inside tracks to avoid jammed rollers and uneven door movement.
  • Weather Seal Inspection: Evaluate the bottom seal for cracks or gaps, replacing it to maintain insulation and keep out pests.

These preventive measures recorded annually can reduce emergency repair calls and prolong the effective functioning of garage door systems in Plantation’s challenging climate.
