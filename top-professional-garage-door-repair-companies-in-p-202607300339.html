DIY Garage Door Repairs: Risks and When to Call a Professional

While some minor garage door maintenance tasks like lubricating hinges or cleaning tracks can be done safely by homeowners, many repairs require professional expertise due to the dangers and complexity involved.

Why Avoid DIY for Critical Repairs?

  • Spring Repairs: Garage door springs are under extremely high tension and can cause serious injury if mishandled.
  • Electrical and Opener Issues: Incorrect wiring or configuration can damage your system or void warranties.
  • Alignment Problems: Improper adjustments can result in door malfunctions and premature hardware failure.

Safe Tasks Homeowners Can Manage

  • Visual inspections for obvious damage
  • Cleaning tracks and removing debris
  • Replacing batteries in remote controls
  • Lubricating rollers, hinges, and springs with manufacturer-recommended lubricants

When in doubt, consulting a licensed Plantation garage door technician ensures safety and preserves the integrity of your garage door system.
