Why Regular Garage Door Testing Matters: Safety and Insurance Perspectives

Garage doors can be surprisingly hazardous if not maintained and tested regularly, especially in busy families or commercial settings.

Child and Pet Safety Considerations

Automatic garage doors move with powerful force. Without properly functioning sensors and reverse mechanisms, they pose crushing risks to children, pets, and even vehicles.

  • Photo-Eye Sensors: These safety beams detect obstacles and instantly stop or reverse door closure.
  • Force Settings: Modern openers allow for precise adjustment of closing force, minimizing injury risk.
  • Monthly Functional Tests: Homeowners are encouraged to test reversal sensors monthly by placing an object under the closing door to ensure it responds correctly.
