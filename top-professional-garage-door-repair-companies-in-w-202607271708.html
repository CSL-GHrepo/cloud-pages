Common Garage Door Problems and Their Professional Solutions

Understanding common garage door issues can help you recognize when it’s time to call a specialist rather than attempt risky DIY repairs. Below are typical problems serviced by Weston’s top garage door repair companies, along with the professional approaches they use for effective resolution.

Broken or Worn Springs

Garage door springs are critical for lifting and lowering the door safely. Over time, springs can fatigue, snap, or wear unevenly—causing the door to become heavy, unbalanced, or completely inoperable.

  • Professional repair: Technicians replace springs with the exact type and tension rating recommended by the manufacturer.
  • Why not DIY: Springs are under extreme tension and improper handling can cause severe injury or damage.
  • Preventative service: Routine inspection and lubrication to extend spring life and maintain smooth performance.

Malfunctioning Garage Door Openers

Openers control the mechanical motion of the door and rely on electrical components and sensors that can fail or become misaligned.

  • Expert diagnostics: Professionals identify issues such as faulty circuit boards, worn gears, and sensor misalignment using specialized tools.
  • Repairs and upgrades: Replacement of key parts or entire openers with energy-efficient, smart-compatible models to enhance reliability and convenience.
  • Safety testing: Ensuring openers comply with safety standards to prevent accidents.

Uneven or Noisy Door Operation

Doors that roll unevenly, jam, or produce excessive noise often have issues with rollers, tracks, or hinges.

  • Track alignment and cleaning: Professionals straighten bent tracks and remove debris to ensure smooth operation.
  • Roller replacement: Installing nylon or steel rollers matched to door weight and usage for quieter, longer-lasting performance.
  • Lubrication and tension adjustment: Reducing friction and wear on moving components.
