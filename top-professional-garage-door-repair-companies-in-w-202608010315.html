Seasonal Garage Door Care: Adjusting Maintenance for Weston’s Weather

Weston’s tropical climate—marked by intense sun, high humidity, occasional tropical storms, and hurricanes—means garage doors experience unique wear patterns. Tailored seasonal maintenance helps to maximize performance throughout the year.

Summer & Early Fall: Preparing for Storm Season

  • Inspect for Rust and Corrosion: Check metal components such as springs, cables, and hinges for salt or moisture-related corrosion and apply protective lubricants or replace parts as needed.
  • Weatherproof Seals: Re-seal and replace worn weather stripping to keep out wind-driven rain and debris during heavy storms.
  • Storm-Ready Reinforcement: Verify the integrity of reinforced panels or add storm shutters recommended for hurricane-prone areas to protect doors against high wind damage.
  • Test Auto-Reverse and Safety Sensors: Ensure sensors are clean, aligned, and functional to prevent accident risks during storm-related power surges or outages.

Winter & Early Spring: Combating Humidity and Temperature Variations

  • Lubricate Moving Components: Cold and humidity can stiffen rollers and springs; routine lubrication reduces friction and wear.
  • Track Alignment Checks: Temperature changes can cause metal to expand and contract, potentially misaligning tracks; professional inspection prevents door jamming.
  • Opener Battery & Power Backup Testing: Test backup power systems in the opener for outages common during winter storms.

Scheduling at least two maintenance visits annually with trusted Weston garage door specialists can help owners avoid costly emergency repairs and maximize door longevity.
