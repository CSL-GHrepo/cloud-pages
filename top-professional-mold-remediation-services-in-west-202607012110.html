Top Mold Prevention Tips in West Palm Beach

Prevention is the best defense. Certified mold remediation experts recommend these key strategies to keep your property mold-free:

  • Maintain indoor humidity levels below 60% using dehumidifiers and air conditioning.
  • Fix any water leaks or drainage issues promptly.
  • Ensure crawl spaces and basements are well ventilated.
  • Clean and repair gutters regularly to prevent water accumulation near your foundation.
  • Use mold-resistant building materials when renovating or repairing damaged areas.

Implementing these simple but effective measures can significantly reduce the risk of mold growth year-round.
