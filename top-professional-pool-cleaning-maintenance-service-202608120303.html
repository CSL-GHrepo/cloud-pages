Specialized Services for Saltwater Pools in Fort Lauderdale

Saltwater pools have surged in popularity due to their softer feel and reduced chlorine smell, but they require unique maintenance approaches tailored to Fort Lauderdale’s environmental factors.

Corrosion Control and Equipment Care

The presence of salt accelerates corrosion on metal components of the pool, including ladders, pumps, and heaters.

  • Regular Inspection: Specialized technicians conduct frequent checks for galvanic corrosion and metal degradation.
  • Use of Anti-Corrosion Products: Application of protective coatings and sacrificial anodes to safeguard metal parts.
  • Salt Cell Maintenance: Routine cleaning and calibration of salt chlorinator cells to ensure efficient chlorine generation without premature wear.

Salt Level Management and Water Balance

Maintaining optimal salinity is critical. Fort Lauderdale’s rainy weather can dilute salt concentration, while evaporation can increase it. Imbalanced salt levels impact chlorine production and water quality.

  • Automated Salinity Monitors: Devices that alert for salt level adjustments to maintain consistent chlorine output.
  • Customized Chemical Treatments: Balancing pH and alkalinity to counteract saltwater’s tendency toward alkalinity shifts.
"Proper saltwater pool maintenance in coastal climates demands a meticulous approach — naively treating it like a traditional pool leads to equipment failure and costly damage," cautions Lisa Ramirez, specialist in saltwater pool systems with AquaSure Services.
