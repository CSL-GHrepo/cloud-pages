Seasonal Pool Maintenance Strategies Specific to Fort Lauderdale

While Fort Lauderdale’s subtropical climate enables year-round pool use, seasons and extreme weather events such as hurricanes still dictate tailored maintenance approaches.

Spring and Summer Preparation

  • Complete pool opening procedures after winter or extended inactivity, including filter deep cleaning and chemical shock treatments
  • Pre-hurricane season inspections focusing on securing pool equipment, trimming surrounding vegetation, and verifying drainage around the pool area
  • Increasing frequency of water testing and cleaning during peak swimming months due to higher bather loads

Fall and Winter Considerations

  • Adjusting chemical maintenance to compensate for cooler evening temperatures that may affect algae growth
  • Performing partial pool covers or solar covers to reduce debris while retaining heat
  • Post-hurricane assessment for damage or contamination with quick remediation to restore safe pool use
