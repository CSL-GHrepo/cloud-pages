How to Maintain Your Pool Between Professional Visits

You can maximize the benefits of professional pool care by following simple maintenance tips in between service visits:

  • Regularly check and adjust chemical levels using test strips or kits.
  • Skim the surface daily to remove leaves and debris.
  • Keep pool equipment clean and free of visible blockages.
  • Maintain proper water levels to ensure pump and filter efficiency.
  • Schedule professional service appointments seasonally, especially before and after heavy usage periods.

By combining personal upkeep with professional services, your pool’s lifespan and safety significantly improve.
