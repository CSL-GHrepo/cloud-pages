Why Choosing the Right Property Type Matters in 2024

Market trends 2024 show a strong buyer preference for move-in ready homes in accessible neighborhoods. This is driven by increasing mortgage rates and affordability challenges, which lead buyers to favor homes that require minimal further investment post-purchase.

According to the National Association of Realtors, in 2023, 65% of homebuyers preferred recently renovated properties, emphasizing “turnkey” condition. For fix and flip investors, this means properties that are structurally sound but cosmetically outdated offer the best balance between cost and profit.

Top Property Types to Target for a Profitable Fix and Flip

1. Single-Family Residential Properties

  • Why: These homes appeal to the largest segment of buyers—including families and first-time homeowners.
  • Typical fixes: Kitchen and bathroom updates, fresh paint, landscaping, flooring replacements.
  • Market appeal: Often located in established neighborhoods or emerging suburbs with good schools and amenities.

Example: A 3-bedroom single-family home purchased below market due to outdated interior finishes can be renovated with modern upgrades costing 10-15% of the purchase price, resulting in a 20-30% net profit on resale.

2. Distressed Investment Properties in Emerging Neighborhoods

  • Why: These homes often come at significant discounts and sit in areas where community improvements are driving appreciation.
  • Renovation scope: Usually deeper renovation projects including roofing, HVAC updates, and structural repairs.
  • Tip: Ensure solid comps showing market value after renovation justifies the upfront cost and timeline.

Example: In regions seeing urban revitalization, a distressed home requiring $30K in work purchased at 40% below market can often resell for 25-35% profit after renovation and holding costs.

3. Multi-Family Residential Properties (Duplexes and Triplexes)

  • Benefit: Multi-family units diversify income streams and appeal to investors or owner-occupants looking for rental income.
  • Renovation focus: Updating kitchens, bathrooms, and common areas while addressing any deferred maintenance.
  • Strategy: Smaller multi-family units typically offer better liquidity than larger apartment complexes for fix and flip investors.

Example: Flipping a duplex in a popular rental market with two updated units can provide both resale profit and potential interim rental income to offset holding costs.

Additional House Flipping Tips for Selecting Properties in 2024

  • Focus on Cosmetic Renovations: Cosmetic updates like paint, flooring, lighting, and fixtures have higher ROI than extensive structural changes.
  • Analyze Market Trends: Pay attention to local inventory, buyer demographics, and economic indicators before purchasing.
  • Consider Holding Costs: Properties requiring long renovation cycles can erode profits through holding costs. Choose projects with manageable timelines.
  • Leverage Local Expertise: Work with real estate agents and contractors familiar with your target market for best price and quality results.

Regional Considerations When Picking Fix and Flip Properties

The ideal property type can vary based on regional dynamics and local economic health.

  • In rapidly growing metro areas, single-family homes near good schools remain highest in demand.
  • Rural or semi-urban areas may see more opportunity in distressed properties where prices are lower, but flips require a careful cost analysis.
  • Some regions have stricter renovation codes and longer permitting processes—important factors to consider in your property flipping strategies.
