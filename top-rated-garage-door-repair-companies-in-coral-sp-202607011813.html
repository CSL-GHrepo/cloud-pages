Practical Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Coral Springs, FL

According to the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission, garage door systems cause an estimated 20,000 injuries annually, underscoring the importance of professional maintenance. Industry studies show regular maintenance can increase operational lifespan by 25% (Door & Access Systems Manufacturers Association, 2023).

Homeowners and business owners in Coral Springs should consider these key maintenance tips:

  • Schedule bi-annual professional inspections and tune-ups to catch wear or damage early.
  • Test garage door safety sensors monthly to ensure proper function; sensor malfunctions are a common cause of door failures.
  • Lubricate moving parts seasonally using manufacturer-approved lubricants.
  • Avoid DIY repairs on springs and cables—these require specific expertise and tools.
  • Replace obsolete garage door openers with modern, energy-efficient models for enhanced reliability.
