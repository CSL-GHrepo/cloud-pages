Coral Springs Garage Door Maintenance Tips to Avoid Emergency Repairs

Preventative maintenance reduces unexpected failures and extends the life of your garage door. Coral Springs garage door companies recommend these actions:

  • Inspect and lubricate moving parts every six months, especially springs and cables.
  • Test the door’s balance and auto-reverse safety features regularly.
  • Clean and clear tracks to prevent debris buildup.
  • Schedule professional tune-ups annually to catch minor issues early.
  • Replace worn weatherstripping to maintain energy efficiency and prevent moisture damage.

Following these steps can save money and avoid the frustration of emergency garage door repair.
