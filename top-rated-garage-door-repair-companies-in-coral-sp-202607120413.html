Enhanced Safety Features

Modern garage doors come equipped with multiple safety mechanisms beyond the traditional automatic reverse function. Some of these advanced features include:

  • Photo-Eye Sensors: Infrared beams detect objects or people in the door’s path, stopping the door from closing unexpectedly.
  • Manual Disconnect Systems: Allow quick disengagement of the opener for emergency manual operation during power failures or mechanical issues.
  • Battery Backup Systems: Ensure garage doors operate during power outages, a critical feature in hurricane-prone areas like Coral Springs.
  • Safety Edges: Pressure-sensitive strips along door bottoms trigger reversal on contact with obstacles.

Expert garage door companies can evaluate your current system’s safety and recommend necessary upgrades to meet the latest industry standards.
