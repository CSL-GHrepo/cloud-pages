DIY Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Coral Springs Homeowners

While professional servicing is essential for safety and complex repairs, homeowners can perform several simple maintenance tasks to prolong their garage doors’ lifespan between professional visits.

  • Regular Cleaning: Keep tracks, rollers, and door surfaces free from dirt and debris to prevent operational issues.
  • Lubrication: Apply silicone-based or garage door-specific lubricants to hinges, rollers, and springs every six months to reduce friction and noise.
  • Visual Inspection: Look for signs of wear such as frayed cables, rust on springs, or loose bolts and fasteners.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Mechanism: Place an object like a 2x4 in the door’s path and close the door to ensure it reverses immediately for safety confirmation.
  • Check Weather Stripping: Replace damaged or worn seals to maintain insulation and prevent pests.

“Taking small proactive steps in maintenance can save homeowners substantial repair costs later, but never attempt spring replacement or cable adjustments yourself due to safety risks,” cautions Tony Ramirez, Coral Springs garage door technician.
