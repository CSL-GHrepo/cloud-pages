DIY Garage Door Maintenance Tips: What You Can Safely Do at Home

While many garage door repairs require professional skills and tools, some routine maintenance tasks can be safely handled by homeowners to extend the life of their door and reduce repair frequency.

  • Visual Inspections: Regularly check for loose bolts, worn rollers, and damaged cables.
  • Lubrication: Use a silicone-based lubricant on rollers, hinges, and tracks every six months to prevent wear and reduce noise.
  • Clean Tracks: Remove debris and dirt to ensure smooth operation, avoiding use of grease that can attract grime.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Feature: Place an object under the door to check if it reverses automatically when closing, ensuring safety compliance.
  • Weatherstripping Replacement: Replace cracked or damaged seals to keep out moisture and pests.

Important Safety Note: Never attempt to adjust or replace springs and cables yourself, as these are under high tension and can cause serious injury. Always call professional garage door technicians for these repairs.
