Maintenance Tips from Davie’s Garage Door Experts for Longevity and Reliability

Aside from emergency repairs and replacements, routine maintenance is crucial in extending the lifespan of your garage door system. Local technicians recommend proactive care customized for Davie’s climate challenges.

  • Monthly Visual Inspections: Look for signs of wear, rust, or loose hardware.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Use weather-resistant lubricants on springs, hinges, and rollers every 3–6 months.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Feature: Place an object under the door to verify sensors detect obstructions and safely reverse operation.
  • Clean Tracks and Door Surfaces: Remove debris and wash doors with mild detergent to prevent dirt buildup and corrosion.
  • Check Weatherstripping: Replace cracks or gaps to improve insulation and prevent water intrusion.

Following these steps can minimize emergency repairs and maintain smooth functioning year-round, fitting well with the preventive maintenance packages many Davie companies offer.
