1. Broken Garage Door Springs

Springs bear the weight of your garage door, enabling smooth lifting and lowering. Over time, frequent use, corrosion due to South Florida's humidity, or sudden breakage can cause springs to snap.

  • Professional Solution: Technicians identify the type of spring (torsion or extension) and replace it with a correctly rated new spring. Attempting to repair springs yourself can be hazardous due to the tension involved, making expert service essential.
  • Example: Davie Garage Pros recently helped a homeowner suffering from a snapped torsion spring caused by rust build-up, restoring full function the same day.

2. Misaligned or Bent Tracks

Garage door tracks guide the door’s movement. When these become bent, dented, or misaligned due to accidents or debris buildup, the door may bind or come off track.

  • Professional Solution: Skilled technicians will straighten or replace damaged sections and ensure the door operates smoothly with minimal friction.
  • Example: Allstar Garage Doors often receives calls after storms in Davie have caused track deformities, offering quick adjustments that prevent costly replacements.

3. Faulty Garage Door Openers

Remote control issues, motor failures, or sensor misalignment can render an electronic opener useless, leaving the door stuck in one position.

Maintaining Your Garage Door: DIY Tips and Professional Support

While many garage door issues require professional intervention, regular maintenance by the homeowner can prevent common malfunctions and extend door life.

Routine Maintenance Recommendations

  • Keep tracks clear of debris and gently clean them with a dry cloth.
  • Lubricate moving parts with silicone-based or garage door-specific lubricants at least twice a year.
  • Test door balance by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door halfway, ensuring it holds steady without dropping.
  • Inspect weather seals and replace them when cracked or brittle.
  • Check safety sensors monthly to verify they are aligned and unobstructed.

When to Call a Professional

Some issues are too complex or dangerous to handle without expertise, such as:

  • Broken springs or cables under tension
  • Motor or electrical system failures
  • Door off its track or sagging panels
  • Persistent noises despite lubrication
  • Safety sensor malfunctions affecting operation

Regular professional inspections can catch developing problems early. Most Davie garage door companies offer maintenance contracts that include seasonal checks and priority emergency service.
