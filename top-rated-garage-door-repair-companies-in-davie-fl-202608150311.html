Seasonal Garage Door Care: Tips from Davie Experts

Seasonal maintenance helps prevent major repairs and extends the life of your garage door system. Davie’s distinct weather patterns necessitate a tailored approach to upkeep.

Spring and Summer Maintenance

  • Inspect and Lubricate Moving Parts: Heat and humidity can cause grease to degrade quickly. Regular lubrication of springs, rollers, and hinges reduces friction.
  • Check Weather Seals: Ensure bottom seals and weather stripping are intact to prevent moisture intrusion during summer storms.
  • Sensor Calibration: Heavy rains can misalign sensors. Testing and recalibration ensure safety features operate correctly.

Fall and Winter Maintenance

  • Test Door Balance: Temperature fluctuations can affect door alignment. Proper balance reduces strain on the opener.
  • Clean Tracks and Remove Debris: Clearing leaves and dirt prevents blockage and uneven wear in colder months.
  • Schedule Professional Inspections: Before the rainy season, ensure springs and cables are in good condition to avoid sudden failures.

Expert providers in Davie often offer seasonal service packages that remind customers and provide timely inspections to keep garage doors in top condition year-round.
