Tips for DIY Garage Door Troubleshooting Before Calling a Professional

While professional service is essential for many garage door repairs, some minor issues can be safely diagnosed or temporarily fixed by homeowners to avoid unnecessary service calls or to prepare for a technician’s visit.

Basic Checks You Can Perform

  • Inspect Sensors: Make sure the safety sensors on either side of the door are clean and aligned. Obstructions or misalignment can cause the door to remain open or reverse unexpectedly.
  • Test Balance: Disconnect the opener by pulling the emergency release cord, then manually move the door halfway. If it doesn’t stay in place or is too heavy, springs may need adjustment or replacement.
  • Listen for Noises: Grinding, squeaking, or banging sounds often indicate a need for lubrication or hardware tightening.
  • Check the Remote Batteries: Replacing remote control batteries is a simple fix for non-responsive openers.
