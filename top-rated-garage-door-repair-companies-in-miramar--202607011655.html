Preventative Maintenance: The Key to Avoiding Unexpected Garage Door Failures

According to the International Door Association, regular garage door maintenance can extend your door’s lifespan up to 10 years.

"Simple maintenance like lubricating springs, checking cables, and balancing the door can prevent costly emergency garage door repair in Miramar," advises Samantha Kerr, a licensed garage door technician in Broward County.

Regular Maintenance Checklist:

  • Inspect and lubricate rollers, hinges, and springs.
  • Check alignment and balance of the door.
  • Test auto-reverse safety features.
  • Clean and inspect garage door tracks.
  • Verify remote control and sensor functionalities.
