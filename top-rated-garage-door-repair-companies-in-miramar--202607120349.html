Preparing Your Garage Door for Hurricane Season in Miramar

Given Miramar’s location in a hurricane-prone region, proactive preparation is critical to safeguarding your property. Garage door repairs and reinforcements should be prioritized ahead of hurricane season to minimize damage risks.

Steps to Hurricane-Proof Your Garage Door

  • Inspect and repair structural damage: Ensure brackets, tracks, and panels are secure and free from corrosion.
  • Install hurricane-rated doors or reinforcements: These feature stronger materials and impact-resistant designs certified to meet IBC standards.
  • Upgrade hardware: Replace standard hinges and bolts with heavy-duty fasteners to withstand high wind pressures.
  • Verify opener battery backup: During power outages, a battery-operated opener allows emergency access and exit.
  • Consult professional technicians: Schedule a pre-storm inspection with Miramar-certified experts to identify vulnerabilities well before the hurricane approaches.
