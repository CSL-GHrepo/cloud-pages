Tips to Maintain Your Garage Door and Avoid Expensive Repairs

Regular maintenance is the key to extending your garage door’s lifespan and avoiding emergency fixes. Here are actionable tips tailored for Miramar residents:

  • Monthly Visual Inspection: Look for rust, worn cables, misaligned tracks, and cracks in panels.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Use garage door lubricant every six months to keep springs, rollers, and hinges functioning smoothly.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Features: Ensure safety sensors are working properly to prevent accidents.
  • Balance Check: Disconnect the opener and raise the door manually; it should stay open halfway without dropping to prevent spring issues.
  • Seasonal Cleaning: Remove debris and dirt from tracks and bottom seals to prevent wear, especially after rainy seasons common in Miramar.

Following these steps can reduce calls for emergency garage door repair Miramar and extend the life of your equipment.
