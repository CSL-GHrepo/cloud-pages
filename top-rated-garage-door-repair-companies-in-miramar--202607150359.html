Understanding Garage Door Safety Features and Their Maintenance

Modern garage doors include multiple built-in safety features designed to protect users and property. Proper understanding and upkeep of these safety mechanisms are vital to prevent accidents and meet regulatory requirements.

Auto-Reverse Mechanism

The auto-reverse safety feature causes the door to automatically reverse if an obstruction is detected while closing. It protects people, pets, and objects from injury or damage.

  • Testing: It’s recommended to test this feature monthly by placing an object under the door during closure to ensure it reverses promptly.
  • Common issues: Sensors may become misaligned or dirty, causing failure of the auto-reverse system and necessitating professional recalibration.

Photoelectric Sensors

Located near the base of garage door tracks, photoelectric sensors use infrared beams to detect movement and obstructions. These sensors must remain clean and precisely aligned for proper function.

  • Maintenance routine: Cleaning sensor lenses and realigning sensor brackets is a simple yet crucial task recommended by all Miramar garage door repair companies.
  • Warning signs: Flickering indicator lights or the door failing to close fully can signal sensor malfunction.

Manual Release and Emergency Features

In case of power failures or mechanical problems, the manual release mechanism allows users to disengage the door from the opener and operate it manually.

  • Inspection tips: Testing emergency release cords periodically ensures they haven’t been damaged or tangled during routine use.
  • Safety consideration: Technicians should verify that manual overrides adhere to safety codes and aren’t compromised by improper modifications.
