Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door Between Professional Service Visits

While professional maintenance is irreplaceable, there are several simple upkeep steps Miramar residents can take to prolong their garage door’s life and ensure safe operation:

  • Monthly Visual Inspections: Check for obvious damage, wear in springs, cables, rollers, and tracks.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply garage door-specific lubricant to rollers, hinges, and springs every 2-3 months to reduce friction and noise.
  • Keep Tracks Clean and Aligned: Remove debris and ensure tracks are vertical and free from dents or bends.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Safety Mechanisms: Place an object in the door’s path during closing to verify the door reverses promptly.
  • Tighten Loose Hardware: Periodically check and tighten bolts and screws to maintain structural integrity.
  • Balance the Door: Disconnect the opener and manually raise the door halfway; a properly balanced door should stay in place without assistance.

Consistently performing these simple checks and minor maintenance tasks complements professional service and can reduce emergency repair calls.
