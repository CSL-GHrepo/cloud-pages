How to Maintain Garage Doors During Hurricane Season in Miramar, FL

Miramar’s location makes it particularly vulnerable to hurricanes and tropical storms. Homeowners and businesses must prepare their garage doors to withstand these weather events. Proper maintenance and pre-storm preparation can reduce damage and costly repairs afterward.

Pre-Storm Inspection Checklist

Before hurricane season, the following should be checked by trusted garage door technicians or diligent property owners:

  • Reinforce Hardware: Inspect and tighten all bolts, screws, and brackets.
  • Evaluate Door Material: Steel doors generally offer better hurricane resistance than wooden doors.
  • Test Automatic Locks and Openers: Ensure they function properly and can secure doors firmly.
  • Inspect Weather Seals: Replace cracked or worn weather stripping to prevent water intrusion.
  • Look for Corrosion: Humidity and salt air can corrode metal parts; timely replacement prevents failure.

Why Hurricane-Rated Garage Doors Matter

Doors that meet Miami-Dade County or Florida Building Code impact standards withstand strong winds and debris. Specialized repair companies in Miramar often advise upgrading to hurricane-rated garage doors or retrofitting existing doors with reinforcements. These doors feature:

  • Heavy-duty panels and frames
  • Stronger track systems designed to resist uplift
  • Impact-resistant glass options, if windows are included

Investing in these solutions safeguards property and helps reduce insurance premiums, making them a smart long-term investment for Florida home and business owners.
