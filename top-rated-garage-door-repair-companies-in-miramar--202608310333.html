Extending Your Garage Door’s Lifespan: Tips from Miramar Experts

Beyond professional maintenance plans, Miramar garage door technicians recommend several homeowner best practices that help maximize door life and save money in the long term.

  • Routine Visual Inspections: Check tracks, rollers, and panels monthly for signs of wear or damage.
  • Lubrication: Apply garage door-specific lubricants to moving parts every 6 months to reduce friction and noise.
  • Test Safety Features Quarterly: Engage safety sensor tests and auto-reverse functions regularly to ensure effectiveness.
  • Weatherstrip Maintenance: Replace seals as needed to prevent water intrusion and heat loss, crucial in Miramar’s humid environment.
  • Avoid DIY Repairs of Critical Parts: Springs and cables require special tools and training; improper handling can cause serious injury.

Following these tips in conjunction with scheduled professional maintenance helps residents avoid sudden breakdowns and costly emergency repairs.
