Expert Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Pembroke Pines

Industry studies show that regular maintenance can reduce garage door repair costs by 25-30% annually. Following are expert tips to keep your system in prime condition:

  1. Schedule Semi-Annual Inspections: Check springs, cables, rollers, and safety features to catch wear early.
  2. Keep Tracks and Rollers Clean: Remove debris and lubricate moving parts with manufacturer-recommended products.
  3. Test Auto-Reverse Safety Sensors: These must function perfectly for security and code compliance.
  4. Balance the Door Properly: An unbalanced door strains the opener and other components, leading to premature failure.
  5. Upgrade to Hurricane-Rated Doors: Pembroke Pines’ location makes storm-resistant doors a wise investment.
