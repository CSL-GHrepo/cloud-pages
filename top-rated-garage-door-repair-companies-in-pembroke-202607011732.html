Expert Tips for Garage Door Maintenance in Pembroke Pines

Routine maintenance is key to extending the life of your garage door and avoiding costly repairs. Industry experts recommend the following steps for homeowners in Pembroke Pines:

  1. Lubricate Moving Parts: Use silicone-based lubricants on rollers, hinges, and springs every six months.
  2. Inspect Safety Features: Test auto-reverse mechanisms regularly to ensure child and pet safety.
  3. Check Weather Seals: Replace worn seals to prevent moisture damage—especially important in Florida’s humid climate.
  4. Tighten Hardware: Garage door vibrations can loosen bolts; tightening them helps avoid breakdowns.
  5. Schedule Professional Inspections: Annual check-ups with licensed technicians catch issues early before they escalate.
