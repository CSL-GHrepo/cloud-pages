Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Pembroke Pines

Proper maintenance can prevent major problems and extend the life of your garage door system. Follow these expert tips:

  1. Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply garage door lubricant to rollers, hinges, and springs at least twice a year to reduce wear and noise.
  2. Inspect Weatherstripping: Check seals regularly to keep out moisture and debris, crucial in the humid Florida environment.
  3. Test Door Balance: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway; it should stay without assistance. A failing balance means springs or cables may need professional adjustment.
  4. Clean Tracks and Hardware: Remove dirt buildup from tracks and tighten loose bolts to ensure smooth operation.
  5. Schedule Professional Maintenance: Even with regular DIY care, hiring local garage door technicians annually ensures problems are identified early.

Industry studies show that preventive maintenance can reduce emergency repair calls by up to 40%, cutting homeowner stress and expense in the long run.
