Common Garage Door Problems Explained by Pembroke Pines Experts

Understanding common garage door issues can help homeowners and business operators recognize when to call in professionals and avoid exacerbating problems:

1. Broken Springs

Garage door springs bear the weight of the door during opening and closing. They can wear out due to metal fatigue or corrosion accelerated by Florida's humid environment.

  • Signs: Loud banging sounds, door imbalance, or doors that won’t open fully.
  • Solution: Immediate replacement by trained technicians as springs are under high tension and dangerous to handle.

2. Misaligned or Damaged Tracks

The metal tracks guide door panels upwards and downwards. Bumps, dents, or shifts caused by vehicle impact or weather events affect door movement.

  • Signs: Jerky movement, scraping noises, or doors getting stuck.
  • Solution: Professional realignment and sometimes replacement of damaged track sections.

3. Sensor Malfunctions

Safety sensors prevent doors from closing on people or objects. Dirt, misalignment, or wiring issues are common causes of sensor failure.

  • Signs: Door reverses unexpectedly or refuses to close.
  • Solution: Cleaning lenses, realignment, or replacement of faulty sensors.

4. Opener Failures

Electronic garage door openers can fail due to motor burnout, wiring problems, or remote control issues.

  • Signs: Door doesn’t respond, partial movement, or remote control failures.
  • Solution: Diagnosis and repair or replacement by technicians skilled in electronic components.
