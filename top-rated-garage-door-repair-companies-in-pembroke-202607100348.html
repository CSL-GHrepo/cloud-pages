Constant exposure to heat and moisture can cause:

  • Corrosion of Metal Components: Springs, cables, hinges, and rollers may rust faster than in drier climates.
  • Warping and Swelling: Wooden or composite doors can absorb moisture, leading to deformation and operational issues.

Local garage door experts recommend materials and finishes designed for Florida’s climate, such as galvanized steel, aluminum with powder coating, or composite doors resistant to moisture damage.
