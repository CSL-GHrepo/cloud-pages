Preparing for Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance in Pembroke Pines

Due to the region’s climate, scheduling seasonal maintenance can help prevent unexpected failures and extend your garage door’s operational life.

Best Times for Maintenance Checks

  • Spring: Address humidity-related issues from the rainy season such as rust and swelling.
  • Fall: Prepare doors for cooler, drier weather by inspecting springs and electronic components.
  • After Storms: Hurricanes or heavy winds may cause misalignments or impact damage that requires immediate inspection.

Maintenance Checklist Provided by Professionals

  • Lubricate moving parts and hinges with manufacturer-recommended lubricants.
  • Inspect and tighten spring tension to avoid sudden breaks.
  • Test safety sensors and auto-reverse features to ensure compliance with federal standards.
  • Examine weather stripping for wear and replace as needed to improve insulation.
  • Clean garage door tracks and clear debris to ensure smooth sliding and reduce noise.
