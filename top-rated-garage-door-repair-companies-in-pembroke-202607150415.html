Common Garage Door Problems and When to Call a Professional

Recognizing signs of garage door distress early can save homeowners time, money, and security risks. While some minor issues may seem manageable, many require expert attention to avoid injury or damage.

Warning Signs of Garage Door Trouble

  • Slow or Jerky Movement: Indicates worn rollers, misaligned tracks, or opener motor issues.
  • Unusual Noises: Grinding, squeaking, or banging sounds often signify the need for lubrication or mechanical repairs.
  • Door Appears Off-Track or Crooked: Misaligned tracks can cause the door to operate unsafely or get stuck.
  • Door Does Not Fully Open or Close: Could be a sensor alignment problem or mechanical obstruction.
  • Remote Controls or Keypad Failing: Electronic issues may necessitate opener system diagnostics or replacement.

When DIY Repairs May Be Unsafe

While minor lubrication or remote battery replacement can be homeowner tasks, most garage door repairs involve high-tension springs and heavy door panels, posing significant safety hazards.

"Garage door springs can store enough energy to cause serious injury if improperly handled. Always consult licensed professionals for spring replacement or adjustment," warns the National Safety Council.

Calling a certified garage door repair company ensures professional tools, knowledge, and safety protocols are in place to handle complex repairs without risk.
