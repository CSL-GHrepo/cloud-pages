Preparing for Seasonal Changes: Garage Door Tips for Pembroke Pines Residents

Although Pembroke Pines enjoys a mild winter, seasonal changes still affect garage door performance and maintenance needs.

Pre-Storm Garage Door Preparation

Hurricane season highlights the importance of securing and maintaining garage doors to withstand high winds and potential debris impact.

  • Inspect Weather Seals and Tracks: Replace worn seals and ensure tracks are clear to maintain airtight closure.
  • Reinforce with Wind-Resistant Hardware: Installation of heavy-duty brackets and braces improves door stability during storms.
  • Test Emergency Manual Release: Ensure you can operate the door manually in case of power outages.

Humidity and Heat Management

High humidity can cause metal components to rust and wood doors to swell. Residents should schedule regular maintenance checks before the summer months to lubricate parts and check door alignment.

Tips Post-Hurricane or Storm

After storms, inspect garage doors for damage such as dents, misalignment, or salt residue buildup. Prompt repairs prevent escalating issues and maintain home security.
