Maintenance Tips for Pembroke Pines Homeowners: Extending the Life of Your Garage Door

Regular maintenance is key to preventing small issues from becoming costly repairs. Here are actionable, expert-backed tips to keep your garage door running smoothly year-round:

  • Lubricate moving parts: Apply a high-quality silicone or lithium-based lubricant to rollers, hinges, and springs every six months to reduce friction and noise.
  • Inspect and tighten hardware: Check bolts, screws, and brackets quarterly to ensure everything remains secure and aligned.
  • Check door balance: Disconnect the opener and manually raise the door halfway—if it doesn’t stay put, the springs may require adjustment.
  • Clean and clear tracks: Remove debris from tracks and wipe them clean to prevent obstruction and uneven door movement.
  • Test safety features: Regularly test auto-reverse and photo-eye sensors for proper functionality to ensure safe operation.

Insider Advice: John Michaels, a South Florida veteran technician, emphasizes scheduling professional inspections at least once a year, especially before hurricane season to prepare for extreme weather impacts.
