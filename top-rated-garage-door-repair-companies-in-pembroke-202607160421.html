Tips for DIY Garage Door Maintenance in Pembroke Pines

While professional servicing is recommended annually, homeowners can take simple, safe steps to keep garage doors in optimal condition between inspections. Below are some expert-recommended maintenance tasks perfect for Pembroke Pines residents:

  • Visual inspections: Check the door panels, track alignment, rollers, and springs for obvious damage or wear.
  • Lubricate moving parts: Apply silicone-based lubricant to tracks, rollers, hinges, and springs every six months to reduce friction and noise.
  • Test safety sensors: Confirm automatic reversal sensors are clean and functional by placing an object under the door and closing it.
  • Balance the door: Disconnect the opener and manually operate the door; a balanced door stays stationary when opened halfway.
  • Clear debris: Remove leaves, sand, and dirt from the tracks and bottom seal to prevent obstruction.

However, homeowners should avoid DIY repairs involving spring replacements or opener motor servicing, as these jobs require specialized tools and expertise to ensure safety.
