Understanding Common Garage Door Problems and Their Solutions

Recognizing typical garage door issues can help homeowners act promptly, potentially preventing more costly repairs down the road. Here are common problems encountered by Pembroke Pines residents and how top-rated companies resolve them:

Broken Springs

Garage door springs bear the weight of the door and assist with smooth opening and closing. Over time, springs can wear out or snap, leading to a malfunctioning door that might become dangerous.

  • Symptoms: Loud banging noises, door stuck in open or closed position, door feels heavy.
  • Solution: Professional replacement is required—DIY attempts can cause injury due to high tension in springs.
  • Maintenance Tip: Regular inspections can identify worn springs before failure.

Misaligned or Damaged Tracks

Garage door tracks guide the door upward and downward. When damaged or misaligned, they can cause the door to jam or operate unevenly.

  • Symptoms: Door makes grinding noises, door moves crookedly, visible gaps or bends in the tracks.
  • Solution: Realigning tracks or replacing bent sections ensures smooth operation.

Faulty Garage Door Sensors

Safety sensors prevent the door from closing on people or objects. Dirty or misaligned sensors can cause the door to stop or reverse unexpectedly.

  • Symptoms: Door refuses to close, opens after closing halfway, blinking sensor lights.
  • Solution: Cleaning lenses, realigning sensors, or replacing faulty wiring.

Worn Rollers and Hinges

Rollers and hinges allow the door to glide smoothly along tracks. Worn or damaged components cause noisy or jerky movements.

  • Symptoms: Creaking or grinding sounds, door movement feels rough.
  • Solution: Lubrication, replacement of rollers or hinges if damaged.
