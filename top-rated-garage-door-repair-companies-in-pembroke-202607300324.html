Seasonal Garage Door Care in Pembroke Pines

Given Pembroke Pines’ sub-tropical climate, seasonal changes can affect garage door performance differently throughout the year. Understanding these seasonal care tips can prevent unexpected breakdowns.

Spring and Summer: Managing Heat and Humidity

  • Inspect Weather Seals: Heat can cause seals to crack, allowing moisture and insects inside.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: High humidity accelerates corrosion; frequent lubrication helps maintain smooth operation.
  • Check for Warping: Doors made from wood or composite materials can warp due to moisture—early detection prevents costly fixes.

Fall and Winter: Preparing for Stormy Weather

  • Secure Tracks and Hardware: Strong winds may loosen screws or bend tracks; regular tightening is essential.
  • Test Safety Sensors: Shorter daylight hours require reliable sensors for safe operation in low light conditions.
  • Emergency Power Backup: Ensure opener backup batteries are functional to handle power outages common during storm seasons.

Local garage door professionals often provide season-specific tune-up packages to keep doors in optimal condition year-round.
