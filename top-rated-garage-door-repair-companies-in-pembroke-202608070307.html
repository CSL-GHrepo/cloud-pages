Expert Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Pembroke Pines

Regular maintenance can dramatically reduce the frequency of garage door repairs. Industry experts recommend the following routine care tips, especially important in the humid and storm-prone South Florida environment:

  1. Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply silicone-based lubricant to rollers, hinges, and tracks at least twice per year.
  2. Inspect Door Balance: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door; it should stay open about three to four feet above the ground without falling.
  3. Test Auto-Reverse Mechanism: Place an object under the door to ensure it reverses when it contacts obstacles.
  4. Check Weather Seals: Inspect seals for cracks or gaps that can invite moisture and pests inside.
  5. Schedule Professional Tune-Ups: Have a certified garage door technician perform a complete safety inspection annually.

According to the International Door Association, well-maintained doors can last over 20 years, while neglected ones often require costly repairs well before then.
