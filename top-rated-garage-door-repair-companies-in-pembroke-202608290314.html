Torsion springs, responsible for counterbalancing the garage door’s weight, undergo intense stress daily and are often the first component to fail. Expert technicians apply precise measurements and torque calculations during spring replacement to ensure optimal door balance and smooth operation.

  • Dynamic Load Testing: After spring installation, specialists perform load tests that simulate real-world door cycles to verify consistent tension and lifespan.
  • Customized Spring Selection: Depending on door weight, size, and usage frequency, technicians select springs rated specifically for the customer’s environment, which is crucial in humid Pembroke Pines conditions that can hasten corrosion.
