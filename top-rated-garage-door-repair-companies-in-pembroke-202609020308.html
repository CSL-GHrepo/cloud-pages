Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door Between Professional Visits

While professional inspections and maintenance are vital, homeowners can take simple actions to keep their garage doors in optimal condition year-round.

Monthly Visual Inspections

Check for signs of wear such as:

  • Visible rust or corrosion on springs and hardware
  • Loose or missing bolts and screws
  • Frayed cables or damaged rollers
  • Dirt buildup on sensors and tracks

Cleaning and Lubrication

Regularly clean tracks and rollers with a mild detergent to remove dirt and debris. Use a silicone-based lubricant on moving parts—avoiding grease which can attract more dirt.

Test Auto-Reverse Systems

Monthly, test the auto-reverse safety feature by placing a sturdy object (like a piece of wood) in the door’s path. The door should reverse automatically upon contact, helping ensure family safety.

Check Weather Seals

Inspect the bottom seal and side weatherstripping for cracks or gaps and replace them if damaged to maintain energy efficiency and prevent pest entry.
