Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Plantation, FL

Regular maintenance is the best way to avoid unexpected breakdowns and costly repairs. Follow these guidelines:

  • Inspect the door and hardware monthly for signs of wear or damage.
  • Lubricate moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and springs twice a year.
  • Test the auto-reverse safety feature regularly to ensure proper functioning.
  • Keep tracks free of debris and check for alignment.
  • Schedule a professional garage door maintenance Plantation service annually.

Proper maintenance can extend the life of your garage door by up to 20 years, according to the International Door Association (IDA).

“Scheduling yearly garage door tune-ups is a simple yet effective way to avoid emergency repairs and keep your garage door functioning flawlessly,” advises Sarah Lopez, a regional garage door maintenance expert.
