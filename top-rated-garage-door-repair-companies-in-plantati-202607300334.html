DIY Garage Door Repairs: When to Attempt and When to Call Professionals

Many Plantation homeowners are tempted to handle simple garage door repairs themselves to save money. While minor tasks can sometimes be safely completed without professional help, it’s critical to recognize the limits to DIY repairs to avoid injury and costly mistakes.

Safe DIY Tasks

  • Lubricating moving parts: Applying silicone spray or garage door grease to rollers, hinges, and tracks.
  • Replacing weather seals: Removing and installing threshold or door bottom seals.
  • Cleaning sensors: Gently wiping safety sensors to ensure unobstructed communication.
  • Tightening hardware: Securing loose bolts and screws to reduce rattling noises.

Tasks to Leave to Experts

  • Spring replacement or adjustment: Garage door springs store a tremendous amount of energy and pose serious safety risks.
  • Motor and opener repairs: Electric components require qualified technicians to diagnose and repair safely.
  • Track realignment: Misaligned or bent tracks need professional tools and experience for accurate repair.
  • Complete door panel replacement: Structural integrity and proper balance depend on professional installation.

Plantation’s top garage door companies often provide free or low-cost consultations to help homeowners decide whether a repair qualifies as DIY or requires expert intervention.
