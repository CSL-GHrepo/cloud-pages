DIY Garage Door Repair Tips and When to Call Professionals

While minor garage door maintenance can be performed by homeowners, understanding what tasks require professional expertise is critical to avoid safety hazards or costly mistakes. Here are some expert guidelines from Plantation’s top garage door technicians:

  • Inspecting and replacing weather stripping to improve sealing
  • Cleaning sensors and photo eyes to maintain safety features

Tasks Requiring Professional Repair

  • Track and Roller Repairs: Realigning or replacing bent tracks involves precision to avoid door binding or failure.
  • Emergency Repairs: When doors get stuck or won’t respond to safety features, professional intervention ensures safe and fast resolution.
