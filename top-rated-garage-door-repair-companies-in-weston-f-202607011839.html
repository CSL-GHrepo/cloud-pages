Maintaining Your Garage Door in Weston, FL: Tips for Longevity

Prevention is always better than costly repairs. Here are actionable tips for garage door maintenance Weston residents should follow:

  1. Regular Inspection: Check springs, cables, rollers, and the door balance every 3-6 months.
  2. Lubrication: Use silicone-based lubricant on moving parts to prevent rust and squeaking.
  3. Test Safety Features: Ensure auto-reverse sensors and emergency release mechanisms function properly.
  4. Clean Tracks: Remove debris from tracks to avoid derailment.
  5. Professional Tune-ups: Schedule an annual service with trusted local garage door technicians.

Industry professionals note that proper maintenance cuts emergency repair calls by up to 40%, saving homeowners time and money over the lifecycle of their garage door.
