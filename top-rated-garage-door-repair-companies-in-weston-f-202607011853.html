Professional Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Weston, FL

Prevent costly repairs by performing routine maintenance. Here’s what experts advise:

  • Inspect and lubricate moving parts: Use silicone spray every 6 months to prevent rust.
  • Test safety sensors monthly: Ensure reverse mechanisms work to prevent accidents.
  • Balance your door: Disconnect the opener and lift the door halfway; if it doesn’t stay put, call a technician.
  • Clean weatherstripping: Replace it if cracked to keep out moisture and pests.
  • Schedule professional maintenance at least once a year to catch issues early.

Industry professionals recommend these regular upkeep steps to avoid emergency situations and extend garage door life spans.
