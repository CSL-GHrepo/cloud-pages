Essential Garage Door Maintenance Tips for Weston Homeowners

Prevention is key to avoiding costly repairs and emergency garage door service calls. Here are actionable maintenance tips recommended by industry professionals:

  • Lubricate moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and springs at least twice a year.
  • Inspect weather stripping regularly to ensure a proper seal against Weston’s humidity and rain.
  • Test safety sensors monthly to prevent accidents and keep your door compliant with safety standards.
  • Check door balance — an unbalanced door strains the opener and parts, leading to premature failures.
  • Schedule professional Weston garage door maintenance annually for in-depth inspection and tune-ups.
According to the International Door Association (IDA), “Up to 95% of garage door service calls can be avoided with regular maintenance and timely repairs.”
