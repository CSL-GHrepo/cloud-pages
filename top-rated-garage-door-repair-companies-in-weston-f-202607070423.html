Tips for Extending the Life of Your Garage Door in Weston, FL

Homeowners and business operators can take several proactive steps to reduce the frequency of repairs and extend their garage door’s lifespan:

  • Schedule Routine Professional Maintenance: At least once a year, have a certified technician inspect all moving parts, sensors, springs, and openers.
  • Perform Regular DIY Checks: Monthly visual inspections for loose bolts, damaged weather stripping, or unusual noises can catch issues early.

  • Avoid DIY Repairs on Springs: Garage door springs are under extreme tension; always use a professional to handle spring replacement or adjustment safely.
  • Upgrade to Quality Components: When replacement parts are necessary, insist on OEM or high-grade aftermarket options to maintain door performance.
