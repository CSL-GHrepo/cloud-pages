Common Garage Door Issues in Weston and How They're Resolved

Material Selection for Longevity and Durability

Garage door companies in Weston educate homeowners on the importance of choosing corrosion-resistant materials and finishes:

  • Aluminum Doors: Lightweight and resistant to rust, aluminum doors are favored but may dent more easily than steel.
  • Galvanized and Powder-Coated Steel: Protective coatings shield doors and hardware from salt-induced corrosion, crucial in South Florida’s salty environment.
  • Fiberglass and Composite Doors: Offer durability with minimal maintenance and resist moisture absorption, ideal for humid conditions.

Maintenance Adjustments for Humid Weather

Technicians advise clients on specific maintenance practices to combat moisture damage and oxidation:

  • Frequent Lubrication: Using specialized lubricants that resist washout from rain and prevent rust on moving parts.
  • Inspection of Weather Seals: Regularly checking and replacing deteriorated seals to prevent water seepage and pest intrusion.
  • Addressing Mold and Mildew: Prompt cleaning of door surfaces and components to limit biological growth that could weaken materials.
