Common Garage Door Problems in Weston and How Pros Fix Them

Garage doors encounter a wide range of issues over their lifespan, especially in the challenging Florida environment. Understanding common problems can help you recognize when to call a professional and what to expect during the repair process.

Broken Garage Door Springs

Given the frequent use and local climate impacts, broken springs are the most common failure point. Springs provide the counterbalance needed for smooth door operation, and when one breaks, the door may become heavy, unbalanced, or impossible to lift.

Professionals diagnose spring type—torsion vs. extension—and assess the cycle life used before proceeding with replacement. Using high-quality, corrosion-resistant springs specifically rated for humid environments ensures longevity despite Weston’s weather.

Misaligned or Damaged Tracks

If your garage door is making grinding noises or seems stuck, track misalignment could be the culprit. Tracks sometimes bend from impact (e.g., accidental bumps from vehicles or storms) or loosen due to worn hardware. Technicians carefully realign and straighten tracks and replace any damaged brackets or rollers during service calls.

Malfunctioning Garage Door Opener

Garage door openers often show symptoms such as slow operation, strange noises, or failure to respond to remotes or wall controls. These issues may arise from worn gears, electrical faults, or sensor misalignments.

Professionals run comprehensive diagnostics using industry tools to pinpoint faults before recommending repair or replacement. Upgrading to smart openers with Wi-Fi capabilities is a popular option to enhance convenience and security.

Weather Seals and Insulation Failure

In Florida’s humid climate, weatherstripping and door insulation degrade faster, leading to energy loss, pest intrusion, and water damage inside the garage. Replacing or upgrading seals seasonally is a cost-effective way to maintain a conditioned, protected garage space.

Sensor and Safety Feature Problems

Modern garage doors include safety photo-eye sensors designed to reverse door closure if an obstruction is detected. Dirt, misalignment, or wiring issues can cause these sensors to fail, preventing the door from closing or opening properly.

Experts clean, realign, or replace sensors and test them extensively to ensure compliance with safety regulations.
