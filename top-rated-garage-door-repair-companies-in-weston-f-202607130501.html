Seasonal Maintenance and Preparation Tips for Weston Homes

Due to Weston’s distinct wet and dry seasons, specific maintenance routines ensure garage door functionality year-round.

Pre-Storm Garage Door Checklist

Before hurricane season or major storms, taking preventative steps minimizes damage and expense:

  • Inspect and tighten all hardware, including brackets, bolts, and screws
  • Check the weather stripping at the bottom and sides of the door for deterioration and replace if necessary
  • Lubricate all moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and springs to prevent rust and stiffness
  • Test opener functionality and ensure remote batteries are fresh
  • Clear any debris around the door tracks and drainage paths

Hiring a licensed Weston garage door technician to perform a full inspection and maintenance before storm season is an investment that can save costly repairs later.

Post-Storm Garage Door Inspection Recommendations

After storms, timely inspections can reveal hidden damage:

  • Examine the door panels and frame for dents, warping, or cracks caused by debris impacts
  • Test all electrical components for moisture intrusion causing corrosion or shorts
  • Verify that the door remains balanced and operates smoothly without sticking
  • Inspect sensors and safety reversal systems to ensure proper function

Addressing post-storm repairs quickly avoids long-term complications, including compromised security and increased wear on the opener motor.
