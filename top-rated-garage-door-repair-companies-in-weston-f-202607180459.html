Preparing Your Garage Door for Seasonal Changes in Weston

Seasonal maintenance can significantly extend the life of your garage door system and prevent unexpected malfunctions. In Weston, the climate brings specific considerations for each time of year.

Winter and Rainy Season Maintenance

Though Florida winters are mild, cooler temperatures combined with increased rainfall during the winter months may cause issues such as moisture buildup and weakened lubrication. Recommended actions include:

  • Inspecting weather stripping and seals to prevent water intrusion.
  • Applying moisture-resistant lubricants to springs and rollers.
  • Checking and tightening hardware loosened by temperature changes.

Summer Heat and Storm Preparation

High summer temperatures and hurricane season present risks including thermal expansion and storm damage. Preventative tips include:

  • Ensuring door balance to avoid stress on openers caused by heat-induced material expansion.
  • Verifying emergency release mechanisms are operational in case of power outages.
  • Consulting technicians for potential shutter reinforcement to withstand strong winds.

Scheduling seasonal tune-ups with trusted garage door repair companies can reduce repair costs and improve overall garage door reliability throughout the year.
