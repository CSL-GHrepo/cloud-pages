Preventive Tips from Weston Experts to Keep Your Garage Door Running Smoothly

Maintaining a garage door properly extends its lifespan and reduces risk of unexpected breakdowns. Professionals from top Weston companies recommend these homeowner tips:

  • Regular Cleaning: Remove dirt, dust, and debris from tracks and moving parts to ensure smooth operation.
  • Lubrication: Apply silicone-based lubricants quarterly on hinges, rollers, springs, and opener chains to reduce friction.
  • Inspect Safety Sensors: Test sensors monthly to confirm they reverse the door correctly if obstructed.
  • Test Door Balance: Manually lift the door halfway and observe whether it stays in place. An unbalanced door signals spring or cable issues.
  • Check Weather Stripping: Replace cracked or peeling seals to keep out moisture and pests.
  • Schedule Professional Maintenance: Annual tune-ups by certified technicians detect early problems before costly repairs occur.
