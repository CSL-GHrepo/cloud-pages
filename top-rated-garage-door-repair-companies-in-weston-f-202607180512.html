Common Garage Door Problems in Weston and How Professionals Fix Them

Garage doors face unique challenges in Weston due to the area’s temperate yet humid climate, exposure to heavy rain, and the occasional tropical storm. Understanding common issues helps homeowners anticipate repairs and appreciate the value of professional expertise.

Typical Issues Caused by Humidity and Salt Air

  • Rust and Corrosion: Metal components, including springs, cables, hinges, and rollers, are susceptible to rust due to moisture and salty air. Professionals apply rust inhibitors and recommend stainless steel parts for replacements.
  • Door Swelling and Binding: Wood doors may absorb moisture, causing expansion that leads to sticking or uneven movement. Regular sealing and proper painting are preventive measures that professionals perform.

Problems Resulting from Wind and Storm Damage

  • Track Misalignment: High winds can knock garage doors off track, affecting smooth operation. Certified technicians realign tracks and reinforce anchors to withstand future storms.
  • Broken Springs and Cables: Storm-related stress can cause springs to snap or cables to fray. Replacement requires specialized tools and safety protocols since springs are under high tension.
  • Water Intrusion and Seal Failure: Heavy rains can expose failing weather seals allowing water into garages, leading to damage. Professionals replace seals and assess door thresholds for proper drainage.

Why DIY Repairs Are Risky for Weston Garage Doors

Attempting to fix garage doors without proper training poses significant dangers as well as risks of causing further damage. Common hazards include:

  • Spring Tension Injuries: Garage door springs store immense energy and can cause severe injury if mishandled.
  • Improper Component Replacement: Using incorrect parts or poor installation compromises door balance and operation, reducing safety.
  • Voided Warranties: Unauthorized repairs or installations can invalidate manufacturer warranties and insurance claims.
