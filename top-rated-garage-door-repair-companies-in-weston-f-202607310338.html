Expert Tips to Maintain Your Garage Door in Weston, FL

Regular maintenance significantly reduces the likelihood of sudden failures. Industry studies show that properly maintained garage doors can last up to 30% longer than neglected systems (Source: Darco Garage Doors Annual Industry Report, 2023).

  • Lubricate moving parts: Hinges, rollers, tracks, and springs should be lubricated twice a year with a silicone-based product.
  • Inspect weather stripping: Replace weather seals to block moisture and debris, which can damage door mechanisms especially in Florida’s humid climate.
  • Check door balance: A misaligned door puts extra strain on openers and springs, increasing breakdown risk.
  • Test safety sensors: Ensure auto-reverse sensors work properly to prevent accidents.

Understanding Garage Door Materials: Pros and Cons for Weston Properties

Steel Garage Doors

Steel is one of the most common materials due to its strength and affordability. Benefits include:

  • Resistance to warping and cracking
  • Compatibility with insulation options
  • Low maintenance with powder-coated finishes

Consideration: Steel doors may be prone to rust if not properly finished or if exposed to salty coastal air, requiring routine inspections and touch-ups.

Aluminum Garage Doors

Lightweight and resistant to corrosion, aluminum doors suit Weston's humid climate well:

  • Ideal for modern, contemporary designs with large windows
  • Less prone to dents compared to steel, but can be softer and more vulnerable to impact
  • Maintenance typically involves cleaning and occasional touch-up painting

Wood Garage Doors

Known for aesthetic appeal and customizability, wood doors provide a classic look but require more upkeep:

  • Suitable for residential properties seeking traditional or rustic styles

Fiberglass and Vinyl Garage Doors
