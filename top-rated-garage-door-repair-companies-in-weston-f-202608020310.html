Tips for DIY Garage Door Troubleshooting Before Calling a Professional

While our strongly recommend relying on certified technicians for repairs, Weston residents can take a few preliminary steps to troubleshoot minor issues safely. Here are some practical tips for basic diagnostics:

  • Check the power supply: Confirm the opener is plugged in and that circuit breakers are not tripped.
  • Inspect sensors: Clean photo-eyes positioned near the floor on either side of your door; obstruction or dirt can prevent closing.
  • Test the remote control: Replace batteries or reprogram if remote signals seem intermittent.
  • Look for visible damage: Examine tracks for dents or debris, and ensure rollers are aligned and moving freely.
  • Listen for unusual noises: Squeaking, grinding, or banging sounds often indicate lubrication issues or component wear.

Attempting to repair springs, cables, or track misalignment is strongly discouraged given the potential for injury. Early identification of symptoms by homeowners can shorten service time and reduce repair costs.
