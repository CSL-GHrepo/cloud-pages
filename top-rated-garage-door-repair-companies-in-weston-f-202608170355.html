Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door Between Professional Visits

While professional maintenance is critical, homeowners in Weston can take simple steps to prolong the life of their garage doors and reduce emergency repairs.

  • Regular Visual Inspections: Check for obvious signs of wear, rust, or misalignment monthly.
  • Clean Tracks and Rollers: Remove dirt, debris, and spider webs that may obstruct smooth door movement.
  • Lubricate Moving Parts: Apply garage door lubricant to springs, hinges, and bearings every 6 months.
  • Test Safety Sensors: Ensure auto-reverse safety sensors stop the door when obstructed, preventing accidents.
  • Balance Testing: Disconnect the opener and manually lift the door halfway—if it falls or rises, springs may need adjustment.

Consistent care helps detect minor issues before they escalate, keeping your garage door in top condition in Weston’s demanding environment.
