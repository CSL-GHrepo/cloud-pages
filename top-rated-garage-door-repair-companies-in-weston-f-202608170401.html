Understanding Garage Door Safety Features and Their Maintenance

Safety is paramount when it comes to garage door operation, especially in residential settings where children and pets are present. Top Weston repair companies prioritize inspection and maintenance of critical safety components.

  • Photo-Eye Sensors: These infrared sensors detect obstructions in the door’s path to prevent accidents. Regular cleaning and alignment checks are necessary to maintain functionality.
  • Auto-Reverse Mechanisms: This feature reverses the door if it hits resistance, protecting against injury or damage. Certified technicians test and calibrate these to comply with safety standards.
  • Manual Release Handle: Located inside the garage, this handle disconnects the door from the opener for manual operation during power outages. Proper positioning and functionality checks should be part of routine inspections.
  • Spring Containment and Tension Systems: Garage door springs hold immense force and require expert handling during repairs or replacements to avoid injury risks.
