Pro Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door Between Professional Visits

While certified technicians provide essential maintenance, homeowners and business operators can take simple everyday steps to prolong garage door health and detect problems early:

  • Visual Inspection: Regularly examine cables, springs, rollers, and tracks for signs of wear or damage.
  • Lubrication: Apply a silicone-based lubricant to moving parts every few months to minimize friction and noise.
  • Balance Test: Disconnect the opener and manually move the door to check if it moves smoothly and stays in place at mid-point.
  • Sensor Cleaning: Wipe photo-eye sensors with a soft cloth to ensure obstacles are detected properly.
  • Remote Battery Checks: Replace remote control batteries annually to avoid operational issues.

These routine checks help catch minor issues before they escalate into costly repairs.
