How Seasonal Maintenance Can Prolong Your Garage Door’s Life in Davie

Given Davie’s unique weather patterns, seasonal maintenance is a crucial element often overlooked by homeowners and businesses. Expert garage door companies recommend scheduling professional checkups at least twice a year — ideally in early spring and fall — to prepare for the harshest weather conditions.

Spring Maintenance: Preparing for Heat and Storms

Spring in Davie often brings humid air, thunderstorms, and high heat, which can accelerate wear on motor components, springs, and seals. Essential spring maintenance services include:

  • Inspecting and lubricating moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and tracks to prevent rust and squeaks
  • Checking weather stripping and seals to prevent moisture intrusion and pest infiltration
  • Testing opener functions and backup batteries
  • Verifying sensor alignment and safety features

Proper spring upkeep improves door performance during intense summer solar exposure and rain events.

Fall Maintenance: Preparing for Cooler Temperatures

Fall brings slightly cooler and drier weather, a good time to catch minor issues before winter. Maintenance tasks often focus on:

  • Tightening hardware to address any winter-induced loosening
  • Replacing worn springs or cables before they fail in colder weather
  • Cleaning tracks and drains around the garage door that often collect debris over the year
  • Testing opener electronics for system reliability

Regular seasonal maintenance not only extends your garage door’s lifespan but also reduces costly emergency repairs and unexpected failures.
