Essential Garage Door Maintenance Weston FL Tips from Professionals

  1. Lubricating moving parts such as rollers, hinges, and springs.
  2. Checking and tightening hardware to avoid loosening from vibrations.
  3. Testing auto-reverse safety sensors monthly.
  4. Inspecting for wear and corrosion, especially given Weston's humid climate.
  5. Scheduling professional inspections annually to detect hidden issues early.
