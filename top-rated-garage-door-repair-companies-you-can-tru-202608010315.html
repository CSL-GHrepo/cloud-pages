Safety Mechanisms and Standards: Protecting Your Family and Property

As garage doors are heavy and operate under significant tension, safety features are crucial to prevent injury or property damage. Weston-area experts follow stringent industry safety standards and incorporate the latest technologies to minimize risks.

Photo-Eye Sensors and Auto-Reverse Features

All modern garage doors are equipped with photo-eye sensors installed near the floor on either side of the door frame. These sensors detect obstacles in the door’s path and halt or reverse its motion to prevent crushing incidents. Top repair companies in Weston test and replace these sensors regularly to ensure compliance with safety codes.
