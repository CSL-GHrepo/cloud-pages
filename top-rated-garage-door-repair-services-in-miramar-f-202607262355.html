Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door Between Professional Visits

While professional maintenance is essential, Miramar homeowners can also take simple steps to keep their garage doors running smoothly:

  • Visual Inspections: Regularly check for visible signs of wear or damage, such as frayed cables, bent tracks, or loose hardware.
  • Lubrication: Apply manufacturer-recommended lubricants to rollers, hinges, and springs every six months to reduce friction and noise.
  • Clean Tracks: Remove debris and dirt accumulation from tracks to prevent jamming.
  • Test Auto-Reverse Function: Place a solid object under the door and ensure it reverses upon contact to safeguard family members.
  • Tighten Loose Bolts: Check and tighten bolts and screws without over-tightening to maintain structural integrity.

Following these steps helps extend the interval between professional visits and decreases the likelihood of sudden breakdowns.
