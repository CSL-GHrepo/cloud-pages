Preparing for Seasonal Garage Door Maintenance in Weston

Weston’s subtropical climate introduces seasonal variables that impact garage door functionality. Planning maintenance ahead of weather changes can prevent costly repairs and extend the door’s service life.

Pre-Storm and Hurricane Season Checks

Before hurricane season (typically June through November), garage door repair experts recommend:

  • Inspecting door and frame integrity for cracks or weaknesses
  • Verifying wind load compliance and reinforcing doors as needed
  • Securing weather seals to minimize water ingress during heavy rains
  • Testing automatic openers’ battery backups and emergency manual releases

Proactive preparation helps avoid damage from strong winds and flooding, preserving home security and structural safety during storms.

Post-Season Evaluations

After the storm season, professional inspections can identify any wear or damage caused by humidity or debris—rust formation, spring weakening, and roller misalignment are common issues addressed during these visits.

Summer and Winter Considerations

Although Weston experiences mild winters compared to northern states, temperature fluctuations and seasonal rains require adjustments in lubrication schedules and hardware checks. Summer heat can cause metal components to expand, increasing wear if not maintained properly.
