The Fort Lauderdale Mold Remediation Process: What to Expect

Understanding what happens during mold remediation helps you set realistic expectations and assess company professionalism:

  • Step 1: Assessment and Mold Inspection Services FL – Certified professionals conduct visual exams and use moisture meters and sampling to detect mold colonies.
  • Step 2: Containment Setup – To prevent mold spores spreading, the affected area is sealed off with plastic barriers and ventilation controlled.
