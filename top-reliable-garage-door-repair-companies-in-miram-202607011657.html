Tips for Maintaining Your Garage Door in Miramar’s Climate

Miramar’s humid subtropical climate can speed up wear on garage door parts if neglected. Experts advise routine maintenance to prolong the life of your garage door:

  • Lubricate moving parts like springs, rollers, and hinges every six months.
  • Inspect weather seals for cracks or gaps to keep out moisture and pests.
  • Schedule annual professional inspections to catch small problems early.
  • Test and adjust door sensors regularly for safety and functionality.

Proactive maintenance by trusted garage door technicians not only prevents emergency repair calls but also reduces overall service costs.
