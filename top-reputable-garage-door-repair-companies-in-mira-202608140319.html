Understanding Garage Door Spring Types and Their Maintenance in Miramar

Garage door springs are essential components that counterbalance the door’s weight, facilitating smooth and safe operation. Miramar’s climate places specific demands on these springs, influencing their types and maintenance.

Torsion Springs vs. Extension Springs: What Homeowners Should Know

There are two primary types of springs used in garage doors:

  • Torsion Springs: Mounted above the door, these springs wind and unwind to control door movement. They are generally more durable and provide smoother operation.
  • Extension Springs: Located on either side of the door, these springs stretch and contract as the door moves. They are typically less expensive but may require more frequent replacement.

In Miramar, torsion springs are often preferred due to their superior resistance to humidity-related corrosion and their enhanced safety profile, as they are less prone to sudden failure.

Signs of Spring Wear and When to Call Professionals

Garage door springs endure significant stress and will eventually weaken. Homeowners should watch for warning signs such as:

  • Door becomes difficult to open manually
  • Loud banging noises during operation
  • Door only opens partially or closes unevenly
  • Visible rust, gaps, or broken coils in springs

Attempting to repair or replace springs without proper training and tools is dangerous. Licensed technicians in Miramar offer safe spring repairs that comply with local safety standards, minimizing risks.

Routine Spring Maintenance Tips

  • Regular lubrication to reduce friction and prevent rust buildup
  • Periodic professional inspection for signs of wear and tension loss
  • Prompt replacement of springs before failure to avoid damage to other door components

Following these practices helps prevent unexpected garage door downtime and costly emergency repairs, especially during Miramar’s hurricane season when garage access is critical.
