Repayment terms are usually short—ranging from 6 months to a year. This aligns well with fix and flip projects in Parkland but requires thorough planning. When I advise clients, I emphasize creating a detailed budget that includes all loan costs, renovation expenses, and carrying costs like property taxes and insurance.

Knowing the typical loan-to-value ratio (LTV) helps manage your investment wisely. For instance, many lenders in Parkland will lend up to 70% of the ARV, leaving you some room to cover renovation costs. It’s important to factor in all expenses to ensure your project remains financially viable.
