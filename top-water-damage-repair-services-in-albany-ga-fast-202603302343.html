Preventative Tips to Minimize Future Water Damage in Albany Homes

While repairs are crucial after damage, prevention remains your best defense:

  • Maintain gutters and downspouts to direct water away from your foundation.
  • Install sump pumps in basements prone to flooding.
  • Inspect roofs regularly for leaks or storm-related damage.
  • Seal cracks in foundation walls and windows.
  • Ensure proper landscaping grading to prevent water pooling near your home.
