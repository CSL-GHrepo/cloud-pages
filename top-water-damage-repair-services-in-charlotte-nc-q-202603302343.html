Steps to Take Immediately After Water Damage in Charlotte

To minimize damage and increase the success of restoration, follow these best practices until professional help arrives:

  1. Turn off electricity in affected areas to avoid hazards.
  2. Stop the source of water if possible (e.g., shut off valves, patch leaks).
  3. Remove valuables and electronics to a dry location.
  4. Use fans or dehumidifiers if safe to do so, but avoid using household vacuums on water.
  5. Contact certified water damage contractors Charlotte trusts, such as Water Damage Repair of North Carolina, for immediate assistance.
