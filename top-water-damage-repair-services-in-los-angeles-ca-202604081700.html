Preventative Tips to Minimize Water Damage in Your Los Angeles Home or Business

While restoration is crucial after an incident, prevention is the best defense. Here are practical steps to reduce water damage risks:

  • Regularly inspect plumbing and appliances to detect leaks early.
  • Seal foundation cracks and maintain proper drainage around your property.
  • Install sump pumps and backflow valves in basements to handle excess water.
  • Educate staff or family on emergency water shut-off procedures.
  • Schedule periodic professional inspections especially before rainy seasons.
