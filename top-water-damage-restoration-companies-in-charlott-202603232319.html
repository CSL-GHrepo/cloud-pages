Preventing Water Damage in Your Charlotte Home

Homeowners can take proactive steps to reduce water damage risk, such as installing sump pumps and backflow preventers in basements prone to flooding. Regularly inspecting plumbing for leaks also helps.

Cleaning gutters and downspouts ensures proper drainage during storms, preventing water from pooling around foundations. Landscaping should facilitate water runoff away from the house.

Upgrading old appliances and water heaters reduces the likelihood of leaks or bursts. Additionally, sealing basement cracks and basement windows keeps water out.

Installing water alarms can alert you immediately to leaks or flooding, enabling quick response before major damage occurs.
