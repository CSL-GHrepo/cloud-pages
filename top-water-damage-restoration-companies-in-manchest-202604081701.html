Preventing Future Water Damage in Your Manchester Property

After recovery, take preventive measures to protect your home or business against future water damage incidents:

  1. Regular Inspection: Check your roof, gutters, and foundation for vulnerabilities before storm seasons.
  2. Install Sump Pumps: In flood-prone basements, a sump pump can redirect water away.
  3. Maintain Plumbing: Regular maintenance can prevent pipe bursts and leaks.
  4. Improve Drainage: Ensure landscaping slopes away from your foundation.
  5. Humidity Control: Use dehumidifiers in basements to deter mold growth.
