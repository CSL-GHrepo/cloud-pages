Steps to Take After Water Damage in Shreveport

While waiting for a restoration company to arrive, you can take key steps to minimize damage:

  1. Turn Off Electricity: Avoid electrical hazards by switching off power if safe.
  2. Stop the Water Source: Shut off main water valves if flooding is caused by leaks or broken pipes.
  3. Remove Valuables: Move any irreplaceable or vulnerable items to a dry area.
  4. Ventilate: Open windows and doors if weather permits to promote air circulation.
  5. Contact Local Water Damage Contractors: Call professionals like Water Damage Repair of Louisiana immediately to schedule assessment and service.
