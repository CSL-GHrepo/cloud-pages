Tips for Homeowners and Businesses to Minimize Water Damage

  1. Respond Quickly: Contact restoration professionals immediately at the first signs of water damage.
  2. Turn Off Water Sources: If safe, stop water flow to prevent further flooding.
  3. Document Damage: Take photos and videos for insurance claims.
  4. Ensure Proper Ventilation: Use fans and dehumidifiers once water is extracted.
  5. Monitor for Mold: Watch for visible or musty odors indicating mold growth.
