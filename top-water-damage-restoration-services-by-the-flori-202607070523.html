Environmental and Health Safety Practices During Restoration

The Florida Restoration Pros prioritize environmental responsibility and occupant health throughout every restoration project. Water damage often introduces contaminants and biological hazards, so strict protocols are essential.

Safe Handling of Contaminated Water

Water flooding properties is classified by contamination level:

  • Category 1: Clean water from broken pipes or rain runoff
  • Category 2: Gray water containing moderate contamination such as washing machine discharge
  • Category 3: Black water, highly contaminated water from sewage, floodwaters, or standing water lingering over 48 hours

The Florida Restoration Pros carefully assess water categories to implement appropriate safety measures, including PPE for workers, disposal of contaminated materials, and sanitization of affected areas.

Use of Eco-Friendly Cleaning Agents and Disinfectants
