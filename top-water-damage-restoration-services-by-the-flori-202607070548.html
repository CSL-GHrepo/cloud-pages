Thermal Imaging and Moisture Detection Technologies

Thermal imaging cameras have become an essential component in professional water damage assessments. These infrared cameras detect temperature variations behind walls, under floors, and inside ceilings—helping technicians locate hidden moisture pockets faster and with non-destructive means.
