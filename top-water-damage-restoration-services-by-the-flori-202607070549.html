Understanding Different Types of Water Damage Coverage

Insurance policies vary widely in their coverage of water damage, especially in Florida where flooding can fall under separate flood insurance requirements. The Florida Restoration Pros provide expert guidance to clarify distinctions between:

  • Clean Water Damage: From plumbing leaks or appliance overflows, usually covered under standard homeowner policies
  • Gray Water Damage: Water that may be contaminated, such as from washing machines or sump pumps, with more limited coverage
  • Black Water Damage / Flooding: Floodwaters often require separate flood insurance policies; guidance on claim filing is critical

Understanding these distinctions upfront helps clients avoid surprise gaps and plan mitigation accordingly.
