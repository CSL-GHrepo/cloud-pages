Disclosure Requirements in Parkland, FL

Florida law mandates that sellers disclose certain known defects or issues about the property to the buyer, including but not limited to structural problems, environmental hazards, or previous repairs. Even though cash buyers typically buy homes “as-is,” withholding critical information can lead to legal consequences.

  • Material Defects: Sellers need to openly communicate known defects like roof leaks, plumbing problems, or pest infestations.
  • Mold and Environmental Concerns: Any history of mold or environmental hazards such as proximity to flood zones must be disclosed.
  • Past Insurance Claims: Some buyers might request information about prior insurance claims associated with the property.

Consulting with a real estate attorney before finalizing contracts can help verify that disclosures comply with Parkland and Florida state laws.

Contracts and Sale Agreements Customized for Cash Deals
