Key Mold Prevention Tips for Florida Homes

Once mold is removed, preventing its return is the best strategy. Certified West Palm Beach mold remediation pros recommend these essential tips:

  • Maintain indoor humidity between 30-50% using dehumidifiers or HVAC systems.
  • Fix plumbing leaks and water intrusions promptly.
  • Ensure proper ventilation in kitchens, bathrooms, and basements.
  • Use mold-resistant building materials in renovation projects.
  • Regularly clean and inspect areas prone to moisture, such as crawl spaces and attics.
  • Landscape properly to direct water away from your home’s foundation.

Ignoring these measures increases mold risk and associated health problems.
