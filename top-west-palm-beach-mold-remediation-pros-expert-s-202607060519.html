Key Mold Prevention Tips FL Residents Should Know

Prevention is always better than remediation. Here’s what West Palm Beach residents can do to minimize mold risks:

  • Fix leaks promptly—whether pipes, roofs, or flood-related water intrusion.
  • Maintain indoor relative humidity between 30-50% with dehumidifiers.
  • Ensure proper ventilation in kitchens, bathrooms, and basements.
  • Regularly clean and inspect gutters and downspouts.
  • Use mold-resistant building materials after repairs.
