Protect Your Property: When to Schedule Indoor Mold Inspection FL

Annual or bi-annual indoor mold inspections by local professionals can save thousands by catching mold early. Consider scheduling an inspection if you experience:

  • Persistent musty odors or visible mold growth indoors
  • Recent flooding, plumbing leaks, or roof damage
  • Health symptoms like coughing, allergies, or respiratory irritation
  • High indoor humidity levels exceeding 60%
  • Plan to sell or refinance your home
