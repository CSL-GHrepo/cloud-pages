What does a typical West Palm Beach mold inspection include?

A West Palm Beach mold inspection generally involves a visual examination of your property, moisture mapping using infrared cameras, air quality testing to identify mold spores, and sampling surfaces to determine mold species. Certified mold inspectors also assess sources of moisture—critical in West Palm Beach’s humid climate—to recommend proper remediation.
