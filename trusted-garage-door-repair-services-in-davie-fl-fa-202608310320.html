Common Garage Door Problems and How Professionals Fix Them

Garage doors are complex mechanical systems, and several issues can arise over time that require professional repair. Understanding common problems can help homeowners recognize when it’s time to call in experts, preventing minor malfunctions from turning into costly replacements.

Broken Garage Door Springs

Garage door springs are under immense tension and play a vital role in the door’s ability to open and close smoothly. Springs typically last between 7 to 12 years depending on use and environmental conditions.

  • Signs of Failure: Loud bangs, difficulty lifting the door manually, or the door hanging unevenly.
  • Professional Repair: Technicians use specialized tools to safely release tension and replace torsion or extension springs, ensuring balance and smooth operation.

“Attempting to replace garage door springs yourself is extremely dangerous. Proper tooling and training are required to handle the stored energy and ensure safety.” — Garage Door Repair of Davie technician

Garage Door Opener Malfunctions

Garage door openers are electronic systems that can develop problems due to wear, electrical issues, or outdated components. Common causes include:

  • Faulty sensors causing the door to reverse unexpectedly or not close fully.
  • Worn-out drive belts or chains producing noise or inconsistent motion.
  • Remote control glitches or signal interference.

Professional technicians diagnose opener malfunctions using advanced troubleshooting equipment and can repair or upgrade systems to modern smart openers with Wi-Fi connectivity.

Off-Track Garage Doors

Garage doors run on metal tracks that keep them aligned during movement. Tracks may become bent, loose, or obstructed, causing the door to derail and not function properly.

  • Damage Causes: Improper use, vehicle impact, or accumulation of debris and rust.
  • Repair Approach: Technicians will realign tracks, replace damaged sections, and clean and lubricate for optimal function.

Worn or Broken Cables

Cables help balance the weight of the garage door alongside the springs. Over time, cables can fray or snap, creating hazardous conditions and preventing door movement.

  • Replacement requires careful adjustment to synchronize with springs.
  • Technicians perform complete inspections to ensure all components maintain proper tension and alignment.
