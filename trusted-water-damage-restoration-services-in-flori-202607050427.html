How to Act Immediately When You Face Water Damage

If you experience water damage in Florida, quick and calm action is crucial. Follow these steps while waiting for professionals:

  • Turn off the main water supply if the flooding is due to plumbing leaks.
  • Disconnect power in flooded areas to avoid electrical hazards.
  • Remove as many belongings as possible from the affected area to dry.
  • Document damage with photos for insurance claims.
  • Contact The Florida Restoration Pros immediately for emergency water extraction Florida residents rely upon.

Delaying water damage restoration invites mold growth, structural weakening, and can even affect your family’s health and safety—risks no Florida resident should take lightly.
