Preventing Water Damage Before It Starts

Prevention is always better than cure. Simple routine checks, like inspecting roofs and gutters, can prevent many water intrusion issues. Ensuring downspouts direct water away from foundations and checking for cracks or leaks in plumbing can save a lot of headaches down the line. Installing sump pumps in basements is a proactive measure during high-risk seasons. Additionally, landscaping that slopes away from your house helps keep water from pooling around your foundation. Educating yourself on signs of early water intrusion can help you catch problems early, saving money and reducing stress.
