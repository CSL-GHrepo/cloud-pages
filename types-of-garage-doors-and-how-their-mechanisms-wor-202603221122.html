Maintenance Tips for Garage Doors in Parkland

Regular maintenance can extend your garage door’s lifespan and prevent unexpected breakdowns. In Parkland’s humid environment, lubricating moving parts every six months is highly recommended. Use a silicone-based lubricant on hinges, rollers, and tracks to reduce friction and prevent rust.

Check the balance of your garage door by disconnecting the opener and manually lifting the door. It should stay in place without sagging or drifting. If it doesn’t, it’s time for a professional tune-up. Additionally, inspect the springs for signs of wear or damage—never attempt to adjust springs yourself due to high tension risks.

Clean the tracks periodically to remove dirt and debris that can cause misalignment. Tighten loose hardware, and replace worn rollers to enhance smooth operation and reduce noise.

Scheduling bi-annual professional inspections ensures any potential issues are caught early. This proactive approach saves money and keeps your garage door functioning safely for years.
