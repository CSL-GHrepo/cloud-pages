7. Routine Maintenance Services for Impact Windows

Beyond repairs, routine maintenance is a key service offered by Impact Window Repair of Florida, aimed at maximizing the lifespan and performance of impact windows. Preventive maintenance addresses minor issues before they escalate into costly repairs or replacements, ensuring your windows remain hurricane-ready year-round.

Maintenance tasks typically include:

  • Seal and gasket inspections: Over time, weather seals and gaskets can deteriorate, leading to air and water leaks. Trained technicians check the elasticity and integrity of these seals and replace them as needed.
  • Hardware lubrication and adjustment: Hinges, locks, and handles are lubricated and adjusted for smooth operation, preventing premature wear.
  • Frame cleaning and corrosion prevention: Frames are cleaned using non-abrasive, weather-appropriate products. Aluminum and vinyl frames are inspected for rust or structural weakening and treated accordingly.

  • Drainage pathway clearing: Many frames include designed drainage to prevent water buildup. These pathways are cleaned to prevent blockages and water ingress.

Scheduled maintenance plans help residential and commercial clients keep their windows compliant with Florida building codes and insurance requirements while avoiding emergency repairs during hurricane season.
