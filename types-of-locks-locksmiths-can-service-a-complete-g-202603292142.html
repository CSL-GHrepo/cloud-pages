Understanding Lock & Security Terms in Parkland

Getting familiar with industry terms can help you make informed decisions about your security. For instance, a 'deadbolt' is a lock that provides additional resistance against forced entry. 'Rekeying' involves changing the internal pins of a lock so a new key operates it, without replacing hardware. 'Access control systems' are electronic setups that regulate who can enter specific areas, often used in commercial settings.

Another common term is 'high-security locks,' which are designed to be pick-proof and resistant to tampering. 'Master key systems' allow one key to open multiple locks, ideal for property managers. Knowing these terms helps when discussing security upgrades or repairs with our technicians.

When selecting locks, understanding the difference between mechanical and electronic systems is vital. Mechanical locks are traditional, while electronic locks often integrate smart technology for remote access.
