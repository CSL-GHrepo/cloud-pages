Key Terms in Water Damage Restoration

Moisture meters are devices used to detect hidden water behind walls and under floors. They help ensure thorough drying and prevent mold growth.

Structural drying involves using specialized equipment like industrial dehumidifiers and air movers to remove moisture from building materials. It’s essential for restoring stability post-flood.

Mold remediation is the process of cleaning and removing mold colonies that can develop after water intrusion, safeguarding health and property value.

Water extraction is the initial step where standing water is physically removed from your property using powerful pumps and vacuums, crucial for limiting damage.

Classification of water damage refers to categorizing water sources (clean, gray, black) to determine appropriate cleanup and safety procedures.
