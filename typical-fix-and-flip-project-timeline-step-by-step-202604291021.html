Quick Overview: What Is the Typical Fix and Flip Timeline?

Generally, the real estate investment timeline for a fix and flip project spans between 90 to 180 days. This period includes:

  • 1-2 weeks for property acquisition and due diligence
  • 2-4 weeks planning and design phase
  • 6-12 weeks for renovation and home remodeling phases
  • 2-4 weeks to list and sell the property post-renovation

According to industry studies, projects that strictly adhere to a detailed fix and flip checklist complete renovations 20% faster than those without one.1

Step-by-Step Fix and Flip Steps and Schedule Guide

Breaking down the process into manageable phases streamlines rehab project management and aligns expectations.

1. Property Acquisition and Initial Assessment (Week 1-2)

  • Search and evaluate potential properties based on location, condition, and market value.
  • Conduct inspections to uncover hidden issues—structural, electrical, plumbing, or pest-related.
  • Secure financing through traditional loans, private lenders, or fix and flip funding programs.

Expert Insight: “Rapid acquisition within 10 days can significantly boost profitability by cutting holding costs,” advises Samantha Morris, a seasoned fix and flip investor with over 50 projects completed nationwide.

2. Planning and Permitting (Week 2-4)

  • Detailed scope of work and renovation schedule must be developed.
  • Ordering materials to match home remodeling phases.
  • Submit permit applications if required by local regulations.

Proper planning minimizes scope creep and budget overruns. According to EPA guidelines, environmentally-conscious materials and waste disposal should be factored in during this phase.2

3. Renovation Phase (Week 4-12)

  • Demolition and structural repairs: Address foundational issues first.
  • Systems updates: Upgrade electrical, plumbing, HVAC.
  • Cosmetic improvements: Flooring, painting, cabinetry, fixtures.
  • Final inspections: Ensure all work complies with codes and standards.

Industry professionals recommend a phased rehab project management approach, focusing on critical path tasks to avoid bottlenecks.

4. Marketing and Sale (Week 12-16+)

  • Stage the property for appealing visuals.
  • List the property with competitive pricing based on market analysis.
  • Negotiate offers with an eye on return on investment (ROI).

Typical market exposure lasts 2-4 weeks; however, patience and pricing strategy can speed sale or maximize profit.

Regional Considerations and Risks

Location impacts the property rehab timeline. For example, strict permitting rules or seasonal weather can extend renovation schedules. Investors should analyze local real estate cycles and contractor availability to refine their project timing.

Sample Fix and Flip Checklist for Effective Project Management

  • Property acquisition and inspection complete
  • Financing secured and budget finalized
  • Detailed renovation scope with timelines
  • Contractors hired and permits approved
  • Material orders placed
  • Milestone inspections scheduled
  • Property staged and listed
  • Offers reviewed and final sale closed
