Breaking Down the Fix and Flip Project Phases

Understanding the timeline starts by analyzing the distinct phases of a fix and flip project:

  1. Purchase Phase: Finding, inspecting, and closing on a property usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. Aggressive investors who are prepared can complete this faster.
  2. Planning & Permitting: The fix and flip process timeline includes finalizing renovation plans, securing permits (if needed), and lining up contractors — typically 1 to 3 weeks.
  3. Renovation Phase: This is the core of the project. Depending on the property's condition and scope of work, renovations can take anywhere from 30 days for minor cosmetic updates to 90 days or more for extensive remodeling.
  4. Listing & Selling: Once renovations are complete, the property is staged, listed, and marketed. The average time to sell a flipped house typically ranges from 30 to 60 days, though this timeline can fluctuate based on market supply and demand.

Example: Typical Real Estate Flipping Schedule

  • Week 1-3: Secure loan, identify property, close purchase
  • Week 4-5: Finalize renovation plan, permits, hire contractors
  • Week 6-14: Renovation (painting, flooring, kitchen/bathroom upgrades)
  • Week 15: Professional cleaning, staging, professional photography
  • Week 16-20: Market the property, conduct showings, negotiate offers
  • Week 20-24: Close sale with buyer

Individual project timelines vary because of:

  • Property condition: Older or damaged homes require longer rehab periods.
  • Scope of renovations: Cosmetic updates versus structural rebuilds; the greater the work, the longer the project.
  • Availability of contractors and materials: Delays in supplies or labor shortages can extend timelines.
  • Market conditions: Hot real estate markets often speed up the selling process, while slow markets lengthen it.
  • Permitting processes: Some municipalities have lengthy approval procedures.

What is the average fix and flip duration from purchase to sale?

How long does the renovation phase typically take in a fix and flip?

The typical renovation period for flip projects ranges from 30 to 90 days. Minor cosmetic projects may be completed in a month, while major structural renovations require up to three months or more. Efficient project management and experienced contractors can help keep renovations on schedule.
