Understanding Points and Fees Beyond Interest Rates in Wichman Hard Money Loans

While interest rates often dominate discussions surrounding hard money loans, borrowers in Wichman, NV should also pay close attention to various points and fees that can substantially impact the overall loan cost. These additional charges can sometimes push the effective borrowing cost much higher than the nominal interest rate suggests.

Origination Points Explained

Origination points are upfront fees expressed as a percentage of the loan amount, typically ranging from 2% to 4% in the Wichman market. For example, a $200,000 hard money loan with a 3% origination fee would require the borrower to pay $6,000 at closing. While this fee compensates lenders for processing and underwriting, it effectively increases the cost of borrowing and should be factored into financial analysis.

Other Common Fees Associated With Wichman Hard Money Loans

  • Underwriting Fees: Some lenders charge separate fees to cover the costs of evaluating the property and loan risks.
  • Processing Fees: Administrative costs related to document preparation and loan servicing.
  • Prepayment Penalties: Certain loans include fees if the borrower pays off the loan before the agreed term, restricting flexibility for early refinancing or sale.
  • Inspection Fees: Property inspections required by lenders to assess condition and value, sometimes passed on to the borrower.
“Borrowers sometimes overlook these hidden costs, leading to surprises at closing,” warns a Wichman loan officer. “Effective due diligence requires looking beyond interest rates to the total loan expense.”

Wichman investors can reduce surprises by requesting a detailed Loan Estimate early and comparing all fees among multiple lenders. Transparent fee structures often indicate lender professionalism and better borrower treatment.
