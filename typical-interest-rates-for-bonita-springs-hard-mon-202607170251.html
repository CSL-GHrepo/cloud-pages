Understanding Points and Fees: Beyond the Sticker Interest Rate

Hard money loan pricing involves more than just the stated interest rate. Points and fees can add significant upfront costs and impact total borrowing expenses.

What Are Points?

Points are upfront fees expressed as a percentage of the loan amount, typically paid at closing. Commonly called origination points, these range from 2% to 4% of the loan value.

For example, on a $300,000 loan:

  • At 3 points, upfront fees = $9,000

Additional Fees to Consider

  • Underwriting Fees: Fees charged for loan evaluation and approval, sometimes bundled with origination.
  • Processing Fees: Administrative charges covering documentation and support activities.
  • Late Payment Penalties: Extra charges incurred for missed or delayed payments.
  • Prepayment Penalties: Some lenders, although less common in Bonita Springs, may charge a fee for repaying the loan early.

Calculating the Effective Interest Cost

Evaluating the combined impact of interest plus points and fees provides a clearer picture of your financing cost. This “all-in” rate can be considerably higher than the nominal interest rate, particularly for short-term loans.

“Borrowers should always ask lenders for APR or an equivalent calculation that includes fees to avoid surprises on total loan costs,” recommends financial advisor Sara Chen, specializing in real estate investment financing.
