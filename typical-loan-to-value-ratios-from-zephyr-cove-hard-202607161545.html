Impact of Property Valuation Methods on Loan-to-Value Determinations

Lenders in Zephyr Cove rely heavily on accurate property valuations to set LTV limits. The choice of valuation method can significantly impact the loan amount a borrower might access.

Appraised Value vs. Purchase Price

Hard money lenders typically use the lower of the property’s appraised value or purchase price when calculating LTV. In markets with volatile pricing like Zephyr Cove, this often results in more conservative loans.

After Repair Value (ARV) With Rehab Loans

For fix-and-flip projects, ARV plays a pivotal role. Lenders may base loan amounts on ARV but often discount it by 10-20% to hedge against unforeseen rehab complications or market downturns.

  • Example: A property with a purchase price of $700,000 and an ARV of $900,000 might receive a loan based on 70% of a conservative ARV estimate of $800,000 rather than the full $900,000.
  • Discounting ARV helps lenders mitigate overvaluation risks inherent in renovation projects.

Comparable Sales Analysis

Lenders also scrutinize recent comparable sales (“comps”) in Zephyr Cove to validate appraisals and ensure realism in market value assumptions, affecting LTV limits.
