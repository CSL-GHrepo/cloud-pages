Understanding Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratios in Kendall Hard Money Loans

One of the most critical factors in securing a hard money loan for property flipping in Kendall, FL is the Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio. LTV measures the loan amount in relation to the property’s current or after-repair value (ARV) and guides lenders on the level of risk they are undertaking.

Hard money lenders in Kendall typically offer loans with LTV ratios ranging from 60% to 75%, depending on the property type, condition, and project scope. For example:

  • Purchase LTV: Lenders may finance up to 65-70% of the purchase price.
  • After-Repair Value LTV: Total loan amounts—including purchase price plus rehab costs—are commonly capped around 70% of the ARV.

Maintaining a conservative LTV protects both the lender and investor by ensuring sufficient equity cushion. For instance, if a property’s ARV is estimated at $400,000, a lender offering a 70% ARV LTV would lend up to $280,000. This amount would generally cover both purchase and approved rehab costs combined.

Understanding specific LTV guidelines from your Kendall hard money lender is essential to properly estimate your down payment, loan proceeds, and leverage potential.

Case Study: LTV Impact on Project Feasibility

Consider a flip project in Kendall listed for $250,000, with estimated rehab costs of $70,000 and a projected ARV of $380,000. Based on a 70% ARV LTV:

  • Maximum loan amount = 70% of $380,000 = $266,000
  • Total required financing = purchase + rehab = $320,000
  • If the lender approves only $266,000, the investor must cover the remaining $54,000 out-of-pocket.

Common Loan Fees and Costs Associated with Kendall Hard Money Loans

While speed and flexibility are big advantages of hard money loans, investors must be fully aware of the fees and costs involved:

  • Origination Fee: Typically ranges from 1% to 5% of the loan amount. This fee covers the lender’s costs to process and approve the loan.
  • Underwriting Fee: A separate fee sometimes charged to assess borrower qualifications and property risk.
  • Document Preparation Fee: Covers the cost of preparing loan agreements and closing documents.
  • Inspection Fees: Lenders commonly require third-party inspections of the property before funding and prior to releasing rehab draws. These can range from $300 to $800.
  • Early Payoff Penalties: Some lenders charge a penalty for paying off the loan sooner than stipulated, though many modern lenders waive these to encourage flips.

For a $200,000 loan, total fees might add up to $6,000 to $10,000 upfront. Including these fees in your budget and financial model is imperative to accurately gauge your total investment and profitability.
