What Does Professional Pool Maintenance in Plantation, FL Include?

Understanding what you get for your money is critical. Professional pool cleaning in Plantation involves more than just a quick skim:

  1. Surface Skimming and Debris Removal: To eliminate leaves, insects, grass, and dirt.
  2. Vacuuming and Brushing: Necessary to prevent algae and stains on pool surfaces and tiles.
  3. Water Chemistry Testing: Balancing pH, alkalinity, chlorine, and calcium hardness to ensure safe swimming conditions.
  4. Filter and Pump Inspection: Checking key equipment for proper function and recommending repairs if needed.
  5. Refilling & Chemical Adjustments: Adding necessary chemicals and water to maintain optimal levels.

Plantation’s subtropical climate demands vigilance — pests like algae thrive without proper care. Industry professionals recommend weekly cleanings especially during summer months to maintain safe and sparkling pools.
