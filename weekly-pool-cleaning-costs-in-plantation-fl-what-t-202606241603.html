Quick Overview: What Does Weekly Pool Cleaning Involve?

  • Skimming debris and vacuuming the pool floor
  • Brushing pool walls and steps to prevent algae buildup
  • Checking and adjusting chemical levels (pH, chlorine, alkalinity)
  • Inspecting pool equipment like pumps, filters, and heaters
  • Emptying skimmer and pump baskets

These tasks not only preserve water clarity and safety but also help extend the life of your pool equipment.
