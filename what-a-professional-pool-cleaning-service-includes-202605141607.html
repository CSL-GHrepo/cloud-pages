2. Pool Chemical Balancing Margate

Proper chemical balancing is paramount to ensure clear, sanitary water and a safe swimming environment. A professional cleaner tests and adjusts:

  • pH levels (ideal range: 7.2-7.6)
  • Sanitizer levels (chlorine, bromine, or salt)
  • Total alkalinity
  • Calcium hardness
  • Cyanuric acid (stabilizer)

According to the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), maintaining balanced pool water prevents microbial growth and safeguards swimmer health, reducing waterborne illness risks by over 70%.
