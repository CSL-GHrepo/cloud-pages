2. Pool Water Testing and Chemical Balancing

Water chemistry is the cornerstone of a healthy pool. Margate pools require expert attention because the region’s heat accelerates chemical reactions and biological growth. A typical pool service Margate FL provides includes:

  • Regular Water Testing: Testing pH, chlorine levels, alkalinity, calcium hardness, and cyanuric acid.
  • Chemical Adjustments: Balancing chemicals to prevent algae blooms, cloudy water, and equipment corrosion.
  • Shock Treatments: Applying shock treatments when necessary to restore water clarity and hygiene.
