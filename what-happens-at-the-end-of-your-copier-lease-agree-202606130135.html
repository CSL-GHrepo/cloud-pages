Copier Lease End Options: What Are Your Choices?

As you approach your copier lease expiration date, three primary options typically present themselves:

1. Returning the Leased Copier

If you choose to return the unit, you’ll enter the copier lease return process. This involves coordinating with the leasing company to schedule a pickup, ensuring the copier meets condition standards, and completing the end of lease copier inspection. Any damage or excessive wear may incur fees, so it’s wise to have your in-house technical team or a third-party professional inspect the equipment beforehand.

2. Copier Lease Buyout

Many lease agreements contain a buyout clause, allowing you to purchase the copier at the end of the term for a pre-negotiated price. This can be financially advantageous if the machine still meets your office needs. According to Business Fleet Magazine, 45% of copier lease clients opt for buyout to avoid the complexities of returning equipment and to extend asset usage.

3. Copier Lease Renewal

Renewing your lease may be ideal if you want to upgrade to newer technology or continue using the same equipment. Renewal terms can include new monthly rates, updated service agreements, or flexible lease lengths. Industry professionals recommend starting renewal negotiations 60-90 days before the lease expiration to secure the best terms and avoid downtime.

Step-by-Step: The Copier Lease Return Process and Inspection

Returning a leased copier isn’t as simple as shipping back a box; it’s a detailed process designed to protect both the lessee and lessor. Here’s what usually happens:

  1. Notification: Contact your leasing company at least 30 days before lease expiration to inform them of your return intentions.
  2. End of Lease Copier Inspection: A professional technician will inspect the copier for physical damage, meter overages, and consumable conditions (like toner levels).
  3. Repair or Maintenance: You may be responsible for repairs to bring the copier back to agreed-upon condition standards.
  4. Scheduling Pickup: Coordinate the date and time for the leasing company or third-party carrier to retrieve the unit.
  5. Equipment Return: The copier is returned, and the lease contract is officially closed after paperwork is signed.
  6. Final Billing: Any outstanding fees for repairs, overages, or unreturned accessories will be invoiced.

Being proactive during these expiration steps can save businesses hundreds or even thousands of dollars in unexpected charges.
