Understanding Hard Money Lending: The Basics

Hard money loans are fundamentally different from traditional bank loans. They are:

  • Fast Approval & Funding: Loans can close within days, a significant advantage in competitive markets like Deerfield Beach.
  • Higher Interest Rates: Due to higher risk and short terms, rates are generally higher than traditional mortgages.
