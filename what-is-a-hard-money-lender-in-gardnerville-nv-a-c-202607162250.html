What is a Hard Money Loan?

Unlike conventional loans backed primarily by a borrower’s creditworthiness, a hard money loan is an asset-based financing method centered on the equity of the real estate property. Typically, hard money loans are short-term, ranging from 6 months to 3 years, primarily used for investment purposes, including purchasing, renovating, or refinancing real estate.
