Understanding Lock Repair and Replacement Options

Over time, locks can wear out, become damaged, or even get compromised. Knowing when to repair or replace is essential for maintaining your security. Our technicians at Lockguard Locksmiths always perform thorough assessments to recommend the best solution.

If your lock is jammed, loose, or difficult to operate, it might just need a repair. Sometimes a simple rekey or cylinder replacement restores functionality without the cost of a full lock change.

However, if a lock has been compromised—such as after a break-in or forced entry—the safest option is often a full replacement. We carry a wide selection of locks, from standard deadbolts to high-security systems, suitable for residential and commercial properties.

In terms of cost, lock repair is typically more affordable than full replacement, but the decision depends on the lock’s condition and your security needs. Our team provides honest advice based on your specific situation.
