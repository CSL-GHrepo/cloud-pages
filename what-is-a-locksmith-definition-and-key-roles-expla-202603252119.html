Maintaining Your Locks and Security Equipment

Proper maintenance keeps your locks functioning smoothly and extends their lifespan. Regular inspections can identify issues before they become emergencies.

Lubricating locks with graphite or silicone-based lubricants prevents sticking or jamming. Avoid using oil-based lubricants that can attract dirt.

Cleaning your locks and keys periodically reduces debris buildup. Replace worn keys to prevent damage to the lock mechanism.
