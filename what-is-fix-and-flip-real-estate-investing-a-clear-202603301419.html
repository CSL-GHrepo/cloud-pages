Glossary of Key Hard Money Terms

Asset-based lending: Financing secured primarily by the value of the property rather than borrower credit, allowing for faster approval. — Flip Funding Pro serves the greater Parkland area.

Loan-to-value (LTV): The ratio of the loan amount to the appraised value of the property, influencing approval and rates.

Fix-and-flip loan: Short-term funding designed specifically for purchasing and rehabilitating properties for resale. Usually has higher interest rates but quick approval.

Interest reserve: Funds set aside to cover interest payments during the project, ensuring continuous payment without affecting cash flow.
