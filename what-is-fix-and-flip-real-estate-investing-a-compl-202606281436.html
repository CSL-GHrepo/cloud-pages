Important Terms Every Parkland Investor Should Know

Understanding industry terminology helps you navigate the hard money lending process confidently. Here are some key terms:

ARV, or after repair value, indicates the projected worth of a property after renovations. It’s the basis for determining your loan amount and profit margin.

Interest rate is the yearly percentage charged on the borrowed amount. Hard money loans tend to have higher rates due to their quick, flexible nature.

Loan-to-value ratio (LTV) defines the maximum loan amount relative to the property’s value. For hard money loans, LTVs are usually around 65-75%, depending on the deal.

Bridging loan is a short-term loan that helps investors cover costs until longer-term financing or sale completes. It’s often used in fix and flip projects.

Repayment terms specify how and when the loan is paid back. Many hard money loans in Parkland have interest-only payments during the project and full payoff at sale or refinance.
