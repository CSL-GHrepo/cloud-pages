Understanding Florida’s Regulatory Environment for Hard Money Lending

Investors working with lenders like Flip Funding Pro need to navigate Florida’s specific rules and licensing requirements related to private money lending. Compliance with these regulations is critical for both borrower protection and lender legitimacy.

State Lending Laws and Licensing

Florida requires hard money lenders to adhere to the Mortgage Brokerage and Lending Act (Chapter 494, Florida Statutes), which outlines licensing, disclosure, and operational standards for mortgage brokers and lenders.

  • Lenders must be licensed or exempt under state law to legally fund loans secured by real property.
  • Proper disclosures concerning loan terms, fees, and risks must be provided before closing.

Borrower Protections

Florida law also includes protections such as limits on prepayment penalties, timely notice requirements, and regulations around foreclosure procedures, ensuring borrowers’ rights are respected.
