Frequently Asked Questions

What typical copier lease maintenance services are included?

Typical maintenance services in copier leases include scheduled preventive maintenance, repair and replacement of faulty parts, consumables like toner cartridges, software updates, and sometimes remote troubleshooting assistance. These services ensure that the leased copier operates continuously without costly downtime.

Does leased copier repair coverage usually include on-site support?

Yes, most leased copier repair coverage includes on-site technician visits to quickly address hardware issues. Many lease agreements guarantee a response time—commonly within 24 to 72 hours—to minimize disruption to your operations.

Are consumables like toner always included in copier support agreements?

Consumables such as toner are commonly included in copier lease service plans, but specifics vary by contract. Some agreements limit the quantity or type of consumables provided, while others may charge extra for high-volume use, so reviewing lease copier service terms carefully is important.

How do copier lease service terms affect overall maintenance and support?

Copier lease service terms spell out what maintenance and support actions the leasing company will provide, including coverage scope, response time, and any exclusions. Clear terms help prevent unexpected charges, define responsibilities, and ensure predictable and reliable copier service throughout the lease.
