1. Weekly Cleaning and Debris Removal

Pembroke Pines’ subtropical climate means leaves, insects, and other debris frequently find their way into pools. Weekly service packages typically include:

  • Skimming the Surface: Removing leaves, bugs, and floating debris to enhance water clarity and prevent staining.
  • Brushing Pool Walls: Preventing algae buildup and staining by brushing pool surfaces, especially around steps and corners.
  • Vacuuming: Getting rid of dirt and settled debris to keep the pool bottom spotless.

Consistent weekly visits not only keep your pool visually clean but also reduce the risk of biological growth and equipment strain.

  • Testing levels of chlorine, pH, alkalinity, and calcium hardness.

Effective filtration is a backbone of pool health. A typical package in Pembroke Pines includes:

  • Heater and Chlorinator Checks: Essential for pools with these systems, ensuring they operate safely and efficiently.
