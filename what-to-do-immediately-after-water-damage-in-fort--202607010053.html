1. Prioritize Safety and Stop Further Damage

Before anything else, personal safety and preventing electrical hazards is paramount. Fort Lauderdale homes and businesses often face water damage from storms or plumbing failures, both of which pose real danger.

  • Turn off electrical power: If safe, switch off the main breaker to avoid electrocution risk. Water and electricity are a deadly combination.
  • Shut off the water source: If the flooding is due to a burst pipe or plumbing leak, locate and close the main water valve.
  • Remove valuables and electronics: Safely move any non-waterproof valuables, furniture, and electronics to dry areas.
  • Avoid wading through water: Floodwaters may be contaminated, and unseen hazards like sharp debris or live wires could be present.

4. Practical Water Damage Repair Tips to Mitigate Loss

While awaiting professionals, taking small steps can protect your property:

  1. Document the damage: Take photos and videos for insurance claims. Detailed records support your case during restoration.
  2. Move wet items: Elevate furniture off wet floors or move to dry areas to reduce water absorption.
  3. Open windows and doors: If weather and security allow, ventilation can accelerate drying, but avoid this during humid or stormy conditions.
  4. Remove excess water: Use wet/dry vacuums for small-scale incidents if you have one; otherwise, wait for professional basement water extraction equipment.
  5. Do not attempt to use household fans and heaters: Incorrect use can drive moisture deeper or cause damage.
