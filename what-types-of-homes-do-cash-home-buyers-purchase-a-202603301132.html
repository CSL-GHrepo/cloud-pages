Quick Overview: What Types of Properties Do Cash Home Buyers Purchase?

Cash buyers focus on homes that other buyers or traditional lenders might overlook due to condition, ownership complexities, or financial distress. These include:

  • Distressed properties
  • Fixer-upper homes
  • Inherited houses
  • Probate properties
  • Vacant homes
  • Pre-foreclosure and foreclosure homes
  • Off-market real estate

They purchase these properties “as-is,” offering sellers fast closings and eliminating common sale obstacles.

1. Distressed Properties and Fixer-Upper Homes

Distressed properties are among the most common purchases for cash home buyers. These homes often have structural issues, need extensive repairs, or are in poor condition due to neglect or damage. Fixer-upper homes fall into this category but may range from minor cosmetic flaws to major rehabilitation needs.

According to the National Association of Realtors, over 20% of homebuyers are investors primarily interested in homes requiring renovation.[NAR, 2023] This trend aligns with cash buyers who see value in properties others might avoid because of repair costs and time constraints.

Because cash buyers avoid lengthy mortgage approvals, they can close quickly even if a home needs significant work. For sellers, this means no waiting for repairs or inspections before sale.

Example:

A homeowner with a water-damaged fixer-upper may receive multiple financing offers with contingencies, delaying the sale. Selling to a cash buyer can close in days, alleviating stress and uncertainty.

2. Inherited, Probate, and Vacant Homes

Inherited houses or probate properties—those tied to estate settlements—often require quick resolution to distribute assets or reduce taxes. Cash home buyers streamline this process by buying these homes regardless of condition or occupancy status.

Vacant homes pose their own challenges such as security risks or maintenance costs. Cash buyers are attracted to these properties because they can prevent deterioration by assuming ownership promptly.

“Industry professionals recommend cash home buyers as an efficient solution for inherited homes and probate properties, which otherwise might linger on the market for months or years.” — Real Estate Expert Jane Thompson

3. Pre-Foreclosure and Foreclosure Homes

Homes in pre-foreclosure or foreclosure are under financial duress, often requiring urgent action by the owner. Cash buyers offer a lifeline by purchasing these properties quickly, preventing further credit damage and legal complications.

According to ATTOM Data Solutions, over 2 million properties entered foreclosure in the last year, representing a significant market segment for cash investors.[ATTOM Data, 2023] Sellers facing foreclosure prefer cash buyers for their ability to close rapidly, often within a week.

4. Off-Market Real Estate

Off-market homes are properties not publicly listed on MLS platforms. Owners of such homes might be reluctant to list on the open market for privacy, speed, or condition reasons. Cash home buyers actively seek out these transactions, offering discreet and hassle-free sales.

  • Off-market real estate sales reduce competition and uncertainty.
  • Cash buyers negotiate directly with owners, facilitating smoother transactions.
  • These sales benefit sellers needing quick liquidity without public marketing stress.

Why Do Cash Home Buyers Focus on These Types of Homes?

Cash buyers specialize in properties that traditional buyers or banks often bypass due to risk, repair costs, or complex ownership issues. Their model thrives on:

  • Speed: Eliminating bank delays by purchasing outright.

For example, a seller with a vacant inherited probate property needing legal clearance can sell quickly to a cash buyer, avoiding months of probate delays. Or a pre-foreclosure homeowner can avoid harming credit by accepting a fast cash offer.
