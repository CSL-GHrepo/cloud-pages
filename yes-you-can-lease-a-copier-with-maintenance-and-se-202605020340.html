What to Look For in Copier Lease with Service Agreements

Not all lease agreements are created equal. To ensure you get the best service package, verify the following:

  • Coverage Scope: Confirm the agreement includes parts, labor, and on-site repairs.
  • Response Times: Understand the vendor’s guaranteed repair or replacement times.
  • Consumables: Check if toners, drums, and other consumables are included or billed separately.
  • Technology Refresh Options: See if the lease allows easy upgrades to newer models.
  • Service Levels: Evaluate whether the service matches your business’s uptime requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a copier lease with service?

A copier lease with service typically includes the copier equipment, routine maintenance, repairs, and sometimes consumables such as toner and drums. These comprehensive agreements are designed to keep your equipment running smoothly while bundling costs into a fixed monthly payment.

How do copier maintenance plans benefit a leased copier?

Copier maintenance plans reduce the risk of unexpected downtime by providing regular inspections, cleaning, and repairs. For leased copiers, maintenance plans protect your investment by ensuring consistent performance during the lease term, saving money compared to out-of-pocket repairs.

Can I upgrade my copier during the lease if I need better features?

Many copier leasing agreements include technology refresh options, allowing businesses to upgrade to newer models during or at the end of the lease term. This flexibility ensures you always have access to the latest features without large capital expenses.

Are managed print services different from copier service agreements?

Yes. Managed print services encompass a broader strategy including monitoring and optimizing print fleets, supply management, and usage reporting, while copier service agreements focus primarily on maintenance and repair of the devices themselves. Both can be bundled with copier leasing to maximize efficiency.
